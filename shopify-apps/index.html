Frequently asked questions

How do I get one of these Shopify apps?

Click Get a quote. We send you the install link, help you set up the rules, and quote any changes you need.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Every ready app has a free plan. The price on each card is the Pro plan, billed monthly through Shopify.

Can you change an app for my store?

Yes. Describe what you need in the quote form. Small changes are often done in a few days.

Do the checkout apps work on every Shopify plan?

The checkout rule apps use Shopify Functions, which run on all current Shopify plans. Tell us your plan and we confirm before you install.

What does Made to order mean?

Apps marked Made to order are not built yet. When you order one, we agree the details with you, build and test it, and install it on your store as a private app, usually within one to two weeks.

Need something that is not in the list?

We build custom Shopify apps and change existing ones to fit your store.
