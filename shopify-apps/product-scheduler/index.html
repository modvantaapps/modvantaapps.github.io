Product Expiry Scheduler

Schedule product publish, unpublish, archive and tag changes

$2.99 per month, Pro plan

Get a quote Ask a question

What it does

Product scheduler: publish, unpublish, archive or tag products automatically at a date and time.

Plan product drops, seasonal ranges and limited offers ahead of time. Pick products, a tag or a collection, choose an action (publish to or hide from the Online Store, set Active, Draft or Archived, or add or remove a tag) and set the date and time in your store's time zone. Add an optional revert date to undo it automatically, for example publish on Friday and hide again on Monday. Every run is logged per product, and failed products are retried.

Features

  • Scheduled publish, unpublish, archive, draft or activate for products
  • Add or remove product tags on a schedule, with an automatic undo date
  • Run history with the result for every product, and automatic retries
