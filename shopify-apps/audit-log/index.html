Product History & Audit

Track product history and price history, and revert any change

$4.99 per month, Pro plan

Get a quote Ask a question

What it does

Product history and change log: every product edit with old and new values, and revert.

See what changed on every product and when. The app keeps a change log of each product edit with the old and new value: title, description, tags, status, price, compare-at price, SKU, barcode and stock per location. Open a product's timeline, or view price history and inventory history as charts. Revert a field to its previous value and export the log to CSV. When a product is created, published, unpublished or deleted, the log also shows the staff member or app that Shopify reports.

Features

  • Change log of every product edit with the old value, new value and time
  • Price history and inventory history charts, and a timeline for each product
  • Revert product changes to the previous value, add notes, export to CSV
