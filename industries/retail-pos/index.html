Odoo for Retail & Point of Sale

A faster, safer Odoo POS for shops and chains, connected to stock, accounting and your website.

Talk to us

How we help

We extend the Odoo Point of Sale for retail: what each cashier may do, receipts and labels, payment terminals, loyalty and gift cards, fiscal receipts where the law requires them, and stock shared between shops and the online store.

Start from our ready-made apps, or let us build the parts that are specific to your company.

What the system covers

  • Cashier access rights
  • Receipts and labels
  • Payment terminals
  • Loyalty and gift cards
  • Fiscal receipts
  • Multi-store stock
