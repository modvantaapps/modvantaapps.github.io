How we help

Transport companies need to know which truck carries what, what each trip cost and whether it made money. We build Odoo systems for freight, haulage and courier companies: trip planning, fleet and fuel, GPS tracking, driver pages for phones, expense and fine claims, carrier labels and customer tracking.
