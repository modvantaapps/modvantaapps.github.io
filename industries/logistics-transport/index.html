Odoo for Logistics & Transport

Trips, trucks, drivers, freight and parcels in one system, from the order to the invoice.

How we help

Transport companies need to know which truck carries what, what each trip cost and whether it made money. We build Odoo systems for freight, haulage and courier companies: trip planning, fleet and fuel, GPS tracking, driver pages for phones, expense and fine claims, carrier labels and customer tracking.

Start from our ready-made apps, or let us build the parts that are specific to your company.

What the system covers

  • Trip planning and dispatch
  • Fleet, fuel and maintenance
  • GPS tracker integration
  • Driver mobile pages
  • Freight quotes and invoicing
  • Carrier labels and tracking

Freight Management

Freight Management and Freight Forwarding for air, ocean and land: direct, house and master shipments, ports, airlines, vessels, vehicles, containers and packages, multi-stop routes, Shipment Tracking events, customs declarations, insurance, Incoterms, service charges, customer invoice and vendor bills with margin, AirWay Bill, Website Freight Quote form, CRM lead per freight request and freight dashboard

Industry solutions

Transport Management, Route Management and Trip Management

Courier Management System

Courier Management and Parcel Management for a courier company: courier requests with sender and receiver, weight, dimension and distance price rules, Courier Tracking number with status timeline, Website Courier Tracking page, delivery agents, pickup and delivery runs, proof of delivery signature, courier invoice, customer portal, rating and courier dashboard

Industry solutions

Fleet GPS Tracking

Fleet GPS tracking and vehicle tracking inside Odoo Fleet. Connect a Traccar server, or let GPS trackers and phones send their positions straight to Odoo. See every vehicle on a live map, replay any trip, get geofence, speeding, idling and fuel drop alerts, keep the Fleet odometer up to date from the tracker and open services when maintenance is due by km.
