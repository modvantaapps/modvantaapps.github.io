How we help

Apps for manufacturing & quality

Quality Management System (QMS)

An ISO 9001 style quality management system for Odoo Community: quality control and quality inspection on receipts, manufacturing and deliveries with measurements, tolerances, photos and sampling, quality alerts, non-conformance reports with 5 why and fishbone root cause analysis, CAPA with effectiveness checks, internal and supplier audits, document control, supplier quality ratings, calibration of measuring equipment, customer complaints, risks, management review, electronic signatures and a sealed audit trail. One module.

Quality Change Control

The audit question: what was this part inspected against?

Manufacturing Process Costing

A material, labour and overhead cost sheet on every bill of materials and manufacturing order. Planned cost from the BoM, actual cost from consumption, scrap, work order time, operator rates and vendor bills, variance split into usage and price, by-product costing, WIP value of open orders, and optional journal entries that put labour and overhead into the finished product value.

Inventory & manufacturing

MRP Flexible Production

Record what a manufacturing order really produced, consume the components as planned or in proportion, record by-products and extra outputs, and follow yield, component variance and late work orders.

Equipment Maintenance and Machine Maintenance

Equipment maintenance and machine maintenance (MRO) on Odoo: maintenance checklists with photos and measurements, planned spare parts taken from stock, material requisitions to purchase, job orders with a timer and a technician mobile view, meter based maintenance from hour meters and odometers, gauge alarms, downtime with MTBF, MTTR and availability, a machine repair workshop with labour on the quotation, and warranty claims with vendor credit notes. New: a maintenance request portal where customers and employees send repair requests for their own machines from the customer portal, a public website form or a QR label on the machine, and follow every step. One module, built on Odoo's own Maintenance, Repairs, Inventory, Purchase and Portal.
