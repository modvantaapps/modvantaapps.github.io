Apps for hotels & restaurants

Hotel Management System

Hotel channel manager with iCal sync for Airbnb, Booking.com and VRBO, OTA booking sync through Channex (Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda), a rate and availability calendar with stop sell, and a hotel booking engine with live availability and card prepayment, plus the hotel PMS behind it: hotel reservation and room booking for a guesthouse, hostel or resort, front desk, guest folios, housekeeping, and a night audit with ADR and RevPAR.

POS Kitchen Display System

POS Restaurant Restrictions

Stop silent voids at the till. Removing or lowering a line that was already sent to the kitchen, deleting an order, a discount above your limit, a price change, a table transfer or merge, a refund, cash in / out and closing the session all ask for an approver PIN and a reason, and every request is logged.

Point of Sale

Hotel POS Charge to Room

An in-house guest eats in the restaurant or orders room service and signs the bill to the room. The till picks the room, the server checks the stay, and the sale lands on the guest folio as an invoice to pay at checkout.

Point of Sale

POS Product Modifiers and POS Pizza Builder

POS Product Modifiers for Odoo 19: Extras, Toppings, Sauces, Cooking and Remove options on any product, with minimum and maximum choices, extra prices and free options - on the receipt and the kitchen ticket.

Foodics Connector

Foodics Connector for Odoo 19: every Foodics POS sale in your books - per order or as one daily entry per branch, with VAT 15%, payments by method and a daily check against Foodics.
