Tour and Travel Management

For travel agencies and tour operators: tour packages with a day-by-day itinerary, fixed departures with seats, prices per adult, child and single room with season and group rules, website tour booking with an online deposit, travellers with passports and visas, hotel and transport suppliers with purchase orders, vouchers, a payment schedule, cancellation charges, profit per booking and travel agent commission.
