School Transport Management

Run your school buses on Odoo: bus routes with stops and pickup and drop times, vehicles with driver and attendant, student transport subscriptions with seat limits and a waiting list, daily trip sheets with boarding check-in and drop check-out, parent alerts when a child boards, is dropped or the bus is late, a parent portal with absence reporting, transport fee invoices with sibling discounts, document expiry that keeps unsafe buses off the road, maintenance reminders, incidents and ridership reports.
