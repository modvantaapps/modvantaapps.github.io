School Transport Management

Run your school buses on Odoo: bus routes with stops and pickup and drop times, vehicles with driver and attendant, student transport subscriptions with seat limits and a waiting list, daily trip sheets with boarding check-in and drop check-out, parent alerts when a child boards, is dropped or the bus is late, a parent portal with absence reporting, transport fee invoices with sibling discounts, document expiry that keeps unsafe buses off the road, maintenance reminders, incidents and ridership reports. One module, built on Odoo's own Fleet, Website, Portal and Invoicing.

Field Service Management

Run your field service on Odoo Community: jobs at the customer address on Project tasks, a drag and drop dispatch board, worksheets and checklists per job type, photos, the customer signature on site, materials from the technician van, travel and work time as timesheets, the service report emailed to the customer, invoicing of time and materials, recurring service contracts, a customer portal with visit booking and a phone-friendly technician page. One module.

Cleaning Services Management

Run a cleaning company on Odoo: client sites with their areas and access details, contracts that plan the visits and invoice themselves, crews and shifts, checklists with before and after photos, supplies from stock, quality inspections, client requests and ratings, online quotes with an instant estimate, a client portal and the profit of every contract. One module, built on Odoo's own Sales, Invoicing, Inventory, Employees and Website.
