Dental Clinic Management

Diagnostic Lab Management

Run a diagnostic center, pathology lab or medical lab on Odoo: a test catalogue with reference ranges by age and sex, panels, patient registration, sample collection with barcode labels, home collection, lab results with out-of-range and critical flags, delta checks, two-person pathologist validation, a PDF lab report with QR verification, a patient portal, invoicing per payer and referring-doctor commission. One standalone module - no hospital system needed.

Industry solutions

Retail Pharmacy Management

Run a medical store, chemist or drug store on Odoo: a pharmacy POS that asks for the prescription and offers generic substitutes, a medicine master, batch and expiry on all stock with FEFO, a near-expiry report, expired sales blocked, a controlled drug register that writes itself, batch recalls, supplier returns of expired stock and reorder by sales velocity. One module, built on Odoo's own Point of Sale, Inventory, Purchase and Invoicing.

Hospital Ambulance Management

Video Consultation & Telemedicine

See patients by video from the appointment you already booked. Each video appointment gets a protected room on Odoo Discuss, the patient gets the link and passcode by e-mail, signs consent and pays in a waiting room, and the doctor starts the call with one click.
