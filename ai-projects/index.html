Questions about our AI work

Are these real projects?

Yes. Each one is working software we wrote and maintain. Most of the Odoo ones are also on the Odoo Apps Store, so you can install and try them yourself.

Can you build something like this for us?

Yes. Most client projects start from one of these and are adapted to the client's data, process and language. That is faster and cheaper than starting from zero.

Which AI model do you use?

The one that fits the job and your rules: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or a model running on your own server when data must not leave the company.

What does it cost to run?

You pay the AI provider for what you use, on your own API key. Projects that call a model have a budget cap so the bill can't run away.

Do you work outside Odoo and Magento?

Yes. We also build AI features into custom web apps, Shopify stores and WhatsApp. Tell us what you use.

Want one of these for your business?

Tell us your process and the tools you use. We show you a demo and send a fixed quote.
