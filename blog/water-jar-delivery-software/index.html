Water Jar Delivery Software: Deposits, Empties and Routes

Why a bottled water business needs a container ledger, and how standing orders, returnable bottle deposits, coupon books and run reconciliation work in Odoo.

A bottled water business does not really sell bottles. It lends them. Every 20 litre jar that leaves the van has to come back, and the deposit you charged for it has to match what the customer actually holds. That is the part generic delivery software gets wrong, and it is where water businesses lose money.

This article explains what a water delivery system must track and how our Water Delivery Software does it on Odoo.

The container ledger

The heart of a water business is a ledger per customer and container type: opening balance, bottles delivered, empties collected, written off, count adjustments, and deposits charged, refunded or kept.

In our module, the ledger lines cannot be edited or deleted. A correction is a new line, so the balance always has an audit trail. The stop sheet shows the driver what each customer holds before knocking on the door.

Deposits that are charged only once

When a customer ends up holding more bottles than they paid a deposit for, the deposit for the difference is charged on that stop. A normal exchange (one empty back, one full delivered) is not charged twice.

Refunds are just as strict: you can only refund deposits for bottles the customer no longer holds. The refund is posted as a credit note and paid out in cash, or moved into the prepaid wallet.

Standing orders and nightly planning

Regular customers have standing orders: which days, how many bottles, every N weeks, start and end dates, and pause dates. A nightly job plans tomorrow's runs and suggests the load. Holidays can close one route or all of them.

Routes have a driver, van, capacity, area and working days. You drag stops into order, and the run follows it. The open stops can be opened in Google Maps in that order.

At the door and back at the depot

The driver confirms each stop: bottles delivered, empties collected, coupons taken, cash received, who received it and a signature. Drivers see only their own runs, and drivers using the portal can confirm from their phone.

Back at the depot you count the full bottles, the empties and the cash, then Reconcile. Reconciling invoices the cash stops, registers the cash and posts the stock moves. Any variance between expected and counted is visible straight away.

Coupons, wallets and dispenser rental

  • Coupon books are invoiced at the customer's price list. One coupon is used per bottle, the earliest expiry first, and unused value is shown as a liability.
  • Prepaid wallets record top-ups as customer payments. A prepaid stop can be refused when the wallet does not cover it.
  • Dispenser and cooler rental tracks units by serial number and invoices the monthly fee automatically.

Once a month, Bill deliveries creates one invoice per customer for account, prepaid and coupon stops.

Getting started

The app runs on Odoo 19. If you want us to set it up with your containers, routes, drivers and existing customer balances, or to add something specific to your business, request a quote.
