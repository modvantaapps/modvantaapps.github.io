How to Choose a Payment Gateway for Odoo

What to check before adding a payment provider to Odoo eCommerce, invoices and POS: countries, methods, webhooks, refunds, capture and reconciliation.

Odoo ships with a set of payment providers, but many local and regional gateways are not among them. Choosing one is not only about fees. The way a gateway confirms payments, refunds them and reports them decides how much manual work your finance team does every month.

1. Check where and how your customers pay

Start from your customers, not the gateway. Which cards, wallets, bank transfers or buy-now-pay-later methods do they use in your country? A gateway that covers them on its hosted page saves you from building anything on your website.

2. Where in Odoo will you take payments?

Payments can happen in three places:

  • the website shop at checkout,
  • invoices and payment links sent to customers,
  • the Point of Sale, through a terminal or a QR code.

Check which of these the gateway's Odoo app supports. Our payment gateway apps say so on each page.

3. Ask how payments are confirmed

The safest integrations never trust what the customer's browser reports. Odoo should confirm the payment with the gateway itself, through a signed webhook or by asking the gateway's API. Ask:

  • Are webhooks signed, and does the app check the signature?
  • What happens if webhooks arrive out of order or not at all?
  • Does the app check the amount and currency against the order?

4. Refunds and manual capture

Find out whether you can refund in full and in part from Odoo, and whether the gateway supports holding an amount and capturing it later. Manual capture is useful when you confirm stock or a booking before charging.

5. Reconciliation

Fees and payouts are where hidden work appears. A good integration lets your payments reconcile with invoices automatically, and makes fees and payouts visible to accounting.

6. Test before you go live

Use the gateway's test mode on a copy of your database. Pay, refund, cancel and let a payment expire. Check what the customer sees and what accounting sees after each case.
