Ready-Made Odoo App or Custom Development? How to Decide

When an existing Odoo app is enough, when it needs customising, and when a custom module is the right choice, with the questions to ask before you pay for either.

Most companies that move to Odoo reach the same point: the standard apps cover most of the work, but one process does not fit. The choice is then between a ready-made app from the Odoo Apps Store, a ready-made app adapted for you, or a module written from scratch.

Here is how we help clients decide.

Start with what already exists

Before paying for development, search the Odoo Apps Store and the Modvanta app catalogue. For common needs such as payment gateways, shipping carriers, marketplace connectors, POS features and country tax reports, a tested app usually exists, costs far less than custom work and can be installed the same day.

A ready-made app is the right choice when:

  • the process is common in your industry,
  • you can follow the app's way of working with small changes in how your team works,
  • you need it running quickly.

Customising an existing app

Often an app does 80% of what you need. Adding fields, a report in your layout, a different approval step or a link to another system is usually much cheaper than starting from zero, because the tested core stays the same.

Ask whether the change can be made as a separate module that extends the app. That keeps your change safe when the app itself is updated.

When custom development makes sense

A custom module is worth it when:

  • the process is what makes your business different,
  • several apps would have to be combined and still leave gaps,
  • you need an integration with a system that has no connector, such as a supplier portal or a government service.

Questions to ask before you pay

  1. Who owns the code? For custom work, you should receive the source code.
  2. Which Odoo version and edition is it for? Community and Enterprise have different apps available.
  3. How is it tested? Ask whether it is tested on a clean database and as a normal user, not only as the administrator.
  4. What happens at the next Odoo version? Code written with standard Odoo inheritance is far easier to upgrade.
  5. Is the price fixed? A clear scope makes a fixed quote possible.
