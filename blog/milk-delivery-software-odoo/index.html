How to Run a Milk Delivery Business on Odoo

Daily milk subscriptions, delivery routes, driver round sheets on the phone, prepaid wallets and holidays: how a doorstep dairy round works in Odoo.

A doorstep milk business looks simple from the outside: the same customers, the same products, every morning. Behind it is a daily puzzle. Who gets how many litres today, which customer is on holiday, which route is closed, what each driver delivered and who still owes money. Most dairies start with a notebook or a spreadsheet and outgrow it at a few hundred customers.

This guide explains how a milk round is set up in Odoo with our Milk Delivery Software, and what you should check in any milk delivery system before you choose one.

The four things a milk delivery system must handle

  1. Subscriptions. Each customer has a standing order: product, quantity per delivery, price, delivery days and a start date. It can be paused or cancelled without losing the history.
  2. Routes. Customers belong to a route with a driver, an area and delivery weekdays, and the stops are kept in delivery order.
  3. The daily sheet. Every morning the system must turn active subscriptions into one delivery sheet per route, skipping public holidays, closed routes and customers on vacation.
  4. Payment. Customers either pay in advance into a wallet, or are invoiced for what was delivered.

If a system cannot generate the day's sheets automatically from subscriptions, someone in the office will still be doing it by hand.

How it works in Odoo

Routes and subscriptions

You create a route for each driver or area and choose the delivery weekdays. Then you add subscriptions: customer, product, quantity, unit price and route. Each subscription has a status (active, paused or cancelled), so a customer who stops for a month is paused, not deleted.

Generating today's sheets

Under Milk Delivery, Operations, Generate Today's Sheets builds one delivery sheet per route from the active subscriptions. It can also run automatically every day. Holidays and customer vacations, set under Configuration, are skipped when the sheets are generated.

Drivers deliver from their phone

Drivers do not need an Odoo login. Each route has a private link that the driver opens on the phone. It shows today's stops in order, with the address and products, and the progress of the round. The office can also mark a whole sheet delivered or print it.

Prepaid wallets and invoices

Customers can pay a deposit into a prepaid wallet. When a delivery is marked delivered and the balance covers it, the wallet is charged. For example, a deposit of 30 minus a delivery of 3.20 leaves 26.80. Deliveries that are not covered by the wallet can be invoiced in bulk with Create Invoices.

What to check before you choose a system

  • Can customers pause without cancelling?
  • Are holidays applied to all customers, one route or one customer?
  • Can drivers work without a paid user licence?
  • Does the wallet refuse a charge when the balance is too low, instead of going negative?
  • Does billing end up in your accounting, or in a separate tool you must reconcile?

Getting started

The app is available on the Odoo Apps Store for Odoo 19, Community and Enterprise. If you want it set up for you, with your products, prices, routes and existing customers loaded, or changed for the way your dairy works, request a quote.
