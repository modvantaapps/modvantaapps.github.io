Frequently asked questions

Can you build a private app just for our store?

Yes. A custom app is installed only on your store and you get the source code.

Can you replace our Shopify Scripts?

Yes. Checkout, discount, payment and delivery rules can be rebuilt as Shopify Functions, which is what Shopify now supports.

Do you work on themes too?

Yes, through sections, blocks and theme app extensions, so your changes do not disappear when the theme is updated.

Can Shopify talk to Odoo?

Yes. We build and maintain a Shopify connector for Odoo and adapt it to how you work.
