About Modvanta

A software team for Odoo, Shopify, Magento, custom apps and AI, building systems that fit how your business works.

Our story Request a quote

Who we are

Modvanta is a software development company. We build business software on Odoo, online stores on Shopify and Magento, and custom web apps, portals and AI tools for companies that need something that fits how they really work.

We started by publishing ready-made apps on the official Odoo Apps Store. Today most of our time goes to client projects: transport and logistics systems, delivery platforms, payroll and tax for local rules, marketplace connectors and point-of-sale tools.

We work remotely with clients in Europe, Africa and Latin America, in their time zone and in plain English.

How we build

The standards every project and every app meets.

Security reviewed

Every new release goes through a review of routes, access rules, uploads and secrets, so a module does not open a hole in your Odoo.

Tested on clean databases

We install and test modules on a fresh Odoo database, including as the non-admin users who will work with them.

Clean, upgradeable code

Standard Odoo models, views, access rules and inheritance, so upgrades and other modules keep working.

Built from official sources

Integrations and localization follow the provider's published API and the authority's specification.
