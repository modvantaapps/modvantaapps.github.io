Who We Are

Who We Are

Modvanta publishes ready-made apps on the official Odoo Apps Store: payment gateways, shipping carriers, marketplace connectors, Point of Sale extensions, accounting tools, HR and payroll, and country-specific compliance. Each app is written for Odoo 19 and sold with its source code.

We also work directly with companies: custom modules, integrations with their suppliers and platforms, complete industry systems, upgrades and support. Many projects start from one of our published apps and add what is specific to the client.

Outside Odoo, we build extensions for Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce, and connect Magento stores to Odoo.

How we build

Our engineering principles

Security reviewed

Every new release goes through a review of routes, access rules, uploads and secrets, so a module does not open a hole in your Odoo.

Tested on clean databases

We install and test modules on a fresh Odoo database, including as the non-admin users who will work with them.

Clean, upgradeable code

Standard Odoo models, views, access rules and inheritance, so upgrades and other modules keep working.

Built from official sources

Integrations and localization follow the provider's published API and the authority's specification.
