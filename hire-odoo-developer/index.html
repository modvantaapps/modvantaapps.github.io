Three ways to work with us

Pick what fits the size of the work. You can switch later.

Fixed-price project

A written scope, a fixed price and a timeline before any work starts. Best for a new module, an integration or a migration.

Block of hours

Buy a block of developer hours and use it for changes, fixes and small features as they come up. Unused time is reported every month.

Dedicated developer

A developer who works on your Odoo every week, with your backlog, your Git repository and a regular call with your team.

All services

Frequently asked questions

Can I hire an Odoo developer for a small job?

Yes. Small fixes and changes are common. A block of hours suits a list of small jobs; a single change can be quoted as a fixed price.

Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses in Europe, the Middle East, Africa, the Americas and Asia, and plan calls around your time zone.

Which Odoo versions do you work with?

Mainly Odoo 17, 18 and 19, Community and Enterprise, on your own server, Odoo.sh or Odoo Online where custom code is allowed.

Who owns the code?

You do. Custom code written for you is delivered to your repository and is yours to keep, change or move to another partner.
