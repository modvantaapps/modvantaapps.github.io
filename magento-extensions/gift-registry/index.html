What it does

Let customers create a registry for a wedding, a baby, a birthday or a housewarming, add the products they wish for, and share it. Guests buy from the registry and the gifts are shipped to the registrant.

This extension lets customers create a registry for a wedding, a baby, a birthday, a housewarming or a custom occasion, add the products they wish for and share it. Under My Account > Gift Registries they set the event date, privacy and shipping address, and add products with an Add to Gift Registry button on the product page. Guests open the registry by its link or through Find a Gift Registry and buy the remaining gifts, which ship to the registrant while buyers see only first name and city, never the full address. Purchased quantities are tracked and reverted on cancellation or refund, and the owner receives purchase emails, event reminders and a thank-you list. In the configuration you choose registry types, limits, when purchases count, search options, email templates and expiry after the event. A couple can share a wedding list with guests in other cities.

Features

  • Registry types: wedding, baby, birthday, housewarming and custom
  • Wished quantity and priority per product
  • Add to Gift Registry on product pages
  • Privacy: public and searchable, by link only, or private
  • Share link and guest search
  • Gifts are shipped to the registrant, whose address is hidden from the buyer
  • Purchased quantities are tracked, and corrected when an order is cancelled or refunded
  • Thank-you list for the registrant
  • Notifications to the registrant and event reminders
  • Admin grid of registries
  • GraphQL support

Where you find it in the admin

  • Customers > Gift Registries

Gift Registry: questions

Which Magento versions does Gift Registry support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does Gift Registry cost?

$30 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install Gift Registry?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-gift-registry, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. It pulls in the free Modvanta Core package automatically. We can also install and configure it on your store.
