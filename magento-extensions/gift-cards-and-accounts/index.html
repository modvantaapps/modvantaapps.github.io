What it does

Sell and redeem gift cards. Each card is an account with a unique code, a balance and a full history; shoppers apply the code in the cart or checkout and the balance is reduced when the order is placed.

This extension lets you sell and redeem gift cards. Each card has a unique code, a balance, an optional expiry date and a full history. To sell cards you mark a virtual product as a gift card, and when the invoice is paid, or when the order is placed, a card is created and its code emailed to the recipient. Shoppers enter codes in a block next to the discount code on the cart page or in the payment step, and the amount shows as its own total line. Under Marketing > Promotions > Gift Card Accounts you create cards, adjust balances, resend emails and export codes, and codes can also be generated in bulk from the command line. You can set the code format, card lifetime, email template and lockout after failed guesses. Typical uses are holiday gifting or a batch of codes for a corporate client.

Features

  • Gift card accounts with random codes, balance, status and optional expiry date
  • Create cards in the admin, or generate them in bulk from the command line with a CSV export
  • Gift card products that issue a card when the order is invoiced, with an email to the recipient
  • Apply a gift card in the cart and checkout
  • Protection against code guessing
  • Balance is deducted when the order is placed and restored when it is cancelled
  • Refund to a gift card from a credit memo
  • Codes are masked in the admin grid
  • Full balance history per card
  • REST and GraphQL support

Where you find it in the admin

  • Marketing > Promotions > Gift Card Accounts

Gift Cards & Accounts: questions

Which Magento versions does Gift Cards & Accounts support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does Gift Cards & Accounts cost?

$45 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install Gift Cards & Accounts?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-gift-cards, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. It pulls in the free Modvanta Core package automatically. We can also install and configure it on your store.
