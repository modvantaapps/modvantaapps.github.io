EU Peppol EInvoicing

Generates structured EU e-invoices (EN 16931) from Magento invoices and credit memos in UBL 2.1, UN/CEFACT CII D16B or Factur-X / ZUGFeRD (PDF/A-3 with the embedded CII XML) format. Documents are validated, stored, and can optionally be sent to a Peppol access point over a generic REST API.

What it does

Generates structured EU e-invoices (EN 16931) from Magento invoices and credit memos in UBL 2.1, UN/CEFACT CII D16B or Factur-X / ZUGFeRD (PDF/A-3 with the embedded CII XML) format. Documents are validated, stored, and can optionally be sent to a Peppol access point over a generic REST API.

This tool turns invoices and credit memos into structured EU e-invoices following EN 16931: UBL 2.1 for Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 and XRechnung, CII, or Factur-X / ZUGFeRD PDFs with the XML embedded. Documents are generated from the admin, the command line, or automatically after an invoice or credit memo is saved, and checked against built-in rules. Valid documents can be sent to a Peppol access point over a REST API, with status polling and retries. Sales > Operations > EU e-Invoices lists every document with status and downloads, and invoice and credit memo pages get a document panel. Customers see no storefront change. Merchants enter seller details, Peppol ID, payment means, IBAN, due-date offset, VAT category and exemption codes, how shipping is mapped, XRechnung mode and access point settings. A German B2B seller sending XRechnung to public buyers is a typical case.

Features

  • Three syntaxes: (Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 / XRechnung UBL),
  • Generation for invoices and credit memos, from the admin, the CLI, or automatically through a queue consumer after an invoice or credit memo is saved
  • Built-in EN 16931 business rule validation; the CLI can additionally check against an official XSD
  • XRechnung 3.0 support (CustomizationID, BR-DE rules); the buyer reference (BT-10) comes from the customer attribute
  • Peppol buyer ID from the customer attribute
  • VAT category mapping (zero-rate category, exemption reason and VATEX code), shipping mapped as invoice line or document-level charge, rounding difference reported as Rounding Amount (BT-114)
  • Transmission modes, (testing) and, with status polling and retries by cron
  • Admin grid of documents and a document panel on the invoice and credit memo view pages

Where you find it in the admin

  • Sales > Operations > EU e-Invoices

Good to know

This module supports e-invoicing compliance. It does not certify compliance and does not constitute legal or tax advice. Check the output against your national requirements and your access point's rules.

EU Peppol EInvoicing: questions

Which Magento versions does EU Peppol EInvoicing support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does EU Peppol EInvoicing cost?

$30 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install EU Peppol EInvoicing?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-einvoice, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. We can also install and configure it on your store.
