What it does

Subscribe & save: per-product subscription plans, a "One-time purchase / Subscribe & save X%" choice on the product page, honest cart pricing, subscription profiles created at checkout, recurring orders by cron or CLI with pluggable payment handlers (offline, deterministic mock token, UPI AutoPay e-mandate skeleton with pre-debit notice), retries, per-cycle idempotency, renewal reminders and self-service management including one-click cancellation.

This extension lets customers subscribe to products at a discount and receive repeat orders on a schedule. The merchant creates a plan per product under Catalog > Inventory, with intervals from one week to six months, a discount percentage and optional minimum or maximum deliveries. The product page then shows a preselected "One-time purchase" option beside a subscribe choice with an interval select and the discounted price. At checkout a subscription profile is created, and a cron job every 15 minutes places recurring orders, with payment retries and renewal reminder emails. Under My Account > My Subscriptions customers skip a delivery, pause, change the interval or address, or cancel with one button and one confirmation page. The Subscriptions section of Stores > Configuration sets the run hour, retry attempts, fallback shipping method, payment handler and email templates. For example, a coffee shop can offer monthly deliveries.

Features

  • Plans per product: enabled, offered intervals (1, 2, 4 weeks; 1, 2, 3, 6 months), discount %, minimum deliveries, optional maximum deliveries
  • Product page: radio choice with "One-time purchase" preselected (no pre-ticked subscription), interval select, the discounted price next to the regular one (simple products), the reminder policy
  • Cart: the choice becomes the quote item option (separate line from one-time purchases) and a visible line option "Subscription: Every 2 weeks, 10% off the current price..., cancel online any time"
  • Profiles at checkout: one profile per interval with items, qty, locked discount, schedule (anchor day), next run date / time, shipping address and method, payment method, payment handler
  • Recurring orders: cron (every 15 minutes)
  • Payment handlers
  • Retries: N attempts (default 3) every M hours (default 24); the customer is emailed after each failure; after the last one the profile becomes "Payment Failed" and the customer is emailed
  • Idempotency: one run row per profile and cycle with the unique key
  • My Account > My Subscriptions: list, detail (items, discount, next delivery, address, terms, history), skip next delivery, pause / resume, change interval, change address (address book), cancel
  • Emails: confirmation, renewal reminder N days before (default 3), UPI pre-debit notice, payment retry / failure, pause, resume, skip, change, cancellation, completion
  • Admin: Sales > Operations > Subscriptions (Modvanta) grid and view (items, schedule, payment, addresses, terms, run history with idempotency keys)

Where you find it in the admin

  • Sales > Operations > Subscriptions (Modvanta)
  • Catalog > Inventory > Subscription Plans (Modvanta)

Good to know

The UPI AutoPay handler and the cancellation rules support compliance with the RBI e-mandate framework for recurring payments and with consumer rules on subscriptions; the module does not certify compliance and is not legal advice.

Subscriptions & Recurring Orders: questions

Which Magento versions does Subscriptions & Recurring Orders support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does Subscriptions & Recurring Orders cost?

$50 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install Subscriptions & Recurring Orders?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-subscriptions, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. It pulls in the free Modvanta Core package automatically. We can also install and configure it on your store.
