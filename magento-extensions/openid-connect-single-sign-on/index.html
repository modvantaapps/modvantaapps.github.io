What it does

Let staff sign in to the admin panel, and optionally customers sign in to the store, with their account at your OpenID Connect identity provider, such as Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Keycloak or Auth0. Enter the issuer URL, client ID and secret; endpoints and signing keys are discovered automatically. Uses the authorization code flow with state, nonce and PKCE.

It lets staff sign in to the admin panel, and optionally customers sign in to the store, with their account at your identity provider, so anyone you disable there loses access. You create a web application at the provider, enter the issuer URL, client ID and secret under Stores > Configuration, and copy the redirect URIs shown there; endpoints and keys are discovered automatically. A Sign in with button carrying your provider name appears on the admin and customer sign-in pages. Admin users are matched by email and can be created with a default role for your own domains; customer accounts can be created on first sign-in. You can limit email domains, choose when an email address is trusted, and hide the admin password form, with an emergency bypass. For example, a company on Okta can give its support team admin access without sharing passwords.

Features

  • Sign in with your provider button on the admin and customer sign-in pages
  • Administrators matched to existing admin users by email
  • Optional automatic creation of admin users with a chosen role, limited to your email domains
  • Customers matched by email, or created on first sign-in if you allow it
  • Only verified email addresses are accepted unless you choose to trust the provider
  • Optional list of allowed email domains
  • Option to hide the admin password form, with an emergency way back
  • Inactive admins and locked customers stay blocked
  • Client secret stored encrypted; failure reasons written to the system log

Good to know

OpenID Connect only: no SAML, no role or group mapping and no single logout. Tested end to end with Keycloak; other providers that follow the OpenID Connect standard use the same flow. A live store needs HTTPS.

OpenID Connect Single Sign On: questions

Which Magento versions does OpenID Connect Single Sign On support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does OpenID Connect Single Sign On cost?

$30 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install OpenID Connect Single Sign On?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-single-sign-on, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. We can also install and configure it on your store.
