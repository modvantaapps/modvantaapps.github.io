What it does

Opt-in charity donations in the cart and the Luma checkout: round up to the next whole amount, give a fixed amount or choose your own, for a charity or campaign you select. The donation is a separate order total that is invoiced, refunded, reported and paid out like the rest of the order.

This tool lets customers add an optional charity donation in the cart and the checkout sidebar. The merchant adds charities or campaigns under Marketing > Checkout Donations > Charities with logo, description, dates and goal. Shoppers can round the order up to the next whole amount, pick a fixed amount, or enter their own, and choose the charity; nothing is pre-selected and the donation can be removed before ordering. The donation is a separate order total and shows as a Donation line in the cart, checkout, orders, e-mails and invoice PDFs. It is not taxed by default, and refunds follow a chosen rule with a switch on each credit memo. Reports > Donations shows collected, refunded and net amounts per charity with CSV export for payouts. Merchants edit the label, intro text, amounts, limits and an optional receipt e-mail. It suits a seasonal charity campaign.

Features

  • Charities / campaigns (Marketing > Checkout Donations > Charities): name, logo, description, registration number, website, active from / to (store time zone), goal
  • Donation options (cart page block and a Luma checkout sidebar block): round-up to the next multiple of a unit
  • Separate total (quote collector at sort order 500: after subtotal, discount, shipping and tax, before grand total 550 and before gift card / store credit / reward deductions at 700+)
  • Not taxed by default. Optional donation tax class: fixed / custom donations are then treated as tax-inclusive
  • Orders, invoices, credit memos: amounts on,, (+ charity name and type on the order, order grid columns "Donation" and "Donation Charity")
  • Display: totals row "Donation (Charity)" in cart, checkout, customer order / invoice / credit memo views and prints, admin views, order / invoice / credit memo emails, and invoice / credit memo PDFs
  • Reports (Reports > Modvanta > Donations): per charity and period collected (invoiced), refunded and net, CSV summary and payout detail (one line per invoice / credit memo), formula-injection safe
  • Donation receipt email (optional, once per order after the invoice with the donation is saved), sent outside the area emulation so sample-data mail plugins do not drop it
  • REST for headless:,,, admin
  • Optional integrations: the Modvanta Order Editor extension keeps the donation fixed on edited orders; the Modvanta Webhooks extension event

Where you find it in the admin

  • Marketing > Checkout Donations > Charities
  • Marketing > Checkout Donations > Settings
  • Reports > Modvanta > Donations

Good to know

Legal notes. The store collects donations on behalf of the charities and is responsible for paying them out and for any registration, fundraising, consumer protection and data protection rules that apply (for example commercial co-venturer / professional fundraiser rules in some US states, the UK Charities Act 1992 Part II statements, Indian FCRA / 80G rules for receipts). Tax treatment of donations collected at checkout varies by jurisdiction: in many places a voluntary donation is outside the scope of VAT / GST / sales tax (no supply), which is why the donation is not taxed by default; a tax class can be configured if your adviser says otherwise. The receipt email is a confirmation from the store, not a tax receipt unless the charity issues one. Donations are strictly opt-in: nothing is pre-selected or added automatically (no "basket sneaking", CCPA India Dark Patterns Guidelines 2023, Annexure 1 item 2; EU UCPD Annex I). This module supports, but does not certify, compliance with any of these rules; check them with your legal and tax advisers.

Checkout Donations & Round Up: questions

Which Magento versions does Checkout Donations & Round Up support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does Checkout Donations & Round Up cost?

$30 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install Checkout Donations & Round Up?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-checkout-donations, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. It pulls in the free Modvanta Core package automatically. We can also install and configure it on your store.
