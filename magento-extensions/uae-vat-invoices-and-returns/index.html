What it does

UAE VAT (5%) helper: VAT calculation for UAE destinations through the core tax engine, supplier and customer TRN, "Tax Invoice" / "Simplified Tax Invoice" labelling, AED amounts with the exchange rate used, GCC / export / designated-zone regimes with evidence notes, and a VAT 201 support report by emirate.

This extension helps merchants selling to the United Arab Emirates charge 5% VAT and issue tax invoices in the UAE format. When enabled in the UAE VAT section of Stores > Configuration, tax for UAE addresses is calculated at the standard, zero or exempt rate by product tax class, while GCC and other foreign orders are treated as GCC supplies or exports with an export evidence note. The merchant enters the supplier TRN, Arabic legal name, the simplified invoice threshold, designated zone keywords and AED conversion settings; buyer TRNs come from the address VAT number. Invoices and credit memos are labelled "Tax Invoice", "Simplified Tax Invoice" or "Tax Credit Note" with AED amounts and the exchange rate, in admin and on PDFs. A VAT 201 support report by emirate, with CSV export, sits under Reports. For example, USD orders get AED totals at the 3.6725 peg.

Features

  • UAE VAT 5% for UAE destinations through Magento's tax engine; zero-rated and exempt product tax classes
  • Regimes: domestic, designated zone, GCC supplies and exports, stored with evidence on the order
  • Supplier TRN in the configuration; buyer TRN from the address VAT number or a customer attribute (format check)
  • "Tax Invoice" / "Simplified Tax Invoice" / "Tax Credit Note" labelling with AED amounts and the exchange rate used, on the admin views, core PDFs and the Modvanta Pdf Templates extension variables
  • Export evidence note on export orders
  • VAT 201 support report by emirate with CSV export

Where you find it in the admin

  • Reports > Modvanta > UAE VAT 201 Report

Good to know

This module supports compliance with Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 on VAT and its Executive Regulation (Cabinet Decision No. 52 of 2017, in particular Art. 59 tax invoices and Art. 30 export evidence). It does not certify compliance and is not tax advice. Verify your treatment with Federal Tax Authority (FTA) guides and public clarifications or a tax adviser - especially for designated zones and supplies to other GCC states.

UAE VAT Invoices & Returns: questions

Which Magento versions does UAE VAT Invoices & Returns support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does UAE VAT Invoices & Returns cost?

$25 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install UAE VAT Invoices & Returns?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-uae-vat, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. It pulls in the free Modvanta Core package automatically. We can also install and configure it on your store.
