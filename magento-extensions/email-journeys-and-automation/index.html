What it does

Lifecycle email automation: welcome / post-purchase, shipping and delivery follow-ups, review requests, replenishment reminders, birthday and win-back emails, built from simple step lists with waits, emails and conditions. Cart abandonment is not handled here — that stays in the Modvanta Abandoned Cart extension.

This tool sends automated lifecycle e-mails. Under Marketing > Email Journeys the merchant creates journeys triggered by a first order, any order, a shipment, an assumed delivery, registration without an order, win-back, birthday, review request or replenishment, then adds steps for waits, e-mails using any e-mail template, conditions such as ordered, opened or clicked, and exits. Audiences can be filtered by customer group, store view, order total, purchased SKUs or categories and country. E-mails can include a single-use coupon with an expiry and product blocks such as purchased items or bestsellers. Marketing journeys only reach newsletter subscribers, every e-mail has one-click unsubscribe, and a weekly cap and quiet hours limit sending. Journeys only start for new events, and cart abandonment is not covered. Statistics show sends, clicks and revenue. A coffee store reminding buyers to reorder is a typical case.

Features

  • Journeys (Marketing > Email Journeys) triggered by first order, any order, order shipped, order delivered, registration without order, win-back, birthday, review request and replenishment
  • Step lists with waits, e-mails (any e-mail template), conditions (ordered / opened / clicked) and exits
  • Audience filters: customer groups, store views, order total, purchased SKUs / categories, newsletter subscribers, billing country
  • Unique single-use coupons per recipient from an auto-generation cart price rule, with an expiry
  • Product blocks: purchased products, the Modvanta Recommendations extension suggestions when installed, or bestsellers
  • Consent checks for marketing journeys, one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058), frequency cap and quiet hours
  • Signed click tracking, optional open pixel (off by default), order attribution and journey statistics
  • A journey only triggers for events that happen after it was created: there is no backfill of past orders or other earlier events
  • Cart abandonment is not included (see the Modvanta Abandoned Cart extension)

Where you find it in the admin

  • Marketing > Email Journeys > Journeys
  • Marketing > Email Journeys > Journey Statistics
  • Marketing > Email Journeys > Settings

Good to know

This module supports compliance with email marketing rules (GDPR / ePrivacy Directive Art. 13, UK PECR reg. 22, India DPDP Act 2023, US CAN-SPAM 15 U.S.C. 7704, Gmail / Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirements of 2024) through consent checks, one-click unsubscribe, a frequency cap, quiet hours and erasure. It does not certify compliance; check your consent wording and lawful basis with counsel.

Email Journeys & Automation: questions

Which Magento versions does Email Journeys & Automation support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does Email Journeys & Automation cost?

$30 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install Email Journeys & Automation?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-email-journeys, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. It pulls in the free Modvanta Core package automatically. We can also install and configure it on your store.
