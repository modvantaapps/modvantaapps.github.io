What it does

Multi-vendor marketplace: seller onboarding with admin approval, seller storefronts at, "Sold by" on the product page, cart, checkout and order e-mails, a seller dashboard in My Account, commission rules with a per-item ledger and credit memo reversals, payout batches with statement PDFs, moderated customer-seller messages, an admin order view grouped by seller, and GraphQL.

This tool turns the store into a marketplace where approved sellers list and sell their own products. Customers apply under My Account > Sell With Us, and admins approve them under Marketplace > Sellers. Each seller gets a storefront page at /seller/, and Sold by appears on the product page, cart, checkout, order e-mails and PDFs. Sellers use a Seller Dashboard in My Account for sales figures, orders, shipping with tracking, price and stock edits, new products, payouts and CSV exports. Commission comes from a seller override, a category rule or a global percentage and fee, recorded per invoiced item and reversed on credit memos. Payout batches respect a hold period with statement PDFs. Customers and sellers can message per order. Merchants set the commission basis, hold period and moderation. A craft marketplace with many small makers is a typical case.

Features

  • Seller onboarding with admin approval
  • /seller/<url-key> storefronts with ratings
  • Sold by on product page
  • Cart and order e-mails
  • Seller dashboard in My Account (own orders, per-item shipping with tracking, own products price/stock, sales and commission summary, payouts, CSV)
  • Commission rules (global, category, seller override, fixed fee) with a per-item ledger and credit memo reversals
  • Payout batches net of refunds and GST TCS/TDS
  • Statement PDF
  • Moderated customer-seller messages
  • Admin order split by seller and GraphQL. Reuses the Modvanta Gst Tcs Tds extension seller registry

Where you find it in the admin

  • Marketplace > Sellers
  • Marketplace > Seller Products
  • Marketplace > Commission Rules
  • Marketplace > Commissions
  • Marketplace > Payouts
  • Marketplace > Messages

Good to know

Tax and legal notes are informational. GST TCS (s. 52 CGST Act) and income-tax TDS (s. 194-O) amounts come from the Modvanta Gst Tcs Tds extension; EU trader traceability (Art. 30 Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, Digital Services Act) is checked through the Modvanta Dsa Trader Verification extension. The module supports these obligations; it does not certify compliance and is not tax or legal advice.

Multi Vendor Marketplace: questions

Which Magento versions does Multi Vendor Marketplace support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does Multi Vendor Marketplace cost?

$50 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install Multi Vendor Marketplace?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-multi-vendor, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. It pulls in the free Modvanta Core package automatically. We can also install and configure it on your store.
