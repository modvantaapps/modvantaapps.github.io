What it does

Trader traceability ("know your business customer") for marketplaces under Articles 30 to 32 of the EU Digital Services Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065). It stores trader records with a verification workflow, enforces suspension on their products, discloses trader information on the storefront and in GraphQL, offers a self-onboarding form and purges personal data after the retention period.

This tool helps a marketplace verify the traders who sell on it under Articles 30 to 32 of the EU Digital Services Act, without certifying compliance. Traders apply through a form at /trader/apply, or admins add them under Customers > DSA Traders, with legal details, VAT ID, ID document, payment account and self-certification. Records move through pending, verified, suspended and rejected, with format checks, optional VIES lookup and an audit log. Products are linked to a trader on the product form, and products of traders who are not verified can be hidden or blocked from the cart. Shoppers see a Sold by section on the product page and a public trader profile. Personal data is purged a set number of months after the relationship ends. Merchants set the enforcement mode, certification text, templates and retention. EU marketplaces onboarding sellers are typical users.

Features

  • Trader records: legal and trading name, address, contact, trade register and registration number, VAT ID, trademark, ID document upload, payment account (IBAN, stored encrypted, last 4 kept)
  • Status workflow:,,; end of contractual relationship; audit log of every action with actor
  • Automatic checks: email, phone, IBAN and VAT format, optional VIES VAT lookup
  • Product link: product attribute (group "DSA Trader"; configurable children use the parent's value),
  • Enforcement for products of pending, suspended or rejected traders or ended relationships: hide from storefront and GraphQL and block add to cart
  • Storefront: "Sold by" disclosure on the product page, public trader profile at, and an application form at
  • Art. 30(5) retention: personal data is purged a set number of months after the relationship ended
  • Art. 32 notice: list or email consumers who bought an illegal product in the last 6 months
  • Email templates for application received, new application (admin), status change and Art

Where you find it in the admin

  • Customers > DSA Traders
  • Customers > DSA Trader Log
  • Reports > Modvanta > DSA Trader Verification

Good to know

This module supports compliance with the EU Digital Services Act. It does not certify compliance and is not legal advice. Have your processes reviewed by qualified counsel.

DSA Trader Verification: questions

Which Magento versions does DSA Trader Verification support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does DSA Trader Verification cost?

$50 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install DSA Trader Verification?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-dsa-trader-verification, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. It pulls in the free Modvanta Core package automatically. We can also install and configure it on your store.
