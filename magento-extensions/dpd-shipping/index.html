What it does

Offer DPD Classic, Home and Express at checkout with your own price table, and create DPD labels from the shipment page through the DPDgroup META-API. A built-in Mock mode lets you try the whole flow without a carrier account.

DPD Classic, DPD Home with Predict and DPD Express 10, 12 and 18 appear at checkout at prices from your own table, with labels created from the admin shipment page. In Stores > Configuration > Sales > Delivery Methods you enter your META-API login and business unit code, choose the products, and set one price per product, country or zone and weight band. A DPD Label panel on each shipment prefills product and weight; one click returns a PDF or ZPL label and saves the 14 digit parcel number as tracking. Predict notifies the recipient by email or text message in your chosen language, parcels between the EU and other countries carry customs data with your EORI and VAT numbers, and tracking shows DPD events. For example, a Belgian shop can price Classic for Belgium and Luxembourg and Express 12 for urgent business orders.

Features

  • DPD products at checkout; choose which to offer
  • Price table by product, zone or country and weight band, validated on save
  • Handling fee, country restriction, maximum weight and your own message
  • Label panel with product, weight and optional size
  • PDF (A6 or A4) or ZPL labels
  • Parcel number added as the tracking number
  • Tracking page with status and history
  • Separate staff permission for labels
  • Mock, Sandbox and Production modes

Good to know

Requires a DPD META-API login and business unit code from DPD; country-specific DPD web-service logins do not work. DPD has no rate service, so checkout prices are the ones you enter. One parcel per label; no parcel shops, returns or cash on delivery. Verified in Mock mode only; test with your DPD pre-production login before going live.

DPD Shipping: questions

Which Magento versions does DPD Shipping support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does DPD Shipping cost?

$30 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install DPD Shipping?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-dpd-shipping, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. We can also install and configure it on your store.
