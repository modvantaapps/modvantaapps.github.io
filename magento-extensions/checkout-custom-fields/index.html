What it does

Ask shoppers the extra questions your business needs during checkout: a purchase order number, delivery instructions, a preferred delivery date, a this-is-a-gift tick box. Add text, text area, dropdown, checkbox and date fields to the shipping or payment step, make them required or optional, and limit them to customer groups or store views. Ask shoppers the extra questions your business needs during checkout, such as a purchase order number, delivery instructions or a gift tick box. Under Sales > Operations > Checkout Fields you add text, text area, dropdown, checkbox or date fields, make them required, set their order and labels per store view, and limit them to customer groups or store views. Each field appears in the shipping step below the shipping methods or in the payment step above the payment methods, and required fields are checked in the browser and on the server. Answers are saved with the order and shown in the admin and My Account, including invoice, shipment and credit memo pages. Email variables print them in order emails, and an Order Comment box can be switched on under Stores > Configuration. For example, a B2B supplier can require a purchase order number on every order.

Features

  • Five field types: one-line text, text area, dropdown, checkbox and date
  • Place each field in the shipping step or the payment step, with a sort order
  • Required fields are checked in the browser and again on the server when the order is placed
  • Limit a field to customer groups, including guests, and to store views; a different label per store view
  • Works for guests and logged-in customers
  • Answers appear on the admin order, invoice, shipment and credit memo pages
  • Customers see their answers on their order, invoice, shipment, credit memo and print-order pages; this can be switched off
  • One-switch Order Comment box, optionally copied into the order's comment history
  • Old orders keep their answers and labels when a field is renamed or deleted
  • Two variables for adding the answers to the order confirmation email

Good to know

Built for the standard one-page checkout of the Luma theme. Fields do not appear in multi-address checkout, in orders created in the admin, or with express buttons that skip the checkout page. Answers are not printed on PDF documents or added to the orders grid, exports or API.

Checkout Custom Fields: questions

Which Magento versions does Checkout Custom Fields support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does Checkout Custom Fields cost?

$30 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install Checkout Custom Fields?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-checkout-fields, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. We can also install and configure it on your store.
