What it does

Give every product a barcode and print labels for shelves, products and parcels. Barcodes are generated from your GS1 company prefix or imported, and labels are produced as a PDF on the server.

This extension gives every product a barcode and prints labels for products, shelves and shipments. A barcode attribute supports EAN-13, UPC-A, Code 128 and custom codes with check digit validation, and missing barcodes can be generated from your GS1 company prefix or imported by CSV. Label templates for A4 and Letter sheets and for thermal rolls are designed under Catalog > Inventory > Label Templates, with barcode, QR code, name, SKU, prices and custom text. Labels are produced as a PDF on the server from the product grid, an order, a shipment, a category or a SKU list, with a start position for partly used sheets. A Barcode Scan page under Sales > Operations lets a USB or Bluetooth scanner open the matching product, order or shipment. In the configuration you set uniqueness, the default template and what scans match. A shop labelling new stock is a typical case.

Features

  • Barcode attribute for EAN-13, UPC-A, Code 128 or custom codes, with check digit validation
  • Generate barcodes from a GS1 company prefix, with uniqueness checks
  • CSV import and export of barcodes
  • Label templates for A4 and Letter sheets, including common Avery layouts, and for thermal rolls
  • Label elements: barcode, QR code, name, SKU, prices and attributes
  • Print from the product grid, a category, an order or a shipment
  • Choose the starting position on a partly used sheet
  • Scan page in the admin that finds the product, order or shipment for a code
  • REST lookup for handheld apps

Where you find it in the admin

  • Catalog > Inventory > Print Barcode Labels
  • Catalog > Inventory > Label Templates
  • Catalog > Inventory > Barcodes (Generate / Import)
  • Sales > Operations > Barcode Scan

Barcode Labels & Scan Lookup: questions

Which Magento versions does Barcode Labels & Scan Lookup support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does Barcode Labels & Scan Lookup cost?

$39 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install Barcode Labels & Scan Lookup?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-barcode-labels, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. It pulls in the free Modvanta Core package automatically. We can also install and configure it on your store.
