What it does

Offer PostNL at checkout at prices you set per service, destination and weight, then create the PostNL label on the shipment page with one click. The barcode is saved as the tracking number and customers follow the parcel from their order. A built-in Mock mode lets you try the whole flow without a carrier account.

Shoppers choose PostNL Standard delivery, Signature on delivery, Deliver to stated address only, or both together, at prices you enter, and you create the label on the shipment page with one click. In Stores > Configuration > Sales > Delivery Methods you add your API credentials, sender address, allowed services and a rate table per service, country or Parcel EU zone and weight band. Routes cover the Netherlands and Belgium both ways, plus standard delivery from the Netherlands to Parcel EU countries. The PostNL Label section preselects the customer's service, announces the parcel, saves the barcode as tracking and offers a PDF A6 or ZPL label, with a button for a second parcel. Customers see tracking in My Orders. For example, a Dutch shop can charge extra for signature delivery on expensive items and ship standard parcels to Germany.

Features

  • Standard, Signature on delivery, Stated address only, and Signature with stated address at checkout
  • Netherlands, Belgium and PostNL Parcel EU countries
  • Your own rate table per service, zone and weight band, checked for mistakes when you save
  • Label as PDF or ZPL from the shipment page, with download
  • Several parcels per shipment, with protection against accidental double submission
  • Tracking number added to the shipment automatically
  • Status and history in the standard tracking window
  • Separate admin permission for labels
  • Mock, Sandbox and Production modes; debug log with the API key masked

Good to know

Requires a PostNL business contract with API access. PostNL has no rate service, so checkout prices are the ones you enter. No pickup points, cash on delivery or destinations outside the EU. Verified in Mock mode only; test in Sandbox with your PostNL key before going live.

PostNL Shipping: questions

Which Magento versions does PostNL Shipping support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does PostNL Shipping cost?

$30 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install PostNL Shipping?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-postnl-shipping, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. We can also install and configure it on your store.
