What it does

Online withdrawal function: a clearly labelled "Withdraw from contract here" button that is available on every page, a confirmation step with a "Confirm withdrawal" button, an immutable log of every statement and an acknowledgement of receipt by email.

This extension gives EU shops the online withdrawal function that Member States apply from 19 June 2026. A "Withdraw from contract here" link appears in the footer of every page, in My Account, on the order view and as a signed link in the order email; guests look up an order with its number and email. The form shows days left and possible exclusions, then the customer presses "Confirm withdrawal". Each statement is stored unchangeably with a content hash, and an acknowledgement email with the full statement is sent at once. Admins review statements under Sales > Operations > Withdrawals and on the order view. In the Withdrawal Button (EU) section of Stores > Configuration the merchant sets both labels per store view, the period, holidays, product exceptions and email settings. For example, a German shop can use "Vertrag hier widerrufen".

Features

  • Storefront: footer link, My Account link, order view button, order email link
  • Access: signed-in owners via; guests via order number + order email (rate limited per IP and per order number, same message for unknown order and wrong email, keys hashed) or via signed links
  • Honest messages: not delivered yet ("you can already withdraw"), period end date and days left, expired period ("you can still send the statement; we will review it"), possible exceptions per item
  • Immutable statement: stores reference, order, name, email, scope, items snapshot, optional reason, statement text snapshot, eligibility snapshot, channel, UTC time
  • Processing (after the DB commit, each step optional, failures logged): the Modvanta Returns extension RMA (resolution refund, configurable reason code; items its rules reject are listed)
  • Admin: Sales > Withdrawals grid, statement page (integrity check, items, text, status change with mandatory reason for rejections, resend acknowledgement, create RMA, download text copy, history)
  • Delivery date (start of the goods period): merchant-entered date > the Modvanta Shipping Aggregator extension "delivered" status of all parcels > last shipment + assumed transit days
  • the Modvanta Eu Guarantee Notice extension: the form links the CMS page (withdrawal is not the guarantee)

Where you find it in the admin

  • Sales > Operations > Withdrawals

Good to know

This module supports compliance with Art. 11a of Directive 2011/83/EU (Consumer Rights Directive), inserted by Art. 1 of Directive (EU) 2023/2673, which Member States apply from 19 June 2026. It does not certify compliance and is not legal advice. National transposition laws decide the details; have your labels, texts and process reviewed by your legal advisor. The right of withdrawal applies to consumers; B2B orders are treated the same way by the module.

EU Withdrawal Button: questions

Which Magento versions does EU Withdrawal Button support?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4 (developed and tested on 2.4.9), with PHP 8.1 to 8.5 and Luma-based themes. If your store runs Hyva or a headless front end, tell us first and we check it before you buy.

How much does EU Withdrawal Button cost?

$30 as a one-time payment for one Magento installation. You get the full source code and a PDF user guide. There is no yearly fee.

How do I install EU Withdrawal Button?

With Composer: composer require modvanta/module-withdrawal-button, then bin/magento setup:upgrade. It pulls in the free Modvanta Core package automatically. We can also install and configure it on your store.
