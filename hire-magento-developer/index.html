What our Magento developers do

Magento services

Custom extensions

Features written to the Adobe Commerce coding standard and delivered with source code.

Checkout and catalog

Checkout fields, rules and fees, product pages, attributes and merchandising.

Shipping and payments

Carrier rates and labels, payment methods and fees.

Search and AI

Frequently asked questions

Which Magento versions do you work with?

Magento Open Source and Adobe Commerce 2.4.x.

Can you fix an extension someone else wrote?

Yes. We reproduce the problem on a copy of the store first, then deliver the fix with you.

Can you connect Magento to Odoo?

Yes. We build a Magento connector for Odoo that syncs orders, products, customers, MSI stock and credit memos.

Who owns the code?

You do. Code written for you is delivered to your repository.

How do I get a price?

Describe the job through the quote form. We reply with questions or a written scope and fixed price.
