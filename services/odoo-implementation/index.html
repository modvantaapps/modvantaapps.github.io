Odoo Implementation & Setup

From the first demo to the day your team starts working in Odoo: configured, loaded with your data and tested on your real cases.

What we do

We set up Odoo for the way your company works: companies and currencies, chart of accounts and taxes, warehouses, price lists, users and access rights. We import your products, customers and opening balances, add the modules you need, and give you a demo database to test every flow before go-live. When you need it, we deploy Odoo on your own server with backups.

Frequently asked questions

Can we try the system before we commit?

Yes. We prepare a demo database with your scenarios so your team can test it before go-live.

Do you host Odoo for us?

We can deploy Odoo on a server you own or rent, with HTTPS and backups, so the data stays in your account.

Can you work with our existing Odoo partner?

Yes. We often build modules or integrations for companies that already run Odoo with another partner.

Which languages can the system use?

Odoo is multilingual. Our modules ship with translations where we have them, and we add the languages your users need.
