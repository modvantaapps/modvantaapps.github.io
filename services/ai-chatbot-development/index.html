How we build a chatbot

Five steps, and you approve each one.

Your data

We connect the bot to what it may use: products, prices, stock, FAQ and policy pages, order data.

Rules

You decide what it can answer, what it can do, which language it speaks and when a person takes over.

Test

We run your real customer questions through it and fix wrong or weak answers before launch.

Launch

It goes live on your website, WhatsApp or inside Odoo, with a log of every conversation.

Improve

We review the logs with you and add answers for the questions it couldn't handle.

Built in

Guardrails come as standard

A chatbot speaks for your business, so it has to be safe to leave running. Every bot we deliver has these.

  • Answers from your content. The bot works from your pages and live data, not from guesses.
  • Hand-off to a person. Difficult or unhappy chats go to your team with the history attached.
  • Spending limits. Monthly budget cap, per-visitor rate limits and spam protection.
  • Read-only by default. AI connected to Odoo can only read until you allow more, field by field.
  • Full logs. Every conversation and every AI action is recorded, including refused ones.
  • Your choice of model. Claude, ChatGPT or any OpenAI-compatible service, on your own API key.

Questions about AI chatbots

Which AI models do you use?

We build on Claude (Anthropic) or OpenAI's ChatGPT models, or any service with an OpenAI-compatible API. You can switch provider later without rebuilding the bot.

Will the chatbot make things up?

It answers from the content you give it: products, prices, FAQ and policy pages, plus live data from your system. When it can't find an answer it says so and passes the chat to a person. No AI is perfect, so we test the bot with your real customer questions before it goes live.

What does it cost to run?

The AI usage is billed to your own API key, so you pay the provider directly and only for what is used. We set a monthly budget cap and rate limits, so a spike in traffic or a spammer can't run up the bill.

Do I need Odoo?

No. We build chatbots for any website, Shopify store or custom system. If you run Odoo, the bot can read live prices, stock and order status and add leads to your CRM.

Can the bot work on WhatsApp?

Yes. We build WhatsApp bots that take orders, answer questions and send updates, connected to your orders in Odoo or in your own system.

Is our data safe?

Your data stays in your system. Only the text needed to answer a question goes to the AI provider you choose. Bots get read-only access unless you decide otherwise, and every action is logged.

Who owns the chatbot?

You do. Custom work is delivered with its source code, and it runs on your server or your Odoo.

How long does it take?

A website chatbot based on our ready-made Odoo app can be live in a few days. A custom bot with integrations usually takes a few weeks. The quote gives you the exact timeline before work starts.

Tell us where it should run and what it should answer. You get a fixed quote and a timeline.
