Custom Odoo Development

When standard Odoo gets you 80% of the way, we build the rest: custom modules that follow your process instead of forcing a workaround.

Get a quote Browse apps

What we do

We design and build Odoo modules for companies whose work does not fit the standard apps: new business objects, approval flows, calculations, documents, customer and driver portals, and website features. Every module is written the Odoo way (models, views, access rules, translations and tests), so it upgrades cleanly and your team can maintain it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I own the code you write for me?

Yes. Code written for your project is delivered to you with its source, and you can keep it in your own repository.

Which Odoo versions do you work with?

Odoo 19 is our main version. We also build and port modules for Odoo 17 and 18 when a project needs it.

Do you work with Community and Enterprise?

Yes. We check which edition you run and build on the apps you already have.

How do you price a custom module?

Send us what the module must do. We review it, ask questions where needed and reply with the scope and a fixed quote before any work starts.
