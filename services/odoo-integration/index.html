Frequently asked questions

Is there already a connector for the system I use?

Check our app catalogue first. If it is not there, send us the API documentation of the system and we will tell you what can be synced.

What happens when the other system is down?

Our connectors log every call, retry where it is safe, and leave failed records visible in Odoo so nothing is lost silently.

Can you extend one of your published connectors for us?

Yes. Many projects start from a published connector with extra fields, rules or flows added for one client.

Do you need our API keys?

Only on your own database, entered by you or your administrator in Odoo settings. Keys stay in Odoo and are hidden from regular users.
