Odoo Localization & Compliance

Odoo that follows local rules: tax returns, e-invoices, fiscal receipts and payroll for the countries you work in.

Get a quote Browse apps

What we do

We build localization modules from the official sources (tax authority specifications, XSD files and published rates) and keep values editable and dated so they can follow the law when it changes. Our catalogue covers tax reports, e-invoicing, fiscal POS receipts, statutory payroll and audit files for many countries.

Frequently asked questions

Is my country covered?

Browse the localization apps. If your country or report is missing, send us the official specification and we will review it.

Who is responsible for tax advice?

Our modules implement published rules; your accountant stays responsible for tax advice and filings.

What happens when rates change?

Rates and thresholds are kept as dated records you can edit, and we update modules when rules change.

Do the modules work with Odoo Community?

Most do. Each app page says which editions and apps it needs.
