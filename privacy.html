Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information Modvanta handles and why. It covers this website, our support email, and the Modvanta extensions for Magento.

Our extensions

Modvanta extensions run entirely on the merchant's own Magento store. They do not send store, customer or order data to Modvanta, and they contain no tracking that reports back to us.

Some extensions connect the store to third-party services the merchant chooses to configure, such as carriers, payment providers, messaging tools or accounting systems. That data goes directly from the merchant's store to the service the merchant selected, under that service's own terms and privacy policy. The merchant is the controller of that data; Modvanta does not receive it.

Purchases

Extensions are sold through the Adobe Commerce Marketplace. Adobe processes the purchase and payment. We receive only the order details Adobe shares with sellers, such as the buyer's name, company and the product bought, and use them to provide support and honour the licence.

Support email

When you email us, we receive your email address, name and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to answer you and to fix problems you report. We keep support emails for as long as needed to help you and for our records, and we do not sell or share them.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising on it. GitHub may log technical information such as IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email modvantaapps@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
