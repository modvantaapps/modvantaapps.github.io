Frequently asked questions

What does Modvanta do?

Modvanta is a software company focused on Odoo. We publish ready-made Odoo apps on the Odoo Apps Store and build custom modules, integrations and complete systems for companies that run Odoo.

Where can I buy your Odoo apps?

Every app is sold on the official Odoo Apps Store. Each app page on this site links to its store page.

Which Odoo versions and editions do your apps support?

Our catalogue is built for Odoo 19. Each app page states whether it works with Community, Enterprise or both. We port apps to Odoo 17 and 18 on request.

Can you customise an app for my company?

Yes. Tell us what you need on top of an existing app and we reply with the scope and a fixed quote.

How do I get support?

Email modvantaapps@gmail.com with the app name, your Odoo version and the steps to reproduce the problem.
