United Arab Emirates

Water Delivery Software for the UAE

For water companies in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the Northern Emirates delivering 5-gallon bottles to homes and offices.

Request a quote How the system works

Built for how you work in the United Arab Emirates

5-gallon bottles and deposits

A deposit ledger per customer: bottles delivered, empties collected, deposits charged and refunded. You always know how many bottles each villa or office holds.

Coupon books

Sell coupon books and redeem one coupon per bottle at the door, the way UAE customers already pay for water.

Dispenser rental

Water dispensers tracked by serial number, with a monthly rental fee invoiced automatically, or included in the water price.

Routes by area

Routes per area and driver, with the stop order you set and the stops opened in Google Maps in that order.

VAT invoices in AED

Invoices and credit notes with 5% UAE VAT in AED, using Odoo's UAE accounting.

Arabic and English

Odoo's interface is available in Arabic. We translate the delivery screens and customer documents as part of the setup.

Set up for your company

We install the system on Odoo 19, load your products, prices, routes, drivers and customers, and change what is specific to your business. The price is fixed before work starts, and we support your team after go-live.

Included

  • Jar types, deposits and prices
  • Routes, vehicles and drivers
  • Customer self-service page
  • Online payments
  • Import of existing customers and jar balances

Frequently asked questions

Does it handle coupon books?

Yes. Customers buy coupon books in advance and the driver redeems a coupon for each bottle delivered.

Can drivers use their phones?

Yes. Drivers confirm each stop on their phone through the customer portal: bottles delivered, empties collected, coupons and cash.

How long does setup take?

Usually two to four weeks, depending on how many routes and customers you have and what we change for you. You get a fixed quote first.

Do I need Odoo already?

No. We set up Odoo for you, on your own server or on Odoo hosting, with this system included.
