Security Guard Software

Sites, contracts, guard rosters, patrols, incidents and invoices for private security agencies.

Request a quote App details

What the system does

Run a private security agency in Odoo: sites, contracts, guard rosters, patrols, incidents and invoices

Guard licences with expiry alerts, QR checkpoint patrols, clock in with a geofence and invoices built from the hours your guards worked.

One app for the daily work of a guarding company: who stands where and when, whether they are allowed to, what they saw, and what the client pays.

Features

Clients and sitesEach site belongs to a client and holds its address, map position, geofence radius, post orders, patrol checkpoints and the guard type, skills and licences it needs.
Security contractsBill rate per hour, minimum billable hours per shift, overtime after a number of hours at a rate factor you agree with the client, invoicing from worked or scheduled hours.
Rosters with checksShift templates and a roster generator for a date range. Double booking, an expired licence, a missing skill or the wrong guard type are refused before the shift is saved.
Guards on their phoneA simple page shows today's and upcoming shifts, clocks the guard in and out with their location, and takes incident reports with photos.
Patrols by QR codePrint a QR label for every checkpoint. Guards scan it with the phone camera while on duty; missed checkpoints are listed on the shift and its report.
MoneyInvoice a contract for a period in one click; the margin per contract is computed from the guards' hourly cost.
