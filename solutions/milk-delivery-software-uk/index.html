United Kingdom

Milk Delivery Software for UK Milkmen and Dairies

For independent milkmen, farm dairies and doorstep delivery companies running daily milk rounds.

Request a quote How the system works

Built for how you work in the United Kingdom

Doorstep subscriptions

Each customer's standing order: products, quantity per delivery, price and delivery days, from milk to eggs, juice and bread.

Round sheets in stop order

One sheet per round every morning, in the order your milkman drives it, skipping the holidays you set and customer holidays automatically.

Holidays and pauses

Customers away for a week? Pause the deliveries for those dates and they restart on their own.

Prepaid accounts

A prepaid account per customer, charged for each delivery, with a clear statement.

Invoices in GBP

Invoices and statements in GBP with the correct VAT rate per product. Most milk is zero-rated; other products use their own rate.

Phone round sheets

Your milkman opens the round on their phone through a private link and marks each delivery.

Set up for your company

We install the system on Odoo 19, load your products, prices, routes, drivers and customers, and change what is specific to your business. The price is fixed before work starts, and we support your team after go-live.

Included

  • Your products, packs and price per area
  • Route and delivery staff setup
  • Customer app or self-service page
  • Payment gateway for wallet top-ups
  • Import of existing customers

Frequently asked questions

Can customers manage their own orders?

Yes, we can set up a customer page where they change quantities and book holidays, as part of the project.

Can it take card payments?

Yes. Odoo connects to payment providers such as Stripe for online top-ups of the prepaid account.

How long does setup take?

Usually two to four weeks, depending on how many routes and customers you have and what we change for you. You get a fixed quote first.

Do I need Odoo already?

No. We set up Odoo for you, on your own server or on Odoo hosting, with this system included.
