How a solution project works

Each solution starts from a working Odoo app, so you see a running system in days instead of waiting months for software written from scratch. We then set it up with your data and add what is specific to your business.

Included in every solution project

  • Installation on your server or Odoo hosting
  • Setup with your products, prices, staff and routes
  • Import of your existing customers
  • Changes specific to your business, quoted up front
  • Training for your team and support after go-live
