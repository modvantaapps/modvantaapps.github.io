Car Wash Software

Plate lookup, live queue, wash bays, memberships and staff commission for car washes.

Request a quote App details

What the system does

Run a car wash in Odoo: plate lookup, a live queue, wash bays, POS sale, memberships and staff commission

Prices per vehicle type, unlimited and prepaid wash memberships checked by the server, a car-ready email and SMS, and online booking.

From the plate at the entrance to the car leaving clean: the right price for the vehicle, a numbered queue, a bay per car, payment at the till and the attendants' commission.

Features

Vehicles by plateFind a vehicle however the plate is typed (spaces, dashes, lower case), with its customer, type, make, model, colour, wash history, visits and spend.
Packages priced per vehicle typeA package is a service with a duration, what is included, a commission rule and a price for each vehicle type.
Queue and baysA numbered daily queue, one car per bay, Start Wash, Ready and Collected; a board of bays with the car in each and today's takings.
Point of SaleA Car Wash button on the till finds the plate, offers the packages at the vehicle's price, and the paid order puts the car in the queue.
MembershipsUnlimited monthly plans or prepaid wash packs, per vehicle or for several vehicles, with daily limits. The server decides what a membership covers.
Staff and customersCommission per wash split between attendants, an email and optional SMS when the car is ready, a rating request when it is collected.
