Law Practice Management

Firms and chambers that want cases, hearings and billing in one place

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Law Practice Management for Odoo 19Who it is forLaw firmsSolo practitionersLegal departments

What it does

Several lawyers, each seeing their own matters, and an administrator who sees everything.

Record what the client came about, the advice given and the fee. Invoice the fee, close one or many advice records with a reason, or create a case from it.

Case number, title, client, lead lawyer and team, stage pipeline, practice area, matter type, court, judge, court reference, opposing party and counsel, tags, terms and conditions.

Courts with their judges, police stations, FIR / police report number and date, and who the client is in the case (plaintiff, defendant, petitioner...).

Hearings per case with date, duration, court, judge, purpose and notes, in a calendar per lawyer. The client and the lawyer get an email when a hearing is created.
