Uganda Payroll

Uganda Payroll for Odoo 19: PAYE with the July 2026 Rates, NSSF 5% / 10%, Local Service Tax and the Monthly Worksheet

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Uganda PayrollUganda HRUganda taxURA PAYEPAYE July 2026NSSF 5% / 10%Local Service TaxLST

What it does

Ugandan statutory payroll on Odoo Community: URA PAYE with the resident rates applied from 1 July 2026 and the earlier bands for older periods, NSSF employee and employer contributions, the Local Service Tax on take-home salary in four instalments, and the monthly PAYE, NSSF and LST worksheet.

Nil to 335,000 UGX a month, 20% to 410,000, 25% to 485,000, 30% above, plus 10% above 10,000,000.

Periods before July 2026 use the earlier resident and non-resident bands automatically.

5% from the employee and 10% from the employer on the gross wage, with a voluntary top-up per employee.

Pay items can be taxed for PAYE but kept outside the NSSF wage.
