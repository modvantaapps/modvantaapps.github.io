Custom Product Tabs

Add custom tabs (Specifications, Shipping & Returns, Size Guide...) to eCommerce product pages

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Product tabsSpecifications tabShipping & Returns tabeCommerce

What it does

Write tab content once in Odoo's editor and show it as tabs on the product page. A tab can belong to chosen products (a Specifications table for one desk) or to every product (your Shipping & Returns policy).

The product's own website description becomes the first tab, Description, followed by your tabs in the order you set.

Features

Specifications, shipping, assemblyeach in its own tab on the product page
