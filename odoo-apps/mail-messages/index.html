Mail Messages Pro

Mail messages, the way a mailbox works: one list, a preview pane, and real message actions in every chatter

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Version 2 adds what people kept asking for: reply by email with Cc and Bcc from the chatter, forward, move and trash a message where you read it, edit with history, lost emails you can still file, conversations, drafts and filter rules. Same price as before.

In Odoo a message lives in the chatter of its record. To find what a customer wrote you have to remember where it was posted, and the chatter only lets you reply to everyone or log a note. This module adds a Messages app that lists the emails, messages and notes of every record you may read, across all apps, with search, filters and a preview pane.

It also puts the missing actions where you read messages: in the chatter of any record and in the Discuss inbox. Reply by email to the sender with Cc and Bcc without notifying the other followers, forward to the people you pick, move an email posted on the wrong record, edit a note with its history kept, archive, or move to a trash you can restore from.

Emails that Odoo could not attach to anything can be kept as lost messages, or open a conversation. Filter rules sort incoming email for you. Nothing is shown with more rights than the user has: a user never sees a message of a record he cannot open, and customers on the portal never see internal notes.

Who it is for

  • Sales teams chasing a customer email. A buyer's question may have landed on a lead, a quotation or the contact itself. Instead of opening each chatter, a salesperson searches one list by word, sender or document and goes straight to it.
  • Support desks that get misfiled emails. When a reply attaches itself to the wrong record, someone with the right group can move it, attachments and all, to the correct document. A note is logged on both records so the change is visible.
  • Managers checking what came in. The Incoming list includes emails that matched no document and emails from unknown senders, which are the ones most likely to sit unanswered.
  • Teams that answer customers carefully. Reply to Sender Only sends the answer to the sender alone and keeps it on the record as an internal note, so other followers aren't notified and it doesn't show in the customer portal.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. A new Messages app appears with All Messages, Emails, Internal Notes, For Me and Trash.
  2. Give the Can move/delete messages group to the users who should refile or delete messages, under Settings > Users & Companies > Users. Administrators already have it.
  3. Open Settings > Discuss. Tick Messages app: system notifications if you also want field tracking and stage changes in the list, and set Empty after (days) for the Messages trash, or 0 to keep binned messages.
  4. Open Messages > All Messages and use the search bar or the filters such as Incoming Emails, Sent to Me, Starred, Unread or With Attachments.
  5. Open a message and use Reply, Reply to Sender Only, Forward, Move, Star or Move to Trash from the buttons at the top.

Mail Messages Pro: questions

Can users see messages they couldn't see before?

No. The list uses Odoo's standard message access rules, so a user only sees messages on records they can already read. Internal notes are never shown to portal users.

Who can move or delete messages?

Only users in the Can move/delete messages group, and only where they also have write access on the records involved. Everyone else can read, reply, forward and star.

Is a message in the trash gone for good?

Not right away. Move to Trash takes it out of the chatter, and it can be restored from the Trash menu until the trash is emptied after the days set in Settings, or deleted permanently by hand.

Do attachments follow when I forward or move?

Yes. Forward sends the original attachments to the recipients you choose, and Move takes them to the new record along with the message.

Which apps does it cover?

Any record with a chatter, such as CRM leads, sales orders, contacts or invoices. It depends only on Discuss and base settings, so it runs on Community and Enterprise.

Does it replace my mail client?

No. It lists the emails Odoo already received or sent through its mail gateway. Mail that never reached Odoo won't appear here.
