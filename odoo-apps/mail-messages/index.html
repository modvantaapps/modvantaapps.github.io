Messages & Emails Hub

Every email and chatter message you can read, in one searchable list - with reply, forward, move and trash

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

In Odoo a message lives in the chatter of its record, so finding what a customer wrote means remembering where it was posted. The Messages app lists the emails and messages of every record you may read, across all apps, and lets you answer, forward, refile or bin them from there.

The Messages app gives every internal user one list of incoming emails, sent emails, chatter messages and internal notes from leads, sales orders, contacts and any other record with a chatter. Each row shows the date, author, subject, a content preview, the document and its type, the message type and the number of files, with Preview and Open buttons. System notifications are left out unless you switch them on.

From a message you can Reply, Reply to Sender Only (the answer is kept as an internal note and other followers are not notified), Forward to recipients you pick with the original attachments, Move it to another record with its attachments, Star it or Move to Trash. The list uses the normal access rules of Odoo, so nobody sees a message the chatter would not show them.

Features

Search it like a mailboxby text in the subject or body, by sender, recipient, document or document type, and filter on incoming emails, sent emails, internal notes, sent to me, written by me, starred, unread, with attachments or without a document. Emails that matched no record and emails from unknown senders can be found and handled too.
