Chronopost Shipping Integration

Ship with Chronopost from Odoo - labels, Chrono Relais pickup points, appointments and tracking

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A Chronopost connector for Odoo 19: Chronopost prices at the checkout with a pickup relay map, a relay or appointment chosen on the delivery order, labels and skybill numbers on validation, return labels, and the tracking history on every parcel.

The module adds Chronopost as a delivery method provider in Odoo and talks to the Chronopost web services: labels, pickup relay search, prices, Chrono Precise appointment slots and tracking.

Each delivery method is one Chronopost product - Chrono 13, Chrono Relais, Chronofresh, Chrono Express International, Chrono Precise and the others of your contract. The price can be a fixed price, a rate table by country, postcode and weight, Odoo price rules, or the live prices of your contract. On validation of the delivery order Chronopost returns the skybill number and the label, attached to the transfer.
