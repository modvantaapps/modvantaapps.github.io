Odoo Keap integration

Keap Connector

Keap contacts, opportunities, tags, notes and tasks kept in step with Odoo CRM.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
KeapContacts and companiesOpportunities and stagesTagsNotesTasksProductsREST hooks

What it does

Paste a Keap Personal Access Token or Service Account Key, or connect your own Keap OAuth app, and press Test Connection. Odoo reads your Keap users, opportunity stages and tags, then brings in companies, contacts and opportunities. Contacts keep their e-mail, phone, address, job title, company and owner; opportunities land in the CRM pipeline on the stage you mapped. Notes become chatter notes and Keap tasks become activities. Work done in Odoo goes back: new contacts, changed phone numbers, a deal moved to Won, a note after a call, an activity marked done.
