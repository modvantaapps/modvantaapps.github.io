Odoo Freshchat integration

Freshchat Connector

Your team answers chats in Freshchat. Your customers, leads and tasks live in Odoo. This puts every Freshchat conversation on the right contact and lead, and lets you answer it from Odoo.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Paste your Freshchat account URL and API key and press Test Connection. Odoo reads your agents, groups and topics, links each agent to the Odoo user with the same e-mail, and from then on reads your customers and conversations. Every message lands in the chatter of the conversation, written by the customer or by the agent who sent it, with files and images as links. A new conversation can open a CRM lead, an opportunity or a project task, owned by the Odoo user of the agent it is assigned to. When the chat is resolved, the whole transcript goes on the contact. Your team can reply, add a private note, assign, resolve and reopen without leaving Odoo, and the Freshchat web messenger can run on your Odoo website with logged-in customers recognised on any device.

Features

Conversations in the chatterevery message posted once as an internal note, customer or agent shown as the author, private notes marked, files and images kept as links
Lead, opportunity or taskone per new conversation, with the first message; the sales team comes from the Freshchat group or topic, the owner from the assigned agent
Reply from Odooreply to the customer or leave a private note, sent as the Freshchat agent linked to your Odoo user, with a fallback agent for everyone else
Assign, resolve, reopenbuttons on the conversation that change it in Freshchat and read it back
Customers both waysFreshchat users matched to Odoo contacts by id, restore id or exact e-mail, inside the right company; Odoo contacts you tick are created or updated in Freshchat
Agents and topicsagents linked to Odoo users, groups and topics linked to sales teams
Transcript on the contactthe full conversation posted on the customer when Freshchat marks it resolved
Webhooksmessage created, assigned, resolved and reopened events, each checked against the RSA signature Freshchat sends; the conversation is then read again from the API
Website chatthe Freshchat web messenger on your Odoo website, hidden on pages you choose, optionally after cookie consent, with name, e-mail and restore id for logged-in customers
Reports and loggraph and pivot by topic, agent and status, and a sync log line for every read and send
