CTT Express Shipping Integration

Ship with CTT Express, the parcel carrier for Spain and Portugal, straight from Odoo: the shipping code and the label when you validate a delivery order, the right service for mainland Spain, the Balearic and Canary Islands, Portugal or abroad, cash on delivery (contra reembolso), automatic tracking, cancel and return pickups at your customer's address.

What it does

Shops and warehouses in Spain and Portugal with a CTT Express contract that want labels, tracking and cash on delivery in Odoo, without typing shipments into the CTT web portal. CTT Express is a normal Odoo delivery method: use it next to your other carriers, on quotations, in the website shop and on delivery orders.

Enter the client id, client secret and client center code CTT Express gave you, then Test Connection. Let the module choose the service by destination (CTT 24 / 48, Baleares, Canarias, Internacional) or always use one service such as CTT 14 or CTT 10. Labels as PDF (thermal or A4 sheets with 1, 3 or 4 labels) or ZPL.

CTT Express has no price API, so the shipping price comes from your contract: a rate table per zone (Spain mainland, Balearic Islands, Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla, Portugal, Azores and Madeira, Andorra, other countries) and weight, a fixed price or the Odoo price rules. Margins and free shipping above an amount work as usual.

Every two hours the tracking history is read for open shippings. The CTT status codes (recogido, en transito, en reparto, entregado, reparto fallido...) become one of: registered, in transit, out for delivery, at a CTT point, delivered, incident, returned or cancelled. Status changes are posted on the transfer, and "Track Online" opens the CTT Express tracking page.

Inventory - Operations - CTT Express lists each shipping with service, zone, parcels, cash on delivery amount and status. Filter on shippings on the way, incidents, cash on delivery or returns, and group by zone or service.

Features

Validate the delivery orderthe shipping is registered with CTT Express, the label is attached and posted in the chatter and the shipping code becomes the tracking reference. With cash on delivery on, unpaid orders are sent with the order total as reembolso (or the amount you type). Each Odoo package is one parcel.
