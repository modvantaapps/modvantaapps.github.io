NACEX Shipping Integration

Ship with NACEX from Odoo - labels, NACEX.shop points, reembolso, returns and tracking

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A NACEX connector for Odoo 19 on the NACEX web service: validating a delivery order books the shipment and attaches the NACEX label, cash on delivery comes from the order, customers pick a NACEX.shop point at the checkout, and the NACEX status history is synced onto every shipment.

The module adds NACEX as a delivery method provider in Odoo and uses the NACEX web service for bookings, labels, NACEX.shop points, returns, prices and tracking - for Spain, Portugal and Andorra.

Each delivery method holds your agency, customer number, department and web service user, the NACEX service (19:00H, 10:00H, Sabado, e-NACEX and the others), delivery to the address or to a NACEX.shop point, packaging, who pays the shipping, Saturday delivery, retorno, insurance, prealerts by email or SMS and the price - a NACEX rate table by zone and weight, or a live quote from NACEX.

Features

All NACEX shipments in one list
NACEX web service account, service and options
NACEX.shop delivery and live quote
Choose a NACEX.shop on the delivery order
NACEX tab on the delivery order
Shipment with delivery note and Andorra value
NACEX at the website checkout
NACEX.shop map for the customer
