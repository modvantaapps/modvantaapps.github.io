Odoo NACEX Shipping integration

NACEX Shipping Integration

Ship with NACEX from Odoo - labels, NACEX.shop points, reembolso, returns and tracking

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A NACEX connector for Odoo 19 on the NACEX web service: validating a delivery order books the shipment and attaches the NACEX label, cash on delivery comes from the order, customers pick a NACEX.shop point at the checkout (needs the Odoo eCommerce app), and the NACEX status history is synced onto every shipment.

The module adds NACEX as a delivery method provider in Odoo and uses the NACEX web service for bookings, labels, NACEX.shop points, returns, prices and tracking - for Spain, Portugal and Andorra.

Each delivery method holds your agency, customer number, department and web service user, the NACEX service (19:00H, 10:00H, Sabado, e-NACEX and the others), delivery to the address or to a NACEX.shop point, packaging, who pays the shipping, Saturday delivery, retorno, insurance, prealerts by email or SMS and the price - a NACEX rate table by zone and weight, or a live quote from NACEX.

Who it is for

  • Spanish webshops shipping next day. A shop on the peninsula that sends most orders with NACEX 19:00H or 10:00H can book the shipment and print the label by validating the delivery order, with the NACEX code and delivery note stored on the transfer.
  • Stores that sell contra reembolso. When part of an order is still unpaid at shipping time, the module can book the rest as cash on delivery and record whether the sender or the recipient pays the NACEX commission.
  • Sellers to the Canary Islands, Ceuta, Melilla and Andorra. These destinations sit outside the EU VAT area. The module sends the value of the goods without taxes and a content description, and shows it in a customs note on the shipment.
  • Shops offering NACEX.shop collection. Buyers who aren't home during the day can pick a NACEX.shop point or locker, either at the website checkout or chosen by your team on the quotation or the delivery order.

Setting it up

  1. Open Inventory > NACEX > Delivery Methods and create a method with Provider set to NACEX, or start from one of the demo methods.
  2. On the NACEX tab enter Agency (del_cli), Customer Number, Department (dep_cli), Web Service User and the password NACEX gave you, then press Test Connection.
  3. Choose the NACEX Service and the delivery mode. For pickup points, set delivery to a NACEX.shop, pick the NACEX.shop Service and set Max. Points Shown.
  4. Set Packaging (tip_env), who pays the shipping, and options such as Saturday delivery, Return (Retorno), Insurance (Seguro) and the recipient prealerts.
  5. Under Price choose a NACEX rate table by zone and weight, a live quote from NACEX (getValoracion), a fixed price or Odoo price rules. Fill the Rate Table if you use it.
  6. For cash on delivery, set COD Commission (tip_ree) and tick Ship Unpaid Orders as COD, or list the COD Payment Providers whose orders should ship contra reembolso.
  7. Validate a delivery order to book it and get the label, then follow the status under Inventory > NACEX > Shipments.

NACEX Shipping Integration: questions

Which destinations can I ship to?

Spain (the peninsula, the Balearic and Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla), Portugal including the Azores and Madeira, and Andorra. Each shipment records its NACEX zone.

What happens when I cancel a shipment?

Odoo sends the cancellation to NACEX while the shipment is still booked or in draft. Once NACEX shows it on its way, the cancel is refused with a message so you can sort it out with your agency.

How do returns work?

You choose per delivery method. The default is a return label the customer takes to a NACEX.shop, which goes back to the address on your NACEX account. The other option has NACEX collect at the customer's address, and the field help suggests checking its parameters against your NACEX manual.

What if the live price quote fails?

With Rate Table when the Quote Fails ticked (the default), Odoo falls back to your own rate table instead of hiding NACEX at the checkout. Without it, the method isn't offered for that order.

Can I try it before my NACEX web service user is ready?

Yes. Demo mode answers every call locally with sample labels, NACEX.shop points and tracking. Three demo delivery methods with rate tables are created when demo data is loaded.

Do I need Enterprise, and which languages are there?

Community is enough: it depends on the Inventory delivery framework and Discuss. Spanish, Catalan and Portuguese translations are included.
