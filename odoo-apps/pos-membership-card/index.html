POS Membership Card

POS Membership Card for Odoo 19: scan the member card at the till, and the member gets the member pricelist and the member discount of the plan - checked again by the server.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Scan the CardType the CodeMember PricelistMember DiscountAuto on CustomerExpired Cards RefusedReceiptServer Checks

What it does

Automatic when the member is selected as customer (setting per point of sale).

Expired or unknown cards are refused with the reason and the end date; cards created after the session was opened are found on the server.

Features

Scan or type the cardscan the barcode of the member card, or use the Membership Card button and type the card code or the member number.
Member pricesthe member becomes the customer of the order, the member pricelist of the plan is set and the member discount of the plan is given on every line, also on the lines added later.
Other discountskeep the discounts already on a line, or replace them by the member discount (setting).
On the screen and the receipta badge with the plan, and the member number and plan printed on the receipt.
POS orders per membershipa smart button on the membership lists its POS orders.
Server checksthe card must be active on the order date, belong to the customer and the company of the order, and no line may get more than the discount of the plan.
