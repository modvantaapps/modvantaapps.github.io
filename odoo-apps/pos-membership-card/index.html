POS Membership Card

POS Membership Card for Odoo 19: scan the member card at the till, and the member gets the member pricelist and the member discount of the plan - checked again by the server.

What it does

Automatic when the member is selected as customer (setting per point of sale).

Expired or unknown cards are refused with the reason and the end date; cards created after the session was opened are found on the server.

Who it is for

  • Gyms and sports clubs with a shop counter. Members buy drinks, supplements or gear at the front desk. The cashier scans the card barcode and the plan's member discount lands on every line, so nobody has to look the member up by name.
  • Retailers running a paid loyalty plan. A store that sells a yearly membership can give members their own pricelist at the till. When a card has run out, the register refuses it and shows the end date.
  • Clubs with several membership tiers. Each plan carries its own pricelist and discount percentage, so a gold member and a basic member get different prices from the same scan.
  • Owners worried about over-discounting. The server checks every paid order. A line can't carry more than the plan's discount, and the card has to belong to the order's customer and company.

Setting it up

  1. Install Membership Management first, then this module, which adds the Point of Sale side.
  2. Go to Membership > Configuration > Membership Plans, open a plan, and on its Membership tab fill in Member Pricelist, Member Discount (%), or both, under Member Prices in the Point of Sale.
  3. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings and find Membership Cards. Choose whether to apply the membership when the member is selected as customer, and whether member discounts keep or replace other discounts on a line.
  4. Print the member card from the membership in Membership Management. The card code on it is the barcode the till reads.
  5. At the till, scan the card, or open the Membership Card button and type the card code or the member number. The member becomes the customer and the member prices are applied.
  6. Open a membership and use the POS Orders smart button to see what that member has bought at the register.

POS Membership Card: questions

What else do I have to install?

Membership Management from Modvanta, which holds the members, plans and card codes, plus Odoo's Point of Sale app. Both run on Community, so Enterprise isn't needed.

What happens when an expired card is scanned?

The register shows a Membership card refused message with the reason, for example that the membership expired on a given date or isn't active. No member prices are applied. Cards created after the session opened are looked up on the server, so new members don't need a session restart.

Does the discount apply to items added after the scan?

Yes. The plan discount is given on every line of the order, including lines added later. You can choose in the settings whether a line that already has a discount keeps it or gets the member discount instead.

Can a cashier change the member discount by hand?

They can edit a line, but the server checks the order when it's paid. If a member line carries more than the plan's discount, or the card doesn't belong to the order's customer or company, the order is rejected with an error.

Does it work with more than one company?

Yes. The register only loads and finds cards of its own company, and an order using another company's card is refused by the server.

Is the membership printed on the receipt?

Yes. The receipt shows the member number and the plan, and the order screen shows a badge with the plan while the cashier is working.
