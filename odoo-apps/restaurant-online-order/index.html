Restaurant Online Ordering

Restaurant Online Ordering for Odoo 19 - Takeaway and Delivery into your POS

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Sell your restaurant menu on your own website without commission: customers pick dishes with their options and combos, choose takeaway or delivery, book ASAP or a time slot, pay online or in cash, and follow the order live. Every order lands in your restaurant Point of Sale as a normal order, so the cashier, the kitchen and your accounting work exactly as they do today.

Everything a restaurant needs to take orders online, in one module.

Your Point of Sale products, categories, photos and descriptions as a fast menu page in your website theme, with badges (new, popular, chef, spicy), dietary and allergen labels, a dietary filter and a basket that follows the customer.

Sizes, cooking, toppings and add-ons come from Odoo product attributes (one choice or several, with extra prices); meal boxes come from Odoo combo products. The customer picks, adds a note for the kitchen and sees the price change.

The customer chooses pickup or delivery. Each uses its own Point of Sale preset, so its pricelist and fiscal position apply and the register shows the right label.

Who it is for

  • Restaurants already running Odoo Point of Sale. Online orders are created as Point of Sale orders in the open session of the register you pick, with the takeaway or delivery preset and the promised time filled in. The register shows a pop-up so the cashier sees each new order.
  • Takeaway and delivery kitchens. Customers order from a /food menu page on your Odoo website, choose pickup or delivery and pick ASAP or a time slot. Slots close when the kitchen hits its Orders per Slot or Kitchen Load per Slot limit.
  • Restaurants with their own drivers. Mark employees as Delivery Driver and assign them to orders. A driver logs in to the /food/driver page on a phone to see their orders, mark them out for delivery or delivered and switch their availability.
  • Places with several outlets. Each outlet has its own register, menu categories, opening hours, delivery zones, payment options and pause button. Reports under Point of Sale > Reporting show online orders, top dishes and driver cash.

Features

Breakfast only in the morningPoint of Sale category hours and per-dish online hours are respected, and a Sold out switch greys a dish out until the next morning.
Geofencingdraw the delivery area on a map, or use a radius in km or a postcode list. The customer's GPS location or address is checked automatically, and the postcodes inside the area fill themselves. Fee, free-delivery threshold, minimum order and extra time per zone.
Geofencingdelivery area drawn on a map, by distance or by postcode

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It adds Point of Sale > Online Ordering with Online Orders, Outlets, Menu Items, Drivers, Delivery Zones and Dietary Labels.
  2. Create an outlet under Point of Sale > Online Ordering > Outlets. Pick the Point of Sale register, the Menu Categories to show and the Opening Hours schedule, and add any Holiday Closures.
  3. On the Takeaway & Delivery tab, tick Takeaway / Pickup and Delivery as needed, choose a preset for each and a Delivery Fee Product. Press Locate the restaurant on the map so distances can be worked out.
  4. Add delivery zones by postcode list, by radius in km or by drawing the area on the map. Each zone has its own Delivery Fee, Free Delivery Above amount, Minimum Order and Extra Delivery Time.
  5. On the Hours & Slots tab, set the Preparation Time, Slot Length, Orders per Slot and the Busy Kitchen thresholds. On the Payment tab, choose Online Payment, Cash on Delivery / at Pickup, or both.
  6. Check each dish under Online Ordering > Menu Items: Sell Online (Food Ordering), Online Hours, Dietary / Allergens labels, Kitchen Load and Menu Badge.
  7. Open the register session, then press Open Online Menu on the outlet to place a test order. Orders are only accepted while that session is open.

Restaurant Online Ordering: questions

Does it need Odoo Enterprise?

No. It depends on Point of Sale for restaurants, Point of Sale online payment, Website, Portal, Employees and partner geolocation. All of these are Community apps.

Do I pay a commission per order?

No. Orders come through your own Odoo website and go into your own register. Online payments go through the payment providers you have set up in Odoo, so only that provider's normal fees apply.

What can the customer see after ordering?

A tracking page that updates on its own as the order goes from received to accepted, preparing, ready, out for delivery and done. Emails go out when the order is received, accepted, ready, out for delivery or cancelled. Logged in customers find past orders under /my/food-orders and can order the same dishes again.

What happens when the kitchen is overloaded?

Pause Ordering, Pause 30 min or Pause 1 hour stop new online orders, and a scheduled job resumes them at the time you set. With the busy thresholds set, the outlet adds extra preparation time or pauses itself when too many orders are in the kitchen.

What happens to orders nobody pays for?

An online order that is still awaiting payment after the Unpaid Order Timeout is cancelled by a scheduled job, which frees its time slot. Another job clears every Sold Out Today switch each night.

What doesn't it do?

It does not connect to Uber Eats, Deliveroo or other delivery platforms, and it has no live GPS tracking of drivers. Orders can't be placed when the register session is closed. Dishes, options and combos come from your Point of Sale products and attributes, so the menu is managed there.
