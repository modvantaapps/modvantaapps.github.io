Advanced Delivery Date Picker

Advanced Delivery Date Picker for Odoo 19 eCommerce

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Datesonly the days you can deliverSlotswith places leftRulesper postcode prefixOrderdate and slot on the sale

What it does

Let customers choose a delivery date and time slot in the Odoo checkout, with minimum notice, a daily cutoff, holidays, slot capacity and rules per postcode - and see the chosen slot on the sales order.

Turn the picker on for a delivery method and the checkout shows the dates you can deliver for that method. Dates follow a working schedule, a minimum notice in hours, a maximum booking horizon and a daily cutoff after which extra days are added. Holidays you enter are left out.

After a date is chosen, the time slots of that day appear, each with how many places are left when you set a maximum number of orders per slot. Postcode rules change the lead days, slot hours, weekdays, cutoff and capacity for postcodes that start with given prefixes.

The chosen date and slot are shown on the order confirmation and stored on the sales order, together with the postcode rule that was applied.
