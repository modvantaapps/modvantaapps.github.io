Contact Follow-Up Cadence

Set how often each contact should be spoken to and get an overdue queue when nobody has. Follow-up reminder, contact frequency, touchpoint tracking, account check-in, customer neglect report, last contacted date.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Activities only remind you of follow-ups someone remembered to schedule. The accounts that slip away are the ones nobody scheduled anything for.

This module lets you give contacts a cadence policy such as Key Account every 2 weeks or Low Touch every quarter. It finds the last real interaction with each contact (a message or email on the contact, or a confirmed sales order), works out when the next contact is due and sets a status: On Track, Due Soon, Overdue or Never Contacted. Overdue and Due Soon queues show who needs a call, and a nightly job can put a To Do on the salesperson of every overdue contact.
