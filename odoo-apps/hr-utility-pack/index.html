HR Utility Pack

Attendance Dashboard, Attendance Report, Employee ID Card, Employee Training, Leave Summary, employee restriction and job position requests in one module. Built on the Odoo Community HR apps you already use - no new attendance or time off data to enter.

What it does

Everything works on the Employees, Attendances, Time Off and Recruitment apps of Odoo

Counters for employees present today, absent, on leave and late, each one opens the list of those employees. The Today tab lists every employee with status, first check in, last check out and hours. Filter by company, department, employee, month and year.

The Attendance tab shows the month for the whole team - one coloured square per employee and day for present, late, absent, leave, public holiday, weekend and upcoming days - and a monthly calendar of one employee with check in and check out times.

Worked hours against expected hours from the working schedule, overtime hours, late arrivals and early departures, employees present per day, hours per week, the top performers by attendance rate and the employees with the most overtime. A Leaves tab lists pending time off requests, leave by type and the public holidays of the month.

Pick employees or a department and a date range. The PDF gives a summary (working days, present, absent, leave, late, early departures, hours, overtime, attendance rate), one line per day with check in, check out and remarks, and the approved time off of the period. The Excel file has a summary sheet, a daily detail sheet and a time off sheet. A third button emails each employee their own report.

Features

Per leave typecarried forward leave, current entitlement, taken this year, planned, pending approval and leave balance. Shown on the employee form, on every time off request and in a Leave Summary report in PDF and Excel for chosen employees or a department.
