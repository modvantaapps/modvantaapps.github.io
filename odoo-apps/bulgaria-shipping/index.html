Bulgaria Shipping

Bulgaria Shipping for Odoo 19 - Econt and Speedy

What it does

Ship with Econt and Speedy (DPD Bulgaria) straight from Odoo. The courier prices the delivery on the quotation, validating the delivery order creates the waybill and attaches the label, the tracking number and the status come back from the courier, and customers choose an Econt office, an Econtomat, a Speedy office or a Speedy automat on a map at the website checkout. That map is Odoo's own checkout location picker, so it needs the eCommerce app. One module, both big Bulgarian couriers, your own courier accounts.

The module adds Econt (Econt Express JSON Delivery API) and Speedy (Speedy / DPD Bulgaria Web API) as delivery method providers of the standard Odoo shipping framework. Everything you already know keeps working: delivery methods on quotations and on the website, margins, free shipping above an amount, packages and Send to Shipper. On top of it you get the waybill with its label, cash on delivery, delivery to offices and lockers, tracking, cancellation, courier requests, the cash on delivery payments report and a handover list.

Who it is for

  • Bulgarian web shops shipping with Econt, Speedy or both. Both couriers become delivery methods in standard Odoo shipping. The courier prices the delivery on the quotation, and validating the delivery order creates the waybill and attaches the label.
  • Shops selling on cash on delivery. Many Bulgarian buyers pay the courier on delivery (nalozhen platezh). The module sends the COD amount on the waybill and can import what Econt and Speedy paid out, matched to each waybill with the expected amount shown alongside.
  • Stores whose customers collect from an office or locker. Customers can pick an Econt office, an Econtomat, a Speedy office or a Speedy automat on the map in the Odoo checkout. Orders paid by cash on delivery only see points that accept it.
  • Warehouses printing waybills every day. Labels come as PDF, or for Speedy also in A6, 4 x A6 or ZPL. You can request a courier pickup for the day's waybills from the delivery orders list, with a date, time window, parcels and weight.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module, then open Inventory > Configuration > Delivery Methods (also under Inventory > Bulgarian Couriers).
  2. Create a delivery method and choose Econt or Speedy as the provider. Tick Demo Mode to try the whole flow before you have an account.
  3. For a real account, enter the API User and API Password: your e-Econt user for Econt, or the Web API user Speedy gave you.
  4. Press Test Connection, then Import Services and Import Sender Profiles, and pick the Courier Service and options such as Open Before Paying or SMS Notification.
  5. To offer collection points, tick Deliver to Offices / Lockers, choose the Point Network and press Import Offices / Lockers.
  6. Publish the method on your website or add it to a quotation, then validate a delivery order to create the waybill and its label.
  7. Use Sync Courier Tracking on a delivery, or let the scheduled job update tracking every two hours.

Bulgaria Shipping: questions

Do I need my own courier contract?

Yes, for real shipments. The module uses your own Econt or Speedy API account, with no middleman. Demo Mode answers every call locally, so you can test without one.

Can I cancel a waybill?

Yes, while the parcel hasn't been handed over. Once the courier reports it as picked up, in transit or delivered, Odoo refuses to cancel and tells you which waybill is already with the courier.

How are offices and lockers kept current?

The list of points is imported into Odoo with address, GPS position and opening hours, and refreshed by a weekly job. Points the courier no longer lists are archived on the next import.

Can I ship from more than one warehouse?

Yes. A delivery method can have a line per warehouse with its own pickup address, contract reference and Econt COD agreement.

Which Odoo apps does it need?

It builds on the standard delivery framework (stock_delivery) and runs on Community or Enterprise. The office and locker picker in the checkout needs the eCommerce app; in the backend you choose the point on the quotation or delivery order.

Is it an official Econt or Speedy product?

No. It's written by Modvanta against the couriers' public APIs and isn't affiliated with Econt Express or Speedy.
