Bulgaria Shipping

Bulgaria Shipping for Odoo 19 - Econt and Speedy

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
couriers in one moduleoffice and locker networksautomatic testsone-time price

What it does

Ship with Econt and Speedy (DPD Bulgaria) straight from Odoo. The courier prices the delivery on the quotation, validating the delivery order creates the waybill and attaches the label, the tracking number and the status come back from the courier, and customers choose an Econt office, an Econtomat, a Speedy office or a Speedy automat on a map at the website checkout. One module, both big Bulgarian couriers, your own courier accounts.

The module adds Econt (Econt Express JSON Delivery API) and Speedy (Speedy / DPD Bulgaria Web API) as delivery method providers of the standard Odoo shipping framework. Everything you already know keeps working: delivery methods on quotations and on the website, margins, free shipping above an amount, packages and Send to Shipper. On top of it you get the waybill with its label, cash on delivery, delivery to offices and lockers, tracking, cancellation, courier requests, the cash on delivery payments report and a handover list.
