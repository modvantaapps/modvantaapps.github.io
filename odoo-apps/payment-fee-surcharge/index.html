Payment Fee & COD Charges

Charge a fee or give a discount for each payment method at checkout

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Add a Cash on Delivery charge, a credit card processing fee or a bank transfer discount to Odoo eCommerce orders. The shopper sees the fee and the new total the moment they pick a payment method, before paying.

Payment methods do not cost a shop the same. Cash on delivery means handling and collection, cards come with processing fees, and a bank transfer costs almost nothing. Standard Odoo charges the same total whichever method the shopper picks.

This module adds Payment Fee Rules. Each rule names the payment methods it applies to and adds a fixed amount, a percentage of the order or both. A negative amount or percentage turns the rule into a discount. Rules can also be limited to some countries and to an order total range, and a percentage fee can be clamped between a minimum and a maximum.

On the payment step of the checkout, when the shopper clicks a payment method, the fee is added to the order as its own line and the order summary is refreshed at once: the fee row appears above the subtotal, and the total that is charged is the total that is shown. Switching to another method replaces the fee, it never adds a second one.

Features

Payment fee rules in one list
Fixed, percentage or both, with a minimum and a maximum
Cash on Delivery charge at checkout
Credit card processing fee
Bank transfer discount
The fee stays on the confirmed order
A normal order line in the backend
