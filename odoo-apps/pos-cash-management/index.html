POS Cash Management

Control the cash drawer from opening to bank deposit: float, blind count, transfers, reports, closing hours and a risk list.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Everything around the cash drawer that Odoo's Point of Sale leaves to spreadsheets, enforced by the server.

Features

Opening cash controlcount as usual, suggest a fixed float in the count, or open with no count at all (last closing balance or a fixed float). The no-count modes are done by the server: no popup.
Blind closing countcashiers type what is in the drawer without seeing the expected amount or the difference; managers still see everything.
No closing countthe server takes the expected cash as counted, whatever the browser sends.
Hide the Daily Sale buttonfrom cashiers on the closing screen.
POS cash in out reportevery cash in and out of every till in one list, pivot and graph, and a PDF per session from the backend or the POS menu.
POS cash transfermove cash from an open or closed session to another open register or to a bank or safe journal. Never more than the cash available; both cash moves are linked to the transfer.
POS auto close sessionevery day at the time you choose, the server closes the open session with the expected cash as counted. Sessions with unpaid orders stay open and get a note.
Closed periodsevery night between two times, a day of the week, or between two dates: the till cannot be opened, except by the users you list.
Cashier risk monitorwhen a session closes, cashiers whose refunds or discounts are above a share of their sales, cash outs above an amount, or a cash difference above a limit are listed with the numbers behind each case, for a manager to mark reviewed or escalate.
