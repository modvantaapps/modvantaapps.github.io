Microsoft Teams Connector

Microsoft Teams in Odoo - add a Teams meeting to any calendar event and post Odoo updates to Teams channels

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Create Microsoft Teams meetings from Odoo calendar events, CRM leads and contacts, with the join link in the invitation. Post Adaptive Cards to Teams channels from Odoo actions - new leads, confirmed orders, paid invoices - through Microsoft Graph or a Workflows webhook.

The Microsoft Teams Connector signs each Odoo user in with their Microsoft account and creates real Teams online meetings. On a calendar event, Add Teams Meeting stores the join link in the video call field, with the meeting ID, passcode and organizer, and the chatter records it. From a CRM lead, Schedule Teams Meeting creates the calendar event with the Teams meeting and the attendees of the lead in one step.

Teams channels are set up in Odoo with their delivery method: Microsoft Graph, posting as a signed-in user, or a Teams Workflows webhook that needs no sign-in. A Post to Teams server action sends an Adaptive Card with a title, message, facts taken from fields of the record and an Open in Odoo button. It can be used from the Action menu, in scheduled actions or in automation rules, and ready-made examples are created for leads, contacts, sales orders and invoices.

Messages are queued with the transaction and posted after the commit with retries. Every message, meeting and API call is logged, and Demo Mode replaces Microsoft with a built-in simulator so you can try everything before you register an app.
