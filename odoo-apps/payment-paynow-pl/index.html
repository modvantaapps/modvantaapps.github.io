Paynow Payment Gateway Poland

Take mBank Paynow payments in Odoo 19: BLIK, cards and pay by link, with refunds from Odoo

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
PaynowmBankBLIKPay by linkCardsPolandPLN

What it does

Adds Paynow, the payment service of the mBank group, as a payment provider in Odoo. The customer pays on the Paynow page with any method of your shop. Every request and notification is signed, and the result the browser brings back is never trusted: Odoo reads it from Paynow.

Features

Hosted payment pagea payment is created for each transaction and the customer is sent to the Paynow page
Open the chosen methodoptional: when the customer picks BLIK or Card in Odoo, skip the method list of the Paynow page
Signed requestsHMAC-SHA256 on every call, GET included, with an Idempotency-Key that makes a retry safe
Signed notificationsa notification with a wrong or missing Signature is refused and changes nothing
Retries on the Paynow pagea rejected payment that the customer retries keeps its reference and takes the new payment ID
Refundsfull or partial from Odoo, with the reason you choose; a refund notification closes it, and an awaiting refund can be cancelled
Failed payment emailsoptional: an email to your accounting team when a payment fails
Payments analysisa graph and a pivot of your Paynow payments by state and month
Lost notificationsa scheduled action checks open payments and refunds
Point of Salewith Odoo online payment; Paynow requires a buyer email, so an order with no customer uses the email of your company, and a clear message says what to set when there is none
API logeach call and notification, with keys and customer details masked
Polishthe module is translated
