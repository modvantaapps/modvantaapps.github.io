Purchase Ops Pack

Purchase Order Payment Status (order payment status: no bill, not paid, partially paid, overdue, paid) with amount billed, amount paid and amount due on the purchase order, bill payment details, register payment from the order and a Payment Status Report. Purchase Advance Payment and Purchase Down Payment (vendor advance bill deducted on the final bill), Purchase History of product prices on the order line, Split Purchase Order, Purchase Revision (purchase order revision history), Supplier Credit Limit with manager approval and hold, Purchase Pricelist (vendor pricelist), Purchase Quotation Number (separate RFQ sequence) and Purchase Order Status (received, billed, paid). Each feature can be switched on or off in Purchase settings.

What it does

Ten purchase tools in one module, each with its own on/off switch

Pay vendors part of an order up front and have it taken off the final bill. Register advance payments on a confirmed order and let the bill pick them up. See what you paid for a product last time, right on the order line. Split an RFQ when part of it has to go out separately. Keep every revision of an order. Stop orders that would take you over the credit you allow yourself with a vendor.

Everything sits on the standard Odoo Purchase, Inventory and Invoicing apps: real vendor bills, real payments, real reconciliation. Each tool can be switched off in Purchase -> Configuration -> Settings, so you only see the buttons you use.
