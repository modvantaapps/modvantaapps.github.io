Odoo Galaxus integration

Galaxus Connector

Sell on Digitec Galaxus from Odoo. The module builds the Galaxus product feeds (stock and price, product data, media, specifications, accessories) and runs the whole merchant order flow over the Galaxus SFTP server: orders in, order responses, dispatch notifications, cancel requests and returns out - in the openTRANS XML format Galaxus documents.

What it does

Galaxus product data feedStock and price feedopenTRANS EDIORDP / ORDR / DELRCancel requests CANP / CANRReturns RETP / SURNStock shortage EOLNGalaxus SFTPSwitzerland and EUGerman and FrenchDemo mode

Tick "Publish on Galaxus" on your products and Odoo builds the feeds Galaxus asks for: stage 1 stock and price (price excluding VAT from a pricelist, VAT rate, free stock per warehouse minus a safety stock, restock time, minimum order quantity and quantity steps), stage 2 product data (brand, category path, titles and descriptions in German, French, English and Italian, weight, packaging size, customs code) and media, and stage 3 specifications and accessories. Galaxus reads them from token-protected HTTPS links or you upload them by SFTP. Every 15 minutes Odoo picks up new orders from the Galaxus SFTP folder, creates the sales order with the customer, the delivery address, Galaxus prices and VAT, confirms it and answers with an order response. Missing stock is cancelled with a supplier cancel notification, a validated delivery sends the dispatch notification with tracking number and carrier, and cancel requests and returns from Galaxus are answered from Odoo.
