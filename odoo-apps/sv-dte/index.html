El Salvador DTE Electronic Invoicing

El Salvador DTE Electronic Invoicing for Odoo 19

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Ministerio de HaciendaDTE JSONFactura 01CCF 03Notas 05 / 06Exportacion 11Sujeto excluido 14Invalidacion

What it does

Factura, Comprobante de Credito Fiscal, notes, export and sujeto excluido invoices as signed DTE JSON, sent to the Ministerio de Hacienda with sello de recepcion, invalidation and contingency.

When an invoice is validated the module builds the DTE JSON exactly as the official Ministerio de Hacienda schemas define it, signs it (JWS RS512) with the certificate file the MH issued to you - inside Odoo, no firmador server needed - and sends it to the recepciondte service. The sello de recepcion, the processing date and any MH observations are stored on the invoice.

Each type gets its numero de control (DTE-01-M001P001-000000000000001) per establishment and point of sale, and a codigo de generacion. The Archivo DTE (JSON with firmaElectronica and selloRecibido) goes to your customer with the PDF, and the printed invoice carries the QR of the public MH query.

Wrong documents are invalidated with the Evento de invalidacion; when the MH or your connection is down, contingency mode keeps signing and later reports everything with the Evento de contingencia.
