OTTO Market Connector

Sell on otto.de from Odoo. Orders arrive as sale orders when OTTO marks them processable, shipments are reported with tracking and return tracking when you validate the delivery, and your product variations, stock and prices go to OTTO Market from Odoo - all through your own OTTO Partner Connect app.

What it does

OTTO Market APIotto.de ordersShipments and multiparcelReturnsReceipts PDFPrice reductionsProducts-V5 listingsBrands and categoriesStock syncPrice syncGermany marketplaceGerman translationDemo mode

Orders, orders to ship, listings, listing problems, returns and failed jobs at a glance. Demo mode shows everything without an OTTO account.

Client credentials token of your OTTO Partner Connect app with the scopes you enable, sandbox or production, order, shipment, listing, stock and price settings on one form.

Processable orders become confirmed sale orders: one line per SKU, OTTO gross prices and VAT rates, delivery fees and discounts, buyer and delivery address. Announced orders wait until OTTO releases the address.

The OTTO tab of the order lists each position item with its state, price, VAT rate, expected delivery, carrier and tracking number. Cancel single items or apply a price reduction from here.
