Montonio Payment and Montonio Shipping

Montonio payments and Montonio shipping for Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania in one Odoo module: bank links, cards and pay later at checkout, parcel machines and labels after it.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Montonio PaymentMontonio ShippingBank LinkHire PurchaseOmniva, DPD, SmartPost, Venipak

What it does

One Montonio store holds your API keys. The payment provider and the delivery methods both use it.

Features

Refunds from Odoofull or partial refunds with the standard Refund button. Montonio confirms each refund by webhook; a rejected refund is shown on the transaction.
Montonio shippingdelivery methods for the carriers active in your Montonio account - Omniva, DPD, SmartPosti (Itella SmartPost), Venipak, Unisend and others Montonio lists - to parcel machines, parcel shops, post offices or by courier.
Pickup point picker at checkoutcustomers choose a parcel machine in the standard Odoo location dialog, searched by postcode or town. The point is stored on the order and used for the shipment.
Labels and trackingvalidating the transfer registers the shipment and attaches the PDF label to it. Tracking numbers, tracking links, drop-off codes and status updates (in transit, ready at the pickup point, delivered) arrive by webhook or the hourly check, and the customer can get an email for each step.
