Romania Shipping

Romania Shipping for Odoo 19 - Fan Courier, Sameday, Cargus

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
couriers in one modulelocker and point networksautomatic testsone-time price

What it does

Ship with FAN Courier, Sameday and Cargus straight from Odoo. The courier quotes the shipping price on the quotation, validating the delivery order creates the AWB and attaches the label, the tracking number and the status come back from the courier, and customers choose an easybox, FANbox or Ship and Go point on a map at the website checkout. One module, three Romanian couriers, your own courier accounts.

The module adds FAN Courier (selfAWB API v2), Sameday (eAWB API) and Cargus (UrgentOnlineAPI) as delivery method providers of the standard Odoo shipping framework. Everything you already know keeps working: the delivery methods on quotations and on the website, margins, free shipping above an amount, packages and the Send to Shipper button. On top of it you get the AWB with its label, cash on delivery (ramburs), lockers and pick-up points, tracking, cancellation, courier pickup orders, the cash on delivery statement and a handover list for the courier.
