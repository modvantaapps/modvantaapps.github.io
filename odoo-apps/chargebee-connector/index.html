Odoo Chargebee integration

Chargebee Connector

Chargebee bills your subscribers. Odoo gets the customers, subscriptions, invoices and payments, booked to the cent.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
ChargebeeAPI v2CustomersItems and pricesSubscriptionsMRRInvoicesCredit notes

What it does

Enter your Chargebee site name and an API key. Each run reads only what changed in Chargebee since the last one. Customers become contacts with their billing address, VAT number and payment terms, and their Chargebee contacts come along as child contacts. Item families, items and item prices become product categories, products and a price list you can read in Odoo. Subscriptions are kept per customer with their items, trial and term dates, next billing date, MRR and coupons. Invoices and credit notes are rebuilt line by line with their discounts and taxes, and the Odoo total is compared with Chargebee's before anything is posted. Payments and refunds are registered and reconciled. A failed card payment shows on the invoice and the customer with the reason Chargebee gave.

Features

Customersname, company, e-mail, phone, VAT, billing address, net term days as payment terms, auto collection and taxability; Chargebee contacts as child contacts
Catalogitem families as product categories, plans, add-ons and charges as service products, every item price with currency, period, pricing model and trial
Couponspercentage or fixed, duration, redemptions and end date, linked to the subscriptions using them
Subscriptionsstatus, items and quantities, trial and term dates, next billing, MRR, cancel reason, with the invoices of each subscription
Invoices to the centone line per Chargebee line, a discount line under each discounted item, Odoo taxes with the same rate; if the totals differ, Chargebee's tax amounts are booked
Invoice statuspending invoices wait in draft, closed ones are posted, voided ones are cancelled
Credit notescustomer credit notes linked to the original invoice, with the reason, applied to the invoices Chargebee allocated them to
Payments and refundsregistered on the right invoice or credit note, one journal per currency if you want, gateway and reference kept, voided transactions cancelled
Failed paymentsthe decline reason is written on the invoice, in its chatter and on the customer
Odoo to Chargebeesend a contact as a customer, or a product as item family, item and item price
Webhooksone button adds the endpoint in Chargebee with basic authentication and a secret URL; each event is read again from Chargebee and handled once
Queue and logdocuments that cannot be booked yet wait and are retried with a growing delay; every run and problem is logged
