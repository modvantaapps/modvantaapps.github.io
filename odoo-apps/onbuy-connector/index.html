OnBuy Connector

OnBuy integration - OnBuy connector for Odoo 19

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Sell on OnBuy, run the stock and orders from Odoo.

Paste the consumer key and secret key from the OnBuy Seller Control Panel (API settings). Use the Test keys first; switch to the Live keys when you are happy. Test connection reads the OnBuy conditions and couriers.

Stock is the free quantity of the warehouse you pick, minus a buffer, with an optional cap. If you have 40 lamps, keep 2 back and two are reserved for an order, OnBuy shows 36. Prices come from a pricelist or the sales price. Only listings that changed are sent, up to 1000 SKUs per call.

Orders awaiting dispatch become confirmed sale orders with the buyer, the delivery address, the VAT-inclusive prices and a delivery line. When you validate the delivery, OnBuy is told: the whole order, or only what shipped, with the tracking number and the OnBuy courier code of your shipping method.

Features

Add productsevery saleable product with an internal reference and a barcode gets a listing row. "Find OnBuy products" looks the barcodes up in the OnBuy catalogue and fills the OnBuy product code (OPC); "Create listings" lists them under your SKU, in the condition you choose, with your handling days and delivery template.
