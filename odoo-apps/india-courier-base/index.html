India Courier Base

The shared foundation for Indian courier integrations in Odoo

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A free framework that Indian courier connectors plug into: AWB and shipment status on the delivery order, tracking events, shipping labels, Cash on Delivery detection, cached pincode serviceability, pickup requests and a full API log with secrets masked.

Every Indian courier integration needs the same pieces around the courier's own API: is this pincode serviceable, is the order Cash on Delivery and for how much, where is the AWB and the label, what is the latest tracking scan, and what exactly did we send to the courier when something went wrong.

India Courier Base provides those pieces once, on top of Odoo's standard delivery method framework. A courier connector only implements the calls to its own API; the base stores and shows the results, logs every call, normalizes the shipment status and syncs tracking every hour.
