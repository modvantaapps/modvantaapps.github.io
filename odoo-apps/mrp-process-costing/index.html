Manufacturing Process Costing

A material, labour and overhead cost sheet on every bill of materials and manufacturing order. Planned cost from the BoM, actual cost from consumption, scrap, work order time, operator rates and vendor bills, variance split into usage and price, by-product costing, WIP value of open orders, and optional journal entries that put labour and overhead into the finished product value.

What it does

Process CostingMRP CostingCost SheetActual CostingMO CostLabour CostOverhead CostBy Product CostingWork Order CostingWIP ValuationVariance

Direct material, direct labour, overhead and service lines with quantity or hours, unit cost and total. Totals per section and cost per unit after the by-product share.

Enter labour and overhead lines yourself, or generate them from the operations and the labour, overhead and operator rates of each work centre.

Percent of material, percent of direct cost, per unit, fixed per order, per work centre hour or percent of work centre labour. Limit a rule to a work centre or product category.

Orders copy the BoM cost sheet scaled to their quantity and rescale when the quantity changes. Lines are spread over the work orders by operation or by work centre.
