Slovakia Payroll

Slovakia Payroll for Odoo 19: Income Tax Advance, Social and Health Insurance, Child Tax Bonus and the Monthly Worksheet

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Slovak statutory payroll on Odoo Community: the monthly income tax advance with the 2026 bands and the non-taxable part, the child tax bonus, social insurance for the Social Insurance Agency fund by fund, the health insurance advance per insurer, and a monthly worksheet with CSV and Excel files, with a Slovak translation.

19% up to EUR 3,665.28, 25%, 30% and 35% above EUR 6,250.86 a month on the taxable wage.

EUR 497.23 a month for employees who signed the declaration with you.

EUR 100 or EUR 50 a month per child by age, within the 29% to 64% limits of the tax base.

Sickness, old-age, disability, unemployment, accident, guarantee, support and reserve fund.
