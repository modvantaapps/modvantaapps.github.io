Freight Management

Freight Management and Freight Forwarding for air, ocean and land: direct, house and master shipments, ports, airlines, vessels, vehicles, containers and packages, multi-stop routes, Shipment Tracking events, customs declarations, insurance, Incoterms, service charges, customer invoice and vendor bills with margin, AirWay Bill, Website Freight Quote form, CRM lead per freight request and freight dashboard

What it does

Book a shipment as Direct, House or Master and ship it by air, ocean or land. The carriage details follow the mode: airline, flight, MAWB and HAWB for air; vessel, voyage and bill of lading for ocean; vehicle and driver for land. Each shipment carries its packages and containers, a multi-stop route, services, customs declarations, insurance, Incoterm and terms.

A ribbon walks the shipment from Draft to Confirmed, In Process, In Transit and Done, writing a tracking event at each step. Every shipment gets a tracking number your customers use on the public Shipment Tracking page. Create one customer invoice and as many vendor bills as the carriers, agents and brokers send, and see invoiced revenue, billed cost and gross margin per shipment.
