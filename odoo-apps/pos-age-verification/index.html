POS Age Verification

An ID check at the till - and a record of every check and every refusal

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Set a minimum age on a product category or a product. When a basket with restricted goods is paid, the till asks the cashier to confirm the ID check or to enter the date of birth, and every result - sold or refused - is written to a log that cannot be edited from the screen.

Odoo Point of Sale has no age check. A shop selling alcohol, tobacco, vapes, knives or energy drinks relies on the cashier remembering, and has nothing to show afterwards that anyone looked.

With this module you put the age limit where it is decided: on the product category (Alcohol = 18, and every sub-category such as Spirits or Wine inherits it) or on one product. When the cashier presses Validate on the payment screen, the till asks once for the whole basket, using the highest age on it.

Each answer is recorded in Point of Sale -> Reporting -> Age Checks, refusals included. The server also refuses to save an order with restricted goods that has no passed check, so a reloaded or tampered till cannot skip it.
