GSTR-2B Reconciliation (India)

Reconcile GSTR-2B with your purchase bills in one click

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Upload the month's GSTR-2B JSON from the GST portal. Every document your suppliers filed is matched against the posted vendor bills in Odoo, and you get four answers: matched, figures differ, in GSTR-2B only, and in your books only.

Input tax credit can only be claimed for bills your suppliers have reported. Checking GSTR-2B against the purchase register by hand in a spreadsheet takes hours every month and the most expensive case - a bill in your books that the supplier never filed - is the easiest to miss.

This module reads the GSTR-2B JSON, lists every document with the supplier GSTIN, number, date, taxable value, IGST, CGST, SGST, cess and ITC status, and matches it to a posted vendor bill of the same company. Suppliers are matched on GSTIN, documents on number - first exactly, then ignoring punctuation and case, which is reported as a number spelled differently.

Bills dated in the return period from GSTIN suppliers that nothing in the statement matched are listed in the In your books only tab, with a warning at the top of the statement. The statement also checks itself against the portal's own per-supplier summary.
