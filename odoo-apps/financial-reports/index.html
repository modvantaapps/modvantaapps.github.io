Dynamic Financial Reports

Every financial report Odoo Community is missing, on screen, in PDF and in Excel

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Balance sheet, profit and loss, cash flow statement, executive summary, trial balance, ledgers, aged receivable and payable, tax report, analytic report, journal audit and the daily books - with comparison periods, branch and company filters, and every line drillable to its journal items.

Odoo Community keeps a complete set of books but has no balance sheet, profit and loss, cash flow statement, trial balance, ledgers or aged balances to read them with. This module adds fifteen reports under Accounting -> Reporting -> Financial Reports, computed from your journal items.

Every report opens on screen, prints to PDF in landscape with the options it was run with, and downloads as an Excel file with real numbers - always exactly the columns and lines on the screen.

Each report checks its own arithmetic and says so at the top: debits equal credits, assets equal liabilities and equity, opening cash plus the flows equals closing cash, the aged total equals the control account. If a check fails you still see the report, with the difference in figures.
