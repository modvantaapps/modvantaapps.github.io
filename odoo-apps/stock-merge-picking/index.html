Merge Picking

Merge delivery orders, receipts and transfers into one

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Three open deliveries for the same customer or three receipts from the same vendor: select them, choose Merge Transfers, and the warehouse works with one document. The stock moves are moved, not copied, so the sales and purchase orders stay linked.

Customers often place several orders in a day, and vendors confirm several purchase orders for the same truck. Odoo creates one transfer per order, so the warehouse picks, packs and validates the same contact several times.

This module merges open transfers of the same contact and operation type into one. You can create a new transfer or keep one of the selected transfers and move the lines of the others into it. The emptied transfers are cancelled and keep a link to the result, or deleted when that is safe.

Because the stock moves themselves are moved, each line still belongs to its own sales or purchase order line. The Delivery button of every merged sales order opens the merged transfer, and the chatter of each document records what was merged.

Features

Merge from the transfer list
Every refusal has a reason
Choose how to merge
One transfer, with its history
Originals kept and linked
Each original points to the result
Sales orders open the merged delivery
Merge button on the transfer
Delete the emptied transfers
Find merged transfers
