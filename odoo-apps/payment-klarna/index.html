Klarna Payment Provider

Offer Klarna Pay later, Pay in 3 and Pay now in your Odoo shop

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A direct Klarna integration with your own Klarna merchant credentials: the Klarna widget on the Odoo payment page, then the whole order life in standard Odoo screens - authorise, capture when the invoice is confirmed, refund from the credit note.

The module adds Klarna as a payment provider on the standard Odoo payment framework and talks to Klarna's own Payments and Order Management APIs with the API credentials of your Klarna merchant account. Choose the region of your account - Europe, North America or Oceania - and Odoo uses the Klarna playground in test mode and production when enabled.

At checkout the customer picks Pay later, Pay in 3 or Pay now in the Klarna widget. The order is authorised at Klarna; with manual capture, Odoo captures when the invoice is confirmed (or when the delivery is validated, or by hand), and a credit note can be refunded with Klarna in one click. Every call is written to an API log.
