Faspay Payment Gateway

Faspay Payment Gateway for Odoo 19 - Xpress, QRIS, Virtual Account, E-Wallets

What it does

Accept Faspay payments in Odoo for Indonesia: the customer pays on the Faspay page with QRIS, a bank virtual account (BCA, Mandiri, BRI, BNI, Permata, Maybank, CIMB, BSI), ShopeePay, OVO, DANA, LinkAja, Kredivo or Akulaku, from the website checkout, an invoice or a payment link. Every notification is checked with the Faspay signature, and Odoo asks Faspay for the status itself instead of trusting the browser.

The Faspay method opens the Faspay Xpress page with every channel active on your account. QRIS, each bank virtual account and each e-wallet open the Faspay page of that channel only.

The order is confirmed by the signed Faspay payment notification, or by Odoo's own Inquiry Payment Status call when the customer comes back. The return link itself never marks an order paid.

With a virtual account the order waits for the transfer. The virtual account number is kept on the payment, and the order is confirmed when Faspay notifies Odoo or the scheduled status check finds it paid. Unpaid payments are cancelled in Odoo after the expiry.

Customers pay an invoice from the portal or a payment link; the invoice is marked paid when Faspay confirms.
