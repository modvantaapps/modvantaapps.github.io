Germany VAT ID Check

For German companies: Odoo asks the Federal Central Tax Office (BZSt) to confirm the EU VAT ID of your customer - with company name, street, postcode and city - before an order, a delivery or an invoice goes out, and keeps every answer as evidence.

Why a VIES check is not enoughWho it is forAccountants and controllers

What it does

A German business that zero-rates an intra-Community supply must show that it acted with the care of a prudent merchant (Section 6a (4) UStG). The tax office expects a confirmation of the customer VAT ID from the BZSt under Section 18e UStG - ideally the qualified one (qualifizierte Bestaetigungsabfrage), which also compares the company name and address with the register of the member state.

Simple confirmation or qualified confirmation, set per company

Mismatch is never counted as confirmed; name and city must match

Option to accept D (not provided by the member state) for street and postcode

Confirmation reused for N days for the same VAT ID, name and address

Who it is for

  • German wholesalers invoicing EU business customers. A German seller who zero-rates a supply to another EU country normally needs a confirmed customer VAT ID on file. This asks the Federal Central Tax Office (BZSt) through its eVatR interface and keeps each answer with the contact and the document.
  • Online shops selling B2B across the EU. Checks can run on contact save, sales order, purchase order, delivery and invoice posting, each set to Ignore, Warn or Block. Orders already paid online aren't held up; a missing confirmation is flagged instead.
  • Accounting teams preparing for a tax audit. Every request is stored in a log that can't be edited or deleted, with the BZSt request ID, time stamp, status code and the raw answer. A PDF record can be filed on the contact and on the document that triggered it.
  • German companies in a multi-company group. Settings, requests and records belong to the company that sent them, so a German entity can run checks without touching the data of sister companies.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It needs Invoicing or Accounting together with Sales, Purchase and Inventory.
  2. Go to Accounting > Configuration > Settings and tick BZSt eVatR VAT ID Confirmation next to the VIES option.
  3. Check the Requesting German VAT ID. Leave it empty to use the company's own Tax ID.
  4. Pick a Confirmation Type, simple or qualified, and set Confirmation Valid For (days).
  5. Set each checkpoint (On Contact Save, On Sales Order Confirmation, On Purchase Order Confirmation, On Delivery Validation, On Invoice Posting) to Ignore, Warn or Block.
  6. Choose the users under Notify On Failure, then open a contact with an EU VAT ID and press Check VAT ID to try it.
  7. Follow the results under Accounting > Customers > VAT ID Confirmations and Failed VAT ID Confirmations.

Germany VAT ID Check: questions

Do I need a BZSt account or API key?

No. The eVatR interface takes your own German VAT ID as the requester and needs no sign-up. The module calls the BZSt directly, with no third-party service in between.

How is this different from the VIES check in Odoo?

Odoo's VIES option tells you whether a number exists. A qualified eVatR request also compares the company name, street, postcode and city with the member state's register and returns a result for each field. A name or city mismatch is never treated as confirmed.

What happens when the BZSt service is down?

An outage is recorded as Service unavailable, not as an invalid VAT ID. By default documents still go through; tick Strict on Outages if you want Block checkpoints to stop documents while the service can't be reached.

Can confirmation records be corrected or removed?

No. Records are kept as the authority answered and can't be changed or deleted, even by an administrator. A request made during an operation that gets blocked is still kept.

Does it re-check customers automatically?

Yes. Scheduled actions confirm queued VAT IDs every 15 minutes and re-check customers invoiced recently once a day, in batches you set. The hourly job refreshes which member states are available.

Does this make my zero-rated supplies compliant?

It gives you a record of the confirmation requests and answers. What evidence your tax office expects for intra-Community supplies is a question for your tax adviser, so please confirm your process with them.
