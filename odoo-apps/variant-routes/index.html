Product Variant Routes

Product variant routes for Odoo 19 inventory: set variant routes (variant route per product variant) such as Replenish on Order (variant MTO, make to order), Buy, Manufacture or Dropship. Routes on variants replace or add to the product template routes, a default route per attribute value, bulk set from the variant list, used by procurement, reordering rules and the replenish wizard.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

In standard Odoo the routes sit on the product, so every variant of it is sourced the same way. Take an office chair in three colours and two sizes. The graphite ones are in stock, the tall sand one is bought in when someone orders it, and the custom colour is always made to order. Odoo makes you split that into separate products. This module puts the routes on the variant instead.

Open a variant, tick its routes, and that is what procurement uses for it: deliveries, MTO, reordering rules, dropship, the replenish wizard and the routes report. Variants you do not touch keep following the product, so nothing changes until you set something.

You can also give an attribute value its routes (every Custom Colour is made to order), and set routes on many variants at once from the variant list.
