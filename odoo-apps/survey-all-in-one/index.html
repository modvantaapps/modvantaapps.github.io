Survey All in One

Eighteen extra question types, skip logic per answer, geofenced surveys and a survey theme with styles, colors and a question grid, added to the standard Odoo Survey app.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Survey All in One for Odoo 19

What it does

New question types appear in the normal question form, next to the Odoo ones.

Participants fill them in the survey page; every answer is checked again on the server.

Answers show up on the review page, in the answers list and on the results page like the standard types.

A Theme tab on the survey sets the look, and a Geofence tab limits where it can be answered.

Features

Survey theme
18 extra question types
Skip logic per answer
Text, date and contact types
Slider, stars, checkbox, address and records
Location, barcode, matrix and signature
