Equipment Allocation

Allocate company equipment to employees and departments. Employees request it, managers approve it, and every issue and return is recorded with the condition, notes and a signature. Due back reminders, replacement requests and a full history per employee and per item.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Equipment Allocation for Odoo 19

What it does

An allocation says who holds which piece of equipment, since when, and until when.

On demand allocations have a return date; permanent ones stay until returned or replaced.

Built on the standard Odoo Maintenance equipment, so your existing equipment list is reused.

Features

All allocations in one list
Issue with condition and signature
Return with condition, signature and overdue days
History per equipment
Replacement requests
History per employee and department
Allocation report
Employee self-service request
Confirm receipt from the portal
Handover slip PDF
