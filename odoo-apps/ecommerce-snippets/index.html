Dynamic Product Snippets

Drag-and-drop product snippets: Showcase grid, Carousel & Tabs with 8 smart product sources

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Product snippetsBest sellers carouselProduct tabsWebsite builder

What it does

Drag-and-drop product blocks for the Odoo website builder - a Products Showcase with tabs, Products Tabs and carousels - that load their products from a product source: Sale Products, New Arrivals, Best Sellers, Featured, Custom, by Category, Recently Viewed, Related or an advanced domain.

Each source is a filter record where you set how many products to show and the sort order. Product cards show image, name, price (with the struck-through list price) and an Add to Cart button.

Features

Product blocks for any pageshowcase, carousels and tabs that fill themselves
