Bus Booking

Bus Booking and Bus Ticket Reservation for Odoo 19

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Bus BookingBus Ticket BookingBus ReservationSeat MapSeater and SleeperTwo DecksLadies SeatsSegment Fares

What it does

Sell bus tickets on your website, at the counter, through agents and on the Point of Sale from one live seat map: seater and sleeper layouts on two decks, routes with boarding points, fares per segment, trips generated by weekday, seat hold with online payment, QR e-tickets, refunds, a passenger manifest and a boarding scanner. One module, built on Odoo's own Website, Sales, Payments, Point of Sale and Invoicing.

Everything a bus operator or travel agency needs, from the seat layout to boarding.

Several operators with their own time zone, logo, helpline and ticket terms. Buses with registration, seat layout, amenities, photos, default driver and conductor, crew seats kept free, and an optional link to the Odoo Fleet vehicle.

Generate a 2+2, 2+1 or 3+2 seater or a 2+1 sleeper deck in one click, add an upper deck, then paint seat types and sleeper berths, erase aisles and gaps and mark ladies seats on a grid. Seats are numbered from the front, with L and U prefixes for berths.

Routes with ordered stops, minutes from the start and km. Every stop has its own boarding and dropping points (bus station, market square, airport junction...) with their own time before or after the stop.
