POS Wallee Terminal

Take card payments in the Odoo Point of Sale on a wallee payment terminal (Switzerland, Germany and Austria). Fetch the terminals of your wallee space, send each sale from the payment screen, refund terminal payments from the POS and keep every result when the terminal takes its time.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
POS Wallee Terminal for Odoo 19

What it does

The module adds "wallee" as a payment terminal of the Odoo Point of Sale. Odoo creates a wallee transaction and asks the terminal you picked to perform it (Cloud Till Interface, long polling). While the terminal works, Odoo reads the transaction state and resumes the terminal call when wallee asks for it (HTTP 543), until the payment is approved or failed. The wallee transaction id and the card brand are stored on the POS payment and the terminal receipt prints on the POS ticket.
