eShipper Shipping Integration

Ship with eShipper from Odoo - live carrier rates, labels, pickups and LTL pallets

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

An eShipper connector for Odoo 19: the cheapest, the fastest or one chosen carrier service on the quotation, the label on the delivery order when you validate it, the pickup booked with the shipment, tracking and pallet shipments with freight class - all through your eShipper account.

The module adds eShipper as a delivery method provider. Each delivery method uses one eShipper account (your eShipper login, sandbox or production) and chooses the service: one carrier service, the cheapest rate or the fastest rate, optionally of one carrier only, and normal, faster or urgent service speed.

Validating the delivery order books the shipment with up to 32 packages, attaches the label PDF and, for cross-border shipments, the customs invoice. A transaction ID is sent with every booking so a repeated request returns the same order instead of booking twice.
