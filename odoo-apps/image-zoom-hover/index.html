Product Image Hover Zoom

Amazon-style magnifying glass zoom on hover for eCommerce product images

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Image zoom on hoverMagnifying glassProduct image zoomHover magnifier

What it does

Move the mouse over a product image and a lens follows the pointer while a large panel next to the image shows that part of the picture magnified, using the full-resolution product image.

Move the mouse away and the page goes back to normal. On phones and small screens the module stays out of the way and Odoo's own tap-to-zoom keeps working.
