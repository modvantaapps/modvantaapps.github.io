SMSA Express Shipping Integration

Ship with SMSA Express from Odoo - AWB labels, cash on delivery, return pickups and tracking for Saudi Arabia and the GCC

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Book SMSA shipments when you validate the delivery order, print the AWB label, collect cash on delivery, hand over the day's parcels with a pickup manifest, book return pickups at the customer and follow every AWB through its tracking history.

The module adds SMSA Express as a provider on the standard Odoo delivery method. It works with the SMSA eCommerce REST API (API key) or with the legacy SECOM web service (passKey). Validating a delivery order creates the AWB, writes the AWB number as tracking reference and attaches the label PDF.

Prices come from a rate table by weight, destination and city, a fixed price, or live prices through a SECOM passKey. Unpaid orders can ship as cash on delivery with the COD amount on the AWB. Contacts get a Saudi National Address tab with building number, short address and district, and the SMSA city and office lists can be fetched from SMSA.
