POS Invoice Journal

Choose the invoice journal on the till, cancel a POS invoice, and book each shop on its own revenue account

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Give a point of sale several invoice journals and let the cashier pick one when the order is invoiced. Cancel a wrong invoice from the order history of the till. Post the sales of each point of sale on its own revenue account. And print the invoices per journal for any period.

A point of sale in Odoo has one invoice journal, so a shop that issues retail and wholesale invoices (or invoices for two activities) has to re-invoice from Accounting. A wrong POS invoice cannot be cancelled from the till, and all POS sales go to the product income account, so two shops cannot be told apart in the books.

This module adds other invoice journals to each point of sale. When the cashier ticks Invoice, the journals appear on the payment screen and one is picked; the server only accepts a journal of that point of sale. The order form has the same Invoice Journal field for invoicing from the back office.

Cancel Invoice in the order history of the till cancels the invoice of an order of the open session and reverses its payment entries; the order stays paid and closes with the session like any other sale. A Sales Revenue Account per point of sale (with a company default) replaces the product income account in the session closing entry, refunds and POS invoices. The Invoice Journal Report prints invoices per journal with totals.
