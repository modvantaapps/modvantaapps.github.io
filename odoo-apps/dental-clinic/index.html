Dental Clinic Management

Run a dental practice in Odoo: patient records with allergies and medical history, an interactive 32 teeth dental chart with per-tooth findings and treatments, dental appointments with email and SMS reminders, treatment plans with progress, prescriptions, invoices with insurance claims, a patient portal and online booking from your website.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Dental Clinic Management for Odoo 19Who it is forDental practicesFront deskDentists

What it does

Three roles out of the box: Receptionist, Dentist and Administrator

Everything below was used on a test database with sample data

Upper and lower arch, FDI (11-48) or Universal (1-32) numbering. Teeth are coloured by finding, planned treatment, done treatment or missing.

Chief complaint, selected teeth, a finding per tooth and surface, diagnosis, a generated case summary, escalation to the administrators.

Treatments per tooth and surface with price and discount, sent to the patient as a PDF, progress in percent, state follows the treatments.

Features

Coverage percent and annual limit per policythe insurer share is invoiced to the insurer with a claim; a rejected claim is billed to the patient.
A public Book a Dental Appointment pagenew patients choose a dentist, a day and a free time.
Under My Account, Dental Careupcoming appointments with a Cancel button, booking of a free time with a chosen dentist, treatment plans and prescriptions as PDF, treatment history.
