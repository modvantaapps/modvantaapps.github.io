Georgia RS.ge Waybill and RS.ge e-Invoice

What it does

Create Revenue Service of Georgia waybills from invoices, credit notes and delivery orders, then save, activate, close or cancel them on RS.ge without leaving Odoo. Send the RS.ge tax invoice with its goods lines, correct it from a credit note, and keep every call in a log.

In Georgia goods move with an electronic waybill registered on RS.ge, and VAT payers issue electronic tax invoices there. Odoo has no Georgian localization for either. This module talks to the two public RS.ge SOAP services: the waybill service and the tax invoice service.

A waybill is made from a customer invoice, a credit note (return of goods), a delivery order or an internal transfer, with the goods, units, prices in GEL, VAT type, bar codes, buyer, addresses, vehicle and driver. You save it, activate it when transport starts (RS.ge returns the waybill number), and close it on delivery.

The tax invoice is sent from the posted invoice: the module finds the buyer on RS.ge, creates the invoice, adds every line with its VAT, links the waybill number and sends it to the buyer for confirmation. A credit note creates the correction invoice. Statuses come back by a scheduled job.
