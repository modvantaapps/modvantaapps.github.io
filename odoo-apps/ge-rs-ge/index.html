Georgia RS.ge Waybill and RS.ge e-Invoice

Georgia RS.ge Waybill and RS.ge e-Invoice for Odoo 19

What it does

Create Revenue Service of Georgia waybills from invoices, credit notes and delivery orders, then save, activate, close or cancel them on RS.ge without leaving Odoo. Send the RS.ge tax invoice with its goods lines, correct it from a credit note, and keep every call in a log.

In Georgia goods move with an electronic waybill registered on RS.ge, and VAT payers issue electronic tax invoices there. Odoo has no Georgian localization for either. This module talks to the two public RS.ge SOAP services: the waybill service and the tax invoice service.

A waybill is made from a customer invoice, a credit note (return of goods), a delivery order or an internal transfer, with the goods, units, prices in GEL, VAT type, bar codes, buyer, addresses, vehicle and driver. You save it, activate it when transport starts (RS.ge returns the waybill number), and close it on delivery.

The tax invoice is sent from the posted invoice: the module finds the buyer on RS.ge, creates the invoice, adds every line with its VAT, links the waybill number and sends it to the buyer for confirmation. A credit note creates the correction invoice. Statuses come back by a scheduled job.

Who it is for

  • Georgian distributors with daily deliveries. Goods moving inside Georgia need an electronic waybill registered on RS.ge. From a delivery order or an invoice you save the waybill, activate it when the truck leaves (RS.ge then returns the waybill number) and close it once the goods arrive.
  • VAT payers issuing tax invoices to business buyers. Send RS.ge e-Invoice on a posted customer invoice creates the tax invoice on RS.ge, adds the lines with their VAT, links the waybill number and sends it to the buyer to confirm. A scheduled job brings the status back into Odoo.
  • Companies handling returns and corrections. A credit note can create an RS.ge correction invoice with the correction type you choose, and a return waybill that refers to the original one with the addresses swapped.
  • Accounting firms running Odoo for Georgian clients. Each company has its own RS.ge service user, and every call is logged with its request, response and duration, the password masked. When RS.ge rejects something, the code is shown with its text from the RS.ge error list.

Setting it up

  1. In the RS.ge e-services portal, create a service user with waybill and tax invoice rights.
  2. In Odoo, open Invoicing > Georgia RS.ge > Configuration > Setup, enter the service user and password, turn Simulation Mode off when you're ready, and press Connect and Sync to load units, transport types, error codes and excise codes.
  3. Go to Invoicing > Configuration > Settings and review the Georgia RS.ge block: RS.ge Connection (URLs, timeout, retries), Waybill Defaults (start address, waybill type, transport type, who pays transport) and Batch Submission.
  4. Give each Odoo unit of measure its RS.ge Unit, and check the RS.ge VAT type on your sales taxes. Products with excise can get an RS.ge Excise Code.
  5. On a posted invoice or an outgoing or internal transfer, press RS.ge Waybill, fill in the vehicle, driver and transport details, then Save on RS.ge and Activate.
  6. On the posted invoice press Send RS.ge e-Invoice, and press Close (Delivered) on the waybill when the goods arrive. All documents are listed under Georgia RS.ge > Waybills, e-Invoices and RS.ge Calls.

Georgia RS.ge Waybill and RS.ge e-Invoice: questions

Does it run on Odoo Community?

Yes. It depends on Invoicing (account), Inventory (stock) and Discuss (mail), all part of Community. Python's lxml and requests libraries must be available on the server, which they normally are.

Can I try it before I have an RS.ge service user?

Yes. Simulation Mode answers every call locally, so you can press each button and see the full flow without sending anything to RS.ge. Turn it off in the setup once your real service user exists.

What if the invoice isn't in GEL?

Waybill and tax invoice amounts are converted to GEL using Odoo's exchange rate on the invoice date.

What happens if RS.ge is down or rejects a document?

Network and server errors are retried with increasing delays, up to the number of retries you set. A rejection shows the RS.ge error code and its text on the document, and the call is kept in the log. Many problems are caught before sending, such as a wrong 9 or 11 digit TIN, a missing vehicle or driver number, or a unit with no RS.ge code.

Can a waybill be changed or cancelled?

While it's saved but not activated you can use Update on RS.ge or Delete on RS.ge. After activation you can close it or cancel it on RS.ge. Refresh Status reads the current state back, and RS.ge PDF attaches the official document.

Does it decide the VAT treatment for me?

No. It sends the VAT type you set on each tax (regular, zero rated or exempt) and the codes you choose. Confirm with your accountant or the Revenue Service which treatment applies to your goods and transactions.
