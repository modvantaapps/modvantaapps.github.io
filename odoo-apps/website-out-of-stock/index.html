Hide Out of Stock Products & Hide Unavailable Variants

Hide out-of-stock products and unavailable variants from your shop

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Out-of-stock products disappear from the shop grid, categories and search - or stay visible after every in-stock product - based on the free stock in each website's warehouse. Keep chosen products visible, and decide what a direct link to a hidden product does. On the product page, colors and sizes that cannot be bought are greyed out or hidden as the shopper picks.

In Odoo eCommerce, products with no stock stay in the shop grid in their normal place. Shoppers click through pages of items they cannot buy, and the products that are actually available get buried.

This module adds one website setting with three choices: show normally, hide out-of-stock products, or show out-of-stock products last. Hiding applies to the shop grid, the search, categories, product counts, filters and product snippets. Showing them last keeps them visible but after every in-stock product, keeping the shopper's own sort order inside each group.

Stock is the free quantity (on hand minus reserved) in the warehouse of the website, or in every warehouse of its company. Products that are not stored, products marked Always Show, and optionally products set to keep selling when out of stock are never hidden. A direct link to a hidden product can stay reachable or redirect to its category or the shop.

Products with variants get the same care on the product page. Odoo lets the shopper pick any color and size and only says afterwards that the combination is sold out. With this module, values whose variant is out of stock, archived or deleted are greyed out and struck through - or hidden - and the selector updates live after every pick. Shop attribute filters and the color swatches on shop cards follow the same rule.

Features

One setting per website
An out-of-stock product
Hide out-of-stock products
Or show them last
Always show selected products
Unavailable variants: greyed out and struck through
Greyed values stay clickable: notify me when available
Or hide unavailable values completely
Shop attribute filters follow availability
Per website, with a per-product override
