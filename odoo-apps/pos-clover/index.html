Clover POS Payment Terminal and Clover Payment Gateway

Take card payments on a Clover from the Odoo Point of Sale, and online with Clover Hosted Checkout

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The cashier presses Clover on the Odoo payment screen, the customer taps or inserts the card on the Clover Flex or Mini, and the receipt prints with the card brand, last four digits and authorization code.

This module turns Clover devices (Clover Flex, Clover Mini and other devices running Cloud Pay Display) into the card terminal of the Odoo 19 Point of Sale. Add a Clover payment method, pick the devices, and the POS sends each card payment to the Clover over Clover's REST Pay Display API.

While the customer pays, the payment line shows Waiting for card with the device name, and the cashier can cancel or force the result. Approved, declined, cancelled and timed-out answers are handled on screen, and approved payments print the card brand, last four digits, entry method and authorization code on the receipt.

Every request is stored as a Clover transaction with Clover's answer, and a reconciliation view groups collected amounts and tips by day. Demo Mode simulates the devices inside Odoo so you can train staff before the hardware arrives.

The same module adds Clover as a payment provider for your Odoo website, invoices and payment links: the customer pays on the Clover Hosted Checkout page, and the payment is marked as paid when Clover's signed webhook arrives.
