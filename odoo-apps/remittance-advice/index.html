Remittance Advice

Remittance Advice - Vendor Payment Advice and Remittance Report for Odoo

What it does

Tell every vendor exactly which bills your payment covers. Print or email a remittance advice from any vendor payment, or a payment advice from a customer payment, listing each bill or invoice with the amount paid now, credit notes, discounts and what was paid on account.

A vendor receives a bank transfer whose reference rarely says what it pays, and their accounts team calls you to ask. This module builds the remittance advice from the payment itself: for every bill the payment settled it prints the bill number, the vendor's own reference, the bill date, due date, total, the part paid now and the balance left. Credit notes applied to those bills appear in brackets, discounts and write-offs on their own line, and any amount not matched to a bill shows as paid on account. Payments that are not reconciled yet spread their amount over their bills by due date, so the advice can go out the same day.
