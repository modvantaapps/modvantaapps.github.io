POS Restaurant Restrictions

Stop silent voids at the till. Removing or lowering a line that was already sent to the kitchen, deleting an order, a discount above your limit, a price change, a table transfer or merge, a refund, cash in / out and closing the session all ask for an approver PIN and a reason, and every request is logged.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
POS Restaurant Restrictions for Odoo 19

What it does

Waiters can still take orders freely; only the sensitive actions stop and ask for approval.

An approver (shift lead, manager) types their PIN on the till; the PIN is checked by the Odoo server, not in the browser.

The cashier picks a reason or types a note, so you know why it happened.

Every approval and every wrong PIN is stored with the order, table, product, quantities, who asked and who approved.

Features

Approval before decreasing a line sent to the kitchen
Wrong PINs are refused and counted
The order continues once approved
Discounts above a limit
Cash in / out
Closing the session
Restricted actions log
Full detail of each action
Your own reasons
