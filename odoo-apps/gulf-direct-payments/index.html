KNET Payment Gateway and BENEFIT Payment Gateway (BenefitPay)

Take KNET debit card payments in Kuwait and BENEFIT payments in Bahrain on your Odoo website, customer portal, invoices and payment links, through your own bank terminal. No aggregator in between: Odoo talks to the KNET payment gateway and the BENEFIT payment gateway directly with your Tranportal ID, password and terminal resource key, and reads the encrypted answer itself. Arabic included.

What it does

The module adds two payment providers. KNET sends the customer to the KNET payment page of your acquiring bank (kpay.com.kw) with the transaction data AES-encrypted with your terminal resource key, exactly like the bank sample kit. BENEFIT registers the payment with the BENEFIT payment gateway and sends the customer to the BENEFIT payment page; the gateway then calls your Odoo back, and Odoo answers with the REDIRECT line the gateway expects.

When the answer comes back, Odoo decrypts it with the resource key, checks the track ID, the Odoo reference and the amount, and only then confirms the order or the invoice. A forged answer cannot be decrypted and is refused. Declined cards show the reason (insufficient funds, expired card, wrong PIN...) in English or Arabic.

Features

This is a direct integrationyou need a KNET or BENEFIT merchant terminal from your bank. It is not a payment aggregator module.
