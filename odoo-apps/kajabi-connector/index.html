Odoo Kajabi integration

Kajabi Connector

Every Kajabi sale, renewal and refund booked in Odoo with its payment, and Odoo sales that give the student access in Kajabi.

What it does

Create a Kajabi API key, paste its client ID and secret into Odoo, press Test Connection and pick your site. Kajabi offers become service products with their price and checkout link. Every purchase becomes a confirmed sale order for the buyer, with the offer, quantity, amount and coupon. Each successful payment, whether a one-time charge, the start of a subscription or a monthly renewal, is booked as a posted invoice with its payment, and a refund as a credit note with the money paid back. Sell a course from Odoo instead and confirming the order grants the Kajabi offer to the customer. Refund it, in Kajabi or with an Odoo credit note, and the purchase is deactivated in Kajabi.

Who it is for

  • Course creators who keep their books in Odoo. Every succeeded Kajabi payment becomes a posted invoice with its payment, including one-time charges, subscription starts and renewals. Sales tax collected by Kajabi goes on its own invoice line.
  • Coaches and memberships with monthly renewals. Each renewal is a separate paid invoice linked to the sale order of the purchase. A refund becomes a credit note that is paid back, and the purchase can be deactivated in Kajabi so access ends.
  • Teams that sell courses from Odoo quotes. Put a product that came from a Kajabi offer on a sale order and confirm it. Odoo grants that offer in Kajabi, creating the Kajabi contact first if needed, and can let Kajabi send its welcome e-mail.
  • Marketers collecting leads with Kajabi forms. Kajabi form submissions become CRM leads for the sales team you choose. Kajabi contact tags map to Odoo contact tags, and Odoo contacts with the tags you pick can be sent to Kajabi.

Setting it up

  1. In Kajabi, go to Settings, Public API and create a user API key with the permissions you need.
  2. In Odoo, open Kajabi > Configuration > Connections, create a connection and paste the API Client ID and API Client Secret on the Connection tab.
  3. Press Test Connection. Odoo reads your Kajabi user and sites, and you pick the Site to sync.
  4. On the Sync tab, tick what to bring in: contacts, offers as products, purchases as sale orders, invoicing of each payment, form leads and payouts. Choose the Payment Journal.
  5. Press Sync Now, then check Kajabi > Purchases and Kajabi > Invoices and Refunds.
  6. On the Webhook tab, press Register Webhooks so purchases, payments, orders, form submissions and tag changes arrive straight away. The hourly schedule picks up anything a webhook missed.

Kajabi Connector: questions

Does it work on Odoo Community?

Yes, on Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise. It needs Contacts, CRM, Sales and Invoicing.

What do I need from Kajabi?

A Kajabi account with access to the Public API and a user API key. Odoo signs in with the client ID and secret, keeps the access and refresh tokens, and renews them itself.

Can I try it without a Kajabi key?

Yes. Offline Sample Mode answers every call from a built-in sample Kajabi site with no network access. Only system administrators can switch it on.

Will running the sync twice book a payment twice?

No. Purchases and transactions keep their Kajabi IDs, and a transaction already booked for the connection is skipped.

Kajabi webhooks are not signed. How is that handled?

The webhook address carries a long random token. Odoo takes only the record ID from the call and reads the record again from Kajabi with your key, so a forged call can't change amounts.

What happens to disputed charges and payouts?

Disputed charges are not booked. They are written to the Sync Log so you can check them in Kajabi. Kajabi Payments payouts are listed on the Payouts tab with amount and status to help with bank reconciliation, but they are not reconciled automatically.
