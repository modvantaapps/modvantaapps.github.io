Odoo YouCan integration

YouCan Connector

Your YouCan orders in Odoo, your Odoo stock on YouCan.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
YouCan and Odoo 19

What it does

Connect a YouCan store with your own YouCan app. Orders come in as sale orders the moment YouCan calls the webhook, with shipping, discounts and the payment method. Stock goes back per variant, and validating the delivery fulfils the order on YouCan with its tracking number.

Every YouCan order becomes an Odoo sale order with its lines, the shipping line, and a discount line so the Odoo total matches YouCan to the dirham. You see the payment method (cash on delivery or card), the payment status and the shipping status on the order.

Customers are found by their YouCan ID, e-mail or phone, but only among the contacts of the company the store belongs to; otherwise they are created with the shipping address. Guest orders work too.

Products are linked by SKU or barcode when you import them, or created in Odoo with their size and colour options as attributes. Odoo products can be sent to YouCan with their variants, and later price and name changes follow.

Stock is the free quantity of the warehouse you choose, minus a safety buffer. Only the difference is sent, so a sale on YouCan in the meantime is not overwritten.
