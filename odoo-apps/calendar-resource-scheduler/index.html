Resource Scheduler

See every person, room and machine side by side

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A resource scheduler for Odoo 19: people, meeting rooms, equipment, vehicles or any record as columns in the day and week views, or as timeline rows in the day, week and month views. Drag items between resources, see working hours and time off, catch double bookings before they happen. No extra calendar licence or licence key is needed.

The standard Odoo calendar puts every meeting of a day in one column. It answers what happens on Thursday, but not who is free on Thursday, and it has no view of rooms, equipment or vehicles at all. Front desks, dispatchers and planners end up ticking attendees one by one to compare calendars.

This module adds scheduler boards. A board has resources (users, employees, rooms and equipment, contacts, work centers or any other model) and sources (meetings, tasks, bookings, field service jobs or any dated record). Each resource gets its own column, or its own timeline row, and each item sits on the resource it belongs to.

Drag an item along its row or column to change the time, drop it on another resource to reassign it, drag its edge to change the length, or drag across free time to create a new item with the start, length and resource already filled in. Working hours, lunch breaks, public holidays and time off are shaded per resource, overlaps are outlined and counted, and each board can ask before, or refuse, a double booking or a move outside working hours.

The board is drawn by the module itself inside the normal Odoo web client. It does not use any paid calendar scheduler plugin, so there is no extra calendar licence to buy and no licence key to enter. Your normal calendar views stay exactly as they are.
