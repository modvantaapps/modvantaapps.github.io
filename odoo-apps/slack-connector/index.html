Slack Connector

Slack in Odoo - notifications, approvals, /odoo lookups and a two-way Discuss bridge

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Post Odoo events to Slack channels as Block Kit messages, let your team confirm an order or approve a request from a button in Slack, look up orders and customers with a slash command, and chat between Slack and Odoo Discuss.

The /odoo slash command answers lookups for sale orders, customers, invoices, products, leads, purchase orders and tasks, always with the Odoo access rights of the Slack member who asks. A Slack channel can also be bridged with an Odoo Discuss channel so messages, replies, edits, deletions and files go both ways.

Everything Slack sends is checked with your app's signing secret, everything Odoo sends goes through a queue with retries and a log, and each company can have its own Slack workspace.
