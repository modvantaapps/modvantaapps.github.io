Thailand VAT PP30

Thailand VAT PP30 for Odoo 19 - PP.30 Return, Sales and Purchase Tax Reports, PP36

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Boxes 1-16of the PP30 form, computed2 reports2 languagesEnglish and ThaiWho it is for

What it does

Prepare the monthly Thai VAT return in Odoo Community: boxes 1 to 16 of the PP30 from your posted invoices, bills and POS entries, the sales tax report and purchase tax report with the buyer's and seller's tax ID and branch, excess VAT carried to the next month, the late-filing surcharge, the branch attachment for combined filing, PP36 for VAT on payments to foreign suppliers and a VAT registration turnover check. You file and pay on the Revenue Department system yourself.

Choose the month and compute. Sales at 7%, at 0% and exempt sales, output tax, purchases with creditable input tax and input tax come from the posted documents of the month; boxes 8 to 16 follow the PP30 instructions: tax payable or excess, excess brought forward from last month, net tax, surcharge, penalty and the totals. The Buddhist Era year and the deadline are shown.

One line per tax invoice and rate, with date, number, buyer, the buyer's 13-digit tax ID and head office or branch, value and VAT; credit notes come in negative. Entries without a customer (such as a POS session) are listed as a daily sales summary. Tax IDs that fail the check digit are flagged.

Supplier bills with the supplier's tax invoice number, tax ID and branch. Withholding taxes, input tax you mark as not creditable and PP36 taxes stay out of boxes 6 and 7.

A PP30 worksheet with every box in Thai and English, the tax ID, head office or branch and the tax month, ready to copy into the filing. It is a worksheet, not the official form.
