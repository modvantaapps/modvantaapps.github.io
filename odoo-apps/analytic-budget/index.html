Budget Management for Odoo Community

Budgets per analytic account, with purchase orders and vendor bills checked against them

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Plan a budget per analytic account, see planned, committed, actual and remaining amounts at any time, and stop or send for approval any purchase order or vendor bill that would take an account over its budget.

Odoo Community has analytic accounts but no budgets. A department can spend past its budget without anyone noticing until the month is closed, and purchase orders that are confirmed but not billed yet do not show up anywhere as money already promised.

This module adds Budgets. A budget has a period and one line per analytic account, optionally limited to a budgetary position (a set of general accounts) and split by month or quarter. For every line it shows the planned amount, the committed amount (confirmed purchase orders not billed yet), the actual amount (posted analytic items), what remains and the percentage used.

Purchase orders and vendor bills are checked when they are confirmed or posted. Depending on the control of the budget, an excess is ignored, logged as a warning on the document, blocked, or sent to a Budget Approver who approves or refuses it.

Features

All budgets with their figures
Budget form with one line per analytic account
Monthly or quarterly split
Over budget: the purchase order is blocked
Budget Check and approval request
Approval queue
Approve or refuse the excess
Budget analysis
Drill down to the analytic items
Company budget control
