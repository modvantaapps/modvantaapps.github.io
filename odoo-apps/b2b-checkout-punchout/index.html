PunchOut Catalog

Let business buyers shop your Odoo website from inside their procurement system

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Your Odoo shop becomes a PunchOut catalog. The buyer's procurement system opens a signed-in shopping session, the cart goes back to it instead of being checked out, and the approved purchase order (cXML OrderRequest) confirms the matching quotation in Odoo.

Large companies buy through procurement systems. They want suppliers to offer a PunchOut catalog: their employee clicks your shop inside the procurement system, fills a cart, and sends it back for approval. Without PunchOut, a supplier on Odoo is left out of those purchases.

This module adds PunchOut buyers to Odoo Sales. For cXML buyers it answers the PunchOutSetupRequest with a one-time start page, signs the shopper in as the buyer's shop user, replaces checkout with a Return cart button and posts the cart back as a PunchOutOrderMessage. For OCI buyers it accepts the OCI login with HOOK_URL and returns the cart as OCI NEW_ITEM fields.

When the approved purchase order arrives as a cXML OrderRequest, the quotation built from the cart is updated with the approved quantities, given the buyer's PO number and delivery address, and confirmed. Every message in and out is logged.
