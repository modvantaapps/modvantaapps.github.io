Tally Connector

Customers, suppliers, the chart of accounts, products and your sales, purchase, credit note and debit note vouchers go from Odoo to a TallyPrime company over Tally's own XML gateway - with a dry run, a mapping table and a log of every voucher sent.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Send your Odoo books to TallyPrime

What it does

Many Indian businesses run Odoo for sales and operations while their accountant works in Tally. This connector posts Odoo's records to the Tally company over Tally's XML gateway on your network: no middleware and no cloud service in between.

Customers and suppliers become ledgers, accounts become ledgers, products become stock items, and invoices, bills, credit notes and debit notes become vouchers with the right debit and credit signs. Each type of record has its own schedule and can be switched off.

Every record sent is linked to its Tally counterpart and logged with exactly what was sent and what Tally answered. Problems are stopped in Odoo with a plain explanation - for example a contact renamed in Odoo, which would otherwise create a second ledger in Tally.
