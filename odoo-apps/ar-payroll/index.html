Argentina Payroll

Argentina Payroll for Odoo 19: Aportes, Contribuciones, Ganancias 4ta Categoria and SAC

What it does

Liquidacion de sueldos for Argentina on Odoo Community. Each payslip works out the employee aportes, the employer contribuciones, ART and life insurance, the SAC and the Impuesto a las Ganancias withholding on the cumulative ARCA tables. A monthly F.931 summary gives you the totals to check before you file.

Each month the payslip takes the remunerative pay, caps the employee aportes base at the ANSES maximum and works out jubilacion 11%, INSSJP 3% and obra social 3%. Contributions go on the full pay: 20,40% or 18% under Ley 27.541 (with the $ 7.003,68 detraccion per worker), obra social 6%, your ART rate plus the fixed amounts, and the compulsory life insurance premium.

From the December 2026 period the payslip also works out the Fondo de Asistencia Laboral: 1%, or 2,5% for MiPyMEs and non-profits, taken out of the employer contributions as RG ARCA 5907/2026 sets it, and split between SIPA, PAMI, Fondo Nacional de Empleo and Asignaciones Familiares with the rates of its Anexo. The printed recibo follows the model of Decreto 407/2026: employer contributions first, then gross pay and deductions, net pay, and the labour cost summary with its pie chart.

Ganancias de 4ta categoria is cumulative from January: gross pay of the year, less aportes, union dues and the F.572 deductions, less the accumulated personal deductions of that month (ganancia no imponible, deduccion especial, spouse and children). The month's scale gives the tax, and the payslip withholds the difference with what was already withheld.

Features

En espanolaportes y contribuciones con topes ANSES, Ganancias 4ta categoria con las tablas acumuladas de ARCA, SAC en junio y diciembre proporcional a los dias trabajados, cuota sindical, neto a bruto, resumen para el F.931, Fondo de Asistencia Laboral (FAL) desde el devengado diciembre 2026 y recibo de sueldo segun el modelo del Decreto 407/2026.
