Warehouse Restriction

Warehouse Restriction - User Warehouse, Stock Restriction, Location Restriction, Picking Type Restriction

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
stock models filtered by record ruleslevels: warehouse, location, operationone-time price

What it does

Give every user only the warehouses, locations and operation types they work in. Everything else is hidden from their lists, dropdowns and stock figures, and a transfer into a forbidden location is refused when they validate it.

Tick Restrict Warehouses on a user and pick the warehouses they may use. All locations and operation types of those warehouses are allowed, unless you narrow them to some locations (child locations are included) or some operation types. The user then sees only those warehouses, locations, operation types, transfers, stock moves, move lines, scrap orders and on hand quantities. Receipts from vendors and deliveries to customers keep working because partner, virtual and transit locations stay open. If a transfer, scrap or inventory adjustment would still touch a forbidden location, validation is refused with a clear message.
