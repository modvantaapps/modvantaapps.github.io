Failed Payment Recovery (Dunning)

Recover declined card payments instead of losing the customer

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

When an off-session card charge fails, a recovery case starts: retries on a schedule you set, an email asking for a new card when the card is dead, and a stop with a task for your team when retrying is pointless.

This module opens a recovery case for every failed off-session payment made with a saved card. It reads Stripe's decline code and sorts the failure: a soft decline (for example insufficient funds) is retried on your ladder of days, a card problem (for example an expired card) pauses retries and emails the customer a link to add a new card, and a hard decline (for example a stolen card) stops at once and gives the case to a person.

Retries go through Odoo's normal payment flow against the saved card. When a retry succeeds the case is marked recovered and the customer is told; when the ladder runs out, the final action you chose runs.

Features

Saved-card charges fail all the timeinsufficient funds, an expired or replaced card, a temporary hold. Odoo records the failed transaction and stops - no retry, no message to the customer, no way to collect a new card.
