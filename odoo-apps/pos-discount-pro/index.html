POS Discount Pro

POS Discount Pro for Odoo 19: order and line discounts in percent or in a fixed amount, quick buttons, a reason for every discount, complimentary items and automatic free items - on the receipt and in a report.

What it does

POS discount in amount or percentage: POS global discount and POS order discount (fixed amount discount or percent), POS line discount and POS fixed discount per order line, quick discount buttons, POS discount reason (discount reason required before payment, checked on the server), POS complimentary item, POS free product (automatic free item), POS discount amount on the receipt and a discount report.

Features

One Discount popupthe selected line or the whole order, in percent or in an amount, with a live preview of the money taken off.
Fixed amount discounts2.00 off a line stays 2.00 off when the quantity changes; 5.00 off the order stays 5.00 off when lines are added or removed (it is spread over the tax rates the same way Odoo does for a percentage).
Quick discount buttons5, 10, 15, 20% - or your own values per point of sale.
Discount reasonsManager approval, Damaged item, Staff discount, Price match... your own list, per company and per point of sale.
Reason required (optional)the Apply button stays disabled until a reason is picked; a discount typed on the numpad is caught when the cashier presses Payment, and the reason is asked there.
Checked on the servera paid order with a discount and no reason, a complimentary line that is not free, or more free items than the products allow is refused when the order is saved.
Complimentary itemone button gives the selected line for free (100%) with a complimentary reason; press it again to undo.
Automatic free itemon a product, set a free item and a rule (1 free gift bag for every 2 shirts); the free line is added, updated and removed by itself.
On the receipt and the invoicethe amount, the reason and "Complimentary" or "Free item" are printed under each line; the reason is copied to the invoice line.
Discount Analysisevery discount, order discount and free item, with its amount, by reason, product, cashier and day (list, pivot, graph).
