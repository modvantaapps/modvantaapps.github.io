Odoo Yousign integration

Yousign Connector

Send quotations, invoices and contracts for Yousign e-signature from Odoo, and get the signed PDF back on the record

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
YousignYoutruste-signatureSES, AES, QESTemplatesSigned webhook

What it does

Paste your Yousign API key, choose which documents can be signed, and "Send with Yousign" appears in the Action menu of quotations, invoices, purchase orders, contacts or any other model. Your customer signs in Yousign. Odoo follows the request, and when everybody has signed it attaches the signed PDF and the audit trail to the original record. Yousign now trades as Youtrust; the API and its addresses are unchanged, and this module talks to it directly.

Features

Any documentdocument types link a model to its PDF report, the contact who signs, an approver and a counter-signer
Three sourcesthe PDF of the record, a PDF already attached to it, or one of your Yousign templates with its placeholders
Signers, approvers, followersin order or all at once; followers get a copy without signing
Signature levelsimple, advanced or qualified signature, with a code by email or SMS, per account, per document type or per request
Where people signa signature box on the first or last page at the position you set, or smart anchors printed in your own report
Reminders and expiryautomatic reminders every 1, 2, 7 or 14 days, an expiry date and a Send Reminder button
Status in Odooout for signature, signed, declined (with the reason), canceled or expired, and the status of each signer
Signed PDF on the recordthe signed document and the audit trail are attached to the quotation, invoice or contact, with a to-do for the sender
Cancel with a reasondeal called off, errors in the document or other, plus a note to the signers
Signed webhookregister it from Odoo; each call must carry a valid X-Yousign-Signature-256, then Odoo reads the status from Yousign itself
