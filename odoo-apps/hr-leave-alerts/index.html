Leave Notification and Leave Alert

Everyone who needs to know about time off, knows

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Emails to the manager, HR and the team when time off is asked for, approved, refused or cancelled. Limits per leave type and per department. A morning list of who is off today.

Odoo Time Off already tells the employee when a request is approved or refused, and gives the approver a to-do. Payroll, the line manager when they are not the approver, and the rest of the team hear nothing.

Features

Each time off type can carry limitsdays per request, days per calendar month and the minimum notice in days. Each department can say how many people may be off at once. Every limit either warns (the request goes through, with a yellow note and an email to the manager and HR) or blocks it. HR entering time off for someone else is warned, never blocked.
Three daily jobsa reminder a few days before approved time off starts, a who is off today email to HR and to each department manager, and an optional weekly or monthly summary of the time off ahead.
