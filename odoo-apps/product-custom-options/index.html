Product Custom Options

Sell customizable and personalized products on your Odoo website

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Add custom options to a product page - engraving text, dropdowns, radio buttons, checkboxes, multiple choice, number, date, color, file and image upload - each with its own extra price, carried into the cart, the sales order and the customer portal.

Odoo variants work for a fixed list of sizes and colours, but not for a name to engrave, a logo to print, a delivery date or a set of extras that each cost something. Creating a variant for every combination is not possible.

This module adds custom options to products. An option can be a text field, a text area, a dropdown, radio buttons, a checkbox, multiple choice, a number, a date, a colour picker, a file upload or an image upload. Options and each of their values can carry an extra price, and an option can be required.

The shopper fills the options on the product page and sees the total of the add-ons. The choices and the extra price are added to the cart line, and they stay on the sales order line, the quotation PDF and the customer portal, with uploaded files kept as attachments. Options can also be defined once on an eCommerce category and reused by every product of that category.
