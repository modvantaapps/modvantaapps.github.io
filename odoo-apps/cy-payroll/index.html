Cyprus Payroll

Cyprus Payroll for Odoo 19: PAYE with the 2026 Tax Scale, Social Insurance 8.8%, GESY / GHS and T.D.63

What it does

Cyprus statutory payroll on Odoo Community: income tax withholding by the T.D.59A method with the 2026 scale, Social Insurance and the employer funds up to the maximum insurable earnings, the GESY / GHS contribution, the monthly contributions and PAYE worksheet with the late-payment charge, the yearly T.D.7 worksheet and the T.D.63 emoluments certificates.
