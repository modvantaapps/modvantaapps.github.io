Rwanda Payroll

Rwanda Payroll for Odoo 19: PAYE, RSSB Pension, Maternity, RAMA, CBHI and the PAYE / RSSB Worksheet

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Rwanda PayrollRwanda HRRRA PAYERSSBRSSB Pension 6% + 6%Maternity Leave 0.3%RAMA 7.5%Occupational Hazards 2%

What it does

Rwandan statutory payroll on Odoo Community: PAYE on the RRA bands with the casual labourer and second employer rates, vehicle and housing benefits in kind, the RSSB pension, maternity leave, medical scheme (RAMA) and occupational hazards contributions, CBHI on the net salary, and the monthly PAYE and RSSB worksheet.

0% up to 60,000 RWF a month, 10% to 100,000, 20% to 200,000 and 30% above, as editable dated bands.

Casual labourers pay 15% above 60,000; an employer that is not the first employer withholds 30%.

Company vehicle 10% and housing 20% of the employment income; other benefits in kind at market value, taxed but not paid in cash.

Maternity leave 0.3% + 0.3% and occupational hazards 2% paid by the employer.

Features

Per employee and in totalPAYE for the RRA and each RSSB scheme, due date, CSV, Excel and a printed register, after TIN, NID and RSSB number checks.
