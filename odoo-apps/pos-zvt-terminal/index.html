POS ZVT Terminal

Take card payments in the Odoo Point of Sale on a German or Austrian card terminal (Kartenterminal) over the ZVT ECR interface (Kassenschnittstelle): payment, refund, reversal, end-of-day and cancel from the POS, the terminal messages on the payment screen and the terminal receipt on the POS receipt.

POS ZVT Terminal for Odoo 19

What it does

ZVT is the cash register interface of most card terminals in Germany and Austria, the EFT/POS terminals that take EC-Karte (girocard) and credit cards. Terminal makers such as Worldline, CCV, Ingenico and Verifone ship terminals that speak ZVT; set yours to ZVT over TCP/IP and Odoo talks to it. The module adds "ZVT Terminal" as a payment terminal of the Odoo Point of Sale. When the cashier picks the card payment method, Odoo sends the amount to the terminal with the ZVT Authorization command, shows the terminal messages ("Insert card", "Please wait...") under the payment line, stores the result with the card name, the last four card digits, the trace and receipt numbers and the authorisation, and prints the customer copy of the terminal receipt on the POS receipt.

Who it is for

  • Bakeries, kiosks and shops in Germany and Austria. Most card terminals sold there speak ZVT, the standard cash register interface for girocard and credit cards. This lets the Odoo POS send the amount to that terminal, so the cashier no longer types it in twice.
  • Restaurants and stores on hosted Odoo. A browser can't talk to a terminal on the shop network, and a hosted Odoo server can't reach it either. The shop relay is a small Python program on a computer in the shop that polls Odoo over HTTPS and talks to the terminal locally, with no firewall port to open.
  • Shops that close the till with a Kassenschnitt. End-of-Day can be run from the terminal form, or automatically when the POS session closes. The totals per card brand and the end-of-day receipt are kept in Odoo.
  • Odoo partners setting up German retail clients. Terminal address, port, password, receipt handling and time-outs are all on one terminal form, with buttons to test, register and check status. A bundled ZVT simulator lets you try the flow before the real terminal is on site.

Setting it up

  1. Ask your terminal supplier to enable the ECR (Kassen) interface over TCP/IP and give you the terminal's IP address, port (usually 20007) and 6 digit password.
  2. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > ZVT Terminals and create a terminal.
  3. Choose the Connection: Odoo server connects to the terminal (TCP/IP), or Shop relay connects to the terminal. For the relay, press New Relay Token and Download Relay, then run it on a shop computer with Python 3.
  4. Fill in Terminal Address, Port and Terminal Password, and pick how receipts are handled (Odoo receives the receipt lines, or the terminal prints its own receipts).
  5. Press Test Connection, then Register. The Terminal ID (TID) is filled in from the registration.
  6. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Payment Methods, set Use a Payment Terminal to ZVT Terminal, choose the terminal and add the method to your Point of Sale.
  7. Tick End-of-Day on Session Close on the terminal if you want the Kassenschnitt to run when the session closes.

POS ZVT Terminal: questions

Has it been tested on a real terminal?

No. It was written from the public ZVT specification and tested against a ZVT terminal simulator that ships with the module. Check with your supplier that your model offers ZVT over TCP/IP before you buy, and plan a first test payment with them.

What happens if the network drops in the middle of a payment?

The transaction is set to result unknown, and Odoo asks the terminal for its last stored transaction. It records the payment as approved only if the trace number and amount match, otherwise as not charged. Until that's settled, no new card payment starts on that terminal. A cron also checks unfinished transactions every minute.

Does it need the Odoo IoT Box?

No. The module doesn't use the IoT Box. The Odoo server opens the TCP connection itself in server mode, or the shop relay does it in relay mode.

Can the cashier refund or cancel a card payment?

A refund order sends a ZVT refund. The Reverse button on a paid line, or Reverse on Terminal in the back office, sends a reversal (Storno) of a payment in the open batch. Cancel on the payment screen sends an abort, which the terminal honours only if the card hasn't been used yet.

What's not supported?

OPI, dynamic currency conversion, tips, pre-authorisation and fuel or vending commands aren't included. RS232 terminals only work through the relay with pyserial, and the serial transport was tested at the framing level only.

Is card data stored in Odoo?

Only the last four digits of the card, along with the card name, trace and receipt numbers and the authorisation. Point of Sale managers see every attempt, cashiers only their own, and nobody can edit the records. The screens and terminal messages are available in English and German.
