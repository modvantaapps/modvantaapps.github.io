Agile CRM Connector

Agile CRM integration - Agile CRM connector for Odoo 19

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Your Agile CRM pipeline, in Odoo CRM.

What it does

Companies, contacts, deals, notes and open tasks come over from Agile CRM; each milestone of each track lands on the Odoo stage you pick, and Won or Lost close the opportunity. Opportunities your team works in Odoo can go back to Agile CRM, with their notes and planned activities.

You enter your Agile CRM domain (the part before .agilecrm.com), your user email and the REST API key from Admin Settings, API & Analytics. "Test connection" reads your tracks and lists every milestone; ones whose name matches an Odoo stage are filled in, Won and Lost are marked as outcomes.

"Sync now" (and the hourly job) reads companies, then people, then deals. Sam Rivera arrives under Bluebird Logistics with his title, phone and tags; the "Fleet tracking rollout" deal becomes a 12,500 opportunity at 60% in the Proposal stage, with Sam as customer and the owner matched to an Odoo user by email. A deal in the Won milestone is marked won. Records whose Agile CRM update time did not change are left alone.

Notes on deals and contacts are posted as internal notes, once each. Open tasks become activities on the opportunity or the contact: a CALL task is a Call activity with the same due date.

Switch on "Send Opportunities to Agile CRM" and opportunities changed in Odoo (all of them, or only some sales teams) are sent as deals, their customer as a contact first, plus internal notes and planned activities posted since the last send.
