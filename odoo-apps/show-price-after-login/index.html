Hide Price

Hide price on the website until customers log in: show price after login (login to see price), hide prices and hide add to cart for guests, catalog mode, call for price / price on request / contact for price button with a request form and email. B2B price visibility per website or per product.

What it does

Show prices only to customers who log in, or ask them to request a price

Turn your eCommerce shop into a catalogue for visitors who are not logged in. With Show Price After Login, guests see Login to view price on the shop and a login button on the product page instead of the price and the Add to cart button. Once a customer signs in, prices and the cart work as usual.

For products you price case by case, choose Contact For Price: the product shows your own message and a Request Price button that opens a short form (name, email, phone, quantity, requirement). Each request is saved in Odoo with a chatter, and an email is queued for your company and for the customer. The setting is per website, and each product can follow the website or override it.
