Mastercard Payment Gateway (MPGS / MIGS)

Accept cards through Mastercard Payment Gateway Services, the gateway many banks give their merchants (formerly MIGS), in Odoo eCommerce, the customer portal, invoices and payment links. Cards are entered on the gateway Hosted Checkout with 3-D Secure; capture, void, refunds and saved cards work from standard Odoo screens.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The module adds "Mastercard Gateway" as an Odoo payment provider. When a customer pays, Odoo opens a Hosted Checkout session on the gateway and sends the customer to the gateway payment page (or shows the gateway card form embedded in a page of your website). After the payment, Odoo reads the order from the gateway and confirms, authorizes or declines the transaction. Webhook notifications signed with your notification secret keep Odoo up to date even when the customer closes the browser.
