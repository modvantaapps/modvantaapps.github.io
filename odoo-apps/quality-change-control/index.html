Quality Change Control

The audit question: what was this part inspected against?

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Quality Change Control for Odoo 19Quality Change ControlInspection Plan RevisionInspection Plan ApprovalRevision ControlQuality Document ControlEngineering Change RequestECO Approval

What it does

When a tolerance changes, last month's inspection records must still show last month's limits, the instructions the inspector followed and the work instruction in force that day. With this add-on an inspection plan is changed through a new revision that is reviewed, approved and signed before operators get it, and every completed check keeps the revision it was made with. Auditors see who changed what, why, and who approved it.

Change control for inspection plans and controlled documents.

New Revision on an approved plan opens Rev B as a draft with the same reference. Draft, In Review, Pending Approval, Approved - In Use and Superseded. All revisions of a plan are one click away.

Only the approved revision creates quality checks on receipts, manufacturing orders and deliveries. A draft Rev B never reaches the shop floor. Approving Rev B supersedes Rev A at once.

Optional reviewer, then approver, each with a to-do. The author cannot approve their own plan. Rejection sends it back to the author with the reason.
