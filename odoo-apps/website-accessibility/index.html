Website Accessibility Toolbar and WCAG Audit

An accessibility toolbar for visitors - and an audit that finds what to fix

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A keyboard-friendly toolbar lets visitors enlarge text, raise contrast, highlight links and more. An automated WCAG 2.1 scan of your own pages and products lists the concrete defects - missing alt text, unlabelled fields, empty links, skipped headings - with the criterion, severity and how to fix each one.

The European Accessibility Act and the ADA put pressure on online shops to be usable with screen readers, keyboards and magnification. A toolbar alone does not make a site conformant; the defects in the pages themselves have to be found and fixed.

This module adds both. Visitors get a toolbar with text size, high contrast, inverted colours, highlighted links, stop animations, a readable font, a bigger cursor, a reading guide and keyboard navigation, with a reset and a link to your accessibility statement.

Features

You get an audita scan that fetches your published pages and product pages and records each defect with its WCAG criterion and level, severity, the page, the offending markup and a fix hint. Optional server-side fixes can add derived image alt text, the document language, skip links and visible focus outlines while pages are rendered.
