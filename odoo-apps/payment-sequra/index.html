seQura Payment Provider

Offer seQura in Odoo 19: pay later, pay in 3 and instalments, with your own seQura account

What it does

Adds seQura as a payment provider in Odoo. The module talks to seQura directly from your Odoo, with your own seQura keys. The customer chooses how to pay and fills in the seQura form; seQura approves the order, Odoo confirms it, then reports the deliveries so that the shipped carts stay right.

Features

The products of your contractOdoo asks seQura which products suit this order and lists them; with one product the form opens straight away
Identification formthe dynamic seQura form inside your checkout
IPNapproved orders are confirmed and orders under review go on hold, with the documented answers: gone when the cart changed, conflict, retry when seQura cannot be reached
SignatureX-SeQura-Signature on the raw body, or a token when no secret is configured. A wrong one is refused
Shipped cartsgoods wait in the unshipped cart and move when the delivered quantity of the sale order is complete; services and payment links count as shipped
Refundsa partial refund adds a discount to the cart, a full one empties it, through the Order Update API
Authorize and Captureif your contract has it: authorize, then capture or void from Odoo
Webhookscancelled and denied orders, cancellation requests shown on the payment, risk level kept
Instalment widgetthe seQura promotion on the product and cart pages of the website
Point of Salewith Odoo online payment, for an order with a customer. seQura checks who buys, so a walk-in sale gets a clear message asking to add the customer
API logeach call and notification, with customer details masked
Spanish and Catalanthe module is translated
