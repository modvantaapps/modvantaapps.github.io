ClickSend SMS Gateway

Send every Odoo SMS through your ClickSend account: chatter SMS, SMS templates, mass SMS to contacts and automation rules. Delivery receipts update the SMS status, customer replies land in the chatter of the record you texted from, and the account balance is checked for you.

What it does

ClickSend as SMS providerDelivery receiptsInbound SMS to chatterSTOP opt-outBalance checkLow balance emailSMS log with priceWebhook or pollingMulti-company

The module adds "Send via ClickSend" next to "Send via Odoo" and "Send via Twilio" in Settings -> Send SMS. From then on every SMS that Odoo sends for that company goes out through the ClickSend REST API, so all the standard SMS features of Odoo keep working: Send SMS from the chatter, SMS templates, mass SMS from a list of contacts, automation rules with a Send SMS action and SMS marketing when you have it installed.

ClickSend answers with a message id, a price and the carrier. Delivery receipts then move the SMS from Sent to Delivered (or Failed with the gateway reason) on the chatter notification.

When a customer replies, the text is posted in the chatter of the record the SMS came from (a sales order, an invoice, a contact...), as a message from that contact, so the salesperson sees it straight away. A reply of STOP puts the number on the Odoo phone blacklist and START takes it off again.
