IndiaMART Integration

Every IndiaMART enquiry, buy-lead, PNS call and WhatsApp enquiry becomes an Odoo CRM lead: in real time with the Push API, and on a schedule with the Pull API so nothing is missed.

What it does

IndiaMART sends your seller account enquiries, buy-leads, PNS calls, catalog views and WhatsApp enquiries. This module reads them with the IndiaMART Lead Manager APIs and creates CRM leads or opportunities with the buyer, company, phone, email, address, product and message, assigned to your sales team, without copying anything by hand.

Features

Pull APIA scheduled job calls the IndiaMART Pull API (crmListing v2) with a start time 5 minutes before the previous end time, the strategy IndiaMART recommends, so a lead saved at the boundary is never missed. The 5-minute rule is respected locally.
Push API (real time)Each account has its own webhook listener URL. Paste it in IndiaMART (Push API, Other) and leads arrive the moment the buyer sends them. The listener answers HTTP 200 so IndiaMART does not retry.
No duplicatesEvery record is stored once by its UNIQUE_QUERY_ID, whether it came from the push, the pull or the history import. Overlapping windows only increase the "times received" counter.
Repeat buyersA buyer who sends a second enquiry or calls again is added to the open lead of the same buyer (same email or mobile) as a chatter note, or gets a new lead: your choice.
Lead types as tagsDirect Enquiry, Buy-Lead, PNS Call, Catalog View and WhatsApp Enquiry become tags, plus your own tags, source, medium and campaign.
AssignmentSales team, one salesperson or round robin between several, and a follow-up activity (call, email...) due in N days for the salesperson.
