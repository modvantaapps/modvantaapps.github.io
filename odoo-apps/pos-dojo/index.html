POS Dojo Terminal

Take card payments in the Odoo Point of Sale on a Dojo card machine (Dojo Pay at Counter, UK and Ireland). Fetch your Dojo terminals, send each sale or refund from the payment screen, answer signature checks and keep every result, even when a connection drops.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
POS Dojo Terminal for Odoo 19

What it does

The module adds "Dojo" as a payment terminal of the Odoo Point of Sale. Odoo talks to the Dojo API: it creates the payment intent, opens a terminal session on the card machine you picked and follows it until the result is known. The payment line shows the card machine messages (Present card, Enter PIN...), the card, the authorisation code and the transaction id are stored on the POS payment, and every sale or refund is kept in a Dojo transaction log.
