What it does

Collect the IGTF (impuesto a las grandes transacciones financieras) on every payment received in divisas, pay it to special-taxpayer suppliers, show it on the invoice as the SENIAT requires, and print every invoice, quotation and purchase order in bolivars and dollars at the BCV rate - updated automatically from the Banco Central de Venezuela.

Everything works inside the standard Odoo invoice, payment, sale and purchase screens.

Register a payment in divisas in a cash (or foreign) journal and the 3% IGTF is added on top: the customer pays 103, the invoice is settled for 100, 3 go to your IGTF perceived account.

IGTF is perceived only when your company is a special taxpayer (agente de percepcion) and only on journals outside the national banking system. Payments in bolivars never carry IGTF.

Paying a special-taxpayer supplier in foreign currency adds the IGTF it charges and books it to your IGTF expense account. Suppliers that are not special taxpayers: no IGTF (Decreto 4.647 art. 5).
