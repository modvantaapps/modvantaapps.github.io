POS Web Serial Scale

POS Web Serial Scale for Odoo 19: weigh products at the till with a USB or RS232 scale read straight by the browser - no IoT Box, no driver, no local service.

What it does

In Odoo 19 Community the point of sale reads a scale only through an IoT Box. This module lets the POS browser tab open the serial port of the scale itself (Web Serial API, Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer). The weight appears in the normal Odoo scale window and the price follows it.

Features

No IoT Boxplug the scale into the POS computer (USB or RS232 with a USB adapter), click Connect scale once and choose it. Later sessions reconnect by themselves.
Live weight in the Odoo scale windowgross, tare, net and price, refreshed several times per second; the navbar button shows the current weight.
Stable weight onlyMoving, Overload and Under zero are shown, and Order stays disabled until the scale reports a stable reading that stayed the same for N readings. Zero cannot be ordered.
Add When Stableoptionally the product is added by itself as soon as the weight is stable.
Scale profilesprotocol, baud rate 1200 to 115200, 7 or 8 data bits, parity, stop bits, RTS/CTS flow control, request command, line end, unit and stability rule. One profile can serve many registers.
Five protocolsToledo 8217 (W), MT-SICS (SI), A&D standard format (Q), continuous text output and your own regular expression.
Unitsreadings in kg, g, lb or oz are converted to the unit of the product.
Tarekept in the till, or sent to the scale (MT-SICS and A&D T command).
Typed weight when no scale is connectedper point of sale. A live reading always wins over a typed one.
Min / max weight per producta typed weight outside the range needs a "Use anyway" confirmation, and the server checks it again: a typed weight that is not allowed, or out of range and not confirmed, is refused.
Weighed Sales reportevery weighed line with its source (read from the scale or typed by the cashier), out-of-range flag and cashier.
