DB Schenker Shipping Integration

Book DB Schenker land transport from Odoo - pallets and part loads, labels, waybills and tracking

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A DB Schenker connector for Odoo 19: validating a delivery order books the shipment with DB Schenker, stores the freight labels and the waybill, and keeps the booking status and tracking events up to date - with prices from a rate table, Odoo rules or the live price service.

The module adds DB Schenker as a delivery method provider in Odoo and books land transport shipments through the eSchenker booking web service: DB SCHENKERsystem for pallets and parcels, part load and the other land products of your contract.

Each delivery method is one DB Schenker product with its incoterm, services and price mode - a fixed price, a rate table by destination and weight, Odoo price rules, or the live Price Online service for Schenker Sweden accounts. On the delivery order you set the pickup date, delivery services, pallet spaces, loading metres, insurance, pre-notices and cash on delivery for that transfer.

Features

All bookings in one list
eSchenker access, product, price and pickup on the delivery method
Part load next to DB SCHENKERsystem
DB Schenker tab on the delivery order
Booking with documents and status
Pickup window and tracking events
Prices at the website checkout
