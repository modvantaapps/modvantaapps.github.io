POS Product Internal Reference

Show the product internal reference (SKU) in the point of sale

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The product's Internal Reference on the POS product cards, on the order lines of the cart and on the receipt - each switched on or off per point of sale.

This module shows the product's Internal Reference in a small label on each product card, in brackets before the product name on the order lines, and on the printed or emailed receipt.

Each of the three places has its own switch in the point of sale settings, so a shop can show codes on the cards for staff but keep them off the receipt, or the other way round.

Who it is for

  • Hardware and spare-parts counters. Staff at a parts counter usually sell by code, not by name. Showing the Internal Reference on the product card and the cart line means they can match what the customer reads off a box or a quote.
  • Shops with look-alike variants. When the same item comes in several sizes or colours, names alone get confusing on a busy till. For variants the till shows each variant's own reference, so the right one goes into the order.
  • Wholesale and trade sales. Trade buyers often check receipts against their own purchase lists. Printing the code before each line on the receipt makes that check quick for them.

Features

Many products look alike on a tillthe same chair in two colours, five sizes of the same box. Staff and customers check codes, not names, but the Odoo point of sale does not show the internal reference.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It only needs the standard Point of Sale app.
  2. Open your products in Inventory or Point of Sale and fill in the Internal Reference field. Products left blank show no code at all.
  3. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings and pick the point of sale you want to change.
  4. Under Product Internal Reference, tick or untick On product cards, On order lines and On the receipt. All three are on by default.
  5. Save, then open a new POS session so the till picks up the new settings.

POS Product Internal Reference: questions

Which field does it display?

The product's Internal Reference, which Odoo stores as the default code. A product with variants shows the reference set on that variant.

Can I show codes to staff but keep them off the customer receipt?

Yes. The receipt has its own switch, separate from the product cards and order lines, so you can turn just that one off.

Are the settings shared by all my tills?

No. The three switches are stored per point of sale, so a trade counter can show codes while a front shop hides them.

Which Odoo edition does it need?

It depends only on Point of Sale, so it works on Community and Enterprise. It doesn't change how products are searched, priced or stocked. It only adds the code to what's displayed.
