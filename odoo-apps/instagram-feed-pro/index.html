Instagram Feed Pro

Show your Instagram on your Odoo website - and sell from it

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Your Instagram posts and Reels appear on the website as a grid, masonry wall, carousel or slider. Tag products on a post and visitors open it, see the product hotspots and add the product to the Odoo cart without leaving the page.

Instagram Feed Pro connects an Instagram account with an access token and synchronises its posts, Reels and videos into Odoo, with captions, likes, comments and (optionally) view counts. A scheduled action is set to sync every 30 minutes, and Sync Now syncs on demand.

On the website, the posts are shown by blocks you add in the website editor (Social category) or on the ready-made /instagram page: a 4-column grid, a masonry wall that keeps each post's proportions, a carousel with arrows and a one-post-at-a-time slider. Clicking a post opens a lightbox; Reels and videos play there.

In the backend you tag Odoo products on a post with a hotspot position. The lightbox then shows the products with price, View Product and Add to Cart. Add to Cart uses Odoo's own cart, so prices, pricelists and stock rules stay the same as in the shop.
