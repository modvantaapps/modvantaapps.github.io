UAE VAT201 Return

Compute the FTA VAT201 return - boxes 1a to 14, emirate by emirate - from your own posted invoices and bills, open the journal items behind every figure, and run pre-filing checks before you key the return into EmaraTax.

What it does

The UAE localisation of Odoo already loads the VAT201 definition with the UAE chart of accounts, and every posted invoice line already carries its tax grid. What Community lacks is the engine that adds them up. This module is that engine: it reads the same VAT201 definition and totals your posted journal items for the tax period.

Standard rated supplies are split across the seven emirates exactly as the UAE taxes of Odoo tag them. Boxes 8, 11, 12, 13 and 14 are calculated the way the definition says, including the Adjustment column and box 7 adjustments to imports, which you type in with a reason that stays with the return.

The module does not connect to the FTA. It produces the figures and the evidence; you enter them on EmaraTax and record the FTA reference back on the return.
