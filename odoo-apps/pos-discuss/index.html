POS Discuss

Chat with the team without leaving the register: the real Odoo Discuss opens over the Point of Sale, with an unread badge and a new message alert.

What it does

Cashiers spend the whole shift in the POS, where Odoo shows them no messages. This module adds a Discuss button with a badge and opens Discuss in a panel over the till.

Who it is for

  • Stores with several tills on one floor. A cashier who needs a price check or a manager's approval can message a colleague from the register instead of leaving the customer at the counter.
  • Retail chains with a head office. Office staff already post in Discuss channels. With this module the cashiers see those channels too, and the badge tells them when something new is waiting.
  • Restaurants and cafes. Front of house can send a note to the kitchen or the manager while an order is still open on screen, then close the panel and carry on with that same order.
  • Teams that switch between web, phone and till. Messages read in the back office or the mobile app stop counting on the POS badge as well, because it reads the same unread state Odoo already keeps.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. Discuss in the POS is switched on by default for every point of sale.
  2. To change it, go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings, select the point of sale at the top and find the Discuss in the POS setting.
  3. Leave Alert and sound on a new message ticked if cashiers should get a notification with the author and the first words of the message.
  4. Set Refresh the badge at least every to the number of seconds you want. The minimum is 5, and the badge also updates at once when a message arrives over the live connection.
  5. Save, open a session and tap the Discuss button next to the cashier name to open Discuss over the register.

POS Discuss: questions

Does it need Odoo Enterprise?

No. It depends on Point of Sale and Discuss (mail), both included in Community.

Is this a separate chat tool?

No. The panel loads Odoo's own Discuss web client, with the back office menu bar hidden. Direct messages, channels, files, reactions and search work as they do in the back office.

Will the cashier lose the order when opening the panel?

No. Discuss opens over the register and closing it brings the register back as it was, including the order in progress.

What can a cashier see in the panel?

Only the chats and channels that user is already a member of in Discuss. The module doesn't change access rights, and the badge counts unread conversations plus the Inbox, leaving muted conversations out.

Does it work when the POS is offline?

No. Discuss needs a connection to the server. The register itself still works offline as usual, the chat just waits until the connection is back.

Can I turn it off for one shop?

Yes. The setting is per point of sale. Untick Discuss in the POS for any till where you don't want the button.
