What it does

books every prescribed session without double booking

A therapy plan holds the referral, the condition, the number of sessions prescribed and the date the prescription runs out. Plan Sessions books the whole course on the chosen pattern (every day, weekdays, Mon-Wed-Fri, Tue-Thu or weekly) as appointments with the therapist and room. If any slot clashes, nothing is booked and every busy slot is listed.

A session cannot be closed before the initial assessment, while a reassessment is due, beyond the number prescribed or after the prescription ends. The therapist records pain before and after, modalities, the exercises done, a pain map and a SOAP note. Closing the session bills it by the plan's rule or draws one session from the patient's prepaid package.

Assessments record pain, a function score, range of motion against the normal range, muscle strength and outcome scales. The plan shows the change since the initial assessment, flags a clinically meaningful improvement or a plateau, and prints a progress report, an attendance sheet and the home exercise programme.
