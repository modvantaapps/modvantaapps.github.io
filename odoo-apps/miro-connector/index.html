Miro Connector | Miro Integration for Odoo

Miro boards next to the Odoo projects, tasks and leads they belong to, with cards and tasks kept in step.

What it does

Connect your own Miro app and Odoo imports the boards of your team. Link a board to a project and every card on it becomes a task: rename a card in Miro and the task follows at the next sync, change the task's due date or assignee in Odoo and the card follows. Sticky notes can turn into tasks too, and open tasks that are not on the board yet can be added as cards. From a project or a lead you create a new board in one click, and the board form shows the board itself through Miro Live Embed. Sharing, member roles and deleting boards stay with managers.
