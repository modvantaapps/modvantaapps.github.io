Meta Catalog

Facebook Catalog and Instagram Shopping for Odoo

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Put your Odoo eCommerce products in a Meta catalog for Facebook Shop, Instagram Shopping and dynamic ads. Push products straight to Meta through the Graph API with delta updates, or give Commerce Manager a token-protected feed URL in XML, CSV or TSV - or both. Variants, pricelists, sale prices, currencies, stock, Google categories, brand, GTIN and MPN are built from your Odoo data, every Meta error lands on the product it belongs to, and the content IDs are the ones your Meta Pixel sends.

Everything a shop needs to sell from its Odoo catalog on Facebook and Instagram.

Products and variants are sent with the catalog items_batch endpoint in batches of up to 5,000. The batch handle is checked afterwards, so you see what Meta accepted and what it refused.

A token-protected scheduled feed URL in RSS 2.0 XML, CSV or TSV. Paste it into a data source in Commerce Manager; regenerate the token any time. Cached so Meta fetches are fast.

One item per variant with item_group_id, colour and size from your attributes, the variant link and image - or one item per product if you prefer.

Who it is for

  • Odoo webshops starting on Facebook and Instagram. A shop that wants its products in a Meta catalog for Facebook Shop, Instagram Shopping or catalog ads can build that catalog from the eCommerce products it already has, without maintaining a spreadsheet by hand.
  • Stores whose prices and stock change often. Each scheduled run compares the product with what was sent last time and only pushes new or changed items through the Graph API, so a price cut or a sold-out variant shows up at Meta on the next run.
  • Fashion and footwear sellers. Variants go out as one item each with a shared item_group_id, colour and size come from your attributes, and a size system such as EU, UK or US can be set per product or as a catalog default.
  • Advertisers running Advantage+ catalog ads. Catalog content IDs can be set to match what your Meta Pixel sends, and individual products can be kept out of catalog ads, Marketplace or Instagram tagging with a tick box.

Features

Every item is checked before it leaves Odooprice, image, brand/GTIN/MPN, category, description, HTTPS link, text length. Facebook Shop and Instagram Shopping ready counts on the catalog.
Newchannel visibility, column mapping and size system
Newshipping, product sets and unit price
Add shipping rates on the catalogcountry, optional region, service and price. Every item gets the shipping field (US:CA:Ground:9.99 USD), and a price of 0 shows the Free Shipping overlay in ads.

Setting it up

  1. Go to Website > eCommerce > Meta Catalog > Catalogs and create a catalog for your website.
  2. Choose which products go in: Published products only, eCommerce categories or a Product filter, then pick the pricelist, language, warehouse for stock and One item per variant or One item per product.
  3. Press Check products to see what Meta will receive and which products still miss a price, image, brand, GTIN or category.
  4. Pick the sync method. For the feed, choose XML (RSS 2.0), CSV or TSV and paste the Feed URL into a data source in Meta Commerce Manager.
  5. For the Graph API push, enter the Meta catalog ID and a system user Access token, then press Test connection.
  6. Map your eCommerce categories to Google categories under Website > eCommerce > Meta Catalog > Google Categories, and fill in shipping rates or extra columns on the catalog if you need them.
  7. Leave the scheduled action to push changes every 15 minutes, or press Sync changes. Meta's errors appear on the item and on the product.

Meta Catalog: questions

Do I need the Graph API, or is the feed enough?

Either works. The feed URL is token-protected and cached, and Commerce Manager fetches it on its own schedule. The Graph API push sends only changes and stores Meta's answer for every batch. You can also use both on the same catalog.

Which Odoo apps does it need?

It depends on eCommerce with stock (website_sale_stock), which is in Community. Python's requests library must be available on the server for the Graph API calls.

What happens when I unpublish or remove a product?

On the next sync the item is either deleted at Meta or set to archived, depending on the Products leaving the catalog setting. Data Meta rejected isn't sent again until you change the product.

Can I run catalogs in more than one currency?

Yes. Each catalog has its own pricelist, so you can make a second catalog for the same website with a pricelist in another currency. A pricelist discount or a separate sale pricelist is sent as sale_price with its effective dates.

Will Facebook Shop or Instagram Shopping be switched on for me?

No. The module fills and maintains the catalog and shows how many items are Facebook Shop and Instagram ready. Turning on Shops, Instagram Shopping or catalog ads is done in your Meta account, which has its own approval rules.

Are Google product categories included?

Yes. The Google product taxonomy ships as data with the module. You map your eCommerce categories once, or set a category on a single product.
