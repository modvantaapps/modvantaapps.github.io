Agriculture Farm Management

Agriculture management in one module: farms and fields on a map, crop planning per season, field activities that use real stock, labour and machinery, a farm job cost sheet with budget vs actual and cost per hectare, crop scouting, farm weather records from Open-Meteo with frost and rain alerts, harvest lots sold through Sales, livestock management for dairy farm, poultry farm, goats and sheep, and a contract farming crop request portal.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Agriculture Farm Management for Odoo 19Who it is forCrop farms and plantationsDairy, poultry and mixed farmsAgribusiness and contract farming

What it does

One app for the field, the barn and the office

Everything below was used on a test database with sample data

Soil type, pH, organic matter, N, P, K and EC per field, with a soil test history and the lab report attached. The latest test becomes the field soil profile.

Crop master with days to harvest, expected yield, GDD base temperature, varieties with their own yield and seed rate, and an activity template per crop.

Land preparation, sowing, irrigation, fertiliser, spraying, weeding and harvest take seed, fertiliser and chemicals out of the farm stock location with real stock moves. Undo returns the stock.

Features

Draw each field boundary on OpenStreetMapthe area in hectares and acres is measured for you. The farm map shows every field coloured by status with its crop. No API key.
Pick field, season, crop and varietyharvest date and expected yield are computed. Confirming creates the field activities and the budget. Overlapping plans on a field are blocked; draft plans stay as scenarios.
Weekly, every 10 days, fortnightly or monthlyone customer invoice or sales order per buyer and period, by a daily job or in one click. Buyer statement PDF.
A morning milk collection for the dairy co-oplitres, fat and SNF from the milk records of each cow, the rate per litre from the fat / SNF price chart, and the invoice of the billing period.
Lactation curve of one cowdaily milk by days in milk for her current and her previous lactation.
Fertility reportcalvings, calving interval, services per conception, conception rate and days open for every cow.
Profit per animal for one monthmilk revenue from the collections minus breeding, feed and vet costs, cow by cow.
Farm dashboardarea under crop, season cost against budget, open scouting, milk and eggs, field work and weather alerts.
A fieldclick the map to draw the boundary, the area is measured in hectares and acres.
A done fertiliser activityproduct taken from stock, labour hours and tractor hours costed.
Dairy herdmilk per head per day, feed per litre, feed and vet cost, daily ration from stock.
A cow under treatmentthe withdrawal period is shown and her milk is discarded automatically.
The customer portala partner farmer follows his crop requests.
The public website formanyone can request a crop, no account needed.
