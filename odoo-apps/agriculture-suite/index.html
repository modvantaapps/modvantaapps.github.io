Agriculture Farm Management

Agriculture management in one module: farms and fields on a map, crop planning per season, field activities that use real stock, labour and machinery, a farm job cost sheet with budget vs actual and cost per hectare, crop scouting, farm weather records from Open-Meteo with frost and rain alerts, harvest lots sold through Sales, livestock management for dairy farm, poultry farm, goats and sheep, a crop request portal, crop requests turned into projects and tasks, contract farming and outgrower management with input advances recovered at settlement and a contract farmer portal, farm machinery in Fleet with fuel and machine-hour logs costed to the field, water quality tests, a farm incident register, and agriculture production from harvest to manufacturing order. Farm ERP for crops, dairy and poultry management.

What it does

One app for the field, the barn and the office

Everything below was used on a test database with sample data

Soil type, pH, organic matter, N, P, K and EC per field, with a soil test history and the lab report attached. The latest test becomes the field soil profile.

Crop master with days to harvest, expected yield, GDD base temperature, varieties with their own yield and seed rate, and an activity template per crop.

Land preparation, sowing, irrigation, fertiliser, spraying, weeding and harvest take seed, fertiliser and chemicals out of the farm stock location with real stock moves. Undo returns the stock.

Who it is for

  • Arable farms and plantations. Growers who plant several fields each season want to know what a crop really cost per hectare. Field activities take seed, fertiliser and chemicals out of farm stock and log labour and machine hours, and the job cost sheet sets that against the crop plan's budget.
  • Dairy farms selling milk to a co-op or creamery. When the buyer pays by fat and SNF, the farm needs the same numbers the dairy uses. Milk collections are priced from a fat and SNF price chart, and a daily job bills each buyer per period with an invoice or a sales order.
  • Poultry and mixed livestock farms. Flocks are counted by head, while cattle, goats and sheep are tracked one animal at a time. Egg records, feed consumed from stock, vaccination schedules and mortality by cause all sit in the same app as the fields that grow the feed.
  • Agribusinesses buying from outgrowers. Companies that contract smallholders give out seed and cash up front and get paid back from the harvest. Grower contracts record those advances, weigh and grade each delivery, and recover a set share of the advances when the settlement is billed.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It uses Inventory, Sales, Purchase, Invoicing, Employees, Maintenance, Project, Manufacturing, Fleet and Website, and adds an Agriculture app to the main menu.
  2. Go to Agriculture > Configuration > Settings. Turn on Daily weather from Open-Meteo if you want automatic weather, set the frost and heavy rain alert thresholds, and choose the default labour and machine rates.
  3. Fill in Agriculture > Configuration > Crops, Varieties and Seasons, then create each farm under Agriculture > Farms > Farms with its latitude and longitude.
  4. Add your plots under Agriculture > Farms > Fields and draw each boundary on the map so the area in hectares is measured for you.
  5. Create a crop plan under Agriculture > Crops > Crop Plans, pick the field, season, crop and variety, and confirm it to generate the field activities and the budget.
  6. For livestock, set up Agriculture > Livestock > Herds and Flocks and the animals in them. Dairy farms selling by quality also add a price chart under Agriculture > Configuration > Milk Price Charts.

Agriculture Farm Management: questions

Do I need to pay for a weather service or a map key?

No. Weather comes from Open-Meteo, which needs no API key, and fields are drawn on OpenStreetMap. You can also type weather in by hand if the server can't reach the internet.

Does it run on Odoo Community?

Yes. Every app it depends on, including Manufacturing, Maintenance, Project and Fleet, is part of Community. It's a big install, so expect those apps to appear in your menu when the module is installed.

How does it stop milk from treated animals being sold?

Vet treatments carry a withdrawal period. While it runs, milk from that animal, or from a herd treated as a whole, can't be collected, received into stock or billed, and the error message names the animal and the treatment.

Can customers or growers send us requests online?

Yes. Buyers and partner farmers can send a crop request from the /crop-request page or their portal account and follow its status there. Contract growers also get a portal page with their contracts and statement, and they can ask for inputs from it.

What happens if a contract grower delivers wet or poor produce?

Each delivery records gross and tare weight, moisture and any rejected quantity. Deliveries above the contract's standard moisture are shrunk to a dry weight, so only the accepted quantity is valued at settlement.

Does it give financial or agronomic advice?

No. It records what you plan and what happens, and it calculates costs, yields and alerts from your own data and thresholds. Spray rates, withdrawal periods and price charts are the values you enter, so check them with your agronomist, vet or buyer.
