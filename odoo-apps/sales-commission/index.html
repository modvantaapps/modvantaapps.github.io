Sales Commission

Pay your salespeople, team leaders and external agents the right commission, at the right moment: on the confirmed order, the posted invoice or the money actually received. Commission plans and rules, splits, targets with accelerators, clawback, approval and settlement to vendor bills or payroll, with reports, My Commission and an agent portal.

What it does

Pay commission on money received, without spreadsheets

Everything a sales manager needs to calculate, approve and pay commission.

Salespeople plans assigned to people or whole sales teams, agent plans for external agents and partners. Validity dates, a standard rate, and as many rules as you need. Several plans side by side, per company.

Rules by product, product category (with sub-categories), customer, customer tag, sales team, discount band and minimum margin. Pay a percentage of the untaxed amount, the total or the margin, a fixed amount per unit or per line, a tiered rate by order size or by margin - or exclude a product.

Each plan earns on the confirmed sale order, the posted invoice or the paid invoice. On paid invoices every partial payment earns its share - the commission grows with each payment and is never counted twice.
