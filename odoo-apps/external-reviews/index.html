Trustpilot & Google Product Reviews Import

Show your Trustpilot and Google reviews on your Odoo product pages

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Connect a Trustpilot business unit and a Google place, sync their reviews into Odoo (by button or daily), and show them on product pages with an average score, a 5-to-1 star breakdown you can filter by, verified badges and a link back to the source.

Reviews your customers leave on review platforms help sell, but they live on someone else's site. Odoo's own product ratings start at zero.

This module stores one connector per review source: Trustpilot (public business-unit reviews) or Google (Places API, which returns up to five reviews per place - Google's limit). A sync imports new reviews and updates ones the reviewer edited, without duplicates; a daily scheduled sync runs for every connector you include.

On the shop, every product page gets a Customer Reviews block: average, star breakdown, review cards with date, stars, Verified badge and Read on Trustpilot link, and Show more. Reviews can be mapped to one product or left for the whole storefront, and any review can be hidden without deleting it.
