B2B Company & Contact Hierarchy

Company hierarchy for B2B contacts: hierarchy level (headquarters, regional office, subsidiary, division, branch), ultimate holding company, subsidiary management and a one-click list of the whole corporate group. Parent child accounts, corporate group, company tree.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Odoo already lets a company contact have a parent company. What it does not tell you is where a company sits in the group: is this the holding company, a regional office or a single branch? And from the holding company you cannot easily see everyone who belongs to the group.

This module adds a Hierarchy Level to every contact (Global Headquarters, Regional Headquarters, Subsidiary, Business Division, Branch Office, Department / Unit), works out the Ultimate Holding Company by walking up the parent chain, counts the group, and adds a Subsidiaries button that opens every company and person under the account.
