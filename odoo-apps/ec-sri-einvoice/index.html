Ecuador SRI Electronic Invoicing

Issue Ecuador SRI electronic documents from Odoo Community: access key, signature, sending, authorization, RIDE, plus the monthly ATS file and a Form 104 worksheet.

What it does

This module was built from public documentation: open-source projects, integrator documentation and public store pages. The format has not been checked against the current official SRI schema or the SRI test environment, because the official site could not be reached when it was built.

The invoice list shows where each document stands: to send, received, authorized, not authorized or error.

One button signs the XML, sends it to the reception service and reads the authorization. The access key and the status stay on the invoice.

Each document keeps its access key, authorization and the answer of every call, with a job that retries pending documents.

Facturas, credit notes, debit notes and withholdings with filters for pending, not authorized and authorized.
