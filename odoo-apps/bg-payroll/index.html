Bulgaria Payroll

Bulgaria Payroll for Odoo 19: Social and Health Insurance, 10% Income Tax and Declaration 1 and 6 Worksheets

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Bulgarian statutory payroll on Odoo Community in euro: the state social insurance funds, the universal and professional pension funds and health insurance split between employer and employee, the minimum and maximum insurable income of 2026, the flat 10% income tax with the child and disability reliefs, the Declaration 1 and 6 figures and the annual tax recalculation, with a Bulgarian translation.

Pension, general illness and maternity, unemployment and work accident, employer and employee.

Universal fund 2.8% / 2.2%, move to the state fund, professional fund for categories I and II.

Minimum insurable income per employee and the maximum of 2,111.64 then 2,300 EUR from August 2026.

On the taxable income less the employee contributions, never below zero.
