Order Fraud Score & RTO Risk

Stop fake cash on delivery orders before you ship them

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Every website order gets a fraud score from weighted rules when the customer reaches the payment step. Low risk orders keep Cash on Delivery, medium risk orders must pay online, and high risk orders are held for review.

Cash on delivery orders that are refused at the door (return to origin, RTO) cost the shipping both ways and block stock. Most of them can be predicted: a customer who refused a delivery before, cancels a lot, reuses another customer's phone number, gives a two-word address or orders in the middle of the night.

This module scores the cart from 0 to 100 against 16 configurable rules when the customer reaches the payment page. The score falls into a low, medium or high band, and each band has an action: allow, require prepayment (Cash on Delivery is hidden), hold for manual review, or block the order.

Every order keeps its score, risk level, action and a line-by-line breakdown of which rule fired and why. Customers and pincodes get delivered, returned and cancelled counters, and a trusted customer with clean deliveries scores lower.

Features

Low risk: Cash on Delivery stays
Medium risk: prepayment only
High risk: held for review
