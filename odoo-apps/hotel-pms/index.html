Hotel Management System

Hotel channel manager with iCal sync for Airbnb, Booking.com and VRBO, OTA booking sync through Channex (Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda), a rate and availability calendar with stop sell, and a hotel booking engine with live availability and card prepayment, plus the hotel PMS behind it: hotel reservation and room booking for a hotel, guest house, hostel or resort (hostel management included), a drag-to-book tape chart, multi hotel dashboards, a hotel POS and hotel restaurant with charge to room, restaurant tables and kitchen tickets, automatic guest emails (confirmation, deposit, cancellation, checkout, pre-arrival reminder), airport pickup and drop, lost and found, a meals to prepare report, travel agent commission, meal plans and extra person charges, room exchange and stay extension, guest ID documents, VIP guest levels and guest preferences shown on the folio and at check-in, guest feedback, laundry, banquet hall booking, a hotel booking system enquiry form to CRM, front desk, guest folios, housekeeping, and a night audit with ADR and RevPAR.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A hotel management system for a hotel, guesthouse, hostel, B&B or resort, together with the booking engine that fills it. Guests pick their dates on your Odoo website, see live availability and the real price for their stay, enter their details and pay a deposit by card. The booking lands in Odoo as a confirmed folio.

Availability is counted per room type and checked again in the request that writes the booking, and a website booking holds its room while the guest is on the payment page, so the last room is not sold twice.

Who it is for

  • Independent hotels that want direct bookings. A hotel paying OTA commission on every stay wants guests to book on its own site. The /rooms pages show live availability and nightly prices, and the booking takes a prepayment through any payment provider set up in Odoo.
  • Guest houses and B&Bs listed on Airbnb or VRBO. Small properties often juggle the same rooms across two or three listing sites. Each listing publishes its own iCal feed and reads the channel's feed every 30 minutes, so a booking taken on one site closes the night everywhere else.
  • Resorts with a restaurant and bar. Guests eat and drink on site and want it all on one bill at checkout. A POS payment method marked Charge to Hotel Room puts the restaurant bill on the guest's folio by room number.
  • Groups running more than one property. Owners with several hotels need one database but separate rooms, meal plans and halls per property. Each hotel has its own dashboard card with arrivals, departures, occupancy and revenue for the month.

Features

Behind it the front desk works the bookingsarrivals and departures, room allocation at check-in, a room rack with clean and dirty status, extras on the folio, invoicing, and a night audit that posts the room charges and reports occupancy, ADR and RevPAR.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It pulls in Website, Invoicing, CRM, Point of Sale and POS Restaurant, and adds a Hotel app to the main menu.
  2. Create your property under Hotel > Configuration > Hotels, then add Room Types, Rate Plans and Meal Plans in the same Configuration menu.
  3. Add the physical rooms under Hotel > Rooms > Room Rack so each one belongs to a room type.
  4. Go to Hotel > Configuration > Settings and, under Online Booking, choose the Prepayment rule, the time rooms are held while the guest pays, the minimum stay and the booking window.
  5. Publish your room types so they appear on the /rooms page, and make sure at least one payment provider is enabled in Odoo for online prepayment.
  6. To sell on other sites, open Hotel > Channel Manager > Channels, create a channel and map each listing to a room type. Enter the channel's iCal address or set up a Channex connector under Channel Manager Connectors.

Hotel Management System: questions

Which payment gateways does the booking engine support?

It uses Odoo's own payment layer, so any payment provider you can enable in Odoo works. The prepayment can be nothing, the first night, a percentage of the stay or the full stay, set per company. Payment is recorded from the provider's notification, so a guest who pays and closes the tab still gets a confirmed booking.

Do I need a channel manager subscription to connect Booking.com or Expedia?

For iCal sync with Airbnb, VRBO and Booking.com you don't. For Booking.com, Expedia or Agoda through their APIs, the module connects to Channex, so you need your own Channex account, property id and API key. iCal only carries dates, so prices and restrictions are only pushed through Channex.

What happens if a channel sends a booking for a room that's already taken?

It doesn't overbook. A channel reservation goes through the same availability check as a desk booking. If it no longer fits, it's logged as a booking conflict with a to-do for the channel's responsible user, and it clears itself once a room frees up and the channel is read again.

What happens to the deposit when a guest cancels or doesn't show up?

A paid deposit on a cancelled or no-show booking is marked as forfeited by default. If you decide to give it back, the desk presses the refund button, which marks it refunded and logs it in the chatter. The money itself is returned through your payment provider or accounting as usual.

Can my receptionists use it without accounting rights?

Yes. Receptionists work in the hotel's own groups and can open folios and check guests in and out without accounting access. Cashiers at the restaurant till don't need any hotel rights to charge a bill to a room, because the server checks the stay itself.

Which languages does it come in?

English, Arabic, French, Spanish and German. Each user sees the screens in their own Odoo language, and Arabic is shown right to left.
