GoSweetSpot Shipping Integration

GoSweetSpot for Odoo 19 - New Zealand shipping

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Every NZ courier of your GoSweetSpot site, from the Odoo delivery order.

GoSweetSpot connects one account to many New Zealand carriers. This module connects that account to Odoo, so quoting, booking, labels, tracking and cancelling all happen on the sales order and the delivery order.

Rates come from GoSweetSpot when you quote, so the shipping line shows a real rate and not a guess. Each delivery method is one carrier service, the cheapest service of one carrier, or the cheapest carrier of all (Carrier "*"). You choose whether the price is the cost or the cost-centre charge.

One click lists every available rate with the service standard, rural and Saturday flags. Pick one on the quotation and the delivery books that carrier and service. Pick one on the transfer and the shipment is booked with that exact quote.

Validating the transfer books the shipment and attaches the label: PDF 100x175, 100x150, A4 or Labelope, PNG, or 200/300 dpi ZPL. The connote is the tracking reference and the cost lands on the transfer. A print agent printer can receive every label as well.
