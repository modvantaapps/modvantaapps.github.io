Employee Shift & Roster Management

Employee shift scheduling and roster planning for Odoo HR: bulk-generate a weekly/monthly roster, assign employees to shifts, publish the roster with employee notifications, prevent double-booked shifts, auto-log attendance, and give employees a self-service My Shifts view - workforce scheduling, shift roster, staff rota

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Shift schedulingRoster planningEmployee shiftsHR

What it does

Plan shifts for a whole team in one go, publish them, and stop double bookings

The Generate Shifts wizard creates a roster for several employees at once: a shift name, a date range, start and end time, a location and the weekdays to include. Shifts start as drafts in a weekly calendar and a list.

Publishing a shift notifies the employee in Odoo. An employee cannot get two overlapping shifts, and a shift can be marked worked, cancelled or set back to draft. Every employee sees their own shifts under Employees -> My Shifts.
