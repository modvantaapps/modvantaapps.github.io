Odoo SuiteCRM integration

SuiteCRM Connector

Keep SuiteCRM for the sales team and run quotes, invoices and stock in Odoo, with the same customers in both.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
SuiteCRM 8SuiteCRM 7API V8AccountsContactsLeadsOpportunitiesSales Stages

What it does

Create an OAuth2 client in SuiteCRM, paste its ID and secret into Odoo and press Test Connection. Odoo talks to the SuiteCRM API V8 and reads the users, then every account, contact, lead and opportunity. A SuiteCRM account becomes a company in Odoo with its contacts under it, leads land in the CRM as leads and opportunities in the pipeline with amount, probability and stage. Notes, calls and meetings show up in the chatter. Later runs read only what changed, and what your team edits in Odoo goes back to SuiteCRM. Use it to move off SuiteCRM in one go or to keep both running for as long as you need.

Features

SuiteCRM 8 and 7one module for both. For SuiteCRM 8 the API under /legacy is used for you
OAuth2client credentials by default, or the password grant for a named user. Tokens are renewed by themselves
Accounts and contactscompanies with website, phone, e-mail, industry, billing address and description; contacts under their account with title, e-mail, phone and address. Both ways
Leadsname, company, e-mail, phone, title, lead source and status, both ways. Dead leads are marked lost, converted leads are archived with a note
Opportunitiesamount, probability, expected close date, account and salesperson, both ways
Sales stagesthe standard stages and lead statuses are mapped to Odoo stages and lost reasons. A custom stage key that appears on a record is added to the map for you
Notes, calls and meetingsposted once in the chatter of the right record. Internal notes logged in Odoo become SuiteCRM notes
TasksSuiteCRM tasks become Odoo activities and the other way round; completing one completes the other
Changes onlyeach run asks for records with a newer date_modified. A button checks the linked records and archives the ones deleted in SuiteCRM
Sync logone line per record created, updated, sent, archived or skipped, with the reason on errors
