Multibanco Payment Gateway

Take Multibanco, MB WAY, Payshop and card payments in Odoo 19, through ifthenpay or euPago

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
MultibancoMB WAYPayshopcardsifthenpayeuPagoPortugalEUR

What it does

Adds ifthenpay and euPago as payment providers in Odoo. Pick the gateway you have a contract with. The customer gets an entity and a reference to pay at an ATM or by home banking, approves in the MB WAY app, pays in a Payshop agent or uses a card. Odoo confirms the order and reconciles the invoice when the gateway reports the payment.

Features

Multibanco referencescreated through the gateway API with an expiry date; with ifthenpay also offline, calculated with the published algorithm from your entity and sub-entity
Reference on the invoiceoptional: each posted customer invoice in EUR gets a reference, shown on the invoice and printed on the PDF; a new reference for what is left after a partial payment
Paid invoiceswhen the gateway confirms, the payment is posted and reconciled with the invoice by the standard Odoo payment flow
MB WAYthe customer types the phone of the app, then approves there; the waiting page follows the answer
Payshop and cardsa reference to pay in cash, or the card page of the gateway
Checked notificationsthe ifthenpay anti-phishing key, the MB WAY signature and the card return signature; the euPago X-Signature, encrypted webhooks and the API key of the older webhooks. A wrong one is refused
Amount checka notification with another amount does not confirm the payment
One click callbackactivates the ifthenpay callback of every method you have a key for
Refundsfull or partial for MB WAY and cards, from Odoo
Lost notificationsan hourly action checks open ifthenpay payments, stores the gateway fee and closes references past their deadline
Reference emailoptional email to the customer with the entity, reference and amount
Point of Salewith Odoo online payment; a card payment with euPago on an order with no customer uses the email of your company, without notifying it
API logeach call and notification, with keys and customer details masked
Portuguesethe module is translated
