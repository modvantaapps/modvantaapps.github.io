Envia.com & Skydropx Shipping Mexico

Ship across Mexico from Odoo with Envia.com or Skydropx - every carrier's rate, labels, pickups and tracking

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

One Odoo 19 delivery method type for both Mexican shipping aggregators: live rates of every carrier the aggregator returns, cheapest or fastest rules at the checkout, rate comparison on quotations and transfers, labels on validation, carrier pickups, tracking events and customer emails.

The module connects Odoo to Envia.com and Skydropx, the two shipping aggregators most used in Mexico. You add an account per aggregator, load its carriers and services, and create delivery methods that use them.

Each delivery method picks the aggregator, the account and the ship-from address, a rate rule - cheapest rate, fastest, a preferred carrier or a fixed service - allowed carriers and services, package defaults, insurance, Envia cash on delivery and customer emails. Rates come live from the aggregator for every carrier it returns, such as Estafeta, FedEx, DHL, Redpack, Paquetexpress, 99minutos and J&T Express.

Features

All shipments in one list
Aggregator account with its carriers
Rate rule per delivery method
Compare rates on the quotation
Choose the rate on the transfer
Shipping status and customer email on the transfer
Shipment record with tracking events
Schedule a carrier pickup
Every carrier service at the checkout
