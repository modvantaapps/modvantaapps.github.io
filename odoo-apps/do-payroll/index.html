Dominican Republic Payroll

Dominican Republic Payroll for Odoo 19: ISR, TSS, INFOTEP and Salario de Navidad

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Dominican Republic PayrollNomina DominicanaISR AsalariadosTSSAFPSFSSRLINFOTEP

What it does

Nomina for the Dominican Republic on Odoo Community. Each payslip takes the AFP and SFS worker share off the salary, applies the DGII 2026 scale and shows the employer's TSS and INFOTEP. A salary of RD$60,000 gives RD$3,546 TSS and RD$3,486.68 ISR; the employer adds RD$9,774. The DGII's own example (RD$55,000 net gives RD$3,195.88) is one of the tests.

Exempt up to RD$416,220 a year, then 15%, 20% and 25%, editable for 2027.

Slice by slice, as in the DGII example, or the published fixed amounts.

The worker's 2.87% AFP and 3.04% SFS come off the salary before the ISR.

AFP to 20, SFS to 10 and SRL to 4 national minimum wages, as loaded for 2026.

Features

Not includedthe SUIR upload file, the IR-3 and IR-13 returns, and the 2027 scale. Report and pay with your own access.
