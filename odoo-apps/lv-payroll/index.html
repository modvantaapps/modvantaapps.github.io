Latvia Payroll

Latvia Payroll for Odoo 19: VSAOI, IIN 25.5% and the DDZ Employer's Report

What it does

Latvian statutory payroll on Odoo Community. Social insurance (VSAOI) by rate group, the 25.5% wage tax with the tax book allowances, the 105,300 maximum object, the business risk duty and the monthly Darba deveja zinojums (DDZ) as EDS XML, checked against the VID schema.

Employee 10.50% / employer 23.59% (34.09%), state pension age 9.25 / 20.77 (30.02%), service pension or disabled person on a state special pension 9.76 / 21.94 (31.70%). Each share rounded to the cent.

The employer withholds 25.5% on the base after VSAOI and allowances. For persons insured in another state, the part of the monthly pay above 8,775 EUR is taxed at 33%.

Without the tax book the employer withholds 25.5% from the first euro and applies no relief.

The VSAOI object of the year is tracked. Above the cap the normal rate keeps being withheld and the employee is reported with relation type M.

Features

At the main income place onlythe non-taxable minimum (550 EUR in 2026, 570 EUR in 2027), the pensioner's 500 EUR salary share, 250 EUR per dependant, disability 154 / 120 EUR and the repressed person relief.
The monthly report from the payslipsincome, VSAOI, IIN, relation type and risk duty per employee, totals and the amount payable to the single tax account.
