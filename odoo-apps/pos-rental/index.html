POS Rental Management

Rent products from the Odoo point of sale: price any period with the cheapest mix of hourly, daily, weekly and monthly rates, check live availability by unit or serial number, take the security deposit, and handle returns, late fees, damage charges, extensions and balances at the till.

POS Rental Management

What it does

Rentable products open a rent dialog at the till instead of being sold. The cashier picks the period (or a saved tenure), sees how many units or which serial numbers are free for that period and which rentals clash, and the price for the cheapest mix of rates, for example 1 week + 2 days instead of 9 days.

The rent and the security deposit go on the same ticket. Rent can be paid in part when you allow it, and a rental can start later and be handed over at pickup.

The Rentals screen lists what is reserved, out, due today, overdue or has a balance. Returns add the late fee automatically, take a damage charge, and refund or keep the deposit. Extensions are priced and checked against other bookings before they are added. Returned items go back into stock with their serial number.
