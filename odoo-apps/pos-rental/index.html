POS Rental Management

Rent products from the Odoo point of sale: price any period with the cheapest mix of hourly, daily, weekly and monthly rates, check live availability by unit or serial number, take the security deposit, and handle returns, late fees, damage charges, extensions and balances at the till.

POS Rental Management

What it does

Rentable products open a rent dialog at the till instead of being sold. The cashier picks the period (or a saved tenure), sees how many units or which serial numbers are free for that period and which rentals clash, and the price for the cheapest mix of rates, for example 1 week + 2 days instead of 9 days.

The rent and the security deposit go on the same ticket. Rent can be paid in part when you allow it, and a rental can start later and be handed over at pickup.

The Rentals screen lists what is reserved, out, due today, overdue or has a balance. Returns add the late fee automatically, take a damage charge, and refund or keep the deposit. Extensions are priced and checked against other bookings before they are added. Returned items go back into stock with their serial number.

Who it is for

  • Tool and equipment hire counters. A hire shop renting drills, ladders or concrete mixers by the hour or day can quote the cheapest mix of rates, check which serial numbers are free for the period and take a refundable deposit on the same ticket.
  • Party and event rental stores. Tables, speakers and costumes are often booked in advance and collected later. Rentals can be reserved for a future start, handed over at pickup and returned at the till with the deposit refunded or kept.
  • Bike, kayak or games rentals billed by time. For items charged by the minute or hour, a timer runs on the ticket line and the price is set from the time actually used when the customer comes back.
  • Shops with long-term rentals. Monthly rentals can carry recurring invoices, so the outstanding rent is invoiced once per period while the item is still out.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module, then go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings, select the register and switch on Rent from the POS in the Rental Counter block.
  2. Under Rental options choose the deposit and charges products, and whether to allow partial payment of rent, reserving for later and recurring rental invoices.
  3. Open a product, go to its POS Rental tab and tick Rent at the POS. Fill in Price per Hour, Price per Day, Price per Week or Price per Month, plus Security Deposit, Late Fee per Day, Grace Period (hours) and Minimum Rental (hours).
  4. For products without serial numbers set Rental Units to how many you can rent at the same time. Add tenures on the same tab if you sell fixed packages such as a weekend.
  5. For time-billed items tick Time-Based Billing (timer) and set the Timer Unit and Timer Rate.
  6. At the till, pick the rental product, choose the period, check availability and take the deposit and rent. Returns, extensions and balance payments are also handled at the till.
  7. Follow rentals under Point of Sale > Rentals, using Rental Orders, Rental Board and Availability Checker.

POS Rental Management: questions

Do I need Odoo's Rental app or Enterprise?

No. It depends on Point of Sale, Inventory, Invoicing and Discuss, all of which are in Community. The rental orders, board and availability checker come with this module.

How is the late fee worked out?

When an item comes back after its due time plus the product's grace period, the late fee per day is added at the till automatically. The cashier can also add a damage charge and decide whether the deposit is refunded or kept.

Does returned stock go back into the warehouse?

For storable products that left stock through the POS, the return creates a receipt that brings the item, and its serial number if it has one, back into the warehouse.

Are customers reminded before items are due back?

Yes. A scheduled action runs every hour. It flags overdue items, sends a due-back reminder email for items due within the next 24 hours to customers with an email address, and creates the recurring invoices that have come due.

Can two customers book the same unit for overlapping dates?

The till shows availability for the chosen period, per unit or per serial number, and lists the rentals that clash. The Availability Checker in the backend gives the same answer for planning ahead.

Can a rental be extended?

Yes. Rentals can be extended, paid off and renewed. Extensions and balance payments are booked with the Rental Charges product set on the register.
