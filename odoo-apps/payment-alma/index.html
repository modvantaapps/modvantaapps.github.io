Alma Payment Gateway

Offer "Payer en 3x sans frais" the way French shoppers expect it: every Alma plan is its own payment method, shown only when the amount qualifies, with the real schedule and fees from Alma. A "pay in 3x" widget sits under the product price and the cart total. Payments are confirmed by the customer return and by Alma's IPN, refunds and captures go back to Alma from standard Odoo screens.

What it does

What the best-selling Alma module advertises, and what this one adds.

Pay now, 2x, 3x, 4x, 6x, 8x, 10x, 12x and pay later in 15 or 30 days. Each plan is a payment method with the Alma logo, so the customer picks "Pay in 3 installments with Alma" in one click.

One cached call to Alma's eligibility API per amount tells which plans Alma accepts. Plans outside your own range, outside your contract or refused by Alma are hidden. API errors fall back to your rules.

Under the selected plan the customer sees each due date and amount and the customer fees as computed by Alma, before leaving your shop.

Under the product price (updated when the variant changes) and under the cart total: plan pills 2x / 3x / 4x / D+30 with the installment amount, and "available from 50 EUR" hints.
