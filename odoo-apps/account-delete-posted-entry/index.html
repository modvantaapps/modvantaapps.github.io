Delete Posted Journal Entry

Delete a posted invoice or journal entry in one step - and keep a record of it

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A Delete button on posted entries and a Delete Posted Entries action on the list: reset to draft and delete in one step, with a mandatory reason, only where Odoo allows it. Every deleted entry is kept in a read-only log.

A duplicate invoice or a test entry that was posted takes several clicks to remove in Odoo - reset to draft, then delete - and once it is gone nothing says who deleted it or why.

This module adds a Delete button on the form of a posted entry and a Delete Posted Entries action on the list, for members of the Delete Posted Journal Entries group. The wizard lists the entries, asks for a reason and deletes them in one step.

It never forces Odoo. Before anything is touched the wizard says which entries cannot be deleted and why - for example an entry generated by a bank statement line or a Point of Sale session - and then deletes nothing. Every deletion of an entry that had been posted, through the wizard or the standard way, is stored in the Deleted Entries Log with the user, the date, the reason and the journal items.

Features

A Delete button on posted entries
One step, with a reason
It tells you what cannot be deleted, and why
Deleted Entries Log
Full record of each deletion
Restrict deletion to the group
