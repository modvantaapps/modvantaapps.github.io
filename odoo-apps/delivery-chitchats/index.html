Chit Chats Shipping Integration

Ship with Chit Chats from Odoo 19: the cheapest or fastest postage on the quotation, the postage bought and the label PDF attached when the delivery is validated, return labels, drop-off batches and tracking that updates by itself.

What it does

A delivery method type Chit Chats on the standard Odoo shipping framework, connected to the public Chit Chats API v1: shipments, rates, postage purchase, labels, return labels, refunds, batches and tracking.

Features

Postage on the quotationOdoo creates a Chit Chats quote for the order address, weight and items, reads the postage rates and deletes the quote again; the price is the cheapest postage, the fastest one, or one postage type you choose (Chit Chats Slim, Select, Canada Tracked, U.S. Edge, USPS, International Tracked and more), before or after GST/HST, converted to the currency of the order.
Postage bought on validationvalidating the delivery order creates the Chit Chats shipment, buys the postage (waiting while Chit Chats processes the purchase) and attaches the label PDF to the transfer.
One shipment per boxa delivery order with several packages gets one Chit Chats shipment and one label per package, with its weight and size in metric or imperial units.
Customs line itemsevery shipment carries line items with quantity, value, SKU, HS code (or a default HS code), country of origin and weight, as Chit Chats needs for the USA and international destinations.
Optionsinsurance, signature confirmation, package type (parcel, envelopes, letters, USPS flat rate boxes), contents type and duties paid postage with your VAT reference for international shipments.
Drop-off batchesput the shipments you drop off at Chit Chats in one batch from Odoo and print the batch label for the bag or box.
Return labelgenerate a scan-based return label (Canada Post, Purolator or USPS) for a shipment; its PDF and the estimated cost are kept on the shipment.
Cancel and refundcancelling a shipment deletes it while the postage is not bought, or requests the postage refund; the refund shows as voided once Chit Chats processes it.
Trackinga scheduled job reads every open shipment: received by Chit Chats, in transit, delivered or exception, the final carrier and its tracking number, and the tracking events; the state never moves backwards.
Staging and productionthe Environment button switches between chitchats.com and staging.chitchats.com; every call is logged with the token masked, and a demo mode runs the whole flow without an account.
