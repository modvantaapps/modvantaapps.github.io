Falabella Connector

Import Falabella and Linio orders, keep price and stock in sync by SellerSku, and ship or cancel from Odoo: Chile, Colombia and Peru.

What it does

This connector talks directly to the Falabella Seller Center API with your own API key. Orders arrive in Odoo Sales with the buyer, the delivery address and the marketplace currency. Prices and stock go back to your listings by SellerSku, each item is packed and marked ready to ship package by package, and the shipping label lands on the delivery order.

Features

Order importScheduled or on demand, by status and by a created-after window. Items are matched by listing, internal reference or barcode. An order with an unknown SKU is not created half-way: it is noted with the missing SKUs and imported whole on the next run.
Totals that follow Seller CenterEach item comes in at its list price with the difference to the paid price as a line discount, plus one shipping line with the shipping of all items, in CLP, COP or PEN.
Buyers and addressesA repeat buyer is recognised by the national registration number and keeps one customer record. A new shipping address becomes a delivery contact.
Price and stock by SellerSkuProductUpdate feeds per business unit, with offer prices and dates. Publish available, on-hand or forecast quantity minus a safety buffer, from one or several warehouses. The scheduled push sends only what changed.
Feed status per SKUSeller Center works asynchronously, so each feed is checked and each listing shows Pending, Finished or Rejected with the error. A batch that failed to send is never stamped as synced.
Ready to shipOne button packs the pending items and sends SetStatusToReadyToShip package by package. The tracking code and courier are written on the order and the delivery. It can also run when the delivery is validated.
Shipping labelGetDocument downloads the label and attaches it to the delivery order.
CancelThe wizard cancels pending items with the SHORTAGE reason and your detail, then cancels the Odoo order when no item is left. A cancellation on Falabella also reaches Odoo.
Fulfilled by FalabellaOrders shipped from the Falabella warehouse are flagged, never confirmed automatically and cannot be shipped or cancelled from Odoo, so your stock is not reserved for them.
Signed calls and logEvery request is signed with HMAC-SHA256 in your Odoo; the key is never sent. Reads retry on 429 and 5xx, writes are never repeated, and every call is logged, including errors answered with HTTP 200.
