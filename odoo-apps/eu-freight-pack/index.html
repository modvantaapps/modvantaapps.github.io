DACHSER, GEODIS and Palletways Integration

DACHSER, GEODIS and Palletways Integration for Odoo 19

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

One module, three European freight carriers: book pallets and freight from the Odoo delivery, print the carrier labels, follow the tracking and get the proof of delivery for DACHSER, GEODIS and Palletways.

Validating a delivery books the freight at the carrier and attaches the label to the transfer.

Pallets come from your packages, or the transfer is split by a maximum weight per pallet.

Every booking is a freight shipment with its tracking events, proof of delivery and API log.

Features

Three delivery method typesDACHSER, GEODIS and Palletways, each with its own credentials and service settings.
