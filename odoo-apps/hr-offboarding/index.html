Employee Offboarding, Resignation and Exit Interview

Employee Offboarding, Resignation and Exit Interview for Odoo 19

What it does

Run every employee exit in Odoo Community from the resignation letter to the archived user: the employee hands in the resignation from the portal or the backend, the manager and HR approve it, the notice period and the last working day come from the contract, every department signs off its part of the exit checklist, the employee answers an exit interview, and HR prints the experience letter, the relieving letter and the clearance certificate. Built on Odoo's own Employees app and its departure process.

The employee writes the resignation letter on his portal page (or in the backend), can name a main reason and ask for an earlier last day, sees the status, and can withdraw it until it is approved.

The manager approves first (or skip it in the settings), then HR. Both get an activity and an email; refusal needs a reason that the employee receives. HR can also start an exit itself: termination, retirement, end of contract.

Taken from the employee's contract, else the contract type, else the company default - and the shorter probation notice while the trial period runs. The last working day and the days left are computed for you.

When the last working day is earlier than the end of the notice, the shortfall in days and its value from the daily wage are shown, to waive, to recover from the final settlement or to pay in lieu.

Features

Every step of an employee exit in one moduleresign, approve, clear, interview, close.
