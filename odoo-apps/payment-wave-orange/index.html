Wave and Orange Money Payment Gateway (Bictorys)

Bictorys for Odoo 19: take Wave, Orange Money and card payments on your Odoo website and at your Point of Sale.

What it does

A Bictorys payment provider for Odoo. Odoo creates the payment with the Bictorys Charges API and opens the Bictorys checkout page on the right tab; when Bictorys sends its notification, Odoo checks the webhook secret and reads the transaction from the Bictorys API before it confirms the order. At the register it works through the Odoo POS online payment: the customer scans the QR code and pays on the phone.

Features

Wave, Orange Money and cardthree payment methods in Odoo. Wave and Orange Money open the Mobile Money tab of the Bictorys checkout page, Card opens the card tab.
Point of Saleadd an online payment method with the Bictorys provider to a register. The cashier validates, the customer scans the QR code, pays with Wave, and the register shows Payment Successful when Bictorys confirms.
Walk-in customersan order without a customer is paid without any customer details sent to Bictorys (the customer types the phone number on the Bictorys page). Nothing is invented and no Public user name is sent.
Verified notificationsthe X-Secret-Key header must match the webhook secret of the provider, then Odoo reads the transaction from the API with your secret key and checks its reference, amount and currency. A notification alone never confirms an order.
Full refundsrefund a payment from Odoo; it is sent to Bictorys as a refund of the whole transaction.
Test and live keysTest Mode calls api.test.bictorys.com, Enabled calls api.bictorys.com. Test Connection checks the key without creating a payment.
