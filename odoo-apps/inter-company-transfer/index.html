Inter Company Transfer

Inter company rules for Odoo Community - sell to your own company once, and the other side is created for you

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Confirm a sales order for one of your companies and the purchase order appears in that company. Post the invoice and the vendor bill appears there too. Move stock between the warehouses of two companies with one document. Every mirrored document is logged.

When you run several companies in one Odoo database, the same deal is usually typed twice: a sales order in the selling company and a purchase order in the buying company, then an invoice and a vendor bill. This module does the second half for you. It works in both directions: sales order to purchase order, purchase order to sales order, customer invoice to vendor bill and vendor bill to customer invoice.

The generated document is made in the receiving company with its own warehouse, journal, accounts and taxes. Taxes are matched automatically by rate and name, and a tax mapping table covers the ones that differ. A generated document carries a notice that it will not be mirrored back, so the rules never loop even when both companies have every rule switched on.

For goods, an Inter-Company Transfer document moves products from a warehouse of one company to a warehouse of another. It creates the delivery in the sending company and the receipt in the receiving company, priced at the sender's cost. All of this is collected in one Inter-Company Transactions log with the source document, the generated document and the amount.

Who it is for

  • Groups running several companies in one Odoo database. A head office and branches, or a production company and a sales company, that sell to each other and are tired of typing every deal twice.
  • Odoo Community users who need inter company rules. Community has no inter-company rules of its own. This adds them: sales order to purchase order, purchase order to sales order, invoice to vendor bill and vendor bill to invoice.
  • Accountants who want both sides to match. Posting an intercompany invoice creates the vendor bill in the other company with its own journal, accounts and taxes, the invoice number as bill reference, and a link between the two.
  • Warehouse teams moving goods between companies. One Inter-Company Transfer document makes the delivery in the sending company and the receipt in the receiving one, through Odoo's inter-company transit location.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module on Odoo 19. It needs Sales, Purchase, Inventory and Invoicing, and at least two companies in the same database.
  2. Switch to the first company, open Inter-Company > Configuration > Settings and tick Inter-Company Rules. Do the same in every company that should take part.
  3. In each company, tick the documents it should receive: Sales Order -> Purchase Order, Purchase Order -> Sales Order, Customer Invoice -> Vendor Bill and Vendor Bill -> Customer Invoice.
  4. Choose Auto-validate Generated Documents if generated orders should be confirmed and generated invoices posted, or leave it off to get drafts. Pick the Warehouse and the Create Documents As user.
  5. Where taxes differ between companies, add lines under Inter-Company > Configuration > Tax Mapping. Taxes with the same rate and name are matched without a mapping.
  6. Sell or buy using your other company as the customer or vendor. Follow every generated document under Inter-Company > Transactions.

Inter Company Transfer: questions

Does Odoo Community have inter company rules?

No. In Community the Inter-Company Transactions setting only offers an Enterprise upgrade. This module replaces that setting with its own rules, so intercompany transactions work without Enterprise.

Will it work on Enterprise?

It installs on Odoo 19 Enterprise too. Enterprise ships its own inter-company module, so pick one of the two for a set of companies rather than running both.

Can a generated document create another one back?

No. A generated purchase order never makes a sales order in return, and a generated bill never makes an invoice. The generated document shows a banner that says so, which keeps the rules from looping.

What happens if the other side cannot be created?

The source order or invoice still goes through. The error is written to the Transactions log and the source chatter, and you can press Retry on the log once the cause is fixed, for example a product restricted to one company.

How is stock valued on an inter-company transfer?

You choose on the transfer: at the cost of the sending company, or at a price list. The receipt in the other company carries that price.

Does it work between two separate Odoo databases?

No. Both companies must live in the same database, and the other company is recognised by its partner on the order or invoice.
