Bank Reconciliation

The bank reconciliation screen Odoo Community is missing

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Reconcile bank statement lines against open invoices, bills and payments - in full, partly or several at once - write off differences with an account and tax, let reconciliation models do the routine lines, undo anything, and prove it with a bank reconciliation report in PDF and Excel. Bank statements import from CSV, OFX and CAMT.053.

Tick what the transaction pays - one invoice, several, a credit note netted against a receipt, or part of an invoice - add write-off lines for bank charges or payment differences, and validate. The journal entry is written in one balanced step and the documents are reconciled; Undo puts everything back.

Features

Reconciliation models do the routine linesbank fees by label, invoices by reference, amount and partner with a payment tolerance. An auto-reconcile run previews what it would do before anything is written. The bank reconciliation report then shows the balance per bank against the balance per books, item by item.
