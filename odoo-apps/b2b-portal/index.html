B2B Wholesale Portal

Turn your Odoo shop into a purchasing portal for trade customers

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Wholesale buyers know their codes, buy in cases and reorder the same lines every month. Give them a B2B account page, quick order by SKU, agreed contract prices, reorder suggestions, order templates, a trade credit page and a catalogue of their own.

Odoo's shop is built for a consumer buying one item. A trade buyer needs to type forty product codes, order in full cases, see their agreed prices and their credit, and repeat last month's order. This module adds that to the customer portal and the shop.

Products get a minimum quantity, an increment and a case size; quick-order quantities that break them are raised to the nearest allowed quantity and the buyer is told why. Customer agreements hold negotiated prices and committed quantities, and the agreed price is charged on the buyer's cart while the agreement is in force.

In the backend you get B2B customer and product summaries, customer agreements, catalogues per customer or group, order templates, approval rules and credit limit requests to review.

Features

A B2B account page for the buyer
Quick order by product code
The cart charges the agreed price
Agreements and contract prices
Reorder suggestions from order history
Trade credit and limit requests
B2B customers in the backend
Customer agreements
Review credit limit requests
Catalogues per customer or group
