Hijri Calendar, Nepali Date (Bikram Sambat), Ethiopian Calendar

What it does

Each user chooses a secondary calendar in their preferences (or the company sets one for everybody). Every date field then shows the same date in that calendar, and a small calendar button opens a picker in that calendar: choose 1 Ramadan 1448 or 1 Shrawan 2082 and Odoo stores the right Gregorian date.

The date can also be printed under the date of invoices, quotations, sales orders and purchase orders, with Arabic or Devanagari month names and digits. A converter shows any date in all calendars, and the period generator creates the months, quarters and fiscal year of a calendar, such as the Nepali fiscal year (1 Shrawan to the end of Asar), with their journal items.
