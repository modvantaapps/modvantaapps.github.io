Nexi XPay and Nexi Checkout Payment Provider

Take Nexi XPay payments in your Odoo shop - in Italian and English

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The customer pays on the Nexi XPay page and Odoo confirms the order after reading it back from Nexi. Saved cards are charged without another trip to Nexi, card payments can be authorised and captured later, and refunds are sent from Odoo.

The customer is sent to the Nexi page and comes back to the shop; Odoo reads the order from Nexi before confirming. A card saved as an XPay contract is charged directly on the next order. With manual capture, card payments are only authorised and you capture all or part from Odoo; the rest is released. Every call is written to an API log.

A second provider in the same module for shops on Nexi Checkout, the former Nets Easy, with its own secret API key. It does not need an XPay terminal.
