Surgery & Operation Theatre Management

What it does

OT BookingTheatre ScheduleSafety ChecklistAnaesthesia RecordCounts and ImplantsConsumablesSurgery BillingWaiting ListOT Utilisation

The surgery record carries the safety checklist in five phases. The patient cannot be taken into theatre before the pre-operative checks and Sign In are complete (and the consent is given, when the template requires it); the operation cannot start before Time Out and, for limbs and paired organs, the operative side; it cannot end before Sign Out and reconciled swab, needle and instrument counts.

The anaesthetist records the type of anaesthesia, ASA class, Mallampati, airway device, agents given, fluids and the intra-operative vital signs. Implants are scanned from their GS1 UDI barcode. At closure the used consumables and implants leave the theatre's stock location and the operation is charged to the patient's running bill: itemised (surgeon fee, anaesthesia, theatre hours, each material) or as one package price.

Features

Theatre time is booked firsttheatre, patient, operation, surgeon and anaesthetist. The booking is refused when the theatre is under maintenance or overlaps another case, including the cleaning and set-up time between cases, and when the surgeon or anaesthetist is already booked elsewhere. Confirming the booking creates the surgery record from its template.
