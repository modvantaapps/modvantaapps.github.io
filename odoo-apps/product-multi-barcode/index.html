Product Multi Barcode and POS Multi Barcode

Product multi barcode and POS multi barcode: assign multiple barcodes to a single product or variant, scan any barcode in the Point of Sale (POS barcode alias with its own quantity and price), search products by additional barcode in sales, purchase, inventory and invoices, duplicate validation and CSV import

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Multiple barcodesSupplier barcodeCSV importDuplicate checkPOS multi barcodeQuantity per barcode

What it does

Typing or scanning any of those barcodes in a product field - on a quotation, for example - finds the product. A barcode can only be used once, and you can load barcodes in bulk from a CSV file.

Features

A product often carries more than one barcodethe supplier code, an old code, the code on a pack. Odoo stores one. This module adds an Additional Barcodes tab where you list as many as you need, each with a note.
