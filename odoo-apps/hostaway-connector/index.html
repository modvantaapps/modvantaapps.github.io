Odoo Hostaway integration

Hostaway Connector

Hostaway reservations as Odoo sales orders, fees and all.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Hostaway and Odoo 19

What it does

New, modified and cancelled Hostaway reservations come into Odoo with the guest, the stay, fees, taxes, channel commission and balance due. Each booking gets a sales order whose total matches Hostaway. Prices, stay rules and blocked dates go back to the Hostaway calendar.

Enter your Hostaway account ID and an API secret from the Hostaway dashboard and press Connect. Odoo gets the access token itself and keeps it; you never paste a token. Your listings are read and each one gets an Odoo service product.

Reservations come in with the guest and their address, dates, nights, adults, children, infants and pets, the channel (Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, direct and so on), the channel reference, the total, cleaning fee, taxes, channel commission and the balance still due. The sales order puts the fees that are not already inside the total on a fees product and the rest on the listing, one line per night, so a 450 reservation is a 450 order.

You choose which statuses get an order; new and modified do by default. Inquiries, pending requests and owner stays stay out of sales. When a reservation is cancelled, declined or expires in Hostaway, its order is cancelled, unless the invoice is already posted; then the reservation shows a warning.

Guests are matched by email, then phone, only inside the company that owns the Hostaway account.
