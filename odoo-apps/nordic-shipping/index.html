PostNord Shipping and Bring Shipping Integration

PostNord Shipping and Bring Shipping for Odoo 19

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
PostNordBring / PostenService point pickerParcel lockersLabels PDF / ZPLTrackingReturnsPickup booking

What it does

Two Nordic carriers in one module. Print PostNord and Bring labels when you validate a delivery order, let your customers pick a service point or parcel locker at the website checkout, follow every parcel until it is delivered, book pickups and return labels, and collect cash on delivery with Bring - for Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Finland.

Turn on "Customer Chooses the Service Point" and the eCommerce checkout shows the nearest PostNord or Bring points for the delivery address with their opening hours. You can limit the list to manned points or to parcel lockers.

The chosen point is stored on the order. When the delivery order is validated, the parcel is booked to your customer with the point as delivery party (PostNord A7) or pickup point (Bring), so the carrier delivers to the point the customer picked.

A scheduled action reads PostNord Track and Trace and the Bring Tracking API every two hours. Each shipment keeps its event history (in transit, ready at the pickup point, delivered) and every status change is posted on the transfer. "Update Tracking" refreshes on demand.

Inventory - Operations - PostNord / Bring lists every shipment with its status, pickup point, parcels and cash on delivery amount. Filter on parcels on the way, waiting at a pickup point, exceptions or returns.

Features

Validate the delivery orderthe shipment is booked, the label PDF (or ZPL) is attached and posted in the chatter, the tracking reference is filled in and the carrier status is shown. Packages in Odoo become separate parcels of one shipment (multi-parcel).
