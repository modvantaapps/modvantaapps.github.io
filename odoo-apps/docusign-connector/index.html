Odoo DocuSign Connector

Send Odoo documents for signature with DocuSign - and get the signed PDF back on the record

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Add a Send with DocuSign button to quotations, invoices, contacts or any model, choose the recipients and send. Status updates arrive by webhook, the signed document and certificate are attached to the record, and an action can run when everyone has signed.

The connector links your DocuSign account to Odoo. You define signable document types - for example Quotation / Sales Order, Invoice or a contact document - with the PDF report to send, where the signature fields go (anchor text, fixed position or a signature page), and who signs: fields of the document such as the customer and the salesperson, in signing order or all at once.

On the record, Send with DocuSign opens a wizard with the recipients, e-mail subject and message; you can review in DocuSign first or save as a draft. Each envelope is tracked with its status (sent, delivered, completed, declined, voided), who has signed and who is next. When the envelope is completed the signed PDF and the certificate come back to Odoo, and you can void, resend or correct envelopes from Odoo.
