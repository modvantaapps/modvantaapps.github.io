Odoo DocuSign integration

Odoo DocuSign Connector

Send Odoo documents for signature with DocuSign - and get the signed PDF back on the record

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Add a Send with DocuSign button to quotations, invoices, contacts or any model, choose the recipients and send. Quotations need the Odoo Sales app and invoices need the Invoicing app. Status updates arrive by webhook, the signed document and certificate are attached to the record, and an action can run when everyone has signed.

The connector links your DocuSign account to Odoo. You define signable document types - for example Quotation / Sales Order, Invoice or a contact document - with the PDF report to send, where the signature fields go (anchor text, fixed position or a signature page), and who signs: fields of the document such as the customer and the salesperson, in signing order or all at once.

On the record, Send with DocuSign opens a wizard with the recipients, e-mail subject and message; you can review in DocuSign first or save as a draft. Each envelope is tracked with its status (sent, delivered, completed, declined, voided), who has signed and who is next. When the envelope is completed the signed PDF and the certificate come back to Odoo, and you can void, resend or correct envelopes from Odoo.

Who it is for

  • Sales teams closing deals on signed quotes. A salesperson can send the quotation PDF for signature from the quotation itself, see who has signed and who is next, and find the signed copy and certificate attached to the order afterwards.
  • Service and maintenance companies. Businesses that sign service, supply or lease agreements can set a signing order, so the customer signs first and a manager countersigns, with reminders and an expiry date on each envelope.
  • Companies already paying for DocuSign. If your organisation has a DocuSign account, the connector reuses it. Your DocuSign templates can be read into Odoo and used as document types with their roles.
  • HR and admin teams with their own records. Document types aren't tied to sales. Any Odoo model that has a PDF report can get a Send with DocuSign button, with signers taken from fields on that record.

Setting it up

  1. Create an app on the DocuSign Apps and Keys page and note its Integration Key, your User ID and, for JWT, the RSA key.
  2. In Odoo, open DocuSign > Configuration > Connections, create a connection, pick the Environment (Developer or Production) and the Authentication method, and fill in the Keys tab.
  3. Press Grant Consent, then Test Connection. The connection shows the DocuSign account it's connected as.
  4. Under Status Updates choose a webhook per envelope, an account-level Connect configuration, or polling only. Polling also runs as a fallback every 20 minutes.
  5. Go to DocuSign > Configuration > Document Types, choose the Document Model and PDF Report, how Signature Fields are placed, and the Default Recipients with their Signing Order.
  6. Optionally set reminders, expiry and a server action under When Completed, Run, for example to confirm the order once everyone has signed.
  7. Open a record of that model and press Send with DocuSign, check the recipients and message, and send. Track it under DocuSign > Envelopes.

Odoo DocuSign Connector: questions

What do I need on the DocuSign side?

A DocuSign eSignature account and an app created on its Apps and Keys page. The connector supports the JWT Grant with an RSA key and the Authorization Code Grant. A free DocuSign developer account is enough for testing, but envelopes sent there aren't legally binding.

Does it need Odoo Enterprise or Odoo Sign?

No. It depends only on Odoo's mail and web modules, so it runs on Community. It doesn't use or replace the Odoo Sign app. To send quotations or invoices you need the Sales or Invoicing app installed, since the document type points at their report.

Where do the signature fields go on my PDF?

You choose per document type: a signature page added at the end, anchor text printed in your report (for example /sn1/), a fixed page and X/Y position, placing them by hand in DocuSign from a draft, or a DocuSign template that already has them.

What happens if a signer declines or I need to stop an envelope?

A decline is recorded on the envelope with the date and posted in the chatter, and a document type can run a server action When Declined. While an envelope is still out, you can void it, resend it or open it in DocuSign to correct it.

Do I have to open my Odoo to webhooks?

No. Webhooks give faster updates, and you can require an HMAC signature on them. Without a webhook, Odoo polls DocuSign for the status of open envelopes on a schedule.

Can I try it before connecting DocuSign?

Yes. Demo Mode uses a built-in sample DocuSign account, so nothing leaves Odoo. Sample envelopes are created, and Simulate buttons let you play the signer.
