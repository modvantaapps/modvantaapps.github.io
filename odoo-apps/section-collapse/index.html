Collapse Sections

What it does

Long quotations, orders, invoices and bills become easy to read and to build. Collapse a section or collapse all, see the line count and subtotal of every section, insert a line exactly where it belongs, duplicate a line, and copy lines from one document into another - even from a quotation into a purchase order - with Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V.

Collapse, insert and copy paste on every line list with sections.

A caret on every section and subsection of quotations, sales orders, purchase orders, customer invoices and vendor bills folds its lines away. Collapsing a section also folds its subsections.

A small toolbar above the lines opens or folds every section at once and tells you how many lines, sections and collapsed sections the document has.

Each user finds every section as they left it, also after a reload or on another computer. Or open every document fully expanded or fully collapsed - per company, with a personal choice in My Preferences.
