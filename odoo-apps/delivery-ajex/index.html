AJEX Shipping Integration

AJEX Shipping and AJEX courier connector for Odoo: saudi shipping, saudi courier, ksa shipping, saudi arabia shipping. Waybill label PDF or ZPL, tracking, status push, cash on delivery, National Address

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Adds AJEX as a delivery method in Odoo. Validate a delivery order and the AJEX waybill is created, the label lands on the transfer and the tracking number is stored.

Tracking updates every hour and through the AJEX status push, so the whole history is on the transfer. Cash on delivery is filled in from the sales order. Built from the AJEX Order Management API document V2.2.
