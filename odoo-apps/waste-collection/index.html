What it does

Run a waste collection, scrap or recycling business in Odoo: pickup requests from your website, a price list with collection fees and buy back of recyclables, regular collection subscriptions, routes and stop sheets, weighbridge tickets, bins with fill levels, recycling batches that put recovered material into stock, disposal certificates and a customer portal.

A customer asks for a pickup on your website. The request opens a CRM opportunity, you send a quotation and the confirmed order books the pickup. Regular customers get a subscription that creates their pickups every week, every two weeks or every month and is invoiced per period. Each day you plan trips per route, the driver works the stop sheet, records the weight and the customer signature, and the office invoices the fees and buys back the recyclables with a purchase order. Collected material lands in your waste yard, and recycling batches turn it into products you can sell.
