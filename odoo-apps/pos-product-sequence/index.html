POS Product Sequence

Drag product tiles and category buttons into place right on the register. The new POS product order is saved at once, shows up live on the other open registers, and every point of sale can keep its own order.

What it does

POS Product SequencePOS Drag and DropPOS Product OrderPOS Category SequenceOrder per ShopLive on Every RegisterWorks OfflineCopy Order to Shops

Open Actions and press Arrange Products. A bar tells the cashier the register is in arrange mode, and whether the order is for this point of sale only or for every point of sale. Press Done to sell again.

Drag the category buttons the same way. A category only moves between its sister categories. A tap on a category in arrange mode does not open it.

The other open registers that show the same order follow it within a moment, without being reopened. With no connection the new order stays on the register, a badge says it is waiting, and it is saved as soon as the connection is back (while the register stays open). A save the server refuses puts the previous order back.

Arrange Products opens the order of the point of sale as a list with a drag handle; changes there are sent to the open register of that point of sale. Copy Order to Other POS gives other points of sale of the company the same order, and Reset to the Shared Order forgets an own order.

Features

Press a tile and move itthe other tiles make room while you drag. Let go and the new order is stored at once - no save button, no reload. A tap in arrange mode never adds the product to the order.
Shared orderthe register writes the standard POS Sequence of the products and the sequence of the POS categories, the same fields as the backend. Own order: this point of sale keeps its own positions, and products it never arranged keep their shared place. Choose who may arrange: nobody, POS managers, or every POS user.
