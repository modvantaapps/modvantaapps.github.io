Product Price History

Every price you sold or bought a product at, per customer and per vendor, and every change of the product cost with who made it. The last price shows up on the order line while you type the quote.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
What did we charge them last time?

What it does

A customer calls for 10 more chairs. In standard Odoo you open their old orders one by one to find what you gave them in June. Buyers do the same with vendor orders, and nobody can tell you who changed the cost of a product from 86 to 92.

This module writes one history line for each product line of a confirmed sales order, a confirmed purchase order, a posted customer invoice and a posted vendor bill: customer or vendor, date, salesperson or buyer, quantity, unit price, discount, currency, and the net price converted to your company currency. Cancel the order or reset the invoice to draft and its lines leave the history again. Edit a line on a confirmed order and the history follows.

Every change of the product cost is logged too: old cost, new cost, difference in amount and percent, who, when, and where it came from (typed by hand, an import, or the stock valuation of average cost products).

On install it reads the orders and invoices you already have, so the history is full from day one.

Features

Sales price history on the product
Purchase price history per vendor
Cost price history
Last sale price on the quotation line
Recent prices popup
Reorder from the customer price history
Filter and group the history
Price trend graph
Last purchase price on the RFQ
Settings per company
Excel price history report
