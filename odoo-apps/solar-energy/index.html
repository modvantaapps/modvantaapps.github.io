Solar Energy Management

Run a solar installation company on Odoo: from the first enquiry to the site survey, the system design with a yield and payback estimate, the quotation, the installation project, the commissioning certificate, the grid connection paperwork, the warranty registry and the maintenance contract. One module, built on Odoo's own CRM, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Project, Maintenance and Website.

What it does

survey, design, install, commissioning, aftercare

Everything a solar installer needs, from the first call to the tenth maintenance visit.

Turn a CRM opportunity into a solar project in one click (or create the opportunity from the project). Customer category, property type, service type (on-grid, hybrid, off-grid, ground mount, carport, battery retrofit), lead source, site address and coordinates, utility account and contracted load. Kanban pipeline from New to Operational.

Appointment with the survey team (email to the customer, activity for the surveyor), roof type, material, condition and age, roof and usable area, tilt, orientation and azimuth, mounting, shading level and loss, electrical panel, earthing and cable runs, 12 months of bills, checklist, site photos, findings and customer signature. PDF report.

Components from your own products (panel Wp and area, inverter kW, battery kWh, warranty years) or from ready kits. kWp, panel count, DC/AC ratio, storage, panel area, price, cost, margin and price per Wp. Warnings for DC/AC ratio, roof space and contracted load. Several options per project (good / better / best).

Who it is for

  • Solar installers and EPC contractors. If you sell and fit rooftop, ground-mount or carport PV systems, this is solar installation management in Odoo: one record per job from the first enquiry through survey, design, quotation, installation and commissioning.
  • A solar company that wants one ERP instead of spreadsheets. Surveys, system designs, yield estimates and the grid paperwork usually live in separate files. Here they sit next to the CRM opportunity, the sales order, the purchase orders and the installation tasks, all in standard Odoo apps.
  • Installers who also sell battery storage and hybrid systems. Designs take panel, inverter and battery products or a ready kit. The battery mounting task is only added on systems that include storage, and the design shows the backup hours.
  • Teams that earn money after the handover. Warranties are registered per serial number at commissioning, and O&M maintenance contracts plan their own visits as Odoo maintenance requests and invoice each period.

Features

A documented formulakWp x peak sun hours x 365 x performance ratio x (1 - shading loss). Specific yield, coverage of the consumption, year 1 and 25-year savings with degradation and tariff escalation, payback after incentives, CO2 avoided and battery backup hours. A one-click sizing assistant fits the panel count to the target coverage and the roof.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It pulls in CRM, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Project, Maintenance, Invoicing and Website, and creates checklist templates, installation task templates, service types and property types.
  2. Open Solar > Configuration > Settings and enter your default peak sun hours, performance ratio, electricity tariff, tariff escalation, panel degradation and grid emission factor.
  3. On each panel, inverter and battery product, open the Solar tab and set the Solar Component type with its rating (Panel Power (Wp), Inverter AC Power (kW) or Battery Capacity (kWh)) and its warranty years.
  4. Build ready-made packages under Solar > Configuration > System Kits, and create your survey, installation and QA teams under Teams.
  5. From a CRM opportunity, use the Solar Project smart button to open the solar project, then press Plan Site Survey. On the survey, Add 12 Months gives you a row per month for the bills.
  6. On the system design, press Load Kit or Size System, check the yield and payback tab, then press Create Quotation. Confirming the quotation opens the installation project with its tasks.
  7. If you want leads from your website, keep Website solar calculator ticked in Settings and set the indicative price per kWp. The public page is /solar.

Solar Energy Management: questions

Does it run on Odoo Community?

Yes. It is built for Odoo 19 and runs on Community or Enterprise. It only uses standard apps (CRM, Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Project, Maintenance, Invoicing, Website) and needs no API key or outside calculation service.

Where do the yield figures come from?

From a formula you can check: kWp x peak sun hours x 365 x performance ratio x (1 - shading loss). Peak sun hours come from the survey or the company default. The module does not fetch irradiance data from PVGIS or any other online source, so enter the value for your region.

Does it draw the panel layout on the roof?

No. There is no roof drawing or 3D layout tool. The design checks whether the panel area fits the usable roof area from the survey and warns when it does not, along with warnings for the DC/AC ratio and the contracted load.

Can I offer the customer more than one option?

Yes. Make several designs on one project and label them, for example Basic, Standard and Premium. When the customer confirms one quotation, that design is accepted and the other open options are rejected.

How does commissioning work?

Commissioning and QA holds the safety, quality and commissioning checklists plus string tests (expected and measured Voc, Isc, insulation resistance, polarity) and earth resistance. Pass and Issue Certificate is refused while items are open or a value is out of range. A pass numbers and emails the certificate and registers the warranties.

Can customers and subcontractors log in?

Yes, through the Odoo portal. Customers see progress, their PDFs, warranties, the maintenance contract, and can raise service requests or enter meter readings. Subcontractors can accept work orders and report the work done, and you then press Create Bill for the vendor bill.
