Customer Referral Program

Customer referral program: unique referral links that give the referred friend an automatic discount on their first purchase, with referral counts per customer

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Referral programReferral linkRefer a friendFirst order discount

What it does

Every customer gets a referral link; friends get a discount on their first order

Signed-in customers open /referral to get their own link. A friend who arrives through it and shops gets an automatic Referral Discount line in the cart on their first purchase - a percentage or a fixed amount you set.

When the friend's order is confirmed, the referral is counted on the referrer's code and they get an email. Self-referrals and repeat customers are excluded. The module does not issue a reward to the referrer by itself; use the counts to reward them your way.
