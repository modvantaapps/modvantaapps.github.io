Courier Management System

Courier Management and Parcel Management for a courier company: courier requests with sender and receiver, weight, dimension and distance price rules, Courier Tracking number with status timeline, Website Courier Tracking page, delivery agents, pickup and delivery runs, proof of delivery signature, courier invoice, customer portal, rating and courier dashboard

Courier ManagementParcel ManagementCourier TrackingProof of DeliveryDelivery AgentsPickup and Delivery RunsCourier InvoiceCustomer Portal

What it does

Book a parcel for a sender and a receiver, list the pieces with weight and size, and the charge is worked out from your own weight, dimension and distance price rules. Every request gets a request number and a tracking number, and moves through stages you define: New Request, Collected, Dispatched, Out for Delivery, Delivered, Returned or Cancelled.

Every stage change is written to a status timeline with the date and the parcel location. Agents get pickup and delivery runs with a printable run sheet; at the door you take the receiver signature and a photo as proof of delivery. Then invoice the sender in one click, and let customers follow the parcel on your website or in their portal.
