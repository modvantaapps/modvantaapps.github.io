Lightspeed Retail Connector

Every Lightspeed till sale in Odoo: delivered, invoiced and paid.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Lightspeed Retail (X-Series) and Odoo 19

What it does

Connect your Lightspeed Retail X-Series store with your own developer app or a personal token. Closed sales come into Odoo as confirmed sale orders, the stock leaves the outlet's warehouse, and each payment lands in the journal of its payment type. Products, customers and stock levels come across too.

The module reads closed sales every 15 minutes, and straight away when Lightspeed calls the sale webhook. Each one becomes a sale order numbered after the receipt, for the named customer or your walk-in contact, from the outlet's warehouse and sales team.

Lines use the Odoo tax you mapped to each Lightspeed tax. If the receipt total differs by a few cents (rounding, surcharges), a rounding line makes up the difference, so the invoice matches the till to the cent.

With invoicing on, the order is invoiced and each Lightspeed payment line (cash, EFTPOS, card) is registered in its journal. Voided sales cancel the order while it is not delivered or invoiced; otherwise someone gets a to-do.

Features

Lightspeed can stay the stock masterswitch on "Copy stock into Odoo" and the on-hand quantities of each mapped outlet are set to the Lightspeed inventory levels.
