POS Jewellery

Gold billing at the till: every piece is priced by the server from its weight and the rate of the day, and old gold is taken as a payment.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
POS jewelleryJewelry POSGold rate billingMaking chargesOld gold exchange

What it does

A jewellery counter cannot sell from a fixed price list: the price of a piece moves with the metal rate. This module lets managers publish the rate of the day and lets the till price each tagged piece from its own weight, charges and today's rate, with the breakup on the receipt.

Features

Metals and puritiesyour metals (gold, silver, platinum...) and purities with their fineness in parts per thousand.
Rate of the daysell and buy rate per gram per purity and company; a published rate cannot be changed.
Derive by finenesstype one purity's rate and fill the others in proportion to their fineness, then adjust and publish.
Rate validitythe till refuses to price jewellery when the latest rate is older than you allow (default: today only).
Tagged pieceseach piece is a product with purity, gross and stone weight, net weight, wastage %, making charge, stone value, other charges and hallmark ID (HUID).
Making chargeper gram of net weight, percent of the metal value, or a fixed amount.
Server pricing at the tillthe till asks the server for the price at today's rate; pieces not in stock or without a required hallmark ID are refused.
One piece per linequantity stays 1 and the same piece cannot be added twice.
Price breakup on the receiptpurity, net weight, rate, wastage, making, stones and HUID under each piece.
Metal Rates buttonthe rates in force, at the till.
Old gold exchangea payment method that asks purity, gross weight, less weight and melting loss and credits the value at the buy rate.
Server re-checkevery synced order is re-priced; wrong prices, quantities, double sales or over-credited old metal are flagged on the order.
Old metal registerevery old metal intake with weights, fine weight, server value and amount credited.
