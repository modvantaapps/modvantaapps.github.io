POS Stock Alert

See stock on every product card at the till - and stop selling what you do not have

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Each product card in the point of sale shows the on-hand quantity, coloured for in stock, low and out of stock. With Refuse the sale, an order that would sell more than is on hand is refused by the server when it is saved.

A standard Odoo till shows no stock and stops nothing: the same last unit can be sold twice on a busy day, and the cashier only finds out when the customer is waiting for an item that is not there.

This module prints the quantity on hand on each product card - for example 12 in stock, 3 in stock, 0 in stock - with a separate colour for low stock (under a threshold you set) and for out of stock.

The Selling Below Zero policy has three choices: Allow, say nothing; Allow, but warn the cashier; or Refuse the sale. With the policy Refuse the sale, the server checks each order when it is saved against the stock at that moment and refuses it when a storable product would be sold beyond what is on hand. Categories you exempt are never blocked.

Who it is for

  • Small retail shops with thin stock. When there are only a few of something left, cashiers need to see that before they promise it. Each product card at the till shows the on-hand quantity, with a different colour once it drops below your low-stock figure or hits zero.
  • Counters selling last units or serialised goods. If a sale must not go ahead without stock, the Refuse the sale policy makes the server reject the order when it's saved, naming the product, the quantity sold and the quantity on hand.
  • Cafés and food counters that make to order. Dishes built from ingredients you don't count per portion shouldn't be blocked. List their POS categories under Never Block These Categories and the refusal skips them.
  • Shops with a stockroom behind the floor. Some shops prefer a heads-up rather than a block. The warn policy shows the cashier a Low stock notification when an order asks for more than is on hand, and the sale still goes through.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It needs Point of Sale and Inventory.
  2. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Point of Sales and open the till you want to set up.
  3. Tick Show Stock on Products so each product card shows its on-hand quantity. It's off by default.
  4. Set Low Stock Threshold to the quantity where the badge should change colour. The default is 5.
  5. Choose a Selling Below Zero policy: Allow, say nothing, Allow, but warn the cashier, or Refuse the sale.
  6. Add any categories that must never be refused under Never Block These Categories.
  7. Leave Stock Counted From empty to use the till's own source location, or pick another location if your shop floor stock lives elsewhere.

POS Stock Alert: questions

Is the block done in the browser or on the server?

On the server. The till's stock figure is a snapshot from when the session opened, so the refusal is checked against current stock when the order is saved. The badge on the card is there to inform the cashier.

Will it block refunds or services?

No. Only storable products are checked. Refund lines have a negative quantity and are skipped, since a refund adds stock back.

Does showing stock slow down the till?

Quantities are only loaded when Show Stock on Products is ticked. On a very large catalogue that adds some time when the session opens, which is why it's a setting and off by default.

Does the warning stop the cashier?

No. With Allow, but warn the cashier, a notification appears and the sale carries on. Only Refuse the sale stops an order.

Which Odoo edition do I need?

Community or Enterprise. It depends on the standard Point of Sale and Inventory apps.
