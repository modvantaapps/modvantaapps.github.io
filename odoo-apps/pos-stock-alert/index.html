POS Stock Alert

See stock on every product card at the till - and stop selling what you do not have

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Each product card in the point of sale shows the on-hand quantity, coloured for in stock, low and out of stock. With Refuse the sale, an order that would sell more than is on hand is refused by the server when it is saved.

A standard Odoo till shows no stock and stops nothing: the same last unit can be sold twice on a busy day, and the cashier only finds out when the customer is waiting for an item that is not there.

This module prints the quantity on hand on each product card - for example 12 in stock, 3 in stock, 0 in stock - with a separate colour for low stock (under a threshold you set) and for out of stock.

With the policy Refuse the sale, the server checks each order when it is saved against the stock at that moment and refuses it when a storable product would be sold beyond what is on hand. Categories you exempt are never blocked.

Features

Settings per point of sale
Stock on every product card
