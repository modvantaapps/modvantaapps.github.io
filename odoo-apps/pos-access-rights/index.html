Odoo 19 app

POS Access Rights

Decide what each user and each employee may do at the till - and let the server check it again

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Rules per user or per employee, for all shops or chosen ones: no discounts, no price changes, no refunds, no deleting orders or lines, no cash in/out, no closing, no back office, and in restaurants no changes to what the kitchen already has. The till hides or refuses it, and the server checks the saved orders again.

Odoo's Point of Sale gives every cashier the same buttons. This module takes away the ones a person should not use, rule by rule.

A rule applies to a user, or to an employee on a till that logs in with employees (one shared login, many cashiers). An employee linked to a user also gets that user's rules.

Blocked buttons are hidden or refused at the till. The server checks again what the saved order shows: discounts, prices under the pricelist, refunds, payments and payment methods, invoices, tips, pricelist and tax changes, quotations, lines already sent to the kitchen, deleting saved orders, cash moves and closing. A violation is refused, or accepted and flagged for review.

Features

Also includedpayment methods per user and per customer, a default payment method, barcode-only product adding, an IP whitelist and a GPS geofence per point of sale.
