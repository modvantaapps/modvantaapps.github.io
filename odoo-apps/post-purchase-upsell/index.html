Post-Purchase One-Click Upsell

One more sale on the thank-you page - added to the order in one click

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Right after checkout, the order confirmation page shows an offer that fits what the customer just bought, at a discount, with a countdown. One click adds it to the same order - no new cart, no second checkout - and every offer tracks impressions, acceptances and revenue.

The moment after a purchase is when a customer is most willing to buy again, but Odoo's confirmation page only says thank you.

This module adds post-purchase offers. Each offer has a product, a discount (percentage, fixed amount or none), targeting rules (trigger products or categories, minimum and maximum order value, pricelists, website, start and end date, acceptance limit) and its own heading, text, button label and countdown.

When the confirmation page loads, the highest-priority offer the order qualifies for opens in a popup. Accepting adds the product to the order at the offer price, with the product's taxes. If the order is already invoiced, the product goes on a follow-on order with a link to pay for it instead.
