Slovenia FURS Fiscal Verification for POS and Invoices

Slovenia FURS fiscal verification for Odoo 19: ZOI, EOR and QR code for Point of Sale orders and cash invoices, with your own certificate

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
What was tested and what you still need

What it does

Each paid POS order and each invoice on the journals you choose is reported to FURS in real time, signed with your own certificate.

The ZOI is computed first, so the receipt can always be printed.

The QR code and the 60 digit code on the POS receipt and on the invoice PDF.

Business premises and devices registered at FURS, and closed.

Who it is for

  • Slovenian shops and restaurants on Odoo POS. Businesses that take cash or card at a till in Slovenia generally have to report each receipt to the financial administration (FURS) for fiscal verification. The module does this from Odoo when an order is paid and prints the fiscal data on the POS receipt.
  • Service companies issuing cash invoices. If some invoices are paid in cash or by card rather than by bank transfer, you link a FURS device to the journal used for them. Invoices on that journal are verified when they're posted, and other journals are left alone.
  • Businesses with several premises or a market stall. Each business premise is set up in Odoo as real estate, with its cadastral data and address, or as a movable premise of type A, B or C. Every premise holds its own electronic devices, and each device keeps its own gapless numbering.
  • Shops with a weak internet line. The protective mark (ZOI) is computed in Odoo before FURS is contacted, so a receipt can still be printed when FURS doesn't answer. The record waits in a queue and is sent again later with the subsequent-submit flag.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It needs Point of Sale and the Slovenian accounting localization (l10n_si), and the company must have a valid 8-digit Slovenian VAT number.
  2. As an administrator, go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings, find the Slovenia: FURS block and set the FURS Environment, upload the FURS Certificate (.p12) with its password, fill in the Software Supplier Tax Number or the Foreign Software Supplier Name, and press Read Certificate.
  3. In the test environment, paste the test server's public certificate into FURS Test Server Certificate (PEM), then press Test Connection (Echo).
  4. Open Point of Sale > FURS > Business Premises, create a premise with its Premise ID and type, add its devices on the Electronic Devices tab, and press Register at FURS.
  5. On each user who issues receipts or invoices, open the FURS tab and enter the FURS Operator Tax Number, or tick Foreign Operator.
  6. Back in the POS settings, choose the Electronic device for each point of sale. For invoices, set FURS Device on the sales journal under Accounting > Configuration > Journals.
  7. On sales taxes that aren't plain VAT, set the FURS Amount Type (for example Exempt from VAT or Reverse Charge). Check the result under FURS > Fiscal Invoices with the Without EOR filter.

Slovenia FURS Fiscal Verification for POS and Invoices: questions

Is this module certified or approved by FURS?

No. It was written from the published FURS technical specification and checked against it in automated tests, but no authority has approved it. Fiscal verification rules depend on your business and can change, so confirm your obligations with your accountant or with FURS before going live.

What happens when FURS is down or slow?

The fiscal number and ZOI are created first, so the receipt prints with them and a line saying the EOR will be obtained later. Temporary failures leave the record waiting, and a scheduled action tries again every five minutes, marking it as sent subsequently. Refusals with a real error code are kept as Refused by FURS with the message, and you can send again after fixing the cause.

What if the operator tax number or premise registration is missing?

The sale itself goes through. The POS order gets a FURS tab showing the problem and a Try again button, and the receipt states that it isn't fiscally verified. For invoices, posting continues and a note on the chatter explains what to fix before pressing Verify at FURS.

How are returns and refunds handled?

A POS return or a credit note refers to the original fiscal invoice when one exists. A verified invoice can't be reset to draft or cancelled, so the correction has to be a credit note.

Are there limits I should know about?

Fiscal invoices must be in euro, and each order or invoice line may carry only one tax. The pre-numbered paper invoice book isn't supported, and bulk sending of old invoices isn't included. The till needs to reach the Odoo server for the receipt to show its fiscal data.

Does it work with multiple companies?

Yes. Premises, devices and fiscal invoices are restricted per company, and each company has its own environment and certificate settings.
