Slovenia FURS Fiscal Verification for POS and Invoices

Slovenia FURS fiscal verification for Odoo 19: ZOI, EOR and QR code for Point of Sale orders and cash invoices, with your own certificate

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
What was tested and what you still need

What it does

Each paid POS order and each invoice on the journals you choose is reported to FURS in real time, signed with your own certificate.

The ZOI is computed first, so the receipt can always be printed.

The QR code and the 60 digit code on the POS receipt and on the invoice PDF.

Business premises and devices registered at FURS, and closed.
