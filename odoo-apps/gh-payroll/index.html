Ghana Payroll

Ghana Payroll for Odoo 19: PAYE with the September 2026 Bands, SSNIT Tier 1 and Tier 2 and the GRA DT 0107A Schedule

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Ghanaian statutory payroll on Odoo Community: GRA PAYE on the Act 1178 bands from 1 September 2026 and the earlier 2026 bands before, SSNIT 5.5% and 13% with the insurable earnings limits, Tier 1 and Tier 2, the optional third tier, bonus and overtime final tax, personal reliefs, and the DT 0107A schedule in the GRA uploadable CSV layout with the SSNIT schedules.

First GHS 588 nil, then 5%, 10%, 17.5%, 25%, 30% and 35% above GHS 50,000 a month from 1 September 2026.

Payslips of January to August 2026 use the Act 1111 bands automatically.

Optional employee and employer rates; the employee part is relieved up to 16.5% of basic salary.

Bonus up to 15% of annual basic at 5%, the rest taxed with salary; junior staff overtime at 5% / 10%.
