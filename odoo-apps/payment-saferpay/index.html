Saferpay Payment Gateway

Accept Worldline Saferpay payments in Odoo: cards, TWINT, PostFinance Pay, PayPal and the other payment means of your Saferpay terminal, on the website checkout and in the customer portal. Automatic or manual capture, partial captures, void, partial refunds and saved cards, in test or production.

What it does

Saferpay JSON APIPayment PageTWINTPostFinance PayVisa / Mastercard / AmexPayPalManual and partial captureVoidPartial refundsSaved cards (Secure Card Data)NotificationsGerman, French, Italian

The module adds Saferpay as a payment provider in Odoo. Enter your customer ID, terminal ID and JSON API user, press Test Connection and Sync Payment Methods: Odoo reads the payment means of your terminal and switches on the matching Odoo payment methods. At the checkout the customer chooses card, TWINT, PostFinance Pay or PayPal and pays on the Saferpay Payment Page. When the customer comes back, and when Saferpay calls the notification URL, Odoo asks Saferpay for the result (Assert) and confirms the order. Card payments are captured at once or kept as authorizations that you capture in full or in parts, or void. Refunds are sent from the payment in Odoo, also partially. Customers can save their card and pay with it later.
