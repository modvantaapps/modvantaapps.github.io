Public Holidays for 250 Countries

The public holidays of 250 countries and territories in Odoo Time Off: pick a country and a region, the current year and the years ahead are loaded and kept up to date.

What it does

Public holidays change every year and every country, state and province has its own. Without them, Time Off counts a closed day as a working day and the working schedule plans work on it. This module ships a built-in public holiday calendar for 250 countries and territories and 626 regions (German states, Spanish communities, US states, Indian states, Swiss cantons and many more) and writes the days into the company public holidays of Odoo Time Off. A monthly scheduled action adds the new years and corrects holidays whose date moved. No external service, no API key: it works on servers without internet access.

250 countries and territories, 626 regions from a built-in calendar for 2024 to 2036; other years come from the python holidays package when it is installed on the server.

Public holidays, plus bank holidays, optional (restricted) holidays, half days and government holidays where the country has them; the form only offers the types the country publishes.

Names in English, in the local language or both (for example Neujahr (New Year's Day)).

Features

For the whole company or one working scheduleclose every schedule of the company, or only the schedule of an office in another country.
Monthly updatenew years added, moved holidays corrected, withdrawn holidays removed, every change posted in the chatter.
Your own holidays stay untoucheda day already covered by a public holiday (typed by hand or from another source) is left alone, so Odoo never refuses an overlap.
Local civil daythe holiday covers the day in the timezone of the working schedule, stored in UTC as Odoo expects.
Calendar eventsoptionally an all-day event in the Odoo Calendar for every holiday, shown to the internal users of the company (no invitation e-mails).
Time off durations followemployees' time off over a new public holiday is recomputed by Odoo (one day less).
