Odoo plentymarkets integration

plentymarkets Connector

plentymarkets orders in Odoo, stock and prices back to plentymarkets

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
plentymarkets REST APIPlentyONEMulti-client and referrers

What it does

Retailers who sell through plentymarkets but run stock, purchasing or accounting in Odoo keep two systems in step by hand. This connector logs in to the plentymarkets REST API, imports the orders in the status you choose with their items, addresses, VAT, coupons and shipping costs, sends Odoo stock and sales prices to the plentymarkets variations, and reports the shipping package with the tracking number and the new order status when the delivery is validated.

Who it is for

  • Multichannel retailers on plentymarkets (PlentyONE). Orders from the webshop, Amazon, eBay, Otto and other referrers come into Odoo by status range, with their items, VAT rates, billing and delivery addresses, coupons and shipping costs.
  • Businesses running several clients in one plentymarkets system. Set Client (Plenty ID) to limit a connection to one store, or leave it at 0 to import every client. On the Referrers tab each referrer can get its own sales team, or Do Not Import to keep it out of Odoo.
  • Warehouses that count stock in Odoo. Odoo free quantity minus the Stock Buffer is written as a stock correction to the plentymarkets warehouse ID you set, 100 variations per call. With Push Prices on, the gross price goes to the plentymarkets sales price you choose.
  • Shippers who need tracking back in plentymarkets. When an Odoo delivery is validated, Odoo creates the shipping package with the tracking number and sets the Status After Shipment, 7 by default.

Features

Orders by statusPick a status range to import (for example 5 to 5.99, cleared for shipping). Orders are read by their last change, so status changes arrive too.
Gross prices and VATItem prices come in with their VAT rate; coupons, surcharges and shipping costs become their own lines and the order total matches plentymarkets.
Multi-client and referrersLimit a connection to one client (Plenty ID). Every order referrer (webshop, Amazon, Otto...) can have its own sales team or be skipped.
VariationsVariations are matched to Odoo products by variation number or barcode.
Stock correctionsOdoo free quantity (minus a buffer) is sent as a stock correction for your plentymarkets warehouse, in batches of 100.
Sales pricesThe gross Odoo price is written to the sales price you pick.
Shipping package and statusValidating the delivery creates the shipping package with the tracking number and sets the status after shipment.
CancellationsOrders that reach a cancelled status are cancelled in Odoo; cancelling from Odoo sets the cancelled status in plentymarkets.

Setting it up

  1. In plentymarkets, create a back end user with REST API rights for orders, items and stock.
  2. In Odoo, open Sales > plentymarkets > Systems, create a system with the System URL (for example https://p12345.my.plentysystems.com), the REST User and the REST Password, then press Test Connection.
  3. Set Import From Status and Import Up To Status (5 to 5.99 by default), the cancelled range, and the Status After Shipment.
  4. Enter the plentymarkets Warehouse ID, the Sales Price ID and the Shipping Package ID, and pick the Odoo warehouse and optional Stock Buffer.
  5. Press Read Referrers and give each referrer a sales team or tick Do Not Import.
  6. Press Import Variations, then open Sales > plentymarkets > Variations and link by hand any variation that didn't match a product.
  7. Press Import Orders once to check the result. The schedule then imports orders every 15 minutes and pushes stock and prices every hour.

plentymarkets Connector: questions

Is it for Odoo Community or Enterprise?

Both, on Odoo 19. It depends on Sales, Inventory with delivery, Invoicing and Discuss, which are in Community.

How does Odoo sign in to plentymarkets?

It posts the REST user and password to /rest/login and keeps the token it gets back. When the token runs out, Odoo logs in again. The token is never written to the API Log.

How are variations matched to products?

First by variation number against the Odoo internal reference, then by barcode. Variations with no match stay unlinked until you pick the product yourself.

Does it use webhooks?

No. The plentymarkets REST API offers no way to register webhooks, so Odoo reads orders changed since the last run on a schedule.

What happens when an order is cancelled?

Orders that reach the cancelled status range are cancelled in Odoo. Pressing Cancel in plentymarkets on an Odoo order sets the Cancelled From Status in plentymarkets and cancels the Odoo order.

Can I try it without a live system?

Yes. Sample Mode answers every call with sample data and sends nothing to plentymarkets. The API Log keeps calls for 30 days by default, set by Delete API Log After (days).
