POS Navbar Tabs

Every preset and every pricelist of the Point of Sale as a tab in the navbar: one tap switches dine in, take away or delivery, or re-prices the order.

What it does

POS presets in the navbar and POS pricelist tabs: every preset (dine in, takeout, delivery) and every pricelist of the Point of Sale as a tab next to the order tabs. One tap switches the preset or the pricelist in POS, the active one highlighted, per shop.

Features

Presets as tabsone button per available preset next to the order tabs, the active one in the colour of the preset.
Standard preset flowa tap applies the preset like Odoo does: its pricelist and fiscal position, and the customer, address or time slot it asks for.
Pricelists as tabsone button per available pricelist (and Default Price when the shop has none): one tap re-prices the order.
Always in syncswitching a preset that carries a pricelist highlights that pricelist tab too.
Preset imagesthe image of a preset is shown on its tab (option).
Hide the preset buttonthe tabs can replace the preset button under the order.
Product screen or every screenby default the tabs are shown where the order is edited; a paid order is never changed from them.
Respects cashier rightscashiers with minimal rights get no pricelist tabs, like the standard pricelist button.
Per Point of Saleeach shop chooses preset tabs, pricelist tabs, or both.
