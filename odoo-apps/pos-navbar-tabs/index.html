POS Navbar Tabs

Every preset and every pricelist of the Point of Sale as a tab in the navbar: one tap switches dine in, take away or delivery, or re-prices the order.

What it does

POS presets in the navbar and POS pricelist tabs: every preset (dine in, takeout, delivery) and every pricelist of the Point of Sale as a tab next to the order tabs. One tap switches the preset or the pricelist in POS, the active one highlighted, per shop.

Who it is for

  • Restaurants and cafes using presets. A counter that takes dine-in, take-away and delivery orders can switch between them from a row of tabs beside the order tabs, instead of opening the preset button and picking from a list.
  • Shops with member or trade price lists. If a cashier often moves an order to a member or wholesale pricelist, a tab for each available pricelist re-prices the order in one tap and shows which one is active.
  • Tills on tablets. The tabs sit in the navbar and scroll sideways when a shop has many presets or pricelists, so they stay usable on a narrow screen.
  • Groups with different shops in one database. Each point of sale has its own options, so a restaurant can show preset tabs while a retail shop shows only pricelist tabs, or neither.

Features

Presets as tabsone button per available preset next to the order tabs, the active one in the colour of the preset.
Standard preset flowa tap applies the preset like Odoo does: its pricelist and fiscal position, and the customer, address or time slot it asks for.
Pricelists as tabsone button per available pricelist (and Default Price when the shop has none): one tap re-prices the order.
Always in syncswitching a preset that carries a pricelist highlights that pricelist tab too.
Preset imagesthe image of a preset is shown on its tab (option).
Hide the preset buttonthe tabs can replace the preset button under the order.
Product screen or every screenby default the tabs are shown where the order is edited; a paid order is never changed from them.
Respects cashier rightscashiers with minimal rights get no pricelist tabs, like the standard pricelist button.
Per Point of Saleeach shop chooses preset tabs, pricelist tabs, or both.

Setting it up

  1. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings and select the point of sale at the top.
  2. Switch on Odoo's own Presets and/or Flexible Pricelists for that point of sale and choose which presets and pricelists are available. The tabs only show what is available there.
  3. Find the Navbar Tabs setting and tick Presets as tabs, Pricelists as tabs, or both.
  4. Optionally tick Hide the preset button under the order, Show preset images on the tabs, or Show the tabs on every screen (not only the product screen), then save.
  5. Open the register. The tabs appear next to the order tabs, and tapping one switches the preset or pricelist of the current order.

POS Navbar Tabs: questions

Is this Community or Enterprise?

Both. It depends only on the Point of Sale app and adds no backend menus, just settings on the point of sale and the tabs in the register.

Does a preset tab do anything different from Odoo's preset button?

No. A tap calls Odoo's standard preset switch, so the preset's pricelist and fiscal position are applied and Odoo still asks for a customer, address or time slot when the preset requires one.

Can a paid order be changed from the tabs?

No. Once an order is finalized the tabs ignore taps, so a paid ticket can't be moved to another preset or re-priced from there.

Will every cashier see the pricelist tabs?

Cashiers with the minimal role don't get pricelist tabs, which matches how Odoo treats its own pricelist button. Preset tabs are shown to everyone working that register.

What if the shop has no default pricelist?

Then a Default Price tab is added next to the pricelists so the cashier can go back to standard prices.

Do I have to remove the standard preset button?

No. Hiding it is a separate option. Leave it unticked and both the button and the tabs are available.
