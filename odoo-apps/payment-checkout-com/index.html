Checkout.com Payment Gateway

Take Checkout.com payments in your Odoo shop, invoices and portal

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The customer pays on the Checkout.com Hosted Payments Page and Odoo confirms the order only after reading the payment back from Checkout.com. Save cards, authorise now and capture later, refund from Odoo and see every API call in a log.

The module adds Checkout.com as a payment provider on the standard Odoo payment framework, so it works wherever Odoo takes online payments: the eCommerce checkout, invoices paid from the customer portal and payment links. The customer is sent to the Checkout.com Hosted Payments Page, where the card is typed and 3-D Secure happens; card numbers never reach Odoo.

When the customer comes back, and when a webhook arrives, Odoo reads the payment from Checkout.com and checks its reference, amount and currency before the order is confirmed. Captures and refunds are sent from the normal Odoo buttons, and every call, answer and webhook is written to an API log.
