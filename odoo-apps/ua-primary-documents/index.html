Ukraine Primary Documents

Ukrainian primary documents from Odoo: act of completed works, invoice for payment, invoice-waybill and delivery note, with the requisites the Law on accounting requires.

What it does

Ukrainian companies document every business operation with a primary document. This module prints the four documents used every day from customer invoices, sales orders and delivery orders, in Ukrainian, with the mandatory requisites of Article 9 of the Law of Ukraine on accounting and financial reporting: document name, date, company, content and volume of the operation with units, positions and names of the responsible persons, and their signatures.

Features

Four documentsAct of completed works (services), invoice for payment, invoice-waybill and delivery note, each as a printable PDF report.
From invoices, orders and deliveriesCustomer invoices: all four. Sales orders: invoice for payment, act and delivery note. Delivery orders: delivery note with the prices of the sales order.
Requisites of Article 9Supplier and buyer with EDRPOU code, IPN, address and IBAN, document number and date, lines with units, quantities and prices, positions and names of the signers on both sides.
Amount in wordsTotal and VAT in words in Ukrainian, with correct genders and plural forms of hryvnia and kopiyka.
VAT payer or notVAT payer layout (without VAT, VAT, total with VAT), single tax payer with group, or non VAT payer.
ContractsContracts with partners (number, date, validity) printed as the basis of each document; the active contract is picked automatically and passed from the order to the invoice.
Signers and power of attorneyThe director by default or another signer per document, the buyer signer from the contact, power of attorney number, date and person on delivery notes.
Signature and stampOptional signature and stamp images printed on the documents.
