NETOPIA Payments Gateway

Card payments through NETOPIA Payments in Odoo 19, confirmed by a notification Odoo can verify

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
NETOPIA PaymentsNetopia MobilPayCardRONAPI v2Romania

What it does

Plata cu cardul prin NETOPIA Payments, direct din magazinul Odoo, din facturi si din linkuri de plata. Clientul introduce cardul pe pagina NETOPIA, nu in Odoo.

Adds NETOPIA Payments (API v2) as a payment provider. Odoo starts the payment, sends the customer to the NETOPIA card page, and marks the order or invoice paid when the notification from NETOPIA passes the key check, or when Odoo reads the status from NETOPIA itself.

Features

NETOPIA hosted card pagethe customer types the card on NETOPIA; Odoo only sends the order, never card data
Notification checkthe Verification-token is an RS512 token checked with your NETOPIA public key; the issuer, your POS signature and a SHA-512 hash of the body must all match
Amount and currency checka paid notification for another amount or currency puts the payment in error instead of confirming it
Status on returnwhen the customer comes back, Odoo asks NETOPIA for the status; it does not trust the browser
Scheduled follow-uppayments still open after a few minutes are read again from NETOPIA, so a missed notification does not leave an order unpaid
Sandbox and liveTest mode talks to the NETOPIA sandbox, Enabled to the live platform
Refund flagrefunds are made in the NETOPIA admin; when NETOPIA reports the credit, the transaction is flagged
API logevery call and notification, with the API key, POS signature and customer contact details masked
