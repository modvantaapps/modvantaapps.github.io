Invoice Stock Move

Invoice Stock Move - Delivery and Receipt from Invoice, Bill and Credit Note

What it does

You invoice directly, without a sale order, or book a vendor bill without a purchase order - and the stock stays wrong because Odoo only moves goods from orders. This module creates the delivery order from the customer invoice, the receipt from the vendor bill and the return from the credit note, automatically when you post or with one button, with lots and serial numbers, and it never moves the same goods twice.

Everything a stock-from-invoice module should do, built on the standard transfers and stock moves.

Post a customer invoice and its storable products leave the warehouse: a delivery order to the customer (or the delivery address) with the invoice number as source document.

Post a vendor bill and the goods are received into stock. The receipt is valued at the bill price (without taxes, in company currency), so average and FIFO costs are right.

A customer credit note brings the goods back, a vendor refund sends them back to the vendor. Each return is linked to the original transfer and takes the same lots; customer returns are valued at the cost of the original delivery.

Features

Choose per company and per journalcreate the transfer when the invoice is posted, only with the Create Transfer button, or never. The button also works on draft invoices.
Optionalthe transfer and its stock moves are dated on the invoice date instead of today, for invoices you book after the fact.
