Merge Invoices

Merge several draft invoices or bills of one partner into one document

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Select draft customer invoices, vendor bills or credit notes of the same partner and merge them into a new document or into one of them - with a section per source, the sales and purchase order links kept, and a log of what was merged.

A customer who placed three orders in a week often wants one invoice, and a supplier who sends one bill for two purchase orders has to be matched against two draft bills. Standard Odoo has no way to combine draft invoices or bills.

This module adds Merge Invoices to the Action menu of the invoice, bill and credit note lists, and a Merge button on a draft document. A wizard shows the documents, their totals, and anything that prevents the merge. Choose to create a new document or merge into one of the selected ones, to cancel the originals (kept and linked) or delete them, to keep every line or merge identical lines, and to add one section per source document.

The merged document keeps the links to the sales order and purchase order lines, so invoiced and billed quantities on the orders stay right. Its chatter lists every document merged into it, and each cancelled original shows which document now holds its lines.

Features

Merge Invoices in the Action menu
The merge wizard
One invoice with a section per order
A log of what was merged
Each original knows where its lines went
Merge button on a draft bill
Merge into an existing bill
Purchase order links kept
Clear reasons when documents cannot be merged
