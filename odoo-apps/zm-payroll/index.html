Zambia Payroll

Zambia Payroll for Odoo 19: PAYE 2026 Bands, NAPSA, NHIMA and Skills Levy

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Zambia PayrollZambian PayrollPAYEZRANAPSANHIMASkills Development Levy2026 Tax Bands

What it does

Zambian payroll on Odoo Community. Each payslip applies the ZRA's 2026 PAYE bands to the gross pay, deducts NAPSA up to the earnings ceiling and NHIMA on basic salary, and works out the employer's skills levy. The ZRA's own example, K15,000 of taxable pay, comes out at K3,176 PAYE, and the payslip matches it.

First K5,100 at 0%, K7,100 at 20%, K9,200 at 30% and 37% above, editable.

Capped at the monthly earnings ceiling, so a K50,000 salary pays K1,861.80.

On gross emoluments, leaving out gratuity and redundancy pay.

Mark allowances as taxable or not, and which ones skip the skills levy.

Features

Not includedcasual and daily-paid worker tables, e-filing with the ZRA, and NAPSA or NHIMA portal uploads. Pay and file with your own access by the 10th of the next month.
