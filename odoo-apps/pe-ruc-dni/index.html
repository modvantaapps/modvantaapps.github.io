Peru RUC DNI Lookup

Consulta RUC and consulta DNI inside the Odoo contact form

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Type a RUC or a DNI and the contact is filled with the official data: razon social or RENIEC name, fiscal address with ubigeo, SUNAT status and condition, retention agent and good taxpayer flags.

Pick your provider in Settings (Decolecta / apis.net.pe, apiperu.dev or json.pe) and paste the token of your account. A SUNAT button next to a RUC, or a RENIEC button next to a DNI, fills the contact; it can also happen as soon as you finish typing the number.

The ubigeo is mapped to the district, province and department of the Peruvian localization, so the address is complete. Natural persons get their names and surnames in separate fields and a clean APELLIDOS NOMBRES contact name.

Without any token, the official SUNAT padron reducido file can refresh the status and condition of every contact with a RUC once a week. Contacts and invoices of taxpayers that are not ACTIVO or not HABIDO show a warning.
