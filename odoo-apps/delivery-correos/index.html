Correos Shipping Integration and Correos Express Connector

Ship with Correos and Correos Express from Odoo - labels, offices, CityPaq lockers and tracking

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

One Odoo 19 delivery method type for both Spanish carriers: Correos and Correos Express prices at the checkout with an office and CityPaq locker picker, pre-registration and labels on validation, cash on delivery, customs for the Canary Islands, returns and the status history on every shipment.

The module adds a Correos delivery method type with a Correos / Correos Express selector. Correos shipments use the PreRegistro web service; Correos Express shipments use its REST web services. Both share the same shipments dashboard, tracking and API log.

Each delivery method is one product - Paq Estandar, Paq Premium to an office, CityPaq locker, Correos Express Paq 24, PaqPunto and others - priced by a rate table by zone and weight, a fixed price or Odoo rules. Validating the delivery order pre-registers the shipment and attaches the label; cash on delivery, insurance and Saturday delivery can be set per method or per transfer, and customs values are filled for the Canary Islands.

Features

Shipments dashboard by status
Correos account and product
Correos Express on the same screen
Choose an office or CityPaq locker
Correos tab on the delivery order
Shipment with customs for the Canary Islands
Correos Express shipment with courier pickup
Prices at the website checkout
CityPaq locker map at the checkout
