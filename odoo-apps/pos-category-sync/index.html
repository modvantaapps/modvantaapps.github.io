Sync POS Category

Odoo keeps two category trees: product (internal) categories and POS categories. This module builds the POS tree from the product tree and keeps them in step, so you set up a category and its products once.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A sync wizard creates POS categories for the product categories you choose, parents first, so the till gets the same hierarchy. A POS category that already has the same name under the same parent is reused instead of duplicated.

A second step puts every product of those categories in its POS category, and can make them Available in POS at the same time.

After that, three settings keep the trees in step: new product categories get a POS category, products created in or moved to a category follow it, and renames and moves are copied to the POS category.
