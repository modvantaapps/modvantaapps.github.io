Odoo Shopify integration

Shopify Connector

Orders, stock, fulfilments, refunds, returns, payouts and abandoned checkouts between your Shopify store and Odoo, on the Shopify GraphQL Admin API, with webhooks for real time and scheduled jobs behind them.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Shopify Connector for Odoo 19

What it does

Shopify orders become Odoo sales orders with customer, lines, taxes, shipping and discounts.

Stock is published to each Shopify location from the Odoo locations you map.

Validating the delivery sends the fulfilment back to Shopify; refunds and returns come back to Odoo.

Payouts, taxes and fiscal positions are handled on the accounting side.

Who it is for

  • Shopify merchants who run stock and accounts in Odoo. Orders arrive by webhook and by a scheduled import every 15 minutes, with taxes, discounts, shipping and the customer. You decide per store whether to confirm when paid, always confirm or keep a quotation, and whether to invoice and register the payment once Shopify marks the order paid.
  • Brands with several warehouses or Shopify locations. Each Shopify location is mapped to an Odoo stock location in the Location Mapping table. Stock goes out every hour as On Hand, Free to Use or Forecasted, and orders are delivered from the warehouse Shopify assigned.
  • Shops offering local pickup or seeing fraud attempts. With Click & Collect (Local Pickup) on, pickup orders show their pickup location and a Ready for Pickup button that tells Shopify to notify the customer. Hold High-Risk Orders keeps risky orders unconfirmed and blocks their deliveries until a manager presses Release Fraud Hold.
  • Bookkeepers reconciling Shopify Payments. Payouts are imported daily under Shopify > Accounting > Payouts. Create Bank Statement and Reconcile turn each payout into a statement with the fee and transfer lines, and Profit Analysis lists product cost, Shopify fees and net profit per order.

Setting it up

  1. Create an app in the Shopify Dev Dashboard, or use an older custom app made in the Shopify admin, and give it the order, product, inventory, location, customer and fulfilment order scopes.
  2. In Odoo open Shopify > Configuration > Stores and create a store. Enter the Shop Domain (mystore.myshopify.com) and pick the Authentication type: Client ID and secret, or an Admin API access token.
  3. Choose the Warehouse, then press Test Connection. This loads your Shopify locations so you can map each one on the Products & Stock tab.
  4. On the Orders tab set Import Orders With Status, Import Orders Updated After, Confirm Orders and the order defaults such as Fallback Pricelist, Sales Team and Shipping Product.
  5. Run Shopify > Synchronise and pick an operation: link existing Odoo products by SKU, import Shopify products, import customers or export linked products.
  6. If Odoo has a public HTTPS address, press Register Webhooks. Then switch on Auto Import Orders and Auto Sync Stock on the Automation tab.

Shopify Connector: questions

How do I connect Odoo with Shopify?

Create a Shopify app, then add a store under Shopify > Configuration > Stores with the shop domain and the app credentials. Press Test Connection, map the locations, link products by SKU through Synchronise and register the webhooks. Orders start flowing on the next import.

Does this Odoo Shopify connector need Enterprise?

No. It depends on Sales, Inventory, Invoicing, CRM and Manufacturing, which are all in Odoo Community 19. CRM is used for abandoned checkouts and Manufacturing for kit stock.

What goes back from Odoo to Shopify?

Stock levels, product data with images and prices, fulfilments with the tracking number when a delivery is validated, returns made in Odoo with Create Return in Shopify, and order edits sent with Update in Shopify. Cancelling works in both directions.

Can it turn abandoned carts into leads?

Yes. Tick Abandoned Checkouts to CRM on the store and an hourly job creates CRM opportunities from Shopify abandoned checkouts, with a sales team and salesperson you choose.

How was this Odoo and Shopify integration tested?

It ships with a test suite that mocks the Shopify GraphQL Admin API using payloads shaped like Shopify's own examples. Demo data is included so you can look around before you connect a real store.

What doesn't it do?

It doesn't sync gift cards, subscriptions or Shopify POS, and it doesn't buy shipping labels. Order lines are created once: later Shopify updates change payment, fulfilment and cancel status, not the lines. Exporting a product replaces its variant list, so variants that exist only in Shopify are removed.
