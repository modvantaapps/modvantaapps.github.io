Odoo JustCall integration

JustCall Connector

Your team calls and texts from JustCall. Customers and leads live in Odoo. This puts every call, recording and SMS on the right record and opens the JustCall dialer with one click.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
JustCallClick to CallDialerCall LogRecordingsVoicemailMissed CallsSMS

What it does

Paste your JustCall API key and API secret and press Test Connection. Odoo reads your JustCall users and links them to Odoo users by e-mail. From then on every call lands in Odoo with its direction, outcome (answered, missed, voicemail or not answered), duration, agent, disposition, notes, recording link and voicemail transcription, and is posted on the contact and on the open lead with that number. A missed inbound call gives the agent a call-back activity. Texts sent and received in JustCall are logged too. On a contact or a lead, one button opens the JustCall dialer with the number, and Send SMS texts from your own JustCall number.

Features

Click-to-callopens the JustCall dialer with the number of the contact or lead, and passes the Odoo record along with the call
Call logdirection, answered, missed, voicemail or not answered, duration, agent and JustCall number, with list, pivot and graph views per user and outcome
Disposition and notesthe call outcome and the notes your agent wrote in JustCall, in the chatter
Recordings and voicemailrecording link and voicemail transcription on the call and in the chatter
On the right recordcalls are matched on the phone number whatever its format and posted on the contact and on the open lead with that number
Missed callsa call-back activity for the agent who missed it, due today, with the reason JustCall gives
SMSinbound and outbound texts logged and posted; send one from a contact or a lead with your JustCall number
Unknown numbersoptionally create a contact, or open a CRM lead for an unknown inbound caller
