Vietnam Payroll

Vietnam Payroll for Odoo 19: BHXH, BHYT, BHTN Insurance, Personal Income Tax and PIT Finalisation

What it does

Vietnamese statutory payroll on Odoo Community: compulsory social, health and unemployment insurance with the 2026 caps, the 2% trade union fee, personal income tax with the 2026 table of 5 to 35% and the 15.5 million / 6.2 million family deductions, NET to GROSS salaries, the 05/KK-TNCN return figures and the year-end finalisation, booked on the Vietnamese accounts, with a Vietnamese translation.

8% / 17.5%, 1.5% / 3% and 1% / 1% on the insurance wage, per employee participation.

20 x the base salary (2,340,000 then 2,530,000 VND from July) and 20 x the regional minimum wage.

Regional minimum wage from the employee, the work address or the company, with a warning.

The 2026 progressive table after insurance and the 15.5 M / 6.2 M family deductions.

Who it is for

  • Foreign-owned companies with staff in Vietnam. A subsidiary that already runs its books in Odoo can compute BHXH, BHYT, BHTN, the trade union fee and personal income tax on each payslip, instead of keeping a separate payroll spreadsheet for the Vietnamese team.
  • Employers that agree salaries as NET. Many Vietnamese offers are quoted as take-home pay. When an employee is marked for NET salary, the payslip grosses the regular pay up so the person receives the agreed net after insurance and tax.
  • Accounting service firms in Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City. A firm preparing payroll for several clients gets the monthly or quarterly PIT return figures, an insurance worksheet per employee and the year-end finalisation figures, each as CSV and Excel.
  • Companies paying casual workers and non-residents. People without a labour contract, or with one under three months, fall under flat withholding, and non-residents under a flat rate. The employee's Income Tax Status decides which rule the payslip applies.

Setting it up

  1. Open the company form, go to the Vietnamese Payroll tab and tick Vietnamese Statutory Payroll. Enter the Enterprise Tax Code (MST), the Social Insurance Unit Code, the Minimum Wage Region and the PIT return frequency.
  2. In the same tab, under Vietnamese Payroll Accounts, click Find Accounts in the Chart or pick the payable accounts for social, health and unemployment insurance, the trade union fee and personal income tax yourself.
  3. On each employee, open the Vietnamese Payroll tab and fill in the Personal Tax Code (MST), Social Insurance Number, Income Tax Status, Registered Dependants and which insurances apply. Tick the NET salary and finalisation options where they apply.
  4. Check the dated values under Employees > HR & Payroll > Vietnamese Payroll > Configuration > Insurance and Income Tax Rates, and map your pay codes under Pay Component Mapping.
  5. Generate the period's payslips from the HR & Payroll engine's Payroll Periods. Insurance, union fee and income tax are filled in on every slip of an enabled company.
  6. Under Vietnamese Payroll, create an Insurance Worksheet and a PIT return, click Compute, then Prepare Worksheet Files or Prepare Return Files to get the CSV and Excel.
  7. After you declare and pay through your own tax and insurance access, use Mark Filed and Record Payment so the due dates and payment status stay current.

Vietnam Payroll: questions

Do I need Odoo Enterprise Payroll?

No. It is built on the Modvanta HR and Payroll engine for Community (mdv_hr_payroll), which installs with it. It does not use Odoo Enterprise's hr_payroll.

Does it file anything with the tax office or the social insurance agency?

No. It prepares indicator values, CSV and Excel files and printed registers. You submit them yourself through your declaration software or the authority's portal, and it does not produce the XML file of the tax declaration software.

What happens when the rates change mid-year?

Rates are stored as dated records, and there are two shipped for 2026 because the base salary changes in July. When a new decree comes out you can edit the values or use Copy for Next Year to start a new set. Please have your accountant confirm the rates and deductions before you rely on them.

How does it pick the regional minimum wage?

It looks at the employee's own Minimum Wage Region first, then the region on their work address, then the company's region. That region sets the unemployment insurance ceiling.

Can staff use it in Vietnamese?

Yes. The module ships a Vietnamese (vi) translation of its screens, and the printed payslip block shows both English and Vietnamese labels.

Does it book the payroll in accounting?

It can post the period's journal entry to the accounts you choose on the company, such as 3335, 3383, 3384, 3386, 3382 and 642, next to the salaries booked by the payroll engine. The PIT return and worksheet then show what is still outstanding in accounting.
