Low Stock Notification

Know which products are running low before a customer finds out

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A live list of every product below its minimum stock, per warehouse, with immediate alert emails when a product goes low, a digest email with a PDF report, and requests for quotation created from the list in one click.

Odoo replenishes from reordering rules, but nobody is told when a product without a rule runs out, and there is no single list of what is low across the catalogue. Stock-outs are found by the customer.

This module keeps a Low Stock list of every product below its minimum: on hand, forecasted, minimum, shortfall, suggested order quantity, vendor, lead time and since when it is low. Minimums come from your own minimum stock rules - for one product, a product category or all products - and can also be read from Odoo reordering rules.

When a product goes below its minimum an alert email is sent at once, and a digest email with the full list and a PDF report is sent on the schedule you choose. The responsibles of each warehouse get only their warehouse. From the list, select products and Create RFQ makes the requests for quotation, one per vendor.
