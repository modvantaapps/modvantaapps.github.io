POS Customer History

POS Customer History for Odoo 19: the last orders, the products bought, the favourite products and what the customer paid for the products in the cart - in one popup at the till, with reorder in one tap.

What it does

Regular customers ask "the same as last time", "what did I pay for this?" or "which coffee did I take?". Odoo 19 has the order list, but not a view of one customer at the till. This module shows the history of the selected customer in the POS and lets the cashier add products again in one tap.

Features

Last ordersthe last orders of the customer (of every shop of the company, or only this one), with date, receipt number, shop and total; tap an order to see its lines.
Reorderone button adds all the lines of a past order to the current order.
Products boughtevery product the customer bought, with the quantity, the number of orders, the last price and the last date, and an Add button.
Favourite productsthe most bought products as large one-tap buttons.
Cart productsfor each product of the current order, the last times the customer bought it and the price paid; also from a history icon on each order line.
Printthe history goes to the receipt printer (or the browser).
Clear messagesno customer selected, no order yet, a product never bought.
Backend reportCustomer Favourite Products (list, pivot, graph) per customer, product and POS category, and a smart button on the customer.
Safethe history is read on the server for point of sale users only, for the selected customer (and the contacts of his company) in the company of the point of sale.
