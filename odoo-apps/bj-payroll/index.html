Benin Payroll

Benin Payroll for Odoo 19: ITS, ORTB levy, VPS and CNSS

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Benin PayrollPaie BeninITSVPSCNSSORTBIFUe-services

What it does

Beninese payroll on Odoo Community. Each payslip rounds the gross pay down to the thousand, runs the ITS monthly scale, adds the ORTB levy in March and June, and works out the CNSS contributions and the 4% VPS at the same time. An employee on 300,000 FCFA a month pays 33,500 FCFA of ITS and 10,800 FCFA of CNSS. Paie Benin: ITS, VPS, CNSS, ORTB.

0% to 60,000, then 10%, 15%, 19% and 30% above 500,000 FCFA a month.

Salary, allowances and benefits of every kind, transport included, as the code says.

Base down to the thousand, tax to the nearest ten francs; both editable.

1,000 FCFA on the March salary, 3,000 FCFA in June above the first slice.

Features

Not includedthe average-salary method for exceptional pay (art. 126), e-filing on e-services, and benefits in kind at their flat values (enter them as earnings). The register gives you the figures.
