POS Customer Verification

Clean customer data at the till: required fields, no duplicate phone or email, and customers verified with a one-time code sent by SMS or email.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

POS customer verification and POS customer validation: customer OTP sent by SMS or email from the POS or the contact, verified customer badge, verify customer before payment (checked by the server), required fields and unique phone, unique email, unique barcode, VAT or reference for customers created in the POS, POS OTP log.

Features

Required customer fieldsphone, email, street, city, zip, country, tax ID, barcode or reference, chosen per point of sale. The server refuses to save the customer without them.
Unique customer fieldsphone, email, barcode, tax ID or reference: a second active customer with the same value is refused with the name of the existing customer. Phones are compared on their digits, emails without case.
Customer OTP by SMS or emailVerify Customer in the POS customer menu: a 6-digit code goes to the phone (Odoo SMS) or the email of the customer; the cashier types the code the customer reads out.
Safe codesvalid 10 minutes, 5 tries, a new code cancels the previous one, 30 seconds between two codes, stored hashed (never in clear).
Verified badgea green Verified badge in the POS customer list and a check on the Customer button; a Verified ribbon and a Verification tab on the contact.
Badge follows the datachanging the verified phone number or email removes the badge, so a verified customer always means a verified number.
Verified customer before paymentoptional per point of sale: an order with an unverified customer cannot be paid; the cashier can verify on the spot. The server also refuses such an order.
From the contact formSend Verification Code on the contact in the backend, with the same codes and rules.
Verification logwho sent a code to whom, by SMS or email, how many tries and the result.
