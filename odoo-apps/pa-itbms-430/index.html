Panama ITBMS Form 430

Panama ITBMS Formulario 430 worksheet from your Odoo accounting

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Every casilla of the DGI Formulario 430 computed from posted invoices and bills: sales by rate, purchases by destination, credito fiscal, liquidacion and CPC.

Each month, click Compute. Sales are split by ITBMS rate (7%, 7% air transport, 10% alcohol, 10% lodging, 15% tobacco), exports, exempt and non taxed. Purchases and imports go to direct, mixed, export or exempt destinations at 7%, 10% and 15%.

The credito fiscal follows the formulas of the DGI instructivo (non deductible 41, proportional 43 and 47), then the liquidacion with prior credit, ITBMS withholdings, export credit, late surcharge, fine and interest.

The DGI has no upload file for Form 430, so you get the casillas on screen and as an Excel worksheet to type into e-Tax 2.0.
