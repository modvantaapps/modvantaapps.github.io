Real Estate / Property Rental Management

Manage properties, units, tenants, agreements, deposits, and rent invoices

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Property rental managementReal estateLease agreementsRent invoices

What it does

Properties, units, leases and rent invoices in Odoo, with a public listings page

Record your buildings and their units - floor, area, bedrooms, rent, deposit, furnishing, amenities and photos - and the owner of each property. Lease agreements link a tenant to a unit with rent, maintenance charge, security deposit and frequency, and move from Draft to Reserved, Active and Closed.

From an active lease you create rent invoices in Odoo Accounting (scheduled actions can do it, add late fees and warn about expiring leases). Published units appear on a /properties page on your website with filters, a unit page and an enquiry form; tenants can raise maintenance requests from their portal.

Who it is for

  • Landlords with a few buildings. Each property has an owner, a type such as apartment, villa, office, shop, warehouse or land, and its units. A lease ties one tenant to one unit with rent, maintenance charges and a security deposit, and the rent invoices sit in Odoo Accounting.
  • Property managers who track occupancy. The Dashboard counts units by status, leases by stage, leases ending in the next 30 days, open maintenance requests, and rent collected against rent still due on posted invoices.
  • Letting agents who list flats online. Tick Published on a unit and it shows on the /properties page of your website. Visitors filter by city or locality, type, bedrooms and maximum rent, then send an enquiry with a preferred visit date.
  • Tenants who want answers without a phone call. With portal access, a tenant sees their leases under My Rentals, opens rent invoices from a Pay Now link and raises maintenance requests with a category and priority, linked to one of their own leases.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module, then open Property Rental > Properties and create a property with its owner, type and address.
  2. On the property's Units tab, add each unit with Unit No., floor, Area Sqft, bedrooms, bathrooms, expected rent and maintenance amount. Use the Website Listing and Amenities tabs for photos, furnishing and amenities.
  3. Create a lease under Property Rental > Agreements. Pick the tenant, property and unit, and the rent and maintenance amounts fill in from the unit.
  4. Set the Rent Frequency to monthly, quarterly or yearly, and fill in Late Fee (%) and Annual Escalation (%) if you use them. Leave Auto-Generate Rent Invoices ticked to let the daily job bill rent.
  5. Press Reserve to hold the unit, then Activate when the tenant moves in. Print Lease gives you the agreement as a PDF.
  6. Check and post the rent invoices the module creates. Late fees are only added to posted invoices that are past due.
  7. At the end of a lease, press Renew to start the next term with the escalation applied, or Close and then Refund Deposit.

Real Estate / Property Rental Management: questions

Does this property management module need Odoo Enterprise?

No. It depends on Invoicing, Discuss, Product, Portal and Website, which are all in Community.

How is rent invoiced?

Create Rent Invoice on a reserved or active lease makes a customer invoice for the rent, plus a separate line for maintenance charges. A daily scheduled action does the same for active leases whose next invoice date has arrived, then moves that date on by the rent frequency. It stops once the lease end date has passed.

Can the same apartment be leased twice for overlapping dates?

No. Reserve and Activate are refused when another reserved or active lease already holds that unit for any of the same dates.

How are deposits and late fees handled?

The deposit and any Deposit Deduction are recorded on the lease. After Close, Refund Deposit creates a credit note for the deposit minus the deduction. A daily job adds a one-time late fee invoice, at the lease's percentage, for each posted rent invoice that is overdue and unpaid.

What happens near the end of a lease?

A daily job puts a Lease expiring soon to-do on active leases that end within 30 days. Renew creates the next lease for the same length, raises the rent by the Annual Escalation (%) and closes the old one.

What doesn't it do?

It doesn't pay owners their share of rent, sign leases online or invoice the deposit when it's collected. Rent invoices are created as drafts for you to post. There are no separate access roles, so every internal user can edit properties and leases, and website enquiries are not turned into leases for you.
