What it does

Properties, units, leases and rent invoices in Odoo, with a public listings page

Record your buildings and their units - floor, area, bedrooms, rent, deposit, furnishing, amenities and photos - and the owner of each property. Lease agreements link a tenant to a unit with rent, maintenance charge, security deposit and frequency, and move from Draft to Reserved, Active and Closed.

From an active lease you create rent invoices in Odoo Accounting (scheduled actions can do it, add late fees and warn about expiring leases). Published units appear on a /properties page on your website with filters, a unit page and an enquiry form; tenants can raise maintenance requests from their portal.
