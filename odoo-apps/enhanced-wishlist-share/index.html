What it does

Let customers share their wishlist and get told when items are back

Every customer wishlist gets a share link. Customers copy it from their wishlist page and send it to friends and family, who can open a read-only page with the products, prices and links to the shop. Ideal for birthdays, weddings and office purchase requests.

Each wishlist item also gets a bell button. One click subscribes the customer to a back-in-stock email for that product, using the Back in Stock module.
