Video Consultation & Telemedicine

See patients by video from the appointment you already booked. Each video appointment gets a protected room on Odoo Discuss, the patient gets the link and passcode by e-mail, signs consent and pays in a waiting room, and the doctor starts the call with one click. Notes go straight into the clinical record.

What it does

When an appointment is booked as a video consultation, a room is created for it and the patient is e-mailed a link to a waiting room on your website, with a passcode. The waiting room asks for the passcode, the telemedicine consent (signed on screen and stored in the consent register), the fee if you take payment first, and offers a camera and microphone test. The patient is let into the call only when the doctor starts it.

The video call runs on Odoo Discuss, which is part of Odoo, so there is no Zoom or Teams account to buy. You can also use a Jitsi server or a doctor's own meeting link.
