Minimax Connector

Minimax Integration for Serbia: send Odoo customer invoices to Minimax Accounting as bookkeeping postings or full issued invoices, without retyping them.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Minimax Connector for Odoo 19

What it does

Many Serbian companies sell and invoice in Odoo while their accountant works in Minimax. This app connects one Odoo company to one Minimax organisation through the public Minimax API. Posted customer invoices go over as bookkeeping postings (tax bases and revenue totals) or as full issued invoices with rows. They arrive as drafts by default, so the accountant still decides. Customers and items can be linked, created and imported, and the payment status of issued invoices is read back into Odoo.

Who it is for

  • Serbian companies whose accountant uses Minimax. The business invoices in Odoo, but the external bookkeeper works in moj.minimax.rs. Posted customer invoices can go across as bookkeeping postings with tax bases and revenue totals, so nobody retypes them.
  • Firms that want Minimax to hold the full invoice. In issued invoice mode the whole invoice with its rows is created in Minimax, customers and items are created there when missing, and foreign-currency invoices are accepted.
  • Teams that need to know what's been paid. A scheduled job reads the document and payment status back from Minimax, so the Odoo invoice shows whether the accountant has recorded the payment.

Setting it up

  1. Ask Minimax support for client data (Client ID and Client Password), and in Minimax create an application under My Profile to get the API user name and password.
  2. Install the module and go to Minimax > Connections. Create a connection and fill in the Credentials tab, or tick Demo Mode to use the built-in simulator first.
  3. Click Test Connection, pick your organisation, then click Refresh Reference Data so VAT rates, currencies and other codes are loaded.
  4. On the Documents tab choose the document mode (bookkeeping posting or issued invoice), and decide on Confirm in Minimax, Send Automatically, Send Invoices From and Forward VAT Data to SEF.
  5. Fill in the VAT Mapping tab, and in posting mode the Revenue Mapping tab, so every Odoo tax and income account has its Minimax counterpart.
  6. Open a posted customer invoice and click Check Minimax Readiness, then Send to Minimax. The Minimax tab on the invoice shows the result and the payment status.
  7. Use Import Customers, Import Items and Read Stock on the connection to link existing Minimax records, and follow Minimax > Documents and API Log for errors.

Minimax Connector: questions

Which Minimax countries does it support?

Only the Serbian service at moj.minimax.rs. Minimax also runs in other countries, but this connector doesn't target their versions.

What Odoo edition do I need?

It depends only on Invoicing (account), Product and Discuss, so Community works. On the Minimax side you need a subscription with API access and the client data from Minimax support.

Does it file e-invoices to SEF itself?

No. It fills the ForwardToSEF value you choose (group, individual or e-invoice for issued invoices) and can trigger Minimax's own sending. Filing is done by Minimax, and no fiscal receipts are created. Check with your accountant which forwarding option applies to your company.

How are credit notes handled?

In posting mode a credit note can't be sent as a posting, so you cancel the original posting in Minimax instead. In issued invoice mode a credit note for the full invoice goes across through Minimax's copy to credit note action. A partial credit note is refused with a message.

Can I reset a sent invoice to draft in Odoo?

Not while it's still live in Minimax. Odoo blocks the reset and asks you to cancel it in Minimax first, so both sets of books stay the same.

What happens if the login is refused?

Minimax locks an application after several refused logins, so the connection stops trying after the first refusal and marks Authentication Stopped. Fix the credentials and click Test Connection to clear it.
