Contact Relationship Manager, Org Chart & Stakeholder Matrix

Visual org chart of the contacts of each company (organization chart, contact hierarchy, partner hierarchy with "reports to"), stakeholders per CRM opportunity with role, stance and filters, and configurable stakeholder roles (decision maker, influencer, champion, blocker) with a relationship strength score. Stakeholder mapping, buying committee, decision maker tracking, organizational chart.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Winning and keeping a B2B account depends on knowing the people: who signs, who argues for you, who stands in the way, and who answers to whom. Odoo lists the contacts of a company, but not their part in the decision and not the reporting lines.

This module gives each contact person a stakeholder role, a relationship strength from 1 - Cold to 5 - Champion Level, stakeholder notes and a Reports To manager in the same company. The company gets an org chart of its people and a stakeholder matrix you can edit in place. Each CRM opportunity gets its own list of stakeholders with role and stance, so you see the buying committee of every deal, and the pipeline can be filtered on it.

We built the opportunity side because the same person can back one deal and fight the next. The role on the contact is the default; the stance on the deal is what you think today.

Who it is for

  • B2B sales teams selling to committees. When a deal needs sign-off from finance, IT and an operations head, the rep has to know who decides and who is quietly against it. Each opportunity gets its own stakeholder list with a stance per person, so that picture lives in Odoo instead of in someone's notebook.
  • Key account managers. Large customers have a dozen contacts and reporting lines that change every year. Setting Reports To on each person draws an org chart on the company form, coloured by stakeholder role.
  • Sales managers running pipeline reviews. The usual question in a review is who the decision maker is on this one. Filters such as No Decision Maker and Has Blocker pull up the deals where nobody can answer it yet.
  • Service firms with long renewal cycles. Agencies, consultancies and software resellers keep the same accounts for years. A relationship strength from 1 - Cold to 5 - Champion Level, plus tracked changes in the chatter, shows when a sponsor goes cold or leaves.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It needs the Contacts and CRM apps, which are both part of Odoo Community.
  2. Go to Contacts > Configuration > Stakeholder Roles and review the six starting roles. Rename or archive any of them, add your own, and tick Key Role or Blocker where it applies.
  3. Open a company contact and use the Stakeholder Matrix list to set Stakeholder Role, Relationship Strength and Reports To for each person working there.
  4. Open the Org Chart tab on the same company to check the reporting lines. Clicking a box opens that person's contact form.
  5. On a CRM opportunity, open the Stakeholders tab and press Load from customer to copy the mapped people of the customer's company, then set each person's Stance.
  6. In the CRM pipeline, use the Has Decision Maker, No Decision Maker, Has Blocker and Stakeholders At Risk filters to find the deals that need attention.

Contact Relationship Manager, Org Chart & Stakeholder Matrix: questions

Does it work on Odoo Community, or do I need Enterprise?

Community is enough. The module depends only on Contacts, CRM, Mail and Base, so no Enterprise app is involved. It also runs on Enterprise without changes.

Can a customer see the notes we write about them through the portal?

No. The stakeholder role, relationship strength, notes and Reports To fields are limited to internal users, so a portal user can't read them, even on their own contact record. The org chart is also refused for anyone who isn't an internal user.

What counts as an account or a deal at risk?

It's flagged when at least one person has a stakeholder role but none of them holds a role marked Key Role. Out of the box that means Decision Maker and Champion. Companies or opportunities with nobody mapped are left unflagged.

If I change someone's role on the contact, does it change on every opportunity?

No, and that's on purpose. The role and strength on the contact are copied as defaults when you add the person to a deal, but the line on the opportunity is its own record. The same person can be a supporter on one deal and an opponent on another.

Can Reports To point to someone at another company?

No. Reports To only accepts a person from the same company, and the module refuses a contact reporting to themselves or a loop of managers. People with no manager in the company appear at the top of the org chart.

Who can see opportunity stakeholders when several salespeople and companies share the database?

Stakeholder lines follow the access of their opportunity. A salesperson with own-documents access sees stakeholders on their own or unassigned deals, users with all-leads access see all of them, and records are kept to the allowed companies.
