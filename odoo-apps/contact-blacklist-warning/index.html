Customer/Vendor Blacklist & Warning

Flag risky customers/vendors and block confirming sales orders, purchase orders, or invoices for blacklisted contacts. Customer blacklist, credit hold, do-not-sell list, risk flag.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Every business has a few customers who do not pay and vendors it has stopped working with. Usually that knowledge lives in someone's head, and a new colleague happily confirms the next order.

This module adds a Blacklist Status to contacts: Not Flagged, Warning or Blocked, always with a reason. A Warning shows a yellow banner with the reason on the contact. Blocked shows a red banner and stops anyone from confirming a sales order, confirming a purchase order or posting an invoice or vendor bill for that contact.
