TaxJar Connector

US sales tax calculated by TaxJar on your quotations and invoices, broken down by state, county, city and special district, with posted invoices reported to TaxJar, exempt customers kept in sync and one-click tax exemption for all the contacts of a company.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
TaxJar Sales Tax for Odoo 19

What it does

When you quote or invoice a US customer, Odoo asks TaxJar for the sales tax of the ship-to address.

The tax is added to the lines as one combined tax, or one tax per jurisdiction.

Posted invoices are reported to TaxJar as transactions, so filing reports match your books.

Wholesale and other exempt customers are synced to TaxJar with their exemption type, and one click copies the exemption to every contact and delivery address of the company.
