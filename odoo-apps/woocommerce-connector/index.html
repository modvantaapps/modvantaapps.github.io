WooCommerce Connector

Connect your WooCommerce store to Odoo through the WooCommerce REST API: orders, customers, products and variations, stock and prices, refunds and returns, payment gateways, taxes and shipping, with webhooks for real time and an order auto-workflow that confirms, ships, invoices, e-mails and records the payment for you.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
WooCommerce Connector for Odoo 19

What it does

WooCommerce orders become Odoo sales orders with coupons, fees, shipping and taxes.

Products and variations are linked by SKU or barcode; stock and prices go from Odoo to WooCommerce.

When an order is delivered in Odoo, WooCommerce is set to completed and gets the tracking.

Refunds made in WooCommerce become credit notes and refund payments in Odoo.
