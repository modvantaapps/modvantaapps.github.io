Sales Rep Shop On Behalf of Customer

Let sales reps shop the website on behalf of their customers

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A rep picks one of their customers, browses the shop with that customer's prices and taxes, and places the order for them - with every session logged.

Customers phone or email their orders and a rep types them in. With this module the rep uses the website instead: they pick the customer, the shop switches to that customer's pricelist and taxes, and the cart and the order belong to the customer.

Access is locked down. Only users in the Sales Rep group can do it, only for customers they are assigned to (as Salesperson, or listed as an allowed rep), never for a partner that belongs to an employee, and every start, exit and order is a POST form with a CSRF token.

Features

Every session is recordedwho, for whom, when, from which IP address, which orders came out of it, and why it ended. Attempts to shop for a customer who is not assigned are refused and logged too. The log is read-only for everybody it audits.
