Website Sales Urgency & Social Proof

Show shoppers how many people are looking at a product right now

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A live visitor counter and three more social proof lines on every product page - viewing now, viewed recently, in other baskets and sold recently - all counted from real data, and hidden when the number is too small to show.

A shopper on a product page cannot see that other people are looking at the same item, putting it in their basket or buying it. That is the social proof a busy shop has and a plain product page hides.

This module adds up to four lines above the Add to Cart button: how many other visitors have the page open right now, how many people viewed the product in the last hours, how many open baskets hold it, and how many units were sold through the website in the last days. On the shop grid, product cards can show the live viewer count too.

Every number is real. Live viewers come from a heartbeat sent by open product pages, the other three are read from data Odoo already keeps (page tracking, draft carts and confirmed orders). Each line has a minimum: below it the line disappears instead of being rounded up, and the visitor reading the page is never counted in their own figure.

Features

Four live counters on the product page
Live viewer count on the shop grid
One settings block for every counter
Live Viewers report
Turn the counters off for one product
Works on mobile
