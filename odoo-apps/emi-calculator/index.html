EMI & Instalment Calculator

Show the monthly price on your shop - and manage the instalments after the sale

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Show from X / month under the product price and the cart total, let the customer open the full amortisation schedule, choose an instalment plan at checkout, and turn the order into an instalment agreement with invoices per instalment, arrears, late fees, early settlement and a portal page.

Customers buying expensive items want to know the monthly cost before they commit. This module adds an instalment line under the price on the product page and under the cart total, with a View plans dialog that shows every plan's monthly amount, interest, processing fee, total payable and the month-by-month schedule.

At checkout the customer can pay in full or pick an instalment plan. When the order is confirmed an instalment agreement is created with the terms frozen from the plan: price, amount financed, monthly instalment, interest, fee and schedule.

Instalments are invoiced as they fall due by a scheduled job. Missed instalments show in an In Arrears list with days late and a late fee; you can quote an early settlement with a discount on the remaining interest. The customer sees the agreement and schedule in the portal.
