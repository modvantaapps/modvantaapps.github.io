Odoo SaaS Kit

Odoo SaaS Kit - Multi Server SaaS with Docker, Custom Domains and Let's Encrypt

What it does

Publish SaaS plans on your website, let customers sign up for a free trial or pay online, and the module creates their own Odoo instance: a database on this server, or a Docker container on any of your servers (over SSH), on the Odoo version of the plan. It enforces user, app and storage limits, gives each customer a custom domain with a free Let's Encrypt certificate, meters usage for the invoice, backs instances up and restores them.

A visitor builds a plan on the signup page (extra users, optional apps, live price), picks an address and starts the trial or pays online. The queue picks the least loaded healthy server with room, creates the database or the container, installs the apps, sets the company and the customer login, applies the user limit and emails a link to set the password.

Remote hosts are reached with an SSH key; the host key fingerprint is checked on every connection. Every command is an argument list built from validated names, every operation is logged per server, and SSH keys and PostgreSQL credentials are visible to Settings administrators only.

Customers attach their own domain in the portal. When the DNS points to the server, the module requests a free certificate from Let's Encrypt (ACME, HTTP-01), writes the nginx site and renews the certificate 30 days before it expires.

Features

You define plansapps installed, users included and maximum, price per month or year, price per extra user, optional paid apps, free trial days, and now the limits buyers ask for: maximum add-on apps, storage included (warn, suspend or invoice per extra GB), billing of measured users, custom domains allowed, fixed term, and the servers and Odoo version (Docker image) new instances of the plan use.
