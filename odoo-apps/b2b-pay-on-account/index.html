B2B Pay on Account & Credit Limit Checkout

Let trusted trade customers order on account at checkout

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Approved B2B buyers see a Pay on Account option on the payment step with their credit limit, used credit and available credit. Orders within the limit are confirmed on their agreed payment terms; over-limit carts and accounts with overdue invoices are refused.

Wholesale and trade customers usually buy on account and pay the invoice later, on Net 30 or Net 60 terms. The standard Odoo shop only offers online payment methods, so these customers phone or e-mail their orders instead.

This module adds B2B Pay on Account settings to the customer: allow pay on account, a credit limit and the payment terms. When such a customer reaches the payment step of the shop, a Pay on Account card shows the credit limit, the credit already used and what is still available, and a Place Order on Account button when the cart fits.

Placing the order confirms it straight away with the customer's agreed payment terms, without any online payment. If the cart is larger than the available credit, or if the account has overdue invoices, the card explains why and the customer must choose another payment method. Used credit counts unpaid invoices and confirmed orders not yet invoiced.

Features

Approve a customer for pay on account
Live credit at checkout
Order placed on account
Confirmed with the agreed terms
Over the limit: refused
Overdue invoices: refused
