Website Sale Multi-Warehouse

Sell the stock of several warehouses from one Odoo shop

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Give each website an ordered list of warehouses. The shop shows and sells the stock those warehouses hold, and each confirmed order is sent to the warehouse that really has the goods - one delivery per warehouse, with the reason written on the order.

Standard Odoo eCommerce sells the stock of one warehouse, or of every warehouse with the delivery always created in the default one. A shop with a central warehouse and regional stores either hides stock it has, or sells stock it then has to move between warehouses.

With this module a website gets a list of warehouses with a priority, a public name and optionally the countries, states and zip prefixes each one serves. The product page and the cart use the stock of those warehouses, and the product page can show the stock of each warehouse by name.

When an order is confirmed, the module picks the warehouse: one that holds everything if there is one, the nearest one first when that option is on, and otherwise it splits the order with one delivery per warehouse. The choice and its reason are written on the order.

Features

Stock by warehouse on the product page
Only the warehouses that hold it
The cart is limited to what the warehouses can supply
One warehouse when one holds everything
Split when no warehouse holds everything
One delivery per warehouse
The warehouses of a website
A shortcut in the settings
