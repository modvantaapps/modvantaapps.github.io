Laundry Management

Laundry Management and Dry Cleaning for Odoo 19

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Laundry ManagementDry CleaningDry CleanerLaundromatLaundry POSWash and FoldPer kg PricingIroning

What it does

Run a laundry, dry cleaner or laundromat on Odoo: a service catalogue priced per item or per kg, intake at the POS till or the counter, one barcode tag per garment, a production board with quality check and racks, promised dates, pickup and delivery with drivers, partial delivery, prepaid packages, lost and damaged claims, invoicing, daily reports and a customer portal. One module, built on Odoo's own Point of Sale, Invoicing and Portal.

Everything a laundry or dry cleaner needs, from intake to hand-over.

Wash and fold, dry cleaning, wash and iron, ironing only - priced per item or per kg with a minimum weight. A garment x service price matrix (shirt, suit, dress, duvet...), add-ons such as starch, stain treatment or hangers, and the plant steps each service needs.

Standard, express and same day with a surcharge percentage and their own turnaround. The surcharge is added at the till and on the invoice, and the promised date follows the level.

Laundry products for every garment and service are generated from the price matrix. Ring them up in Odoo Point of Sale, add the express surcharge with one button, take payment (or put it on the customer account) - the paid order becomes a laundry order with tags.

Features

Counter orders with garment linesgarment type, service, items or weight and bags, colour, notes, stain and damage conditions noted at intake and intake photos per garment. A customer's standing preferences are kept on the contact.
Driver, operator and manager rightsdrivers see only their own stops, managers set prices and packages, approve claims and hand over unpaid orders.
