What it does

The IIT follows the State Taxation Administration rules: cumulative income, 5,000 yuan for each month employed, special deductions, special additional deductions, the withholding rate table and the tax withheld in earlier months. Social insurance and housing fund use the rates and bases of your city, entered once as dated records.

Nothing is declared to any authority by this module. The registers and CSV files are working lists for the declaration you make yourself.

Features

Cumulative IIT on every payslip
Special additional deductions per employee
Your city rates, dated
Annual one-off bonus taxed separately
Monthly IIT register
Social insurance and housing fund register
Statement for each employee
