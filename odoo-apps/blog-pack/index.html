Website Blog Pack

Give every Odoo blog post the features readers expect

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Reading time, a table of contents built from your headings, an author box, related posts that share a tag and a reading progress bar - added to the posts you already have, with nothing to re-edit.

The standard Odoo blog shows a title, a cover and the text. Readers of a long article cannot see how long it is, cannot jump to the part they need, and reach the end with nothing pointing them to the next post of the same topic.

This module adds five things to every blog post page. A reading time next to the date, computed from the real word count of the post. A table of contents built from the post's own H2 and H3 headings, with links that jump to each section. A thin progress bar at the top of the page. Under the article, an author box with photo, biography and the author's other posts, and a row of related posts that share a tag with the one being read.

Everything is switched on or off per blog from the blog form, so one blog can show all of it and another only the reading time. The reading time is also shown in the blog post list and on each related post card.
