Thailand Withholding Tax

Thai withholding tax for Odoo 19 Community: withhold the right rate when you pay a vendor bill, issue the numbered 50 Tawi certificate, and file PND3, PND53 and PND54 every month with the attachment and the RD Prep text file.

What it does

Odoo Community ships the Thai chart of accounts and eight withholding taxes that are booked on the bill, plus a generic "withholding tax on payment" engine. It has no 50 Tawi certificate, no PND attachment and no filing file. This module adds them on top of the core engine: the tax is withheld when you pay, at the rate valid on the payment date, and every certificate goes into the right monthly return.

Features

Withholding on Register Paymentone line per income type, with the base taken from the bill lines before VAT and only for the part you pay now. Net paid and tax withheld are shown before you confirm.
Right form, right ratePND3 for individuals, PND53 for Thai companies, PND54 for foreign companies not operating in Thailand, PND2 for interest and dividends to individuals; non-resident individuals and foundations get their own rates.
Income type on product or vendorset "Rental", "Service fees", "Transport" or "Accounting" once on the product (or as the vendor default) and the payment proposes the withholding.
1,000 baht threshold per contractno withholding is proposed on a bill below the threshold of the rate (PND3/PND53 instructions).
Numbered 50 Tawi certificateone certificate per payment (WHT/2026/00001), with all income rows of the official form, the PND box ticked, the tax in Thai words, copy 1 and copy 2. Print, send by email, or let the vendor download it from the portal.
PND monthly returnpick the form and the month, load the certificates, get the totals, the due date and the line number of every certificate. Print the return summary and the attachment sheets (6 lines per sheet).
RD Prep text fileone line per income line in the column order of the RD Prep attachment screens, Buddhist Era dates, pipe, comma or tab separator, UTF-8 or Thai Windows (cp874) encoding, optional header row.
Late surcharge1.5% per month or part of a month after the due date, capped at the tax (Revenue Code Section 27), computed on the return.
Tax paid by us (conditions 2 and 3)when you pay the tax for the payee, the income is grossed up, the tax is booked as your expense and the certificate shows condition 2 or 3.
Customer WHT receivedcustomers that withhold from you get the WHT line on their payment, booked on WHT Creditable (114300), and a register of the certificates they send you.
Dated, editable rates with sources60+ rates preloaded from the official Revenue Department forms, each with its source link; close a rate with an end date and add the new one - old payments keep their rate.
No double withholdingif the bill already carries one of the Thai chart WHT taxes, the payment proposes nothing and refuses a second withholding.
