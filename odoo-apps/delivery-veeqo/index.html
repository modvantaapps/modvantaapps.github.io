Veeqo Shipping Integration

Two ways to ship with Veeqo from Odoo 19: buy USPS, UPS and FedEx labels with the Veeqo Rate Shopping API right on the delivery order, or send the order to Veeqo and get the tracking number back.

What it does

A delivery method type Veeqo on the standard Odoo shipping framework, connected to the public Veeqo API: the Rate Shopping API for rates, labels, documents, voids and scan forms, and the Orders API to send an order to Veeqo and follow its shipment.

Features

Rate on the quotationthe rates of your Veeqo account (Amazon Shipping rates for USPS, UPS, FedEx and more) for the order address and weight: the cheapest, the fastest, one service ID, or the cheapest among the carriers you list.
Label on validationvalidating the delivery order books the label and attaches it to the transfer as PDF, ZPL for thermal printers or PNG; the tracking number goes on the transfer.
One label per boxthe Rate Shopping API takes one parcel per request, so each package of the delivery order gets its own rate, label and tracking number.
Service optionsdelivery confirmation (none, signature, adult signature) and every option a rate requires are sent with a valid value; optional insurance with the goods value, seller name and a custom message on the label.
Internationalcustoms items (description, quantity, value, weight, HS code, country of manufacture) for shipments leaving the USA; the commercial invoice returned by the carrier is attached.
Scan formsselect the labels of one carrier and create the scan form (manifest) the carrier scans at pickup; the PDF is kept in Odoo.
Void labelvoid a label in Veeqo before it is on a scan form; the refund follows the carrier policy.
Send orders to Veeqoor let Veeqo ship: the order goes to the Veeqo store and delivery method you choose, with the customer, delivery address, notes and one line per product matched by SKU; the Veeqo order ID and link are on the delivery order.
Order sync and trackinga scheduled job reads the Veeqo order: when it ships, the tracking number and carrier come back to the transfer, then the tracking events (awaiting collection, in transit, out for delivery, delivered); cancel the Veeqo order from Odoo while it is not shipped.
Logged and testableevery call is logged with the API key and label data masked; a demo mode runs both flows without an account.
