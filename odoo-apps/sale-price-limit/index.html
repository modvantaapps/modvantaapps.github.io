Minimum and Maximum Sale Price

Stop quotations going out below your minimum price

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Set a minimum and maximum sale price per product, variant or category. When a salesperson quotes outside the range, Odoo warns, blocks the confirmation or asks a manager to approve it with a reason - and every exception is logged.

In standard Odoo a salesperson can type any unit price or discount on a quotation and confirm it. Nobody sees that a desk went out below cost until the margin report at the end of the month.

Features

This module adds sale price limitsa fixed minimum and maximum, a minimum margin over cost, a maximum discount from the list price or a maximum markup. They can be set on a product, a variant or a whole product category, per pricelist and currency. The price checked is the real unit price after line and order discounts, before tax.
Choose what happens outside the limitswarn only, block confirmation, or require the approval of a Price Limit Approver. Approvals and refusals carry a reason, are written in the chatter and land in a Price Exceptions report. You can also cap the discount each salesperson may give.
