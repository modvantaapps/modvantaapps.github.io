Revolut Payment Gateway

Revolut Payment Gateway for Odoo 19 - Online and Point of Sale

What it does

Take Revolut payments in Odoo eCommerce, the customer portal, invoices and payment links, and at the counter in Odoo Point of Sale. Online, customers pay with the Revolut card pop-up or on the Revolut Hosted Checkout Page (card, Revolut Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay). In the shop, the POS pushes the amount to a Revolut Terminal or shows a QR code the customer pays on their phone.

The module adds Revolut as an Odoo payment provider and as a Point of Sale payment terminal. Each payment creates a Revolut order for the exact amount, with the Odoo reference, the customer and, when they add up, the order lines. Odoo never trusts the browser: the result is always read from the Revolut order, on return, from the pop-up, from the signed webhook or from a scheduled check. Cards can be authorised and captured later, refunds are sent from Odoo, and customers can save their card or Revolut Pay account for the next order.
