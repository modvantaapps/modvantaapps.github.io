Kenya Payroll

Kenya Payroll for Odoo 19: PAYE, NSSF Year 4, SHIF, Housing Levy, P10 and P9A

What it does

Kenyan statutory payroll on Odoo Community. PAYE on the KRA bands with personal and insurance relief and the Tax Laws (Amendment) Act 2024 deductions, NSSF Tier I and Tier II with the 2026 Year 4 limits, SHIF 2.75%, the Affordable Housing Levy and the NITA levy, the monthly P10 values, the NSSF return file and P9A tax deduction cards.

Monthly bands 10% up to 24,000, 25% to 32,333, 30% to 500,000, 32.5% to 800,000 and 35% above, less personal relief of KES 2,400 for resident primary employees.

SHIF, the employee Housing Levy, post-retirement medical fund (KES 15,000), owner-occupier interest (KES 30,000) and pension (KES 30,000) are deducted before tax, as KRA applies them from December 2024.

Pension deduction is the lower of 30% of basic pay, the actual NSSF and pension contributions, and KES 30,000 (P9A columns E1 / E2 / E3).

6% employee + 6% employer up to the lower earnings limit (KES 9,000) and between the lower and upper limit (KES 108,000), with a Tier II contracted-out option.
