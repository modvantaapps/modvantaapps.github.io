Creditsafe Connector

Creditsafe Connector for Odoo 19: the Creditsafe Odoo integration

What it does

Check a customer before you give credit, without leaving Odoo. Find the company on Creditsafe by name, registration number or VAT number, pull the credit report onto the contact, set the customer credit limit and payment terms from the rating, and get an alert when the company changes. The directors can come in as contacts of the company, and the same search is one click away on a CRM lead.

Open a company contact and press Find on Creditsafe. Search by name, registration number, VAT number or Safe Number in any country of your subscription, pick the right company and Odoo orders its credit report. The contact gets the rating (A to E), the credit score, the Creditsafe credit limit and the company status, and the report is kept with the previous rating, legal form, registration data, directors, key financials, up to four years of financial statements and the PDF. From the rating Odoo sets the customer credit limit in your currency - the Creditsafe limit, a percentage of it, or your own amount per rating, with a cap - and the payment terms, and switches on the standard Odoo credit limit warnings. Checked companies are added to a Creditsafe monitoring portfolio; alerts such as a rating or limit change are imported, posted on the contact and trigger a new report.

Who it is for

  • Wholesalers selling on credit to other businesses. A distributor that ships before it gets paid can check a new trade customer's Creditsafe rating and credit score from the contact, before the first invoice goes out.
  • Credit controllers setting limits and terms. Instead of picking a credit limit by feel, you can take the Creditsafe limit, a percentage of it, or a rule per rating from A to E, each with its own payment terms and a maximum cap.
  • Finance teams that watch an existing customer book. Checked companies can go into a Creditsafe monitoring portfolio. Rating, limit and status changes are imported every six hours and posted on the contact.
  • Sales teams qualifying CRM leads. With CRM installed, a lead's Action menu gets Find on Creditsafe, which creates the contact, links it to the lead and orders the report in one go.

Setting it up

  1. Go to Contacts > Configuration > Creditsafe Accounts and create an account. Tick Demo Mode if you want to try it without a subscription.
  2. Choose Sandbox or Production, enter your Creditsafe Connect User Name and password, and click Connect. The subscribed countries and usage are read back from Creditsafe.
  3. Pick the report language, whether to store the PDF report, and how Contact Data from the Report is applied (fill empty fields only, overwrite or never).
  4. Set Customer Credit Limit to the Creditsafe limit, a percentage, or Rules per Rating, and add payment terms per rating if you want them changed too.
  5. Leave Monitoring and Monitor Every Checked Company on if you want alerts, and set Refresh Reports Every (days) for the scheduled refresh.
  6. Give your staff the Reader, User or Administrator access level for Creditsafe in the user settings.
  7. Open a company contact, click Find on Creditsafe, select the right result and get the credit report. You can also start from Contacts > Creditsafe > Find a Company.

Creditsafe Connector: questions

Do I need a Creditsafe subscription?

Yes, a Creditsafe Connect API subscription with its user name and password. Every report you order counts against your Creditsafe usage. Demo Mode answers with sample companies and never calls Creditsafe, so you can try the setup first.

Which Odoo apps does it need?

Only Invoicing and Contacts, so it runs on Community and Enterprise. CRM is optional: if you install it later, Add Creditsafe to CRM Leads on the account adds the lead action. The over-limit warning on sales orders appears when Sales is installed.

Can I search for a company by its Peppol ID?

Yes. If the contact has a Peppol address, the search opens with it filled in, or you can type a participant ID such as 0208:0456123789. The EAS code tells the module which country and field to search. Codes with no company register or VAT number behind them, such as GLN or D-U-N-S, are refused.

Will it overwrite data my team entered on the contact?

Not by default. Contact Data from the Report starts on Fill empty fields only. Creating directors as contacts and logging every changed field in the chatter are both off until you switch them on, and directors who leave are marked as former, never deleted.

Who can order reports, and does it handle several companies?

Users in the Creditsafe User group can order reports, Readers only see ratings and reports, and Administrators set up accounts and rules. Each Creditsafe account belongs to one Odoo company, so a multi-company database can keep them separate.

Does it decide whether to give credit for me?

No. It brings the rating, score and limit into Odoo and can set the customer credit limit and payment terms from your rules. The decision stays with your credit team, and the standard Odoo warning only warns, it doesn't block an order.
