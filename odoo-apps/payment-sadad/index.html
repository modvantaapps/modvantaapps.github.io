SADAD Payment Gateway Qatar

Accept SADAD Qatar payments in Odoo eCommerce, the customer portal, invoices, payment links and the Point of Sale. Customers pay on the SADAD Web Checkout 2.1 page with a credit card, a debit card or the SADAD wallet; Odoo checks every signature and confirms the payment with the SADAD API.

SADAD Payment Gateway for Odoo 19

What it does

The module adds SADAD as an Odoo payment provider. At checkout Odoo builds the Web Checkout 2.1 form with the SHA256 signature of your secret key and sends the customer to SADAD. When SADAD posts the callback and the webhook, Odoo verifies their checksumhash and then reads the transaction again from the SADAD API (List Transactions on your order reference) before marking the order paid, failed or still in progress. Payments SADAD settles after midnight are picked up by a scheduled check. Refunds are sent to SADAD from the Odoo payment.
