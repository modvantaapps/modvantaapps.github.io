Warranty Registration Portal

Let customers register their product warranty online and track it from the portal, with serial capture and automatic expiry. Warranty registration, product registration, serial number tracking, warranty management, extended warranty portal.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Warranty registrationProduct registrationSerial number trackingCustomer portal

What it does

Customers register their product warranty online and see it in their portal

Mark products as Warranty eligible and set how many months the warranty lasts. Customers then fill a short form on /shop/warranty/register: the product, its serial number, the purchase date and their contact details.

Each registration becomes a warranty record with a number, the serial and an expiry date calculated from the purchase date and the warranty months. Signed-in customers see their warranties under My Warranties in the portal.
