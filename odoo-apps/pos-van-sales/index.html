POS Van Sales

Every sales van gets its own stock and its own till: load it in the morning, sell on the route, unload what is left at night.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
POS van salesVan stockLoad and unload vanRoute sales

What it does

Route and distribution sellers sell from a vehicle, not from the shop. This module makes each van a small warehouse with its own point of sale, so the till sells only what is on the van and the office sees what was loaded, sold and brought back.

Features

Sales vana van record with its vehicle (Fleet), its warehouse and its assigned sellers.
Van stock locationcreated with the van, under its warehouse.
Van tillthe van's point of sale gets its own operation type, so every sale leaves the van's stock, not the shop's.
Load van and unload vaninternal transfers from the warehouse to the van and back, validated at once or by the warehouse.
Unload everything leftone click fills the unload with what is still on the van.
Van stock at the tillthe Van Stock button shows what is on the van, with search.
Block sales above van stockpayment is refused when the order asks for more than the van holds (checked against the server's stock).
Only assigned sellersthe server refuses to open a van's till for anyone else (point of sale managers excepted).
Assignment logwho was assigned to which van, from when to when, by whom.
Van on the receipt and ordersthe receipt prints the van and plate; POS orders keep the van and vehicle for grouping.
End of day reconciliationper product: loaded, sold, customer returns, unloaded and still on the van.
