Kogan Connector

Kogan integration - Kogan.com marketplace connector for Odoo 19

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Publish your products to Kogan Australia or New Zealand, keep stock and prices in step by SKU, and turn Kogan orders into sale orders. When the delivery is validated, the dispatch goes back to Kogan with the tracking number.

Paste the seller ID and token your Kogan account manager sends you. Test connection reads the Kogan categories. Start on the test (UAT) environment, then switch the connection to production with the new token.

Add products as listings, pick a category and a brand, and press Publish. Title, description, GTIN, condition, the product image, stock and price go to Kogan in batches, and Kogan's answer for each SKU shows on the listing.

Stock is the free quantity of a warehouse minus a buffer: 15 kettles with a buffer of 1 show as 14. The price comes from a pricelist, with an optional Kogan First price and RRP, your flat shipping rate and handling days. Only changes are sent.

Orders released for shipment come in as confirmed sale orders with GST-inclusive prices and shipping. Validate the delivery with a tracking number and Odoo sends the dispatch lines with the Kogan carrier name of your carrier.

Who it is for

  • Kogan.com sellers in Australia and New Zealand. Each connection is one Kogan store, either AUD for Kogan Australia or NZD for Kogan New Zealand. Orders with the status ReleasedForShipment are imported as sale orders with GST-inclusive prices and a shipping line.
  • Brands listing a catalogue on Kogan. Add products pulls in every saleable goods product with an internal reference as a listing. You pick a Kogan category, brand and condition (new, refurbished or used), then press Publish on Kogan, and the answer for each SKU appears on the listing.
  • Retailers who want stock counted from one warehouse. Stock sent to Kogan is the free quantity of the Kogan Warehouse minus the Kogan Stock Buffer. A product with 15 free units and a buffer of 1 shows 14 on Kogan.
  • Shops shipping with Australia Post, StarTrack or Couriers Please. On the Carriers tab, each Odoo carrier gets the carrier name Kogan accepts. When the outgoing delivery is validated, the dispatch goes to Kogan with that name and the tracking number.

Setting it up

  1. Ask your Kogan account manager for the seller ID and seller token for the Kogan Marketplace API.
  2. Open Sales > Kogan > Connections, create a connection, paste the Kogan Seller ID and Kogan Seller Token, and choose the Kogan Store (AUD or NZD). Set Kogan Environment to Test (UAT) while you try it, or tick Kogan Demo Mode (offline).
  3. Press Test connection. Odoo reads the Kogan categories into the Categories tab and the status turns to Connected.
  4. Under Stock and prices, set the Kogan Warehouse, the stock buffer, the Kogan Pricelist, and if you use them the Kogan First Pricelist, Kogan RRP Pricelist, flat shipping and handling days.
  5. Press Add products, then open Sales > Kogan > Listings, give each listing a category and brand, and press Publish on Kogan. If products are already live on Kogan, press Find live listings instead.
  6. On the Carriers tab, map each Odoo carrier to its Kogan carrier name, and fill the Kogan Default Carrier for carriers you leave out.
  7. Press Import orders to run a first sync by hand. From then on it repeats every 15 minutes while Kogan: Sync on Schedule is ticked.

Kogan Connector: questions

Do I need Odoo Enterprise?

No. The module is built for Odoo 19 and depends on Sales, Inventory with delivery, Invoicing and Discuss. Those apps are all in Community.

Can I test before going live?

Yes, in two ways. Kogan Environment can point at Kogan's Test (UAT) host instead of production, and Kogan Demo Mode (offline) answers from a pretend seller account inside Odoo, so no call leaves the server.

What happens when an order has a SKU Odoo doesn't know?

The Kogan order stays in the Waiting state with a message. Fix the internal reference on the product, then press Retry on the order under Sales > Kogan > Orders.

Why is my listing showing Waiting for Kogan?

Kogan handles product and stock updates in the background as a task. The next sync checks the task and marks each SKU Live or Rejected, or you can press Check Kogan answer on the listing.

Can I refund only part of an order line?

No. The Cancel on Kogan and Refund on Kogan buttons work on whole lines because Kogan does. You pick the lines and a Kogan reason such as Item not available or Damage in transit.

Are there API limits I should know about?

Kogan allows 500 calls a minute, 20,000 an hour and 100,000 a day. If Kogan answers with a rate limit error, the run stops and the next scheduled run carries on from there.
