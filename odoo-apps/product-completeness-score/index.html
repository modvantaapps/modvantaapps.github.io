Product Data Completeness Score

Automatically score product profile completeness (0-100%), display a progress bar, and highlight missing catalog fields. Product data quality score, catalog hygiene, PIM readiness.

What it does

A score on every product that tells you exactly what is missing

Half-finished products slip into the catalog all the time: no picture, no sales description, no barcode. This module gives every product a completeness score from 0 to 100% and lists the fields that are still empty, right at the top of the product form.

Scores update by themselves whenever a product is saved, and two new screens show you which products need work first.
