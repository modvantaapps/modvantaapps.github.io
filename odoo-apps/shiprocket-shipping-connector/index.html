Shiprocket Odoo Shipping Connector

Shiprocket orders, AWBs, labels and tracking from Odoo deliveries

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Validate a delivery order and Odoo creates the Shiprocket order, assigns the courier and AWB, attaches the label as a PDF and follows the tracking. Pincodes are checked and the cheapest, fastest or chosen courier priced before you ship.

The module adds Shiprocket as a delivery method in Odoo 19, built on the India Courier Base module. It checks pincode serviceability, prices the shipment on the sales order, books the shipment when the delivery order is validated, stores the AWB and label, and keeps the courier status up to date from Shiprocket tracking.

Validating the delivery creates the order at Shiprocket and assigns the courier and AWB. The AWB is stored as the tracking reference, the courier status moves to Booked and the shipping label PDF is attached to the transfer; a Shiprocket Manifest button prints the manifest. Refresh Tracking reads the Shiprocket activities - picked up, in transit, out for delivery - with time and location, and maps them to a normalised courier status.

Every call to Shiprocket is written to an API log with the operation, endpoint, HTTP status, duration and the linked transfer, with credentials and tokens masked. Pincode answers are cached so repeated checks do not call the API again.
