POS Change Payment Method After Payment

The customer paid by card, the cashier pressed Cash. Fix the payment method of the paid order in two taps, even after the receipt was printed.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Change payment methodPOS payment correctionSplit payment

What it does

Open the paid order on the Orders screen, press Change Payment, pick the right method or split the amount over several methods, and apply. No refund, no new sale, and the closing count and journal entries use the new methods.

Features

Change Payment buttonon the Orders screen of the till, next to Details, Invoice and Print Receipt.
Replace or splitmove the whole amount to another method or split it; a Fill button puts the amount left on a line.
Change given back foldedcash given and change returned are shown as one net cash amount.
Checked on the serveropen session, not invoiced, no refund, no terminal, online or customer account payment, and the new payments must equal the amount paid.
Managers only (option)restrict it to POS managers; with employee login the employee's role is checked.
Reason (option)require a reason for each change.
Logwho, when, before and after on the order, in the chatter and in Point of Sale -> Payment Corrections; Payment Changed filter.
Apply and Printsave and print the receipt again with the new methods.
Back officeChange Payment button on the paid POS order for managers.
