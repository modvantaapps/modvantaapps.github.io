ShipTime Shipping Integration

Ship with every courier of your ShipTime account from Odoo 19: the cheapest or fastest rate on the quotation, the label PDF when the delivery is validated, pickups, returns, customs invoices and tracking that updates by itself.

What it does

A delivery method type ShipTime on the standard Odoo shipping framework, connected to the public ShipTime REST API: rates from the couriers of your account, shipments, label PDFs, customs invoices, cancellation, tracking and pickups.

Features

Rate shoppingthe delivery method asks ShipTime for the rates of every courier on your account and uses the cheapest one, the fastest one, one courier service you choose, or the cheapest among the couriers you list; the courier, service and transit days are shown with the price.
Price on the quotationthe ShipTime rate before or after GST/HST, converted to the currency of the order, or a fixed price.
Label on validationvalidating the delivery order books the shipment, sets the tracking numbers on the transfer and attaches the label PDF.
Multi-pieceone line item per package with its weight and dimensions, in metric or imperial units; every piece gets its own tracking number.
Cross borderfor a shipment leaving Canada the customs invoice is built from the delivery order (HS code, country of origin, quantity, value, reason for export, who pays duties) and its PDF is attached too.
Pickup schedulingschedule the courier pickup with each shipment, or later for one or many shipments with the date, ready time, close time and pickup location; cancel a pickup from Odoo.
Return labelmake a return shipment from the receiver back to your warehouse with the same pieces; its label PDF is attached to the transfer.
Cancel and voidcancelling the shipment voids the label in ShipTime while the courier does not have it; refusals are shown with the ShipTime message.
Trackinga scheduled job reads the ShipTime tracking of every open shipment: events, in transit, delivered with the name of the signer, or exception; the courier tracking page opens from the transfer.
Courier servicesload the courier services of your account into Odoo and pick one for a delivery method.
Sandbox and productionthe Environment button switches between the ShipTime sandbox and production (OAuth token renewed automatically); every call is logged with secrets masked, and a demo mode runs the whole flow without an account.
