POS Stock Request

Ask the warehouse for stock, straight from the till

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A customer wants six and the shop floor has two. The cashier sends the products on the order to the warehouse as a stock request; the warehouse approves it and Odoo creates the internal transfer - cashiers never get inventory rights.

When a point of sale runs out, cashiers phone or message the back store, and nobody can see later what was asked, by whom, or whether it was answered.

This module adds an Ask the Warehouse button to the till actions. It sends the stocked products of the current order, with their quantities and a note, as a stock request. Requests from the counter are flagged Customer Waiting.

In the back office the warehouse sees each request with the quantity on hand at the shop and at the source location. Approve creates and confirms an internal transfer from the source to the shop; Refuse needs a reason, so the shop gets an answer.
