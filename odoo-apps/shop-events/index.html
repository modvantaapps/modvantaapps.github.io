Price Drop Alert and Shopping Funnel Report

Email shoppers when the price drops, and see where your checkout loses them

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A "Tell me if the price comes down" link on every product, an automatic price drop email, and a funnel of what shoppers did from basket to order.

Under the price on each product page there is a small link. A shopper enters an email address and is told once, by email, when the price comes down. Odoo checks prices on a schedule through your pricelist and sends the email from a template you can edit; every email has an unsubscribe link that works without logging in.

At the same time the module records what shoppers do in the shop: put in the basket, taken out, looked at the basket, started checkout, each checkout step, went to pay and placed the order. Each event is joined to Odoo's own website visitor and to the basket, with the basket value at that moment.

The Shop Events report turns that into a funnel you can pivot and chart, so you can see how many baskets reach checkout, how many reach payment and how many become orders. Events are trimmed after a number of days you choose.
