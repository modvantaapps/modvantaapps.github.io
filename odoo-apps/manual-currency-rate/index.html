Manual Currency Rate

Use the exchange rate you agreed with your customer, vendor or bank on sale orders, purchase orders, payments and expenses. The rate prices the order lines, travels to the invoice or bill, books the journal entries in your currency, values purchase receipts, prints on the documents and is locked once the document is confirmed.

What it does

Manual Currency RateManual Exchange RateCustom Currency RateAgreed Exchange RateExchange Rate on Sale OrderExchange Rate on Purchase OrderRegister Payment RateExchange Rate on ExpenseCarried to Invoice and BillJournal EntriesReceipt ValuationExchange DifferencePrinted on DocumentsRate LockChange LogDeviation AlertBank or Odoo NotationExchange Rate ReportMulti-Company

Tick Manual Rate on a quotation and type the agreed rate. Pricelist prices are converted at that rate, the sales analysis shows the order in your currency at that rate, and the invoice and down payment invoices inherit it. Orders with different rates are never merged into one invoice.

Vendor prices and product costs are converted at the agreed rate, the total in company currency follows it, the vendor bill inherits it and the receipt is valued at it. Orders with different rates are billed separately.

Set a manual rate on customer and vendor payments and in the Register Payment window. The journal entry uses it and Odoo books the exchange difference against the invoice automatically. Optionally start from the invoice rate, or pay an invoice in another currency at a set rate.

Use the rate of the card statement on an employee expense. The total in company currency and the posted entry follow it, and the expense line shows "(manual)" next to the rate.
