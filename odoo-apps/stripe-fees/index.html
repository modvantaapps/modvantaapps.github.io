Stripe Fees — Charge Customers Processing Fees

Pass the Stripe processing fee on to the customer - with clean accounting

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

When a customer pays with Stripe on your website or from an invoice in the portal, a fixed plus percentage fee is added to the amount charged, at a domestic or an international rate. Orders get a fee line so the totals match, and the fee is booked to an income account of your choice.

Card processing fees come out of your margin. Many businesses - especially B2B and low-margin ones - add the fee to what the customer pays instead.

This module adds a Stripe Fees tab to the Stripe payment provider: switch it on, set the domestic and the international fixed fee and percentage, the product used for the fee line and the income account. When a Stripe payment starts, the fee is added once to the transaction amount before Stripe is called, so the card is charged the full amount; saved cards are charged with the fee too.

Customers see a notice under the Stripe payment option at checkout and on invoice payments. A website order gets a Stripe Processing Fee line so its total equals the amount charged, and the payment's journal entry credits the fee to your fee income account while the receivable is reconciled at the original amount.
