POS Product Card

Prices on every POS product card, the original price struck through when the pricelist is cheaper, exact search, product images on cart lines and receipts, and your fast moving products first.

What it does

In Odoo 19 the product cards of the Point of Sale show a name and a picture, but no price. The search finds every product that contains the text you type, and nothing tells the cashier which products sell the most. This module puts the price on the card, shows the saving everywhere, adds exact search and highlights your fast-moving products.

Who it is for

  • Shops running pricelist promotions. When a pricelist makes something cheaper, the sales price is struck through on the card, on the cart line and on the customer display, with the saving shown. You can print the saving on the receipt too.
  • Stores with near-identical product codes. A hardware or spare parts shop may have CM-1, CM-10 and CM-12 side by side. Exact match or Starts with search stops the cashier wading through every code that merely contains the text.
  • Busy counters with a handful of top sellers. Products you mark, plus whatever sold most over the last few days, come first on the screen with a coloured border and get their own Fast Moving button in the category bar.
  • Owners who want margin visible to managers. The cost can appear on each card, and by default only while a POS manager is the active cashier, so regular staff don't see it.

Features

Price on every cardcomputed like the cart line: pricelist of the order, fiscal position and the tax display of the till.
Original price struck throughwhen the pricelist beats the sales price, the sales price is crossed out on the card. Full-price products are left untouched.
Saving on cart lineseach line sold below the sales price shows the original unit price struck through and the amount saved.
Saving on the receiptthe receipt repeats the original price and the saving (its own switch).
Saving on the customer displaythe customer sees the original price and "You save" while the order is built.
Cost on the cardoptional, and by default only while a POS manager is the cashier.
Exact searchContains (Odoo default), Starts with or Exact match on the name, internal reference and barcode. A button next to the search bar switches the mode; Search more asks the server with the same mode.
Search categories and tagsoptionally find products by the name of their POS category or product tag.
Product image on cart lines and receiptsa small picture on each line, so cashier and customer check the basket at a glance.
Fast moving productsthe products you mark and the best sellers of the last days (quantity sold, all tills or this till) come first, with a colored border and a Fast badge.
Fast Moving tabone tap in the category bar shows only the fast-moving products.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module, then go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings and pick the point of sale. The new block is called Product Cards and Search.
  2. Under Prices on Product Cards, choose where the original price appears: on cards, cart lines, receipts and the customer display. Turn on Cost on cards and Cost for managers only if you want the cost shown.
  3. Under Exact Search, set the Search mode to Contains, Starts with or Exact match, and decide on Mode button on the till and Search categories and tags.
  4. Under Product Images on Lines, switch on images for cart lines, receipts, or both.
  5. Under Fast Moving Products, choose whether to show marked products, the top sellers worked out from sales, both, or nothing. Then set the period in days, how many top sellers to include, whether to count all points of sale, Show them first, the Fast Moving tab and a highlight colour. Save.
  6. To mark a product by hand, open it and tick POS Fast Moving on the Point of Sale tab.

POS Product Card: questions

Does it change what customers pay?

No. The card shows what your pricelist, fiscal position and tax display already work out. The order itself is priced by Odoo exactly as before.

How are the top sellers picked?

By quantity sold in paid and posted POS orders of your company over the period you set (30 days by default), either across all tills or just this one. Returned quantities aren't counted, combo products are skipped, and the list is rebuilt each time the POS loads.

What exactly does Exact match compare?

The product name, internal reference or barcode, and those of its variants, after normalising the text so upper and lower case don't matter. Search more, which asks the server, uses the same mode as the till.

Can I limit how many top sellers are shown?

Yes, between 1 and 200, and the period between 1 and 3650 days. The highlight colour has to be a hex value like #e8590c.

Does it need anything besides Point of Sale?

No. It only depends on Point of Sale and works on Community and Enterprise, in shops and restaurants.
