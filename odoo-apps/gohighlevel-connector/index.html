Odoo GoHighLevel integration

GoHighLevel Connector

Your agency runs funnels and pipelines in HighLevel. Quotes, invoices and projects happen in Odoo. This keeps the contacts, deals and appointments the same in both.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
GoHighLevelHighLevel CRMGHLContactsOpportunitiesPipelinesNotesTags

What it does

Paste a Private Integration Token and the sub-account ID, or sign in through your own HighLevel Marketplace app, and press Test Connection. Odoo reads the sub-account, its users, pipelines and stages, lost reasons and calendars. Then every contact comes across with e-mail, phone, address, company name, owner, source and tags, each opportunity lands in the right pipeline stage with its value and status, contact notes appear in the chatter and booked appointments show up in Odoo Calendar with the contact and the assigned user. After the first run only contacts changed since the last one are read.
