Smart Alerts

Warn your team on the record, before the mistake is made

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Rule-based alert banners on the form of any Odoo document: live while editing, as a popup when the record opens, with an acknowledgement logged per user, and as badges in list and kanban views. No view editing and no code.

The things your team must not forget - a large quotation without payment terms, a contact without email, a company without Tax ID - are usually written in a manual nobody opens. Odoo has no general way to put a warning on the record itself.

Smart Alerts lets an Alerts Manager pick a model, build the condition with Odoo's standard domain editor (related fields included), write the message with {field} placeholders and choose a level: info, success, warning or danger. Every matching record then shows the message as a banner at the top of its form.

A rule can also open as a popup when the record is opened, require the user to acknowledge it (logged per user and record), and show as a badge in list and kanban views. Banners follow the record while it is being edited, before it is saved. Rules are evaluated with the rights of the user who looks at the record.
