Wallee Payment Gateway

wallee and PostFinance Checkout Payment Provider for Odoo 19

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
automated testsone-time price

What it does

Take payments through the wallee platform or PostFinance Checkout (the same platform) in the Odoo website shop, on quotations and on the invoice portal: cards, TWINT, PostFinance Pay and the other methods of your space. Hosted payment page, Lightbox overlay or iFrame inside your checkout, manual and partial capture, void, full and partial refunds, saved cards, signed webhooks and an hourly status sync.

Add your space ID, application user ID and authentication key, then click Test Connection, Sync Payment Methods and Set Up Webhooks. Odoo reads the payment method configurations of the space, links the matching Odoo payment methods (card, TWINT, PostFinance Pay, PayPal...) and creates signed webhook listeners for transactions, refunds, captures and voids. At checkout the customer pays on the hosted payment page, in a Lightbox over your shop or in an iFrame on the checkout page. When the customer comes back and when a webhook arrives, Odoo reads the transaction again from the API before it confirms anything. Payments are captured at once or authorised and captured later (in full or partially), voided, and refunded in full or in parts from Odoo. Cards can be saved and charged again with the customer not present.
