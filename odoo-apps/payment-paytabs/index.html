PayTabs Payment Gateway

Accept mada and card payments with PayTabs in your Odoo shop

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The PayTabs payment page as a redirect, inside your checkout or as card fields on your page; Odoo reads every payment back from PayTabs before confirming the order, and saved cards, captures and refunds run from the standard Odoo screens.

The module adds PayTabs as a payment provider on the standard Odoo payment framework, for the eCommerce checkout, portal invoices and payment links. Choose the region of your PayTabs profile - Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt, Oman, Jordan, Kuwait, Iraq, Morocco, Qatar or Global - and the module calls that PayTabs server.

The customer pays on the PayTabs page and comes back; the posted result is checked with the signature made with your server key and the payment is read again from PayTabs before the order is confirmed. Cards can be saved as PayTabs tokens, card payments can be authorised and captured later, and refunds are sent from the Odoo refund button.
