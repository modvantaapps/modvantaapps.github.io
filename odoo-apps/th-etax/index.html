Thailand e-Tax Invoice & e-Receipt

Thailand e-Tax Invoice and e-Receipt for Odoo 19: ETDA XML checked against the official schema, PDF/A-3 with the XML inside, and e-Tax Invoice by Email to the Time Stamp system.

What it does

Thai VAT registered businesses can issue tax invoices, receipts, debit notes and credit notes electronically. This module turns confirmed Odoo invoices into the ETDA e-Tax XML, embeds it in a PDF/A-3 invoice, and sends the e-Tax Invoice by Email exactly as the Revenue Department rules ask: one document per email, to the customer, with a copy to the ETDA Time Stamp system and the required subject line.

Features

ETDA XML, schema checkedTaxInvoice and DebitCreditNote CrossIndustryInvoice documents (ETDA Rec. 3-2560, version 2.0); every file is validated against the ETDA XSD that ships with the module before it is saved.
Document typesTax Invoice (388), Invoice / Tax Invoice (T02), Receipt / Tax Invoice (T03), Delivery Order / Tax Invoice (T04), Debit Note (80) and Credit Note (81), with a company default.
PDF/A-3 with the XML insidethe Odoo invoice PDF is converted to PDF/A-3 and carries the XML as an embedded file; both are attached to the invoice.
Debit and credit notesreason codes of the ETDA standard (CDNG01... DBNS99), the original tax invoice referenced in the XML and in the [CRN] / [DBN] subject.
Cancellation and replacementsend the [CIV] cancellation email, and issue a replacement invoice that references the cancelled one ([INV][new][old], reason code TIVC).
Optional XAdES signaturesign the XML with a certificate stored in Odoo (XAdES-BES, RSA-SHA256) for submission channels that need a signed file.
Thai tax ID and branches13 digit tax ID check (mod 11) for your company and customers, 5 digit branch codes (00000 = head office) sent as TXID.
