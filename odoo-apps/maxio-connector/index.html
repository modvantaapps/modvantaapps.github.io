Maxio Connector

Maxio Advanced Billing (formerly Chargify) bills your subscribers. Odoo books the invoices, payments and credit notes, to the cent.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
MaxioAdvanced BillingChargifyCustomersProducts and componentsSubscriptionsInvoicesPayments

What it does

Enter your Maxio site subdomain and API key, US or EU hosting. Each run asks Maxio only for what changed since the last one. Customers become contacts with their organization, address, VAT number and reference. Product families become product categories, and products and components become service products with their price, interval, trial and unit. Subscriptions are kept per customer with the product, components and quantities, period dates, coupons, total revenue and a monthly MRR figure. Invoices are rebuilt line by line with coupon discounts and taxes, and the Odoo total is checked against Maxio's before posting. The payments on each invoice are registered and reconciled. Credit notes are applied to the invoices Maxio applied them to, and their refunds are registered.

Features

Customersorganization or person, e-mail, phone, address with state, VAT number, reference, tax exempt flag; CC e-mails added as invoice contacts
Catalogproduct families as categories; products with price, interval, trial and initial charge; components with unit price, kind and unit name
Couponspercentage or amount, recurring or not, number of periods and end date
Subscriptionsstate, product, components and allocated quantities, period and trial dates, next assessment, coupons, cancellation message, total revenue, balance, MRR
Invoices to the centone line per Maxio line item with its service period, a discount line under each discounted item, Odoo taxes with the same rate; if totals differ, Maxio's tax amounts are booked
Discount accountcoupon discounts can go to their own account instead of the income account
Invoice statusdraft and pending invoices wait in draft, voided and canceled ones are cancelled, open invoices past their due date are flagged
Paymentseach payment on a Maxio invoice is registered once, with its gateway and transaction ID
Credit notesbooked as customer credit notes, applied to the invoices Maxio applied them to, refunds registered as outgoing payments
Odoo to Maxiosend a contact as a customer, or a product as a monthly product in the family of its category
Signed webhooksone button adds the endpoint in Maxio; every call is checked against the HMAC-SHA-256 signature made with your site shared key, then read again from Maxio and handled once
Queue and logdocuments that cannot be booked yet wait and are retried with a growing delay; info log lines older than 90 days are cleaned up
