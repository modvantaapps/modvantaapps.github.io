What it does

Accept Rapyd payments in Odoo: the customer pays on the Rapyd Collect hosted checkout page with a card, an ewallet, a bank redirect, a bank transfer or cash, from the website checkout, an invoice, a payment link or the Point of Sale QR code. Every request is signed, every webhook signature is checked, lost webhooks are recovered from Rapyd, and refunds, captures and voids are sent from Odoo.

Rapyd Checkout opens the Rapyd hosted page with the payment method categories you enable on the provider; the Card method opens it with cards only. The order lines are shown on the Rapyd page when they add up to the amount, and the page opens in the customer's language when Rapyd supports it.

When the customer comes back, Odoo reads the checkout page and its payment from Rapyd with a signed request: the return link is never trusted. Webhooks are accepted only with a valid signature, and a repeated or late webhook does not change a paid order.

A bank transfer or cash payment stays pending until the customer pays. The customer sees a pending message instead of a page that keeps waiting, the payment codes Rapyd gives are stored on the transaction, and the order is confirmed by the webhook or by the scheduled status check.

With Odoo's Point of Sale online payment, add Rapyd to an online payment method: the register shows a QR code, the customer pays on the Rapyd page from the phone, and the register shows Payment Successful as soon as Rapyd confirms. Orders without a customer use the default country of the provider.
