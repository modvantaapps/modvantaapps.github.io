bol.com Connector

Sell on bol.com from Odoo - offers, FBR and FBB orders, returns and bol.com invoices in one place

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A bol.com Odoo Integration for sellers in the Netherlands and Belgium. Offers are matched to your products by EAN, orders arrive as normal Odoo sales orders, and returns, cancellation requests and bol.com commission invoices are handled without leaving Odoo.

The connector links one or more bol.com seller accounts to Odoo through the official bol.com Retailer API. Your bol.com offers become records linked to Odoo products by EAN. bol.com orders - both Fulfilled by Retailer (FBR) and Fulfilled by bol.com (FBB / LVB) - are imported as Odoo sales orders with the bol.com order number, the customer and the right warehouse.

Features

From there Odoo does the reststock and prices go back to bol.com, customer cancellation requests show up as activities on the order, returns are listed with their RMA number and reason, and the invoices bol.com sends you for commission and fees become draft vendor bills. A dashboard per account shows open work at a glance, and every call is written to a sync log.
