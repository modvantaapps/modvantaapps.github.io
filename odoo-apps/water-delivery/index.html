Water Delivery Management

Run a bottled water or water jar delivery business on Odoo: routes with a stop sequence, standing orders, daily runs planned every night, a returnable bottle deposit ledger per customer, empty bottle collection, bottle deposit refunds, coupon books, a prepaid wallet, water dispenser rental billed monthly, production batches with a release gate, and drivers who confirm every stop in the backend or on their phone through the portal. Reconciling a run invoices the cash stops, registers the cash and posts the stock moves.

What it does

A water business lends bottles, it does not sell them

Opening balance, delivered, empties collected, written off, count adjustment, deposit charged, refunded or kept. Balances are sums of locked ledger lines.

When a customer ends up holding more bottles than they paid a deposit for, the deposit for the difference is charged on that stop. An exchange of empties for full is not charged twice.

Refund only deposits for bottles the customer no longer holds - the wizard refuses more. A credit note is posted and paid out in cash, or the money goes into the prepaid wallet.

Routes with driver, van, capacity, area and working days. Drag stops into order; the run follows it. Open the open stops in Google Maps in that order.

Features

Units by serialin stock, on rental, in service, scrapped. Monthly fee invoiced by a daily job for every started month, skipped when the cooler is included in the water price.
