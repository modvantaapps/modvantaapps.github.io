Mauritius Payroll

Mauritius Payroll for Odoo 19: MRA PAYE, CSG, NSF, Training Levy and PRGF

What it does

Mauritian payroll on Odoo Community. PAYE runs on the MRA cumulative system with the EDF reliefs spread over 13 periods, and the payslip also carries CSG, NSF, the HRDC training levy and PRGF. You get a monthly statement to check your MRA return against.

Each payslip works out PAYE the way the MRA asks employers to: the emoluments of the income year so far, less k/13 of the reliefs on the employee's EDF, taxed on the cumulative brackets of that pay period, minus the PAYE already withheld. The prescribed end-of-year bonus counts as a 13th period. An employee without an EDF pays the flat 15% above Rs 38,462 (or 20% on request), and director's fees can be set to the flat rate.

On the same payslip you get CSG at 1.5% / 3% for the employee and 3% / 6% for the employer around the Rs 50,000 basic wage, CSG on the bonus worked out separately, NSF at 1% / 2.5% between the insurable minimum and maximum, the 1.5% training levy and the 4.5% PRGF. Age limits for NSF (18 to 65) and the levy (up to 70) come from the employee's birthday.

The 2026-2027 income year has the 0 / 10 / 20 / 35% bands from the EDF notes. The 2025-2026 year keeps the 15% Fair Share Contribution above Rs 12 million, worked on its cumulative thresholds. Every rate sits in an editable record per income year.

Who it is for

  • Employers in Mauritius already on the Modvanta HR and Payroll engine. This is an add-on to mdv_hr_payroll, not a payroll engine of its own. Once Mauritian Statutory Payroll is ticked on the company, every payslip you compute also gets PAYE, Fair Share Contribution, CSG, NSF, the HRDC training levy and PRGF.
  • Payroll staff who file the monthly MRA return by hand. The monthly statement adds up PAYE, CSG, NSF, levy and PRGF per employee from the payslips and gives a CSV register with the NIC, NCID or TAN of each person. You key the figures into the MRA portal yourself; the module never sends anything to the MRA.
  • Accounting firms running payroll for several Mauritian clients. Each company has its own ERN and BRN fields, and the statements, EDFs and pay component mapping are kept per company. Rates sit in one editable record per income year, 2025-2026 and 2026-2027 are loaded.
  • Companies starting Odoo payroll in the middle of an income year. The EDF form has opening balances for pay periods already run, emoluments, PAYE and Fair Share withheld elsewhere. The cumulative PAYE then picks up from those totals instead of starting again in July.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. Companies whose country is Mauritius get Mauritian Statutory Payroll ticked for you; for others, open the company form, Mauritian Payroll tab, tick it and fill in the ERN and BRN.
  2. On each employee, open the Mauritian Payroll tab and fill in the NIC / NCID / TAN, the PAYE method (cumulative or director's fees), the flat rate, and untick CSG, NSF or PRGF for staff who are exempt.
  3. Go to Employees > HR & Payroll > Mauritian Payroll > Employee Declaration Forms and record each EDF: income year, date received, dependents, retired or disabled, other deductions, resident and first employment.
  4. Check Mauritian Payroll > Configuration > Pay Component Mapping so each payslip line code is treated as basic wage, PRGF remuneration, taxable only, prescribed bonus, not taxable, or a cut to the basic wage.
  5. Open Mauritian Payroll > Configuration > Income Tax Bands and Rates and compare the 2026-2027 record with the MRA tables for your year. Use Copy for Next Income Year when a new year starts.
  6. Compute a payslip under HR & Payroll > Payroll > Payslips. The Mauritian Statutory block shows the PAYE method, cumulative emoluments, reliefs, chargeable income, tax and each contribution.
  7. At month end, create a record in PAYE, CSG and NSF Statements, choose Monthly or Income year, and press Compute to get the totals and the CSV register.

Mauritius Payroll: questions

Does it need Odoo Enterprise payroll?

No. It depends only on the Modvanta HR & Payroll engine (mdv_hr_payroll), which runs on Odoo 19 Community. It does not use or extend Enterprise hr_payroll.

How does it work out PAYE each month?

It takes the emoluments of the income year so far, takes off k/13 of the EDF reliefs, taxes the result on the cumulative brackets of period k and subtracts PAYE already withheld. A negative result is set to zero, so there is no refund. A prescribed end-of-year bonus counts as an extra period.

What if an employee has not handed in an EDF?

The payslip uses the flat rate set on the employee (15%, or 20% on request) above the exempt limit of Rs 38,462 a month. In July and August it first looks for last year's EDF and uses that until the new one arrives.

Which age limits does it apply?

From the employee's birthday: NSF from 18 until the 65th birthday, the training levy until 70, PRGF from 16. With no birthday on file, the contributions are charged.

Are the 2026-2027 figures final?

The annual bands of 0, 10, 20 and 35% come from the MRA EDF notes. The monthly 20% bracket and the exempt limit are derived from them the way the 2025-2026 schedule does it, so check one test month against the MRA tables before paying real salaries.

What is left out?

Weekly and fortnightly pay, several payments in one period, tax borne by the employer for non-residents, public-sector CSG rates, household employees, the end-of-year bonus calculation itself and journal entries for payroll.
