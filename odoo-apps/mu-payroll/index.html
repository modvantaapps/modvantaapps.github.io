Mauritius Payroll

Mauritius Payroll for Odoo 19: MRA PAYE, CSG, NSF, Training Levy and PRGF

What it does

Mauritian payroll on Odoo Community. PAYE runs on the MRA cumulative system with the EDF reliefs spread over 13 periods, and the payslip also carries CSG, NSF, the HRDC training levy and PRGF. You get a monthly statement to check your MRA return against.

Each payslip works out PAYE the way the MRA asks employers to: the emoluments of the income year so far, less k/13 of the reliefs on the employee's EDF, taxed on the cumulative brackets of that pay period, minus the PAYE already withheld. The prescribed end-of-year bonus counts as a 13th period. An employee without an EDF pays the flat 15% above Rs 38,462 (or 20% on request), and director's fees can be set to the flat rate.

On the same payslip you get CSG at 1.5% / 3% for the employee and 3% / 6% for the employer around the Rs 50,000 basic wage, CSG on the bonus worked out separately, NSF at 1% / 2.5% between the insurable minimum and maximum, the 1.5% training levy and the 4.5% PRGF. Age limits for NSF (18 to 65) and the levy (up to 70) come from the employee's birthday.

The 2026-2027 income year has the 0 / 10 / 20 / 35% bands from the EDF notes. The 2025-2026 year keeps the 15% Fair Share Contribution above Rs 12 million, worked on its cumulative thresholds. Every rate sits in an editable record per income year.
