EU Pay Transparency

The EU Pay Transparency Directive (EU) 2023/970 in Odoo Employees: the gender pay gap report with every Article 9 indicator, categories of workers of equal value, pay ranges, worker pay requests and the joint pay assessment.

Gender pay gap reportEqual payPay transparencyArticle 9 indicatorsPay rangesJoint pay assessment

What it does

Member States had to transpose Directive (EU) 2023/970 by 7 June 2026. Employers with 150 workers or more report their gender pay gap by 7 June 2027 on the pay of 2026, employers with 100 to 149 workers from 2031, and every employer must answer a worker's request for pay information within two months. This module computes the figures from the employees, their contracts (wage and working schedule) and the variable pay you record or import, so no spreadsheet is needed. It works on Odoo Community: Payroll is not required.

Categories of workers of equal value (Article 4): score each category from 1 to 5 on skills, effort, responsibility and working conditions, with your own weights; set on the job position and kept on every contract version so past reports stay right.

Pay ranges and pay criteria (Articles 5 and 6): range per category, how pay is set and how it progresses; one click writes the range into the job description, a filter shows the jobs still without one.

Gender pay gap report (Article 9) for a calendar year: (a) gender pay gap, (b) gap in complementary or variable components, (c) median gap, (d) median gap in variable components, (e) women and men receiving variable pay, (f) women and men in each quartile pay band, (g) the gap by category of workers split into basic and variable pay.

Features

Pay componentsbonuses, commissions, overtime, allowances and benefits in kind, typed in or imported; the monthly wage is the ordinary basic pay.
The 5 percent rulecategories with a gap of 5 percent or more are flagged; justify them with objective, gender-neutral criteria or mark them remedied; a six-month remedy deadline and an activity are set on submission.
Privacyaverages of small groups are not disclosed (minimum group size), individual figures only for the administrator group, multi-company rules; yearly notice to every worker of the right to information (Article 7(3)); Excel and PDF exports.
