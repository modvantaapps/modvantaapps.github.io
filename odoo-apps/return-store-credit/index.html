Store Credit on Returns & Customer Wallet

Refund returns as store credit - and let customers spend it at your checkout

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Turn a customer credit note into wallet credit with one click (or automatically). Customers see their balance in the portal, apply it in the cart, and the wallet is debited only when the order is confirmed - all in a ledger that cannot be rewritten.

When goods come back, many shops prefer to keep the money in the business: the customer gets store credit instead of a card refund. Odoo has credit notes, but no customer wallet a shopper can spend on the website.

This module adds a store credit wallet per customer, company and currency. Issue Store Credit on a posted customer credit note puts its amount in the wallet (the accounting stays on the credit note). Each entry is recorded in a ledger with its source, and can expire after a number of months.

Logged-in customers see Store Credit in their portal, and the cart shows a box to apply any part of their balance. The credit becomes a negative line on the order, capped at the balance and at the order total, and the wallet is debited once, at confirmation - an abandoned cart never burns credit.
