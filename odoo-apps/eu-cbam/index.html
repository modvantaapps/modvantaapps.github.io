EU CBAM for Odoo 19: Embedded Emissions on Imports, Quarterly Report XML, Default Values and Certificate Estimates

What it does

EU importers of steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers and hydrogen, indirect customs representatives, and Odoo partners who need CBAM data next to purchasing and inventory.

The 569 codes of the Commission goods list with sector, category and whether indirect emissions count.

CN code, TARIC code, net mass per unit, country of origin and usual installation on each product.
