POS All in One Retail

POS All in One Retail: the retail features of Odoo Point of Sale in one module, each one a switch

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Reorder and return rules, fixed and percent discounts with a maximum and a manager PIN, bag charges, packaging prices, stock on product cards, lot auto selection, internal transfers, pay later credit limits, settling invoices at the till, customer history, sale and purchase orders from the till, foreign currency cash, hidden buttons, auto lock, theme styles, list view, item count and PDF reports.

A retail shop usually ends up installing a dozen small point of sale add-ons, each with its own settings page and its own way of doing things. This module puts the features a shop asks for most in one place: one settings tab on the point of sale, every feature a switch, all of them off when you install it.

Everything the cashier sees is on the till you already use: extra buttons in the Actions list, a price and a stock badge on the product cards, a currency button on the payment screen, a Reorder button on the order list.

The rules that protect money are checked by the server when the order is saved, not only on the screen: a discount over the limit, a refund or a lower price without a manager's PIN, a sale with stock short when the till is set to refuse, a pay later payment over the customer's credit limit. A till that is reloaded, offline or scripted cannot get around them.

PINs are stored as salted hashes. Every approval and every wrong PIN is logged, and five wrong PINs in five minutes pause the check for five minutes.

Who it is for

  • Clothing, gift and hardware shops with a mixed till. These shops often pile up a dozen small POS add-ons for discounts, bags, stock badges and customer lookups. Here each of those is a switch on one tab of the point of sale, and all of them start off.
  • Owners who want discounts and refunds approved. A discount above the limit, a refund, a price below the pricelist or closing the session can each require a manager's PIN. The server checks the rule again when the order is saved, so reloading the till doesn't get around it.
  • Shops that sell on account to regular customers. Pay later payments are held to the customer's credit limit or the shop default, and customers can settle their open invoices at the till with one of the shop's cash or bank methods.
  • Counters that also raise quotes and purchase orders. The cashier can turn the cart into a sale order or quotation, or into a purchase order for a supplier, and request an internal transfer from the same screen.

Setting it up

  1. For each manager who approves, open Settings > Users & Companies > Users, pick the user and set a POS PIN of 4 to 8 digits on the POS PIN tab. A personal discount limit can go there too.
  2. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Point of Sales, open a shop and click the All in One Retail tab.
  3. Tick the features you want, grouped under Orders & Returns, Discounts & Pricing, Manager PIN, Stock, Customers, Sales and Purchase, Payments, and Access and Look.
  4. Fill the options that appear: Maximum Discount (%) and Approving Managers, Bag Products, the stock location and When Stock Is Short rule, Default Customer Record, Default Credit Limit, Accepted Currencies, Restricted Users and Lock After (minutes).
  5. To sell in packs or boxes at their own price, set them up under Point of Sale > Products > Unit Barcodes and Prices.
  6. Open the register. Check approvals under Point of Sale > Reporting > Manager Approvals and print PDFs from Point of Sale > Reporting > POS Reports (All in One).

POS All in One Retail: questions

Which Odoo apps does it need?

Point of Sale, POS Sale, Purchase and Inventory, all available in Community. No outside account or paid service is involved.

Will it clash with single POS modules I already use?

When one of our single POS modules that does the same job is installed, the matching switch here is turned off, can't be ticked, and the tab tells you which module handles it instead.

How are manager PINs stored?

As salted hashes on the user, so they can't be read back, only replaced or cleared by a POS manager. Every approval and every wrong PIN is logged, and after too many wrong PINs the check pauses for five minutes.

How does a fixed amount discount affect taxes?

A fixed amount on the order is spread over the lines as a percentage discount, so taxes and reports stay consistent with Odoo's normal figures.

What can the credit note rule do on returns?

Keep Odoo's default, always create a credit note when the original order was invoiced, or never invoice a return. The server enforces whichever you choose.

Which reports are included?

Sales summary, payments, top products, top customers, top categories, sales by cashier, day-wise sales and a session (Z) report, as PDF for a shop and date range.
