What it does

From 1 October 2026 every VAT-registered person in Sri Lanka issues tax invoices in the format of Gazette Extraordinary No. 2481/22. This module prints Odoo customer invoices in that format and numbers them YYMMM_QQQQ_XXXXX when they are posted.

It also prepares the VAT return worksheet in the layout of form Asmt_VAT_001_E from the Sri Lanka tax grids of your posted entries, and writes the IRD VAT schedule CSV files with the column headers of the IRD templates, checking every row against the rules built into those templates.

Nothing is filed with or sent to the Inland Revenue Department: you upload the files and file the return yourself on IRD e-Services.
