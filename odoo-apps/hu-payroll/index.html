Hungary Payroll

Hungary Payroll for Odoo 19: SZJA, TB, Szocho, Family Allowance and the 08 Return Worksheet

What it does

Hungarian statutory payroll (berszamfejtes) on Odoo Community: the SZJA advance after the tax base allowances in the statutory 2026 order, TB 18.5% with the family contribution allowance, szocho 13%, and the 2608M rows of each person numbered as in the NAV filling guide.

Mothers under 30, mothers of two, three or four children, under 25, personal, first marriage, then family allowance, each limited to the remaining base.

Without amount limit; mothers of two only when born in 1986 or later in 2026.

Up to 715,765 HUF a month from the birth date to the month of the 25th birthday, unless declined.

107,600 HUF for severe disability and 33,335 HUF for first marriage, shareable between spouses.

Who it is for

  • Hungarian employers with families on the payroll. A company in Budapest or Debrecen has to apply each worker's adoeloleg-nyilatkozat: the family allowance per beneficiary dependant, the mothers' allowances and the first marriage allowance. The payslip applies them in the statutory order, each limited to what's left of the base.
  • Businesses employing people under 25. The under-25 allowance is taken from the employee's birth date up to the month of their 25th birthday, so younger staff get it without anyone tracking ages by hand, unless the employee has declined it.
  • Payroll staff filling in the monthly 08 return. Instead of adding up rows from payslips, the payroll clerk computes a worksheet with the 2608M rows per person and the 2608A totals, then types those figures into ANYK or ONYA.
  • Small firms that want to see the full employment cost. Next to the SZJA advance and the 18.5% TB, the payslip shows the employer's 13% szocho, so the owner sees what each worker really costs each month.

Setting it up

  1. Install Modvanta HR & Payroll first, then add the Hungary module. Companies located in Hungary have Hungarian statutory payroll ticked automatically.
  2. On the company form, open the Hungarian Payroll tab and enter the Tax Number (adoszam), the first eight digits of the company tax number.
  3. On each employee's Hungarian Payroll tab, fill in Tax ID (adoazonosito jel) and Social Security Number (TAJ), then the declaration: Mothers' Allowance, Severe Disability, First Marriage Allowance and its share, and for the family allowance the Dependants, Beneficiary Dependants, ill dependants and Family Allowance Share Claimed %.
  4. Check Employees > HR & Payroll > Hungarian Payroll > Configuration > Pay Component Mapping so each pay code is treated as wage income, tax free or reducing.
  5. Create and compute the payslips for the month from HR & Payroll > Payroll > Payslips, then check the Hungarian tax block on each one. The Hungarian Statutory block lists the allowances, the SZJA advance, TB and szocho.
  6. Open Hungarian Payroll > 08 Return Worksheets, create the month, press Compute and then Prepare 2608 Worksheet to get the CSV.
  7. Enter the figures in the 2608 return in ANYK or ONYA, submit it with your own access, and press Mark Filed on the worksheet.

Hungary Payroll: questions

Does it need Odoo Enterprise Payroll?

No. It's built on Modvanta HR & Payroll, a Community payroll base that installs with it. Odoo Enterprise's hr_payroll isn't required or used.

Can it send the 08 return to NAV?

No. It produces a worksheet and CSV with the row numbers of the NAV filling guide, and you enter them in ANYK or ONYA yourself. An ANYK import file isn't generated.

What happens if the family allowance is larger than the tax base?

The unused part turns into the family contribution allowance: 15% of what the base couldn't absorb is set against the TB contribution, up to the TB itself. The worksheet shows this on the related 2608M rows.

How are tax ID and TAJ checked?

The tax ID must be ten digits starting with 8 and the TAJ nine digits. The worksheet also checks the company tax number before it prepares the file, so missing numbers show up before filing rather than at NAV.

Which situations does it not handle?

CSED and GYED allowances, szocho reliefs such as those for career starters, the minimum contribution base for part-time work, cafeteria and fringe benefits, non-residents and the annual tax settlement are not covered. Use your payroll adviser for those cases.

How do I update the rates for next year?

Every rate and allowance amount sits on a dated record under SZJA, TB and Szocho Rates. Press Copy for Next Year and type the new values. The amounts change with each budget, so confirm them with your accountant or NAV's current information booklets before the first January payroll.
