What it does

Auctions run on your own products, your own website theme and your own checkout. The winner pays the winning price through the payment providers you already use, the order is a normal Odoo sales order, and delivery and invoicing follow your usual flow. Bids are an audit trail: every bid, every automatic proxy bid and every extension is recorded.

Everything you need to run auctions on your Odoo website, in one module.

Bidders leave a maximum and the engine bids for them, one increment at a time, only as far as needed. Equal maximums go to the earlier bidder, and a maximum above the reserve lifts the price straight to the reserve.

A fixed increment per auction, or a price-range increment table (for example +1 under 50, +5 from 100, +25 from 1,000) shared by every auction of the company.

Bids stay hidden until the end, each bidder can only raise their own sealed bid, and the highest bid wins (earliest on a tie). The history is revealed when the auction closes.

Features

A bid in the last minutes pushes the end timechoose the window, the extension and how many times it may happen. The page shows that the auction was extended.
