SumUp Payment Provider

Send customers to the SumUp hosted page or show the SumUp payment widget inside your checkout; Odoo confirms the order after reading the checkout back from SumUp, saves cards, refunds from Odoo and keeps a full API log.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The module adds SumUp as a payment provider (the payment acquirer of older Odoo versions) on the standard Odoo payment framework, for the eCommerce checkout, portal invoices and payment links. It is a SumUp integration for online payment only. Cards are always offered; Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal and the local methods are used when SumUp switches them on for your account country. Test Connection reads your business name, country, merchant currency and the payment methods SumUp offers you.

With the hosted page the customer pays on SumUp and comes back; with the payment widget the SumUp card form is shown in your payment page and the card goes straight to SumUp. Odoo reads the checkout from SumUp before confirming, saved cards are charged without typing anything, refunds are sent from Odoo, and every call is written to an API log.

Who it is for

  • Small shops that already use SumUp in store. If you take card payments with SumUp at the counter, this lets the same merchant account take online orders from your Odoo webshop, portal invoices and payment links.
  • European webshops selling in one currency. A SumUp account takes payments in the currency of the country it's registered in. The module reads that currency from SumUp and, by default, only offers SumUp when the order is in it.
  • Shops with regular buyers. With the Payment Widget checkout, customers can save their card and pay with it next time without typing the number again.
  • Merchants who want to try before connecting a live account. Demo Mode fakes SumUp inside Odoo with no key, no network call and no money. You can walk through paid, pending, declined and cancelled outcomes before you go live.

Setting it up

  1. Go to Invoicing > Configuration > Payment Providers and open SumUp.
  2. To try it first, set the state to Test Mode and tick Demo Mode, then place a test order on your shop.
  3. For a real account, untick Demo Mode and paste the Secret API Key (SumUp Dashboard > Settings > For Developers > Toolkit > API Keys) and your Merchant Code.
  4. Press Test Connection. Odoo fills in Business Name, Merchant Country, Merchant Currency, Sandbox Account and Payment Methods at SumUp.
  5. Choose the Checkout: SumUp hosted page (redirect) or Payment Widget on my website. Pick the widget if you want saved cards.
  6. Check Checkout Validity (minutes), Merchant Currency Only, Deactivate Abandoned Checkouts and Keep the API Log (days).
  7. Activate in Odoo the payment methods SumUp lists for your account, enter your live key and set the provider to Enabled.

SumUp Payment Provider: questions

Does this work with a SumUp card reader at the Point of Sale?

No. This module is for online payments only: the website checkout, portal invoices and payment links. Card readers at the till are a separate POS add-on.

Which Odoo edition do I need?

Community or Enterprise both work. It depends on the standard payment and account_payment modules and the Python requests library.

Can I use Demo Mode on a live provider?

No. Odoo refuses to save the provider if Demo Mode is ticked and the state is Enabled. Demo Mode only runs while the provider is in Test Mode, so untick it before you go live.

What happens if a customer abandons the payment?

When a payment fails, is cancelled or expires in Odoo, the module deactivates the SumUp checkout so an old browser tab can't pay the same order later. A scheduled action runs every 15 minutes to confirm paid checkouts and close abandoned ones.

Can I refund from Odoo?

Yes. Use the Refund button on the payment for a full or partial refund. The refund appears as a child transaction, and there's a Check Status on SumUp button on each transaction to read its latest state.

Can I capture payments later?

No. There's no authorize-then-capture option in this module, so payments are charged when the customer pays. Card instalments can be offered in the Payment Widget, but SumUp only allows them for merchants in Brazil, Chile, Colombia and Peru.
