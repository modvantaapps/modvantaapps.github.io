Subscriptions & Recurring Billing

Recurring billing for Odoo Community that adds up

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Sell monthly, quarterly, yearly or weekly subscriptions with free trials, upgrades in the middle of a period, pauses and a customer portal - and invoice every period that falls due from one schedule.

Odoo Community has no subscriptions app. This module adds one: a plan says how often a customer is billed and whether they pay at the start or the end of each period, and the subscription holds the customer, the products and the price they pay.

When a subscription is confirmed the module writes its schedule - one row per period with the due date, the dates it covers and the amount. An upgrade or downgrade from a given day charges that period at the old price for the days before and the new price for the days after. Paused days are not charged, and a trial is free until it ends.

Invoice what is due is one click on a subscription, and a daily scheduled action does the same for every running subscription. Charges that are already on an invoice are never billed twice. Customers see their own subscriptions and what each period cost on the portal.

Features

Every subscription with its MRR
A schedule you can read period by period
Price history that is kept, not guessed
Pause and resume
Plans with free trials
Charges due across all subscriptions
Recurring revenue report
The customer's subscriptions on the portal
What they have been billed, and by which invoice
