Square Payment Gateway

Take Square payments online and on the Square Terminal from Odoo

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The Square card form on your Odoo checkout, invoices collected on a paired Square Terminal, saved cards, authorise-and-capture, refunds, disputes pulled into Odoo and a full API log.

The module adds Square as a payment provider on the standard Odoo payment framework, for the eCommerce checkout, portal invoices and payment links. The card is typed in Square's Web Payments SDK form, so the card number goes to Square and Odoo only receives a token. Connect with the access token of your own Square application or with Square OAuth, and pick the Square location that takes the payments.

Pair a Square Terminal with a code, then collect an invoice on it from Odoo: the amount is sent to the terminal and the invoice is paid when the customer taps the card. Saved cards are stored at Square, card payments can be authorised and captured later, refunds go back to Square, and disputes are synchronized with a responsible user.
