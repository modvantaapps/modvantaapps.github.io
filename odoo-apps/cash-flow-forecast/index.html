Cash Flow Forecast & Payment Planning

Cash flow forecast and cash projection for Odoo accounting: a weekly, monthly or 13-week cash flow forecast built from posted invoices and bills, sales and purchase orders, recurring items and planned inflows and outflows, with the opening balance read from your bank journals. Liquidity planning, cash planning, treasury forecast, receivables and payables forecast, scenarios, forecast vs actual, minimum cash alerts, Excel export. Cash flow forecasting, cash flow planning, cash flow management, payment planning, accounting forecast, draft/approve/close workflow.

What it does

Pick a date range and daily, weekly, monthly or quarterly periods. The plan reads the opening balance of your bank and cash journals, every open customer invoice and vendor bill by its due date, sales and purchase orders not yet invoiced, recurring items such as payroll, rent, VAT and loan instalments, and the manual entries you add. Each period shows opening balance, cash in, cash out and closing balance, and the closing balance rolls into the next period.

Run the same plan as a base, optimistic or pessimistic scenario, set a minimum cash buffer to see the first period that falls short, and compare the forecast with the cash that really moved through your bank accounts. The forecast never posts a journal entry.
