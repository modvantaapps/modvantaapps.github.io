Odoo Loyverse integration

Loyverse Connector

Ring it up in Loyverse. Find it in Odoo with the invoice paid.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Loyverse POS and Odoo 19

What it does

Every Loyverse receipt comes into Odoo as a confirmed sales order, delivered from the store warehouse and, if you want, invoiced and paid in the journal of each payment type. Refunds become credit notes. Items, customers and stock levels come along too.

Paste a Loyverse access token (Back Office, Integrations, Access tokens) and press Test Connection. Odoo reads your business name, stores, payment types, taxes and categories. Each store gets an Odoo warehouse, each payment type an Odoo journal (cash types go to the cash journal, the rest to the bank), and each Loyverse tax is matched to the Odoo sales tax with the same rate and the same "included in price" setting.

A sale receipt becomes a confirmed sales order with the receipt number, store, customer, lines, line discounts, modifiers (for example Milk - Oat), tip and surcharge. The delivery is validated so stock leaves the store warehouse. In the "invoiced and paid" mode the invoice is posted and every payment of the receipt is registered in its journal, so a receipt paid half cash, half card shows up in both.

A refund receipt becomes a credit note against the invoice of the original receipt, posted and paid back in the same journals. A receipt cancelled in Loyverse cancels its order when no invoice is posted yet.

Items and their variants (up to three options, such as Size) become Odoo products with variants, with SKU, barcode, price, cost and category. Customers are matched inside your company by Loyverse ID, email or phone, never across companies, and keep their points balance. You can also send an Odoo product or contact to Loyverse with one button.
