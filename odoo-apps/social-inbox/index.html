Team Inbox

One shared inbox for your support emails and website live chat

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Customer emails sent to your team address and visitor chats from Odoo Live Chat land in one queue. Agents answer both from a three-pane workspace, with assignment, SLA deadlines, canned replies and internal notes.

New conversations can be assigned automatically (round robin or least busy), get first-response and resolution deadlines from your SLA policies, and show On Track, At Risk, Breached or Met. An analytics dashboard shows response times, resolution rate, channel mix and workload per agent.

Features

Agents open the Agent Workspacethe queue on the left, the full thread in the middle, the customer and internal notes on the right. A reply typed there goes back by email for email conversations and straight into the visitor's chat window for live chat.
Channels supportedemail (through Odoo's mail gateway) and website live chat (Odoo im_livechat). It does not connect WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram or SMS.
