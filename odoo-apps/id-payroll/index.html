Indonesia Payroll

Indonesia Payroll for Odoo 19: PPh 21 TER, BPJS and BPA1 (1721-A1)

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Indonesian statutory payroll on Odoo Community. Monthly PPh 21 with the TER rates of PP 58/2023, the December Pasal 17 calculation of PMK 168/2023, gross-up, BPJS Kesehatan and BPJS Ketenagakerjaan (JHT, JP, JKK, JKM) on every payslip, the BPA1 annual slip and Coretax eBupot XML import files.

The PTKP status picks category A, B or C, and the monthly gross is matched to the band "above X up to and including Y". Gross includes the employer-paid JKK, JKM and BPJS Kesehatan, as PMK 168/2023 requires.

In December, or the month an employee leaves, the year is recalculated with Pasal 17: biaya jabatan (5%, max 500,000 a month), employee JHT and JP, zakat, PTKP, PKP rounded down to the thousand, minus tax already withheld.

When the year was over-withheld, for example for a leaver, the last payslip shows the refund as an earning line, so it is paid back in the same run.

4% employer and 1% employee on basic wage plus fixed allowances, capped at 12,000,000, with the UMP / UMK floor (off for micro and small businesses) and 1% per extra family member.

Who it is for

  • Indonesian employers withholding PPh 21 under TER. A company in Jakarta or Surabaya paying monthly salaries can have the TER category picked from each employee's PTKP status and the monthly rate applied on the payslip, with the full Pasal 17 recalculation done in December or in the month someone leaves.
  • HR teams filing BPMP and BPA1 in Coretax. Instead of typing withholding slips into the Coretax converter, the payroll team generates the BPMP XML each month and the BPA1 slips and XML at year end, then imports them in eBupot.
  • Employers that pay the tax on staff's behalf. Where the company carries PPh 21 for some employees, the gross-up method adds a tunjangan PPh allowance equal to the tax, so the employee's take-home pay isn't reduced.
  • Firms in sectors covered by PPh 21 DTP. If the company's main KLU is on the 2026 PMK 105/2025 list, eligible employees still have their tax computed and reported, and the government-borne amount is paid to them in cash on the payslip.

Features

Per employeethe employer pays a PPh 21 allowance (tunjangan PPh) computed so that the allowance equals the tax, monthly and in December.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module on top of Modvanta HR & Payroll. Companies whose country is Indonesia get Indonesian Statutory Payroll switched on when the module installs.
  2. Open the company form, go to the Indonesian Payroll tab and fill in Withholder NPWP (16 digits), ID TKU, Main KLU, Withholding Slip Signer, the BPJS company numbers, JKK Risk Class and Minimum Wage (UMP / UMK). Tick Micro / Small Business if the minimum-wage floor doesn't apply to you.
  3. On each employee's Indonesian Payroll tab, enter NIK (16 digits), PTKP Status, PPh 21 Method, Tax Object Code and the BPJS Kesehatan and Ketenagakerjaan numbers. Set Extra Family Members (BPJS Kes.) or Not a JP Participant where relevant.
  4. Under Employees > HR & Payroll > Indonesian Payroll > Configuration > Pay Component Mapping, tell the module which payslip lines count as salary, fixed allowances, bonus or THR, and zakat.
  5. Run the month's payslips under HR & Payroll > Payroll > Payslips. The Indonesian Statutory block shows the TER category, rate, gross and, in the last tax period, the annual figures.
  6. Each month, open Indonesian Payroll > Coretax and BPJS Files, choose BPMP - monthly slips (XML) and the payroll period, and press Generate File.
  7. At year end, open BPA1 (1721-A1) Slips, press Compute from Payslips and Confirm, then create a BPA1 - annual slips (XML) file and use Load BPA1 Slips before generating it.

Indonesia Payroll: questions

Do I need Odoo Enterprise Payroll?

No. The only dependency is Modvanta HR & Payroll, which is a Community payroll base installed automatically. Odoo's Enterprise hr_payroll isn't used.

Does it upload to Coretax, SIPP Online or e-Dabu?

No. You import the XML in Coretax under eBupot with your own DJP login. The BPJS portals keep their upload templates behind a login, so the module gives a BPJS contribution register in CSV for you to reconcile against them.

What happens when an employee leaves in the middle of the year?

The month their contract ends is treated as the last tax period. The year is recalculated with Pasal 17, and if too much was withheld the payslip gets a PPh 21 Over-withholding Refund line. DTP employees are the exception: government-borne tax isn't refunded.

What if an employee has neither a valid NIK nor an NPWP?

Untick Valid NIK / NPWP on the employee and the PPh 21 is increased by the surcharge. It's off by default because a registered NIK now counts as the tax ID.

How do I keep the JP wage ceiling and other rates current?

TER bands, Pasal 17 brackets, PTKP, biaya jabatan and BPJS rates are dated records under Configuration. BPJS Ketenagakerjaan changes the JP ceiling every year, so add the new figure as a dated line under PTKP and BPJS Parameters. Check any rate change with your tax consultant or the DJP and BPJS announcements before you rely on it.

Is the interface available in Bahasa Indonesia?

Yes. The module ships an Indonesian translation file, so users who switch Odoo to Indonesian see the payroll labels in that language.
