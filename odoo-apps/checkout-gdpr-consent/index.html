GDPR Consent Log at Checkout

Record every terms acceptance and marketing opt-in at checkout

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A required terms and conditions checkbox and an unticked newsletter opt-in on the Odoo eCommerce payment step - checked on the server - with a consent log that keeps the date, time, IP address, browser, terms version and the exact wording shown.

To show that a customer accepted your terms, or agreed to receive marketing, you need more than a ticked box: you need to know when, which version of the terms, from where, and what the text said. Standard Odoo checkout does not record that.

This module adds two checkboxes above the payment methods: I have read and accept the terms and conditions (with the terms version and a link to the terms page), and an unticked opt-in for news and offers. Payment is refused on the server until the terms box is ticked.

Every tick and untick is saved as a Consent Record with the customer, email, order, website, terms version, the wording shown, the IP address and the browser. Records cannot be edited afterwards. The order shows when the terms were accepted and which version; the contact shows whether marketing consent is given.
