Omise Payment Gateway (Opn Payments)

Take cards, PromptPay, TrueMoney and Thai mobile banking in Odoo through your Omise account.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
OmiseOpn PaymentsPromptPayTrueMoneyCards with 3-D SecurePayNowFPXDuitNow

What it does

Adds Opn Payments (formerly Omise) as a payment provider for shops and service businesses with an Omise account in Thailand, Singapore, Malaysia or Japan. The customer picks a method in Odoo, on the website, a portal invoice or a payment link. Odoo then opens the Omise payment window: card numbers are typed there and go straight to Omise, Odoo only gets a one-time token. Odoo creates the charge, sends the customer to the 3-D Secure or wallet page when Omise asks for it, shows the PromptPay or PayNow QR code itself, and reads the final result back from Omise. Refunds and captures are made from the payment in Odoo.

Features

CardsVisa, Mastercard, JCB and the brands on your account, entered in the Omise.js window; 3-D Secure when Omise asks for it
ThailandPromptPay QR, TrueMoney, Rabbit LINE Pay, ShopeePay, GrabPay, Alipay and mobile banking: KBank, SCB, Bangkok Bank, Krungthai and Krungsri
Singapore and MalaysiaPayNow, GrabPay, Alipay HK, Touch 'n Go, DANA, GCash, KakaoPay; FPX, DuitNow and Boost
QR codesPromptPay and PayNow codes are shown on an Odoo page that refreshes until the payment arrives; you choose how long a code stays valid
Account countrysets the settlement currency (THB, SGD, MYR or JPY), the charge limits and which methods are offered; JPY amounts are sent without decimals
Multi-currency cardsfor Thai and Singapore accounts that have it: card payments in USD, EUR, GBP and the other currencies Omise lists
Test Connectionreads your Omise account and stores the country, the minimum and maximum charge and the methods Omise has turned on for you
Manual captureauthorize the card at checkout, capture or reverse it from Odoo later
Refundsfull or partial, only where Omise marks the charge as refundable (PromptPay charges cannot be refunded through Omise)
Webhookseach notification is checked against its Omise-Signature, then the charge is read again from Omise before anything changes
API logevery call and notification with its status; card data, names and contact details masked
