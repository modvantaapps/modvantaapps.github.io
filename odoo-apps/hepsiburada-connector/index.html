Hepsiburada Connector

Hepsiburada orders in Odoo, stock and prices out, packages and labels from the delivery

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Listing APIOrder API (OMS)Stock and price uploadsPackages and labels

What it does

Hepsiburada entegrasyonu: siparisler Odoo satis siparisi olarak gelir, stok ve fiyat Odoo'dan gider, paket ve kargo etiketi teslimattan olusturulur.

Connect your Hepsiburada store with the merchant ID, the service key from the seller panel and your integrator user name. Paid orders arrive as confirmed Odoo sales orders with the buyer, the delivery address, the VAT Hepsiburada charged and the commission of every line. Stock and prices go back in batches of 1,000, and when you validate the delivery Odoo creates the Hepsiburada package and reads the cargo tracking number.

Features

Orderspaid order lines are grouped by order number into one sales order, confirmed if you want. An order already in Odoo is never created twice, and an order with an unknown merchant SKU waits with a note until you link the product.
Listingsimport your listings and link each one to the product whose internal reference or barcode is the merchant SKU. Open or close a listing for sale from Odoo.
Stockfree quantity of the chosen warehouse minus a buffer you set, pushed every hour and on demand.
Pricesfrom a price list, or a fixed price per listing.
Upload resultseach stock or price batch is checked with Hepsiburada; refused SKUs are shown on the listing with the reason.
Packagesvalidating the delivery creates the package with the line quantities, parcels and desi; the cargo barcode, tracking number and tracking link come back to the delivery.
Labels and invoice linkdownload the cargo label as PDF or ZPL, and send the link of the posted invoice to the package.
Cancellationslines the buyer cancels on Hepsiburada leave the Odoo order (the order is cancelled when nothing is left); cancel unpacked lines from Odoo with a reason code.
Own carrierif you ship with your own cargo, send in transit, delivered and not delivered statuses from the delivery.
Log and rolesevery API call is logged without the key. Users do the daily work; only managers see the service key and change accounts. Multi-company.
