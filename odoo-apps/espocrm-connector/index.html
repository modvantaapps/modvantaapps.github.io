EspoCRM Connector

EspoCRM and Odoo 19 kept in step: accounts, contacts, leads and opportunities, both ways

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

EspoCRM REST API - API key or HMAC - Both ways - Signed webhooks - Calls and meetings in the chatter

EspoCRM is a good CRM for sales and support teams, but quotes, invoices and stock usually sit in Odoo. This module joins the two through the EspoCRM REST API. Accounts become Odoo companies, contacts become their people, leads become CRM leads and opportunities become CRM opportunities with their amount, probability, close date and stage. Closed Won and Closed Lost are won and lost in Odoo as well, and Dead leads are marked lost. New records and edits made in Odoo go back to EspoCRM. If you register the webhooks, EspoCRM tells Odoo about changes within its webhook queue run, and Odoo checks each request's signature before it reads the record again.

Features

Accounts and contactsname, website, e-mail, phone, industry, billing address and description; contacts under their account
Leads both waysname, company, e-mail, phone, title, status, source and amount
Opportunities both waysamount, probability, close date, account, contact, salesperson, lead source and stage
Won, lost and deadClosed Won and Closed Lost become won and lost; Dead leads are lost; converted leads are archived
Stage mapevery opportunity stage, lead status and lead source mapped to an Odoo stage, lost reason or source
Calls and meetingsposted once in the chatter of the account, contact, lead or opportunity they belong to
Odoo notes to the streaman internal note on a linked record is posted to its EspoCRM stream
Webhookscreate and update events with the documented Signature check and a secret address; delete events are not used
API key or HMACboth authentication methods of EspoCRM API users
Link by handtype the EspoCRM ID of an existing record and press Pull from EspoCRM
UsersEspoCRM users matched to Odoo users by e-mail
No deletesnothing is deleted in EspoCRM; a button archives in Odoo what was deleted there
Company saferecords are matched only inside the company of the connection
Logevery record read or sent, with errors kept for 60 days
