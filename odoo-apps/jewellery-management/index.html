Jewellery Management

Jewellery Management for Odoo 19 - Gold Rate, Karigar, Hallmark and Old Gold

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Rate per purityPrice breakupKarigar ledgerNo POS neededbuilt on Sales, Purchase, Inventory

What it does

Run the back office of a jewellery shop in Odoo Sales, Purchase, Inventory and Invoicing: publish the gold and silver rate of the day per purity, price every piece from its net weight with wastage, making charges and stones, buy and exchange old gold, send metal to karigars and track their loss, keep the HUID register, lend pieces on approval, book rates against an advance and run gold savings schemes. No Point of Sale needed.

Each morning you type the fine gold rate (or let your own rate-feed key fetch it) and the module writes the sell and buy rate of every purity by fineness: 24K, 22K, 18K, 14K, silver 925, platinum 950. A jewellery piece is a product with its purity, gross weight, stones and less weight; the net and fine weight follow. When you add it to a quotation, the line is priced at the day's rate: metal value, wastage, making charge (per gram, percent or fixed), stones and other charges. The quotation is re-priced at confirmation, the rate used is locked, and the quote and invoice print the price breakup with the HUID.
