Knowledge Base, Wiki and Help Center

Knowledge Base, Wiki and Help Center for Odoo 19

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Knowledge BaseInternal WikiCompany WikiHelp CenterKnowledge PortalDocumentationFAQSOP

What it does

One knowledge base for your team and your customers: an internal wiki with workspaces, sections and nested articles written in the Odoo editor, templates, version history with diff and restore, ranked full-text search, permissions per article, a public help center with SEO and feedback, a customer portal, share links, review dates, read statistics and read-and-acknowledge for policies. One module on Odoo's own Website, Portal and Discuss.

Every search in the backend, the portal and the help center is logged. "Searches without an answer" lists what people looked for and did not find, grouped by the words they typed, with a "Write an article" button - so the knowledge base grows where readers need it.

Everything a team wiki and a customer help center need, in one module.

Organise knowledge in workspaces (Support, HR, Operations...) with sections and sub-sections, and nest articles as deep as you need. A home screen shows published, draft, in-review and review-due counts per workspace; an article tree shows the structure at a glance.

Eight ready templates (how-to, FAQ, troubleshooting, SOP, policy, release notes, meeting notes, onboarding) and a Save as template button, so nobody starts from a blank page and every article looks the same.

Features

Write with Odoo's own editorheadings, lists, tables, images, video, code and the / command. Reading time and word count are kept on every article, and a table of contents is built from the headings in the help center, the portal and the PDF.
