POS Salesperson

Credit every POS sale to the salesperson who made it

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

In Odoo's Point of Sale the cashier is the only person on an order. This module adds a salesperson - for the whole order or for each line - shows it on the receipt and gives you sales by salesperson in reports and the session report.

In many shops the person who rings up the sale is not the one who sold it: a floor seller helps the customer, a cashier takes the payment. Commission and targets then need the seller, and Odoo's POS only records the cashier.

With this module the cashier picks the salesperson from the Actions menu, or the till asks for one when Pay is pressed if a salesperson is required. With per-line salespersons on, a single line can be credited to someone else, for example the colleague who sold the lamp.

The salesperson is saved on the order and its lines, printed on the receipt, shown in the Orders list and form, and counted line by line in Sales by Salesperson and in the session's daily sales report.
