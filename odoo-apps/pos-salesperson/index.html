POS Salesperson

Credit every POS sale to the salesperson who made it

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

In Odoo's Point of Sale the cashier is the only person on an order. This module adds a salesperson - for the whole order or for each line - picked from a searchable list with photos, shows it on the receipt and gives you sales by salesperson in reports and the session report.

In many shops the person who rings up the sale is not the one who sold it: a floor seller helps the customer, a cashier takes the payment. Commission and targets then need the seller, and Odoo's POS only records the cashier.

With this module the cashier picks the salesperson from the Actions menu, or the till asks for one when Pay is pressed if a salesperson is required. With per-line salespersons on, a single line can be credited to someone else, for example the colleague who sold the lamp.

The salesperson is saved on the order and its lines, printed on the receipt, shown in the Orders list and form, and counted line by line in Sales by Salesperson and in the session's daily sales report.

Who it is for

  • Furniture, electronics and fashion stores with floor staff. A seller walks the customer around the shop, then someone else rings it up at the till. Odoo only records the cashier by default, so this adds a separate salesperson to the order and prints it on the receipt.
  • Shops where two sellers share one basket. With salesperson per order line on, a single line can be credited to the colleague who sold it. The sticky option keeps giving the next lines to that person until someone else is picked.
  • Stores that work out commission from POS sales. Sales by Salesperson counts each line for the person it's credited to, and the session's daily sales report gets a table per salesperson. You take those totals into whatever commission method you use.
  • Shops with sellers who don't log into Odoo. If the Employees app is installed, the salesperson list can come from employees, so floor staff don't need a user account or a POS login of their own.

Setting it up

  1. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings and choose the shop.
  2. Turn on Salesperson, then pick the Salesperson Source: Users, or Employees if the Employees app is installed.
  3. Fill Allowed Salespersons (or Allowed Salesperson Employees) to limit the list for this shop. Leave it empty to allow everyone in the company.
  4. Switch on Salesperson per Order Line if single lines can go to a different seller, and Keep Line Salesperson for Next Lines if you want that choice to carry on.
  5. Decide on Salesperson Photos, Require Salesperson and Cashier is Default Salesperson, then save.
  6. In the POS, use Salesperson or Line Salesperson from the Actions menu. Check results under Point of Sale > Reporting > Sales by Salesperson.

POS Salesperson: questions

Does it calculate commission?

No. It records who sold each order and line and reports the sales per person, with lines, quantity, untaxed amount and total. The commission rates and payouts are up to you or another module.

Will the cashier field change?

No. The cashier (Employee) on the order stays exactly as Odoo sets it. The salesperson is a separate field on the order and on each line.

How is a line counted if it has no salesperson of its own?

It's counted for the order's salesperson. A line with its own salesperson is counted for that person instead, which is shown in the Credited To column on the order form.

Can I stop orders going through without a salesperson?

Yes. With Require Salesperson on, the till opens the picker when Pay is pressed, and the server also refuses an order that arrives without one.

What happens on refunds?

The refund line keeps the salesperson of the sale it reverses, so the return is charged back to the right person in the report.

Do I need Enterprise or the Employees app?

Neither. It only depends on Point of Sale, so it runs on Community. The Employees app is only needed if you want employees rather than users as the salesperson source.
