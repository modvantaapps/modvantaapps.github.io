Cameroon Payroll

Cameroon Payroll for Odoo 19: IRPP, CAC, CNPS, CFC, FNE, RAV and TDL

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Cameroon PayrollPaie CamerounIRPPCACCNPSCFCFNERAV

What it does

Cameroonian payroll on Odoo Community. Each payslip takes 30% professional expenses and the CNPS pension off the gross, removes the 500,000 FCFA abatement on a yearly basis and withholds IRPP and the 10% CAC, then adds CFC, RAV and TDL. An employee on 750,000 FCFA a month pays 83,142 FCFA of IRPP and 8,314 FCFA of CAC, the same as the DGI indicative table. Paie Cameroun: IRPP, CAC, CNPS, CFC, FNE, RAV, TDL.

10% to 2 million, 15% to 3 million, 25% to 5 million and 35% above, on yearly net income.

Taken off the gross, capped at 4,800,000 FCFA a year as the 2026 code says.

On the yearly income; nothing is withheld below 62,000 FCFA gross a month.

Pension 4.2% + 4.2% and family allowances up to 750,000; work accident on all pay.

Features

Not includedthe DIPE file format itself, e-filing on the DGI or CNPS portals, and benefits in kind at their flat rates (enter them as earnings). The register gives you the figures.
