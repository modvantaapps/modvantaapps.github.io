DATEV Export

Export your Odoo bookings to DATEV - checked before export, ready for your tax adviser

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Create DATEV posting batches (Buchungsstapel), debtor and creditor master data, account labels and payment terms from Odoo in the EXTF format DATEV imports. A check lists every problem before the file is written, and exported entries can be locked (Festschreibung).

German companies usually hand their bookkeeping to a tax adviser who works in DATEV. This module turns Odoo's posted journal entries for a period into a DATEV posting batch (EXTF_Buchungsstapel.csv) and also exports Debitoren / Kreditoren, Sachkontenbeschriftungen and Zahlungsbedingungen, plus documents with PDF as DATEV XML.

Before writing the file, the export wizard checks every entry and shows errors and warnings with the record they belong to - for example a document number that DATEV would shorten, or customers that still need a DATEV debtor number. Each export is stored with its file, record count, total amount, a SHA-256 checksum and the entries it contains, so nothing is exported twice by accident.
