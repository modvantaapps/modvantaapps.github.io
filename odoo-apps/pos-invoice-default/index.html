POS Invoice Default

The Invoice button already on at the till - always, only with a customer, or mandatory

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Per point of sale: pre-select Invoice on the payment screen, keep it for orders with a customer only, make it mandatory so cashiers cannot switch it off, and decide what happens to the invoice PDF after each sale: download it, open it in a new tab, open the print dialog, or skip it. The invoice can also be emailed to the customer on its own.

In a shop where most sales need an invoice, the cashier has to remember to press Invoice on every order - and every forgotten one means a manual invoice afterwards. When they do press it, the browser downloads a PDF after each sale, and the downloads folder fills up with invoices nobody opens.

This module adds invoice options to each point of sale. Invoice by Default selects the Invoice button when the payment screen opens. With Only when a customer is selected, walk-in sales stay receipt-only and the button switches on the moment a customer is chosen.

For the PDF you pick one of four modes per shop: Download (what Odoo does), Open in a new tab, Open the print dialog, or Don't download. The invoice is created and posted in every mode. Odoo already emails a POS invoice when the customer has an email address; you can make that Always or Never, and with Don't download the PDF and the email can be made in the background so the till moves on to the next customer straight away.

Who it is for

  • Trade counters selling to businesses. A builders' merchant or wholesale counter invoices almost every sale. Making Invoice mandatory on that till means no sale leaves without an invoice, even if a cashier forgets to press the button.
  • Shops with both walk-in and account customers. When the option to invoice only orders with a customer is on, anonymous sales get a receipt and nobody is asked for a name. Pick a known customer and the Invoice button turns on by itself.
  • Busy tills that don't want a PDF every time. Odoo normally downloads the invoice PDF after each invoiced sale. With the download switched off the invoice is still created in Accounting, but no file lands in the browser at the counter.
  • Companies running several shops with different rules. Each point of sale keeps its own settings, so a trade counter can enforce invoices while a retail shop in the same database only pre-selects them.

Setting it up

  1. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings and choose the point of sale at the top of the page.
  2. Make sure that point of sale has an invoice journal set, because the module won't save an invoice default without one.
  3. In the Bills & Receipts section tick Invoice by Default.
  4. Under it, tick Only when a customer is selected if walk-in sales should stay receipt-only, or tick Mandatory (cashiers cannot unselect it; checked by the server) for shops where every sale is invoiced.
  5. Tick Do Not Download Invoice PDF if the browser shouldn't download the PDF after each payment, then save.
  6. Open a session and take a payment. The Invoice button on the payment screen is already set the way you configured it.

POS Invoice Default: questions

Does this need Odoo Enterprise?

No. It only depends on the Point of Sale app, so it runs on Community and Enterprise. Invoicing itself uses the invoice journal you already set on the point of sale.

What stops a cashier getting around the mandatory setting?

The button stays locked on the payment screen and shows a message if someone tries to switch it off. The server also checks: a paid sale that arrives without the invoice flag is invoiced anyway if it has a customer, and refused with an explanation if it has none.

What happens with refunds?

Refunds keep Odoo's normal behaviour. The mandatory rule skips them, and Odoo already invoices the refund of an order that was invoiced. Orders with no lines are left alone too.

If the PDF isn't downloaded, where is the invoice?

It's created and posted as usual and linked to the POS order. You can print or send it from Accounting or from the order in the backend whenever you need it.

Who can change these options?

Only users in the Point of Sale manager group. The module checks this when the settings are saved, so a cashier can't turn the rules off through the configuration.

What if Invoice is on and there's no customer?

That's Odoo's own rule: Validate stays greyed out until a customer is chosen. If most of your sales are anonymous, use Only when a customer is selected rather than a plain default.
