Customer Credit Profile & Limit Approval

Credit score, credit limit request/approval workflow, and an automatic payment-history rating badge on every contact. Credit limit management, credit score, AR risk, customer credit check.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Before giving a customer credit you want to know how they pay, and you want a credit limit that someone actually signed off. Standard Odoo Community keeps none of this on the contact in one place.

This module adds a Credit Profile section to contacts. It holds a credit score (0-999) and credit notes you maintain, a payment history rating (Good, Fair, Poor or No History) worked out from the customer's posted invoices and how many are overdue, and a credit limit workflow: a requested limit is submitted, and a Credit Limit Approver approves or rejects it. Optionally, a sales order that would push the customer's outstanding balance past the approved limit cannot be confirmed.
