Appointments and Time Reservations

Online appointment booking for people, rooms and equipment

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Customers pick a service, a plan or duration and a free time slot on your website; Odoo holds the slot, reserves the room that goes with it, confirms by email with a calendar invitation and keeps the booking, the sale order and your team calendar in step. Your team plans the day on a board with one column per staff member or room.

Group what you rent or book into resource types (therapists, treatment rooms, e-bikes) and link services to them. Each resource follows its Odoo working calendar and leaves; a service can add its own hours, closing days, buffers, minimum notice and how far ahead people can book.

Customers choose the resource, or Odoo assigns one automatically by workload, number of bookings or priority. A service can need an extra resource at the same time - a room next to the therapist - and only offers times when one is free. Durations are fixed, picked from plans with their own price, or chosen by the customer within limits; rentals can be booked by the day.

The website page walks through options, date and time, details and confirm. Visitors can book without an account and confirm with a code sent by email; the slot is held for a few minutes meanwhile and released if they do not finish. Booking questions, complementary products, promo codes, prepayment through Odoo payment providers and an approval step are all optional per service.

Every confirmed booking becomes an event in the Odoo calendar with the customer and the staff member, so it reaches their Google or Outlook calendar through Odoo's own calendar sync. The confirmation email carries an .ics invitation and a printable voucher; reminders go by email and SMS at the times you set.
