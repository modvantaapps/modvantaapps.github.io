Social Media Publisher

Write a social post once, schedule it to five networks, see the likes in Odoo

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A social media publisher and scheduler for Odoo 19 Community: pick Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, LinkedIn, Mastodon and Bluesky profiles, publish now or plan it on a content calendar. Page members write, approvers check, and likes, comments, shares and link clicks come back to Odoo.

Each network gets the post in the form it expects: link card on Facebook, LinkedIn and Bluesky, image carousel on Instagram, several images on Facebook, LinkedIn, Mastodon and Bluesky. Checks warn before submitting when a text is too long for a network or Instagram has no image.

Every customer connects their own Meta, LinkedIn and Mastodon app, so no shared app and no third-party server sits between Odoo and the networks.

Features

Odoo Community has no social marketing appposts are written in each network's own tool and nothing comes back to Odoo. This module adds a content planner (kanban and calendar), one post form for every network, an approval step per page, tracked links with UTM campaign, medium and source, and a list of results per page.
