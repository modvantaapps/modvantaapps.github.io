Pet Care Management

Run a pet care centre or veterinary clinic in Odoo: pet profiles and guardians, vaccinations with automatic reminders, vet visits and medical history, pet grooming and other services, pet boarding with feeding and medication schedules, pet transportation, printable agreements, invoices, guardian ratings and a guardian portal.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Pet Care Management for Odoo 19Who it is forPet care centresVeterinary clinicsBoarding kennels and catteries

What it does

Grooming, day care, boarding and pet taxi in one place, with the guardian, the pet and the bill linked together.

Everything below was used on a test database with sample data

Species, breed, colour, tags, gender, neutered, date of birth and age, weight, microchip, food and grooming brands, allergies, behaviour.

Vaccines with validity in months; next due date, Up to Date / Due Soon / Overdue, a daily email to the guardian and a to-do for the vet.

Reason, weight, temperature, heart rate, symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, prescription, vaccines given and next check-up.
