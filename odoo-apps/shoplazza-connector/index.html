Odoo Shoplazza integration

Shoplazza Connector

Shoplazza orders in Odoo, stock and prices back to Shoplazza

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Shoplazza OpenAPI 2026-07OrdersStockFulfillment

What it does

Shoplazza stores that keep stock and accounting in Odoo usually copy each paid order by hand and type the tracking number twice. This connector reads paid Shoplazza orders into Odoo sales orders, keeps variant stock and prices on Shoplazza in line with Odoo, and sends the fulfillment with the tracking number back as soon as the delivery is validated.

Features

Orders into sales ordersCustomer, billing and delivery address, variants matched by SKU, the tax rate worked out from each line, discount and shipping lines.
Paid orders only, or allPaid orders are confirmed at once; unpaid ones can come in as quotations and are confirmed when Shoplazza marks them paid.
Stock and price by variantOdoo free quantity (minus a buffer) and, if you want, the Odoo price are sent to every linked Shoplazza variant. Unchanged values are not sent again.
Create products on ShoplazzaAdd an Odoo product to the list and press Push Now to create it.
Fulfillment with trackingValidating the delivery creates the Shoplazza fulfillment for the shipped lines, with tracking number, company and link.
Cancel syncOrders cancelled or refunded on Shoplazza are cancelled in Odoo; you can also cancel on Shoplazza from the Odoo order.
Signed webhooksorders/create, orders/paid, orders/cancelled and orders/update, each checked with X-Shoplazza-Hmac-Sha256 and de-duplicated by its delivery ID.
API logEvery call and webhook with its status, kept as long as you choose.
