Austria ebInterface 6.1 XML e-Invoice

Austria ebInterface 6.1 for Odoo 19: the XML e-invoice for e-Rechnung.gv.at and your business customers, checked against the official ebInterface XSD.

What it does

Austrian public authorities only accept e-invoices, and ebInterface is the Austrian XML format. This module builds the ebInterface 6.1 XML from your posted invoices and credit notes, checks it against the official schema, attaches it to the invoice and to the invoice email, and reads ebInterface files you receive into draft vendor bills.

ebInterface 6.1 XML: invoice and credit note (CreditMemo) with the reference to the original invoice, lines, article numbers and GTIN, units, line discounts, tax summary, payable amount

e-Rechnung.gv.at fields: order reference (Auftragsreferenz) per invoice or per customer, your supplier number (Lieferantennummer); both are required for authorities

Features

Checked against the official XSDthe ebInterface 6.1 schema ships with the module; a file that does not pass is never saved and the errors are shown on the invoice
Tax categoriesstandard (S), reduced (AA), exempt (E), reverse charge (AE), intra-EU (K), export (G) from the tax category already on the Odoo tax
PaymentSEPA bank transfer with IBAN, BIC, account owner and payment reference; due date and terms
Foreign currencyexchange information and the tax amounts in your company currency
Send & Printchoose "Austria (ebInterface 6.1)" as e-invoice format on the customer: the XML is created and attached to the email automatically
Importupload a received ebInterface file on the vendor bills: supplier, lines, taxes, dates and payment reference are filled
