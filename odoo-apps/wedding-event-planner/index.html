Wedding Planner and Event Planner

Run a wedding or event agency in Odoo: enquiry, signed quote and deposit, venue diary, vendors, budget, guests, seating and the run sheet of the day

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

A couple fills in the form on your website. The enquiry lands in CRM, and "Plan Event" turns it into an event with the date, venue, package and guest count. The quotation is a normal Odoo quotation: the client signs it and pays the deposit on the portal, and the event books itself when the order is confirmed. From there you work the event: vendor quotes, the budget, the guest list, the tables, the checklist and the timed run sheet. On the day, the event sheet PDF goes to the crew.

Features

Website enquiry to eventthe /event-planner page shows your packages and venues; each enquiry becomes a CRM opportunity, and one button creates the event plan
Signed quote and depositquotations ask for an online signature and a deposit (30% by default); confirming the order books the event and wins the opportunity
Venue diarya second booking of the same venue is refused, including the turnaround time the room needs to be stripped and reset
Packagespriced per guest or as a flat fee; per-guest packages bill the contracted minimum headcount, so 72 guests on an 80-guest minimum still pay for 80
Vendor quotescollect quotes per heading (flowers, photo, music...), accept one and the purchase order is raised; the other quotes are rejected; a vendor who pulls out frees the heading
Vendor insurance and licenceexpiry dates on the vendor; a vendor whose insurance or licence lapses before the event cannot be booked, and you get a to-do 30 days before
Live budgetestimated, contracted (from the purchase orders) and actual (from posted vendor bills) per heading, with the margin of the event
Guest list and RSVPseats per invitation, side or group, meal choice, dietary needs, VIP; guests answer through their own RSVP link and cannot raise their seat allowance
Table seatingtables with a capacity; a table that would hold more seats than it has is refused
Checklistdeadlines counted back from the event date (300, 90, 30, 14 days before...), loaded from the event type and moved when the event is rescheduled
Run sheettimed cues with place, owner and "cannot slip"; record the actual start and see the delay
Staff rostershifts per event; a person already on another event at that time is refused; staff accept or decline from an email link
Payment scheduledeposit and balance (due 14 days before the event by default), each invoiced from the order with one click
Client portal and event sheetclients see the schedule, guests, vendors and payments and download the event sheet; after the event they leave a rating
