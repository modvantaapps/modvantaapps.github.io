Taqnyat SMS Gateway

Send every Odoo SMS through your own Taqnyat account and sender name

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Taqnyat SMSSaudi SMS gatewayArabic SMSScheduled SMSTaqnyat OTP

What it does

Pick "Send via Taqnyat" in Settings > SMS, paste the bearer token of a Taqnyat application, and Odoo stops using IAP credits for that company. The SMS button on a contact, SMS templates, SMS Marketing and automation rules all go out through the Taqnyat REST API, with the sender name registered on your account. Each SMS lands in a log with the Taqnyat message id, its cost in SAR and the reason when Taqnyat refuses it.

Features

Every Odoo SMSchatter SMS, mass SMS on contact lists, SMS Marketing and automated actions use Taqnyat; other companies in the same database keep their own provider
Sender namesread your sender names, their status and destination (KSA or international) straight from Taqnyat, then pick the default one
Scheduled SMSschedule a text for a date and time; Taqnyat holds it and you can still cancel it from the log
Quiet hoursSMS Marketing texts (or every SMS) created at night are handed to Taqnyat as scheduled SMS for 08:00
Taqnyat templateslink an Odoo SMS template to a template from your Taqnyat account and fill its variables from the record, e.g. {{ object.name }}
OTP phone checkVerify by OTP on a contact: Taqnyat Verify sends the code and checks it, and the contact is marked as verified
Balance and alertbalance, account status and expiry date read every 6 hours, with an email when it drops below your limit
Retrieswhen Taqnyat does not answer, the SMS is sent again every 15 minutes, up to 3 times
SMS lognumber, sender, parts, cost, Taqnyat id and error per SMS; resend failed ones in one click
ArabicArabic text counted at 70 characters per part, and the screens translated into Arabic
