POS Returns Control

Every refund at the till gets a reason, a policy check and the right stock move

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The cashier picks why goods came back, faulty items get a note and a transfer to a quarantine location, refunds outside your return window or without an original receipt are refused, and no line can be refunded twice.

Odoo Point of Sale lets a cashier refund anything, for any reason, at any time - and faulty goods go straight back into sellable stock. The refund total tells you nothing you can act on.

With Return Control on, a refund cannot be validated without a reason from your list. Reasons can ask for a short note (Faulty: what happened?) and can be marked as not going back on the shelf.

Returns with such a reason raise an internal transfer to the location you choose (for example WH/Quarantine) for someone to carry the goods there. The server also enforces your return window, can require every refund line to come from an original receipt, and refuses refunding more than was sold, even across two tills.
