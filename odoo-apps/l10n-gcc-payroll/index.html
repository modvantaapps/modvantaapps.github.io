GCC Payroll

GCC Payroll for Odoo 19: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait and Bahrain

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
UAE PayrollSaudi PayrollQatar PayrollOman PayrollKuwait PayrollBahrain PayrollWPS SIF FileGOSI

What it does

UAE SIF, Qatar SIF, Saudi WPS, insurance register

What a Gulf HR and payroll team needs every month and at the end of service.

GPSSA (UAE), GOSI annuities, SANED and occupational hazards (Saudi), GRSIA (Qatar), SPF branches (Oman), PIFSS (Kuwait) and SIO (Bahrain). Employee, employer and government shares, wage bases, ceilings and slices, legacy vs new-law members, rates by date.

Annuity rates for Saudis insured from 3 July 2024 step from 9% to 11% by date, as set by the Social Insurance Law. Legacy contributors keep 9% + 0.75% SANED. Non-Saudis pay only the 2% occupational hazards, charged to the employer.

A Kuwaiti working in Dubai or an Emirati in Riyadh is insured with the home-country scheme (GCC extension): the payslip collects the home insurer's rates automatically from the nationality.

Features

Bahrain expatriatesthe employer gratuity stops at 29 February 2024 and the 4.2% / 8.4% SIO end-of-service contribution takes over on the payslip. Oman: the 9% savings contribution switches on from 19 July 2027.
