Project Task Checklist

Add checklist items to Odoo project tasks, fill them from reusable templates per project or per stage, show the progress on the kanban card, and stop a task being completed while mandatory items are still open.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A task in Odoo has a description, but no list of steps the team can tick off. Teams end up typing checkboxes into the description, where nobody can see the progress, assign a step or check that the important ones were done.

This module adds a Checklist tab to the task with items that are ticked in one click, a counter and a progress bar. Items can be mandatory, assigned to a user, given a due date, or marked as customer items the customer ticks on the portal. Each tick is logged in the chatter with who did it.

Checklist templates add the same steps to every new task of a project, or to a task when it enters a stage. A project or stage can refuse to complete a task while mandatory items are open. Managers get a list of open items per user and checklist measures in the reporting.

Features

A checklist tab on the task
