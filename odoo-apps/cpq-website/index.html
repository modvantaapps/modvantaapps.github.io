CPQ Website Configurator

Let shop customers configure custom products themselves - with a live price, add to cart or request a quote

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The configurable products of the CPQ Product Configurator get a step-by-step configurator on their shop page. Customers answer the questions, see the price build up, then add the configured product to the cart, send it as a quote request, or save it in their portal.

This is the website add-on for mdv_cpq. On the product page of a configurable product the usual add-to-cart block becomes a guided configurator: one step per group of questions, cards for choices with their surcharge, number fields with range and step for sizes, optional extras with quantity, and a review step listing every answer and the components that come with it. A price card next to it shows the base price, each surcharge and the total as answers change.

Signed-in customers can save configurations and find them under My Configurations in the portal, to reorder or request a quote later; a share link lets them send a configuration to someone else. The same compatibility rules, hidden questions and pricing rules as on the backend quotation apply.

Features

Per product you choose how it can be orderedadd to cart, add to cart or request a quote, or request a quote only with the price hidden. In the cart the configured product shows every answer and an Edit configuration link. A quote request creates a quotation for the sales team with the configuration attached and thanks the customer with the reference.
