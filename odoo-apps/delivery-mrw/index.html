MRW Shipping Integration

Ship with MRW from Odoo - Spain and Portugal labels, reembolso, MRW Point and tracking

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

An MRW connector for Odoo 19 on the MRW SAGEC web service: validating a delivery order transmits the shipment and attaches the MRW label, cash on delivery comes from the order, customers pick an MRW franchise or MRW Point at the checkout, and the MRW status history is synced onto every shipment.

The module adds MRW as a delivery method provider in Odoo and uses the MRW SAGEC web service for shipments and labels, the MRW points service and the MRW tracking service. It covers the Spanish peninsula, the Balearic and Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla, and Portugal.

Each delivery method holds your franchise code, subscriber number, department code and SAGEC user, the MRW service (Ecommerce, Urgente 19 and the others), the delivery type - to the address or to an MRW franchise / MRW Point - Saturday delivery, delivery at 8:30, Ecommerce time windows, returns, insurance, MRW notices to the recipient, cash on delivery and the price, from an MRW rate table by zone and weight or a fixed price.

Features

All MRW shipments in one list
MRW SAGEC account, service and options
Delivery to an MRW franchise or MRW Point
Choose an MRW Point on the delivery order
MRW tab on the delivery order
Shipment with Canary Islands customs note
MRW at the website checkout
MRW Point map for the customer
