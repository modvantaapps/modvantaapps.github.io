Kazakhstan Payroll

Kazakhstan Payroll for Odoo 19: IPN, OPV, OPVR, Social Contributions, OSMS, Social Tax and Form 200.00 Worksheet

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Kazakhstan PayrollKazakhstan HRIPNOPVOPVRSocial TaxOSMSVOSMS

What it does

Kazakhstan statutory payroll on Odoo Community and Enterprise: IPN with the 10% / 15% scale and the 30 MRP base deduction, OPV, OPVR, social contributions, OSMS and VOSMS, the 6% social tax, the 2026 MRP and MZP, a Kazakh payslip and the quarterly form 200.00 worksheet with Excel and CSV.

Compute the month and see every tax and contribution on the payslip, with clear warnings when something is missing.

Get the form 200.00 lines month by month and a journal entry on the Kazakhstan chart of accounts.

10% of taxable income up to 8,500 MRP a year, 15% above (year to date at this employer)

5,000 MRP a year (disability groups I-II), 882 MRP a year (group III and other categories)

Features

Rates are dated records, not codecopy them for the next year and edit the values.
Exemptions handled from the employee formpension age (no OPV, OPVR or social contributions), date of birth before 1975 (no OPVR), disability group (OPV, OPVR, VOSMS and the social deductions), military, special state or law-enforcement service, and VOSMS paid by the state.
An employee born in 1971no OPVR, social contributions capped at 7 MZP, IPN after the base deduction.
