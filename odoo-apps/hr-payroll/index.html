Modvanta HR & Payroll

HR and payroll for Odoo Community - from salary contract to payslip, journal entry and final settlement

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Set salary contracts, run a payroll period that creates and computes every payslip with allowances, overtime, bonuses and statutory deductions, approve it, post the payroll journal entry and manage the rest of the employee lifecycle under one HR and Payroll menu.

Each employee gets a salary contract with basic salary, housing, transport and other allowances, a standard deduction, a loan installment and a bank account. A payroll period (for example October 2026) generates one payslip per active contract and computes it, then goes through submit, approve and lock, and creates the payroll journal entry on the accounts you set on the company.

Payslip lines come from the contract and from approved records: overtime hours times the hourly rate (or the hourly wage from the salary contract when no rate is set) and the overtime rate rule, approved bonuses, salary arrears, leave encashments and attendance penalties (each paid or deducted once, in the period of its date, and marked paid when the payroll is locked), statutory deductions such as PF, ESI and income tax by percentage with a salary limit, and more. Payroll analytics sums gross, deductions and net by period and department.

Around payroll the app covers overtime requests, bonuses and incentives, employee loans, a gratuity calculator and full and final settlements, and its HR and Payroll menu also holds salary revisions, leave encashments, salary advances, attendance summaries and penalties, shift roster, probation, contract and document expiry, asset handover, HR letters, employee self-service requests, payslip portal, bank CSV and bank transfer exports, approval levels and an audit log.
