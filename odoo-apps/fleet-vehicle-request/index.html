Fleet Vehicle Request

Fleet Vehicle Request and Pool Car Booking for Odoo 19

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Employees ask for a company or pool vehicle for dates and a purpose. Fleet officers approve, pick a free car and hand it over with odometer, fuel level and photos. The return is logged to the Odoo Fleet odometer and late returns raise a reminder.

Built on the standard Odoo Fleet app - no second vehicle list.

Dates, purpose, destination, passengers and an optional vehicle category. The request is filled with the employee of the logged-in user.

New, Confirmed, Approved, Assigned, Returned, plus Rejected (with a reason) and Cancelled. Menus for requests to approve, to assign and to return.

A vehicle cannot be approved or handed over twice for overlapping dates. Back-to-back bookings are allowed.
