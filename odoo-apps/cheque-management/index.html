Cheque Management

Track every cheque you receive and issue, and print cheques on your own bank's leaf

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Post dated cheques from customers and to vendors follow a clear life cycle - registered, deposited, cleared, bounced, replaced or returned - with journal entries, cheque books, a drag-and-drop cheque layout designer and PDC reports.

Cheques received from customers and cheques issued to vendors are records of their own, linked to the invoices or bills they pay. Each moves through Registered, Deposited / Presented and Cleared, or is Bounced with a reason and bank charges, Replaced by a new cheque, Returned, Endorsed to a vendor, stopped or cancelled - with the journal entries created along the way. A bounced cheque gets a follow-up activity for the person responsible.

Security (guarantee) cheques are kept apart from payments and can be converted to a payment later. Cheque books hold your numbered leaves: the next number is used when you issue a cheque, and every leaf shows as unused, used, cancelled, void or stopped in the leaf register.

Cheques print on your bank's pre-printed leaf. The layout designer places the payee, date, amount in figures and words and other fields in millimetres - drag them on a scanned image of your leaf - and sample layouts for India, UAE / GCC, the UK and US checks are included. Printer calibration corrects offsets per printer, and every print is written to the cheque print log. Print a Cheque prints a one-off cheque without a payment.
