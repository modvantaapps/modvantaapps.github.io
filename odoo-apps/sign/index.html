Digital Signature & Approval

Send a PDF for signature and get it back signed, from inside Odoo

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Upload a PDF as a template, place signature, text, date and tick box fields on it, and send it to one or more people. They sign from a link in their browser, and you get a stamped PDF and an audit trail that cannot be edited.

Odoo Community has no signature app, so contracts, quotes and approvals are printed, signed by hand, scanned and attached, or sent through an outside signing service.

This module adds a Signatures app. A template is a PDF with fields placed on it: signature, initials, text, date and tick box, each assigned to signer 1, signer 2 and so on. From the template you send a request to the people who must sign, either in order (the second signer is invited only after the first has signed) or all at once, with a deadline and a message.

Each signer opens a personal link, with no Odoo account and no password, sees the PDF with their own fields highlighted, draws or types a signature, fills in the rest and submits. When everyone has signed, the request is marked Signed and the PDF is stamped with every value and a reference in the footer. Every step is written to an audit trail with time, IP address and browser. It produces an ordinary electronic signature with an evidential trail, not a qualified eIDAS signature.
