Genome Payment Gateway

Genome for Odoo 19: card payments and Pay by Bank on the Genome hosted payment page, with a signed session and signed callbacks

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Genome hosted payment page - Cards - Pay by Bank (SEPA) - EUR, USD, GBP - JWT session - Signed callbacks

Genome (genome.eu) offers business accounts and merchant payments in Europe. This module adds the Genome hosted payment page to Odoo as a payment provider. Odoo builds the payment session as a JWT signed the way Genome asks (HMAC-SHA256 with the SHA-256 of your payment page secret) and sends the customer to pay.genome.eu. The result comes back as a callback that Odoo checks against its X-Signature header and your API key before it reads a single field, and the paid amount and currency must match the Odoo payment.

Features

Genome payment pagecard numbers and bank logins are typed on pay.genome.eu, never in Odoo
Card and Pay by Bankthe method the customer picks in Odoo opens directly on the payment page
Signed sessionJWT with a fresh ID, issue time and expiry for every payment, as in the Genome docs
Signed callbacksX-Signature (HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body) and X-API-Key checked first
Amount checkthe amount and currency of the Genome transaction must match the Odoo payment
Callbacks in any ordera success that arrives after a decline still confirms the payment, as Genome describes
Pay by Bank stepspayment created and funds on the way keep the payment pending until the funds arrive
Test paymentstest callbacks are accepted in Test mode and ignored once the provider is live
Payment detailsGenome transaction ID, session ID, transaction type and payment method on the transaction
API logevery session, customer return and callback, with customer details masked
