Fleet Cost Accounting

Fleet Cost Accounting - Cost and Profit per Vehicle for Odoo 19

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Every vehicle gets its own analytic account. Bills and invoices tagged with a vehicle land on it, shared bills are split across vehicles by rule, and one report shows the total cost of ownership, the revenue, the profit and the cost per km of each vehicle.

Fleet Cost Accounting connects Odoo Fleet with Odoo Accounting. A "Vehicles" analytic plan and one analytic account per vehicle are created for you, and the Vehicle on a bill or invoice line fills the analytic distribution automatically.

Shared costs such as an insurance policy or a fuel card statement are split across vehicles with Odoo 19 analytic distribution: equal share, by km driven in the period, or fixed percentages. The Fleet service logs follow the split, so no cost is counted twice.
