Product Downloads & Documents

Put manuals, datasheets and certificates on your product pages

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Attach PDF, Word, Excel or ZIP files to a product and they appear in a Downloads and Documents section on its shop page, each with its type, size and description. Keep trade documents for signed-in customers only.

Buyers of furniture, tools and equipment want the assembly instructions, the datasheet and the warranty terms before they order. Odoo's shop page has no place for them, so they end up in emails or on a separate site.

This module adds a Downloads tab to the product form. Each document has a name, a type (Manual, Datasheet, Installation Guide, Warranty, Certificate and more), a short description and the file. On the website the product page shows a Downloads and Documents section listing every visible document with a Download button.

Features

A document can be marked Requires Loginvisitors who are not signed in see a Log in button instead, and are sent back to the file after signing in. Every download is counted, so you can see which documents customers actually open.
