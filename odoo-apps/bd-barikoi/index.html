What it does

Type a Bangladesh address and pick a Barikoi suggestion: street, area, city, ZIP, division, district, upazila, uCode and GPS are filled at once, on contacts, delivery addresses and the website checkout. Geocode whole contact lists, drop a pin on a Barikoi map to get the address, and show your partners and branches on a public map.

Bangladesh addresses are hard to type the same way twice, and foreign map providers miss many local roads. Barikoi is a Bangladeshi location data provider with a public API for address autocomplete, geocoding, reverse geocoding and vector maps.

This module brings Barikoi into Odoo. The street field of a contact searches Barikoi as you type and fills the address in Odoo fields, including the division as the Odoo state and the Barikoi uCode. The same search works on the website checkout, delivery address and My Account forms.
