Omnibus Directive Price History

Show the lowest price of the last 30 days on every discount

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Under the EU Omnibus Directive (2019/2161) a price reduction must state the lowest price of the 30 days before it. This module records every price change in Odoo and prints that line under each discounted price on the product page and the shop grid.

When an EU shop announces a reduction - a struck-through price - it must also show the lowest price it charged in the 30 days before the reduction. Odoo does not keep a price history, so the reference price is not known and the shop cannot prove it.

This module stores a price point every time a product's sales price changes and every time a pricelist rule for a product changes, plus a daily snapshot. When the shop shows a discounted price, it looks up the lowest earlier price in the look-back window and adds a line such as Lowest price in the last 30 days: 119.00 EUR.

Prices that belong to the running promotion are left out of the reference, and a product with no usable history shows no line at all rather than a wrong one. The full log is available in the backend as a list and a graph.
