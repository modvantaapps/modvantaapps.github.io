OpenMeter Connector

Metered usage from OpenMeter, invoiced from Odoo.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
OpenMeter and Odoo 19

What it does

Your product sends usage events to OpenMeter; your accountant invoices in Odoo. This module reads the usage of each customer per meter for a period and prepares one draft invoice per customer, with the products and prices you set in Odoo. It only reads from OpenMeter.

Load Meters lists your meters with key, aggregation and event type. Tick Bill It on the ones you invoice and pick the product. A unit price on the meter overrides the product price, and Divide By turns raw usage into billed units: with 1,000, 1,250,000 tokens become 1,250 units of "AI tokens (per 1,000)".

Load Customers brings your OpenMeter customers with their subjects. Each one is matched to the Odoo contact with the same email in the same company, or created with its billing address if you allow it.

Bill Last Month opens the usage run of the previous month. Read Usage asks OpenMeter for each billed meter, grouped by customer, for exactly that period; usage that belongs to no known customer is listed apart so nothing is lost. Create Draft Invoices makes one draft per customer with one line per meter. Nothing is posted or sent by itself.

Features

Pick where your OpenMeter runsOpenMeter Cloud, Kong Konnect Metering & Billing in the US, EU, AU, ME or IN region, or your own server over https. Paste a server-side API token and press Test Connection.
