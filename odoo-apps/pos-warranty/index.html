POS Warranty

A warranty for every serial number sold at the till - printed on the receipt, found again in seconds

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Mark a product, sell it at the Point of Sale and the customer's warranty is registered for each serial or unit, with its end date on the receipt. Sell an extended warranty with it, look a warranty up later by serial, receipt or customer, open a claim or renew it from the same till.

POS Warranty adds warranty registration to Odoo's Point of Sale, on top of our Warranty Registration Portal (a required add-on, installed with it).

Products marked Register Warranty at POS get one warranty per serial number typed at the till, or one per unit when the product is not tracked. The order must have a customer; the till asks for one before payment.

Extended warranty plans are service products with extra months: the cashier adds one to a line and the warranty of that line is extended when the order is paid. The receipt prints a warranty card with each warranty number, serial and end date.

The Warranty Lookup in the POS finds a warranty by serial, warranty number, receipt or customer. From there the cashier opens a claim (free inside the warranty, with a repair fee line outside it), adds a paid renewal to the order, or prints the warranty card. Refunds cancel the warranty of the returned unit.

Features

In the back officewarranty history, claims with stages, paid warranty and renewal invoices that activate on payment, a renewal wizard, a printable warranty card, expiry reminder emails and analysis.
