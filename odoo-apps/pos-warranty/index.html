POS Warranty

A warranty for every serial number sold at the till - printed on the receipt, found again in seconds

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Mark a product, sell it at the Point of Sale and the customer's warranty is registered for each serial or unit, with its end date on the receipt. Sell an extended warranty with it, look a warranty up later by serial, receipt or customer, open a claim or renew it from the same till.

POS Warranty adds warranty registration to Odoo's Point of Sale, on top of our Warranty Registration Portal (a required add-on, installed with it).

Products marked Register Warranty at POS get one warranty per serial number typed at the till, or one per unit when the product is not tracked. The order must have a customer; the till asks for one before payment.

Extended warranty plans are service products with extra months: the cashier adds one to a line and the warranty of that line is extended when the order is paid. The receipt prints a warranty card with each warranty number, serial and end date.

The Warranty Lookup in the POS finds a warranty by serial, warranty number, receipt or customer. From there the cashier opens a claim (free inside the warranty, with a repair fee line outside it), adds a paid renewal to the order, or prints the warranty card. Refunds cancel the warranty of the returned unit.

Who it is for

  • Electronics and appliance shops selling serial-tracked goods. Phones, watches and coffee machines leave the counter with a serial number the customer will quote months later. Each serial typed at the till gets its own warranty with an end date, printed on the receipt.
  • Retailers that sell protection plans. An extended warranty is set up as a service product with extra months. The cashier adds it to the line it covers, and that warranty is extended when the order is paid.
  • Shops with a repair or service counter. When a customer comes back, the cashier finds the warranty by serial, warranty number, receipt or customer and sees at once whether the repair is free or needs a claim fee.
  • Stores that want repeat warranty income. Products can allow renewal with a renewal product and number of months. Expiry reminder emails go out a set number of days before the end date, and a paid renewal can be added to a POS order.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. Warranty Registration Portal is installed with it as the warranty base.
  2. Open a product and go to the Warranty tab. Tick Register Warranty at POS, choose Free Warranty or Paid Warranty, and set Allow Renewal, Renewal Months and Renewal Product if renewals are sold.
  3. For an extended warranty, create a service product, tick Extended Warranty Plan on its Warranty tab and set Extra Months.
  4. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings and find the Warranty block. Turn on Create Warranty from POS and decide on Customer required, Warranty card on receipt and Email the warranty card.
  5. Turn on Warranty Lookup, Claims and Renewals and pick a Claim fee product for repairs outside the warranty period.
  6. Set Reminder days before end and the Warranty invoice product, then save.
  7. Manage everything afterwards under Point of Sale > Warranty: Warranties, Claims, Analysis and Claim Stages.

POS Warranty: questions

Does it need another module?

Yes. It's built on top of Warranty Registration Portal, which is installed automatically. That base depends on eCommerce and the portal, so those apps are installed too. It also needs Point of Sale and Invoicing, all of which exist in Community.

What if a product isn't tracked by serial number?

One warranty is created per unit sold. For lot-tracked products, one lot covers every unit on the line.

What happens when an item is refunded at the till?

The warranty of the returned serial or unit is cancelled and the history records that it was refunded at the POS. If the same serial is sold again later, its warranty starts over for the new buyer and the history keeps both.

Is a claim on an expired warranty free?

No. Outside the warranty period the claim is paid: the claim fee product is added to the POS order, or the claim can be invoiced from the back office.

How do paid warranties and renewals get activated?

Sold at the POS, they're paid with the order. Registered or renewed from the back office as paid, an invoice is created and the warranty or renewal only applies once that invoice is paid.

Does it handle several companies?

Yes. Warranty and claim numbers use sequences shared across companies, and if a serial already has a warranty in another company, the order gets a note and it isn't registered twice.
