Product Dimensions

Sell and buy by size: length x width x height and pieces on every line

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Type the size and the number of pieces on a quotation, purchase order, invoice or delivery. The line quantity becomes the real square meters, cubic meters or running meters, so your prices, taxes and stock keep working as usual.

Glass cut to size, timber by the running meter, foam blocks by the cubic meter: in standard Odoo a salesperson has to work out the quantity by hand and type it in, and the customer never sees the size they ordered on the document.

With this module you tick Sold by Dimensions on a product and choose how it is measured (length, area or volume) and in which unit your team types sizes (mm, cm, m, inch or foot). Sales order lines, purchase order lines, invoice lines and deliveries then get Length, Width, Height and Pieces columns. The quantity of the line is computed as pieces x size and converted to the unit of measure of the product.

Because the result is the normal Odoo quantity, pricelists, taxes, discounts, delivered and invoiced quantities and stock moves run on it unchanged. The size is printed under the description on the quotation and the delivery slip.

Features

One Dimensions tab on the product
Size and pieces on the quotation
Limits announced while typing
Dimensions follow to the delivery
Dimensions on the invoice
