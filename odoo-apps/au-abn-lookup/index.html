ABN Lookup

ABN Lookup - Australian Business Number validation, ACN and GST registration

What it does

Check every Australian customer and supplier against the Australian Business Register (ABR) from the Odoo contact form: search by business name, ABN or ACN, pick the right business and store its ABN status, entity type and GST registration on the contact.

The module connects Odoo contacts to the ABN Lookup web services of the Australian Business Register. Type an ABN, an ACN or just the business name and Odoo shows what the ABR holds: the entity name, ABN status and the date it took effect, entity type, GST registration and the date it started or ended, registered business names, trading names, the state and postcode of the main business location, DGR and ACNC charity status.

Your own data stays yours. Results only fill empty fields: the ABN goes into the Tax ID when it is empty or holds only an ACN, the ACN goes into the Company ID, and the address is filled only when you ask. A different ABN or a foreign tax ID is kept and the difference is noted. Tick Overwrite when you do want everything replaced.

ABN and ACN checksums are checked before any call, so a typing mistake shows straight away and never costs a lookup.
