Systempay Payment Gateway

Card payments through your Systempay shop, with refunds and capture from Odoo.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Customers pay on the Systempay page of your bank (Banque Populaire or Caisse d'Epargne). Odoo signs the form, checks the signed notification Systempay sends back and lets you refund, capture or cancel without opening the Merchant Back Office.

Enter the 8-digit shop ID and the test and production keys from the Merchant Back Office. Odoo builds the vads_ payment form for each payment (amount in cents, the currency number, a transaction number for the day, your order reference) and signs it with HMAC-SHA-256, or SHA-1 if your shop still uses it.

Systempay then posts the instant payment notification (IPN) to Odoo. Odoo recomputes the signature with the key of the mode the notification came from, checks the shop ID and the transaction number, and only then reads the status and the amount. A test notification is never accepted by a provider in production.

With the REST password filled in, refunds are sent with Transaction/CancelOrRefund: part of a payment, or all of it, more than once if needed. A payment that the bank has not captured yet is cancelled instead. Tick manual capture and payments stop at Authorized until you press Capture (Transaction/Validate) or Void.

The customer coming back to the shop is checked too. Odoo does not trust the return fields for the outcome: it asks Systempay for the transaction, or waits for the IPN.
