Employment Hero Connector

One module that brings Employment Hero employees, leave, timesheets, payslips, rosters and documents into Odoo, with webhooks and two way leave and attendance.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Employment Hero Connector for Odoo 19screens shown on this pagemodule to install, no separate packs

What it does

Built on the public Employment Hero API reference

The module uses the Employment Hero HR API with OAuth 2.0 and PKCE. Employees are created or matched by email, with manager, job title, company contact details, contractor flag and groups as tags.

Approved leave becomes approved Time Off, and timesheet entries become attendances. You can approve or decline pending requests from Odoo, and optionally send Odoo Time Off and attendances back.

Payslips, pay details, leave balances, rosters, documents and certifications are kept as read-only records. Webhooks with signature checks keep it current between scheduled syncs.
