Mongolia Payroll

Mongolia Payroll for Odoo 19: Social Insurance, Health Insurance and PIT

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Mongolia PayrollMongolian PayrollSocial InsuranceNDSHHealth InsurancePIT 10/15/20%KhKhOATTax Credit

What it does

Mongolian statutory payroll on Odoo Community: social insurance (NDSH) by fund and accident class on the minimum wage floor and the 10 x minimum wage ceiling, health insurance, personal income tax at 10% / 15% / 20% on the year total with the monthly tax credit, and monthly and quarterly registers.

Employee pension 8.5%, benefits 0.8%, unemployment 0.2%; employer pension 8.5%, benefits 1.0%, unemployment 0.5%, each shown on the payslip.

Accident and occupational disease insurance at 0.5%, 1.5% or 2.5% (10.5 / 11.5 / 12.5% employer total), set on the company with an override per employee.

Contributions on at least the minimum wage (792,000) and at most 10 x the minimum wage (7,920,000); part-time workers without the monthly floor.

Insurance deducted first, then 10% up to 120M, 15% to 180M and 20% above on the income so far this year, so the higher bands apply in the month they are reached.

Features

Per employeeregister number, insurance book, salary, base, employee and employer social and health insurance, as CSV.
Per employeetaxable income, credit, PIT withheld and the year-to-date totals the quarterly report asks for, as CSV.
