What it does

Buy Deutsche Post postage from Odoo. Validate the delivery order and the module picks the right Deutsche Post product for the weight, pays the Internetmarke from your Portokasse and attaches the stamp to the transfer as a PDF or PNG, with the sender and recipient address printed on it. Registered mail (Einschreiben) gets its tracking number, unused stamps are refunded with one click.

The module adds the delivery method type "Deutsche Post Internetmarke" to Odoo. The Deutsche Post product price list (Standardbrief, Kompaktbrief, Grossbrief, Maxibrief, Postkarte, Warensendung, Einschreiben, Einschreiben Einwurf, Rueckschein and the international letters) is included with its prices, weight and size limits. Odoo quotes the postage on sales orders and in the eCommerce checkout, chooses the cheapest product that fits each package (or always the product you set) and buys the stamps when the delivery is validated. Every stamp is stored with its voucher ID, tracking number, price and status, and can be refunded while it is unused.
