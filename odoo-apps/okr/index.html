OKR - Objectives and Key Results Management

Set company, team and personal objectives for each quarter, measure them with key results, and let Odoo compute the progress. People check in every week with a value, their confidence and a comment, and the dashboard shows at a glance what is on track, at risk or off track.

What it does

Company / team / personal OKRsQuarters and yearsAlignment tree5 metric typesAutomatic progressWeighted scoringWeekly check-insConfidenceStatus by calendarProject tasksOdoo records (count / sum)RemindersDashboard

Objectives say where you want to go, key results prove you got there. The module gives every objective a period (a quarter, a year or your own cycle), a level (company, team or personal), an owner and contributors. Objectives align to a parent objective and can deliver one of its key results, so the company key result follows the team objective automatically. Key results are numbers, percentages, amounts, done / not done or milestone lists. Their progress comes from check-ins, from project tasks done, from any Odoo records counted or summed in the period, or from aligned objectives. The status compares the progress with the time already gone in the period.
