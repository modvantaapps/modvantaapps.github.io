Swiss Post Shipping Integration

Swiss Post shipping integration for Odoo Community (swisspost, Die Post, La Poste Suisse, Schweizerische Post, post ch): swiss post label PDF/ZPL A5 A6 A7, PostPac Priority and Economy, swiss post delivery to PickPost and My Post 24 at the checkout, cash on delivery BLN, GAS return labels, pick@home, tracking, PostPac International waybill with customs, address check, rate table - swiss post integration, swiss post connector, swiss post odoo, postpac, pickpost, swiss shipping, switzerland shipping, schweizer post

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The module adds Swiss Post as a provider on the standard Odoo delivery method, for PostPac Priority, PostPac Economy, PickPost / My Post 24 and international parcels. When a delivery order is validated the consignment is created with the Swiss Post barcode API, the barcode becomes the tracking reference and the label is attached.

Prices come from a rate table by zone and weight or the other Odoo price modes. Services such as signature, signature with insurance, personal delivery, fragile, bulky goods, Saturday delivery and time slot delivery can be set on the method and changed per transfer, and Swiss Post can notify the recipient by email.
