Timesheet Sheet

Weekly timesheet sheets with approval and lock

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Employees fill one sheet per week (or two weeks, half month or month) in a grid, submit it, and the approver approves or refuses it with a reason. Once submitted or approved, the timesheets of the sheet are locked.

Odoo Community records timesheet lines one by one, with no weekly sheet to submit and no approval: a manager has no simple way to confirm that last week's hours are final, and nothing stops an employee changing them after invoicing.

This module groups each employee's timesheets into a sheet per period. The sheet shows a grid of projects and tasks against the days of the period, with the logged, expected and difference totals. The employee types hours straight into the grid and clicks Submit to Approver.

The approver - the employee's timesheet approver, the manager or the project managers, as you choose - sees the sheets waiting for them, approves or refuses with a reason, and can reset a sheet to draft. Timesheets of a submitted or approved sheet are locked for the employee, and every sheet can be printed.

Features

A weekly grid per employee
Submit to the approver
Sheets waiting for my approval
The approver's view

Locked for the employee
