Tip / Donation at Checkout

Add tip or round-up donation during checkout.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Checkout tipDonation at checkoutRound upCharity

What it does

Add a small box to the cart page with your own heading and text, preset amounts (for example $2, $5, $10), a Round up button that rounds the order total to the next whole amount, and an optional custom amount.

The tip or donation becomes one "Tip / Donation" line on the order, without tax, and stays there through checkout and payment. The shopper can remove it with one click.
