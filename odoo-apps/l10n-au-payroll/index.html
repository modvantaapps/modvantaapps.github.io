Australia Payroll

Australia Payroll - STP Phase 2, Superannuation and Long Service Leave

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Scales 1-6PAYG withholding with STSLSGon ordinary time earningsSTPpay event payload and registerLSLbalances and liability by state

What it does

STP Phase 2PAYG withholdingSuperannuationSuperStream SAFFLong service leaveIncome statements

Payslips calculate PAYG withholding from the ATO statement of formulas for the employee's tax scale, including the STSL component, and show the weekly equivalent used. Superannuation guarantee is worked out on ordinary time earnings, and a quarterly or Payday Super liability produces the SuperStream SAFF contribution file.

For every pay run an STP Phase 2 pay event builds the payload and a lodgement register; update events and finalisation declarations are included. Long service leave balances and liability are kept per state, and payment summaries (income statements) show the disaggregation of gross. The module prepares the files; it does not transmit to the ATO.

Features

Each employee gets an Australian Payroll tabTFN declaration (tax-free threshold, foreign resident, Medicare levy exemption, study and training support loan), STP Phase 2 income type and employment basis, super fund and member number, and long service leave state and service start date. Contracts add salary sacrifice, employee additional super and employer extra super.
