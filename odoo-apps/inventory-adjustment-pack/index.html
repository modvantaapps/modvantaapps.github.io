Inventory Adjustment with Cost, Cancel Inventory Adjustment, Reverse Inventory Adjustment

Inventory Adjustment with Cost and Cancel / Reverse for Odoo 19

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Two fixes for Odoo physical inventory. Type the unit cost of the stock you found and Odoo values it at that cost, journal entry included. Applied a wrong count? Cancel it: the stock goes back, the cost is recomputed and the journal entry is reversed.

A Unit Cost column on Inventory -> Physical Inventory. The quantity a count adds is valued at the cost you type, not at the product cost.

Average cost and FIFO follow the typed cost. With perpetual valuation the journal entry is posted at that cost.

Inventory -> Applied Adjustments lists every applied count. Cancel one or many: stock, cost and journal entry go back.

Post a reversal entry (safe in a closed period) or cancel the original entry. One adjustment of a shared entry gets its own reversal.

Features

Switch it on in Inventory settings
Unit Cost on the physical inventory
Every applied adjustment in one list
Valued at the counted cost
Cancel an adjustment
The count is back, ready to fix
Who cancelled, when and why
Reversal journal entry
