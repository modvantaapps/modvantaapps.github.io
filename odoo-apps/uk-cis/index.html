UK CIS Construction Industry Scheme

UK CIS - Construction Industry Scheme for Odoo 19

What it does

Deduct CIS from subcontractor payments on ordinary Odoo vendor bills, build the CIS300 monthly return per HMRC tax month, and send every subcontractor a Payment and Deduction Statement - in Odoo Community.

A contractor paying subcontractors for construction work has to withhold tax at 0%, 20% or 30% depending on the subcontractor's HMRC status, declare it on the CIS300 every tax month and give each subcontractor a statement. This module does that on the vendor bills you already raise.

Each subcontractor carries a UTR, a verification number and date and a deduction status. On their bills every line is typed as labour or materials, and the deduction is taken on labour only. It posts as one tax-free negative line to your CIS liability account, so the bill total is what you actually pay and the VAT stays on the full labour value.

At month end a CIS300 return collects every subcontractor paid in the tax month (6th to 5th) with gross paid, materials, labour and CIS deducted, and creates a Payment and Deduction Statement for each one, ready to print or email. Figures are keyed into the HMRC portal from the return; the module does not call HMRC.

Who it is for

  • Building contractors paying subcontract labour. A firm that pays bricklayers, roofers or groundworkers has to hold back CIS from the labour part of each payment. Here the deduction is added to the vendor bill itself, so the amount you pay out and the amount owed to HMRC both come from the same document.
  • Small builders on Odoo Community. The module only depends on Accounting, so a builder that runs Community and doesn't use any project or construction app can still keep the monthly CIS300 figures in Odoo.
  • Bookkeepers doing the monthly return for a contractor. At the end of each tax month the CIS300 return lists every subcontractor paid between the 6th and the 5th, with gross paid, materials, labour and the amount deducted, ready to type into HMRC's online service.
  • Contractors whose subcontractors supply materials. Products marked as materials are kept out of the deduction, so a plasterer who bills for board and labour on one invoice only has CIS taken from the labour lines.

Setting it up

  1. Create a current liability account for CIS deductions, then open Accounting > Configuration > Settings and select it as the CIS Liability Account in the Construction Industry Scheme block.
  2. In the same block choose the CIS300 Basis: payment date, which is the default, or bill date.
  3. On each subcontractor's contact tick CIS Subcontractor, choose the CIS Deduction Status and fill in the UTR, the CIS Verification No. and the Verified On date.
  4. Open the products you buy as materials and set CIS Category to materials. Lines without that are treated as labour on a subcontractor's bill.
  5. Enter the subcontractor's vendor bill as normal. Click Apply CIS Deduction to see the deduction line straight away, or simply post the bill and it is added then.
  6. Each month go to Accounting > Reporting > CIS > CIS300 Returns, create a return for the tax month and click Generate, then Generate Statements.
  7. After you have entered the figures with HMRC, click Mark Submitted, and use Send to Subcontractor on each Payment & Deduction Statement.

UK CIS Construction Industry Scheme: questions

Does it submit the CIS300 to HMRC?

No. There's no connection to HMRC's systems. The return gives you the totals per subcontractor to key in yourself, and Mark Submitted then records that it's done.

Does the deduction affect VAT on the bill?

The CIS deduction is posted as a separate negative line with no tax on it, so VAT is still worked out on the full labour value. Domestic reverse charge VAT rules for construction are a separate question, so check those with your accountant.

What happens if I press Apply CIS Deduction twice?

The existing deduction line is replaced rather than a second one being added. You can change a line's CIS type on the draft bill and apply again to refresh the amount.

Are supplier credit notes handled?

No. The deduction and the CIS300 return only look at vendor bills. If a subcontractor issues a credit note, adjust the next bill or the return figures by hand and agree the treatment with your accountant.

Who decides whether a subcontractor is gross, net or higher rate?

HMRC does, when you verify the subcontractor. The module stores the status, verification number and date you were given and applies the rate that goes with it. It doesn't carry out the verification for you.

Can it run for more than one company?

Yes. Returns, return lines and statements are limited to the companies the user can access, and the liability account and basis are set per company.
