Ecuador Payroll

Ecuador Payroll for Odoo 19: Rol de Pagos, IESS, Decimos, Fondos de Reserva and RDEP

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Ecuador PayrollEcuadorian PayrollNomina EcuadorRol de PagosIESSDecimo TerceroDecimo CuartoFondos de Reserva

What it does

Rol de pagos on Odoo Community. Each payslip takes the 9.45% IESS personal contribution, shows the 11.15% employer contribution, pays the decimo tercero, decimo cuarto and fondos de reserva monthly or accrues them, and withholds income tax the way Reglamento LRTI art. 104 describes: nothing in January, then the projected annual tax less the gastos personales rebate spread over February to December. At year end it builds the RDEP XML and checks it against the SRI schema.

One twelfth of the month's pay, paid monthly or accrued for December (Codigo del Trabajo art. 111).

One twelfth of the SBU each month, or accrued for March or August by region.

One twelfth of the pay from the 13th month of service, paid with the salary or left for the IESS.

Projected annual income less IESS, the 2026 table, nothing in January, then spread over 11 months.

Features

Not includedthe IESS planilla upload file, utilidades distribution, Formulario 107 printout and IECE / SECAP (enter them yourself if they apply).
