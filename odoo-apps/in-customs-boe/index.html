India Customs Bill of Entry

What it does

Record each import consignment as a bill of entry in Odoo, compute the customs duty line by line, post the customs duty bill and move the duty that is a cost into the value of the goods received.

For exports, prepare the shipping bill from the delivered sale order with its packing list, record the shipping bill number on the export invoice and follow the consignment to the let export order and the realisation of the payment.

Nothing is filed with ICEGATE or any authority: the module keeps your records and documents in Odoo. Duty rates are not shipped; you enter the rates in force for your tariff items.
