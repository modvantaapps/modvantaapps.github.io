Faire Connector

Faire integration - Faire wholesale connector for Odoo 19

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Faire orders land in Odoo net of commission, and ship from Odoo.

Every few minutes the connector asks Faire for orders changed since the last read. A new order becomes a quotation for the retailer, with its delivery address, one line per item at the wholesale price, a separate line for any tester, and negative lines for Faire's commission and payout fee. A 12 x $14.50 candle order at 15% commission arrives as $174.00 of candles, -$26.10 commission and -$5.22 payout fee.

You decide how much happens on its own. Accept new orders automatically or press Accept on Faire yourself; Odoo confirms the order either way. When Faire cancels, the Odoo order is canceled too, unless goods have already shipped. When Faire pays you, the order can be invoiced and the payout registered on your bank journal.

Shipping stays in Odoo. Validate the delivery with a tracking number and the shipment goes to Faire with the carrier, which moves the order to pre-transit and lets Faire tell the retailer.

Products go the other way. Tick Sell on Faire on a product, set the wholesale price, case size and minimum quantity, and send it: each Odoo variant becomes a Faire variant with its SKU and options. Stock is pushed by SKU from the warehouse you pick.
