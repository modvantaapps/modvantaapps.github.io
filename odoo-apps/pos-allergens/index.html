POS Allergens

Know what is in every dish, warn the cashier before a customer gets something they cannot eat, and print the labels.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
POS allergen warningFood allergy profileNutrition facts labelEU allergen declaration

What it does

Allergens and nutrition on every product, allergy profiles on customers, and a check in the till that the server repeats when the order is paid.

Features

Allergen tagsthe EU 14 allergens and the FDA Big 9 are loaded, with codes (GLU, MLK, PNT...), colours and keywords. Add your own.
Contains and may containon every product, with ingredients, a dietary note and nutrition facts per serving.
Customer food allergy profileallergens to avoid, severity (preference, intolerance, allergy, severe) and notes, on the contact and from the till.
Allergen warning in the POSwhen the customer is selected and at Payment: the conflicting items and allergens, Remove buttons, Go back or confirm.
Server checkan order with a conflict cannot be paid until the warning was confirmed; the confirmation text is kept on the order.
Severe allergiesfor a severe allergy, may contain (traces) counts as a conflict too.
Allergen codes on product cardsGLU MLK on the card, and allergens plus nutrition in Product Info.
Allergy alert on kitchen ticketsthe customer's allergies printed on the order ticket sent to the kitchen, and a banner on the order.
Nutrition Facts labelprintable label with % Daily Value (2,000 kcal diet).
EU allergen declarationingredients with allergens in bold, the 14 allergens table and the per 100 g nutrition declaration.
From the bill of materialswith Manufacturing installed, nutrition and allergens are computed from the recipe components.
Allergen dashboardone card per allergen: products that contain it, may contain it, and customers at risk.
