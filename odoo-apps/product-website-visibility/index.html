Product Website Visibility

Put a product or an eCommerce category on several websites, and decide who sees it: selected customers, customer tags, pricelists, countries, signed-in customers or guests. Hidden products are really gone - 404 page, no search result, no sitemap entry, no recommendation.

What it does

Product Multi WebsiteWebsite Product VisibilityHide ProductsCustomer CatalogPrivate CatalogB2B CatalogShow Products After LoginHide Products from GuestsHide Products by CountryPricelist VisibilityHide CategoryBulk Assign

Everything needed to control who sees which product on which website.

Pick any number of websites on the product. Standard Odoo allows one website or all of them; here a product can sit on two of your three shops. The standard Website field is kept in step automatically.

eCommerce categories get the same website list. A category that does not belong to a website disappears from its menu and filters, and (optionally) takes its products with it.

Show a product only to selected customers, customer tags, pricelists or countries - or hide it from them. A company in the list covers all of its contacts.
