What it does

One-click add-on offers on the cart page, right where customers decide

Create small offers - an accessory, a warranty, a refill - and the module shows them in an Add to your order box under the products on the eCommerce cart page. One click on Add puts the product in the cart, with no product page and no configurator in between.

Each offer can have its own headline and text, a special offer price that replaces the normal price on the cart line, and a minimum cart amount so bigger offers only appear on bigger carts. Products already in the cart are not offered again.
