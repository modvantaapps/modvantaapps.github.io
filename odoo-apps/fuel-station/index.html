Fuel Station Management

Run a fuel station, petrol pump, gas station or filling station on Odoo: tanks with tank dip and a tank calibration chart, dip variance posted as fuel loss or gain, nozzle readings per shift, pump attendant cash and card shift reconciliation, fuel sales posted to stock and accounting, fuel delivery into the tank, fuel credit sales with fleet cards and vehicle limits, price changes by date, a lubricant and shop POS and a daily fuel dashboard. One module, built on Odoo's own Inventory, Invoicing, Purchase, Employees and Point of Sale.

What it does

The meters are the truth, and every litre is accounted for

One or many stations. Every tank is a real stock location under the station warehouse, with capacity, low level, dead stock, ullage and fill %. Pumps carry nozzles, each nozzle draws from one tank.

Enter the dip chart of each tank (mm against litres). A dip in millimetres is turned into litres by interpolation, the water level is deducted, temperature and density are kept.

Each posted dip compares the measured stock with book stock and posts the loss or gain to stock. Variances above your tolerance get a review activity for the station manager.

A new shift lists every nozzle with the last closing meter as its opening meter. Enter closing meters and pump test litres; sold litres and amounts are calculated at the price locked when the shift opened.
