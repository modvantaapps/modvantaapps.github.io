Panama Payroll

Panama Payroll for Odoo 19: CSS, Seguro Educativo, ISR, Decimo Tercer Mes and Planilla 03

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Panama PayrollPanama CSSSeguro Social 9.75%Ley 462Seguro EducativoRiesgos ProfesionalesDecimo Tercer MesISR

What it does

Panamanian statutory payroll on Odoo Community: Caja de Seguro Social quotas with the Ley 462 schedule, seguro educativo, riesgos profesionales, the decimo tercer mes in April, August and December, ISR withholding and the DGI Informe 03 (Planilla 03 version 5) as an Excel file.

Employee 9.75% and employer 13.25% on the salary, no ceiling; 14.25% and 15.25% from March 2027 and March 2029.

Employee 1.25% and employer 1.50% on the salary, not on the decimo.

Employer only, at the rate the CSS assigned to the company, with an override per worker for a different activity. Not on the decimo.

Added to the April, August and December payslips: 1/12 of the salaries of the four months before, with CSS 7.25% employee and 10.75% employer.

Features

Per workercedula, CSS number, salary, decimo, CSS, seguro educativo, riesgos and ISR as CSV, plus the total of the CSS planilla.
