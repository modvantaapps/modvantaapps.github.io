What it does

DeCA Spain: the electronic road-transport control document, straight from your delivery orders

Ley 9/2025 made the documento de control administrativo of Orden FOM/2861/2012 digital, and the Resolucion de 5 de junio de 2026 of the Direccion General de Transporte por Carretera y Ferrocarril (BOE-A-2026-12784) sets what the electronic document (DeCA) and the software that produces it must do.

There is no Ministry portal, upload or certification: the shipper or the carrier generates the PDF and hosts it at its own unique URL. This module does exactly that inside Odoo. It fills the shipper, carrier, origin, destination, nature and weight from the delivery order, checks every legal datum before emission, renders a native PDF with an embedded QR code and serves it at a login-free HTTPS URL during the service.

Changes on the road follow the two methods of the resolution: the same PDF and URL with the old value struck through, or a new DeCA when the carrier or the shipper changes. Seven calendar days after the end of the service the public URL closes by itself; the record and every PDF version are kept.
