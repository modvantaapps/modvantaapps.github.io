Odoo Zendesk Connector

Zendesk Connector for Odoo 19 - Tickets, Contacts and Conversations

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Bring your Zendesk tickets into Odoo with their conversation, custom fields and tags, reply to customers from Odoo, and keep Zendesk users and organisations in step with Odoo contacts.

Zendesk tickets are imported into Odoo with the requester, organisation, group, priority, tags and custom fields.

The conversation comes with them; you reply to the customer or add an internal note from Odoo.

Ticket changes made in Odoo are sent back to Zendesk with a safe update, and conflict rules decide who wins.

Zendesk users and organisations are linked to Odoo contacts, with duplicates sent to review.
