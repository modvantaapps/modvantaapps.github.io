FBR Digital Invoicing

Pakistan FBR Digital Invoicing for Odoo 19: customer invoices and debit notes go to the FBR / PRAL DI API, and the FBR invoice number is printed on the invoice with its QR code.

What it does

Sales tax registered businesses in Pakistan must share every sale invoice with FBR in real time through the Digital Invoicing (DI) API run by PRAL. This module connects Odoo Invoicing to that API: when you confirm a customer invoice, Odoo checks it with FBR, posts it, stores the FBR invoice number and prints it on the invoice with the QR code FBR asks for.

Features

Validate, then posteach invoice is first sent to validateinvoicedata; only data FBR calls valid is posted to postinvoicedata, so no invalid invoice is registered.
FBR invoice number on the invoicethe number FBR returns, its date and the item numbers are stored; the invoice PDF prints the number, a 1 x 1 inch QR code and the Digital Invoicing logo you upload.
Sandbox scenarios and productionswitch between the sandbox (with the scenario ID SN001-SN028 per invoice, as FBR requires for testing) and production; each environment has its own security token.
Debit notesdebit notes are sent as "Debit Note" with the FBR number of the original invoice as invoiceRefNo. Credit notes can be sent too once FBR lists that document type for your profile.
Rejections explainedwhen FBR rejects an invoice, the error code, the FBR message and a plain "what to change" line are shown on the invoice.
Retry queueinvoices that could not reach FBR are queued and sent again every 10 minutes; an accepted invoice is never sent twice and cannot be reset to draft.
Products and taxesHS code, FBR unit of measure, sale type, rate text, SRO schedule and SRO item serial on the product; each tax is mapped to sales tax, further tax, extra tax, FED or ST withheld.
Reference data and buyer checksdownload provinces, document types, HS codes, SRO items, transaction types and units from FBR, look up rates, SRO schedules and HS units, and check a buyer with Get_Reg_Type and STATL.
