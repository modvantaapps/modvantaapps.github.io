POS Backend Order

Create, pay and invoice POS orders from the back office, and print or email a POS receipt

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Phone orders, corrections and sales taken away from the till can be entered as real POS orders in the back office: pick a point of sale, add the customer and products, take the payment, invoice it. Any POS order prints as an 80 mm receipt PDF or goes to the customer by email.

In Odoo the POS order form cannot create orders: everything has to go through a till screen. And there is no way to reprint a receipt from the back office or send it to a customer as a PDF.

This module adds New Order (Back Office). It uses the open session of the point of sale you choose, or opens one. Add the customer and the products - prices come from the pricelist, taxes from the product - and the server computes the totals. Odoo's own Payment, Invoice and Return Products buttons finish the flow, with the delivery created and validated like an order from the till.

Every POS order gets Print Receipt (a PDF in till-receipt layout: company, receipt number, lines, taxes, total, payments, change, invoice number) and Send Receipt (the PDF emailed to the customer from a mail template).
