Asset Management (IT Equipment)

Know who has every laptop, phone and licence - and since when

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Issue equipment to employees with asset tags and conditions, keep the full history of every holder, chase overdue loans, track software licence seats and their cost, take requests, run physical audits and let staff confirm what they received from the portal.

Odoo Community already has an equipment register in Maintenance, with serial numbers, categories, vendors and warranty dates, and it can link a piece of equipment to one employee. It does not keep who had it before, when it is due back, in what condition it left, or whether the person ever confirmed receiving it.

This module builds on that same register instead of creating a second one. It adds asset tags, a stock status (in stock, issued, in repair), an issue wizard and a return wizard, and an assignment record for every hand-over with dates, condition out and back, notes and acknowledgement. Overdue Returns lists loans past their due date, and Who Holds What shows equipment and software per person.

Software licences get their own register with total, used and free seats, cost per seat, annual cost and the cost of unused seats, plus a renewal reminder. Employees can request equipment with an approval step, kits group what a new joiner gets, audits compare what is found on the shelf with what the register says, and employees see their equipment in the portal and confirm they received it.
