Mass Mailing Themes

Mass Mailing Themes for Odoo 19 Email Marketing

What it does

12 ready email marketing templates and 27 building blocks for the Odoo mailing editor, all in your brand colours. Add a product grid with real pricelist prices, a live countdown timer and a coupon in a few clicks, then check the email with a built-in checklist and a light, dark and phone preview before you send.

Everything a shop or a B2B team needs to send good-looking campaigns from Odoo.

Monthly digest, flash sale, product launch, event invitation, welcome, cart reminder, order follow-up, season greetings, B2B announcement, minimal letter, we miss you and feedback request - in the standard theme picker of Email Marketing.

Two new block groups, Commerce and Engagement, plus headers, footers and text blocks: product grid, spotlight, coupon ticket, countdown, cart reminder, order summary, price tiers, testimonials, follow us, steps, event details, rating request and more.

Five colours, an email-safe heading and body font, button shape and a footer note in Email Marketing settings. Every theme and block picks them up; each company keeps its own kit. A live preview warns about low contrast.

Who it is for

  • Online shops sending promotional newsletters. A Flash Sale or Season Greetings theme puts a product grid with your pricelist prices, a coupon and a live countdown into one email, without hand-building HTML.
  • B2B distributors announcing trade prices. The B2B Announcement theme has volume price tiers, trade benefits and a request-a-quote button, so a price change goes out looking like your company and not like a stock template.
  • Groups running several companies in one Odoo. Each company keeps its own brand kit of colours, fonts, button shape and footer note, and the themes and blocks pick up the kit of the company that sends the mailing.
  • Marketing teams who've had emails clipped or flagged. Every mailing gets an Email Checklist score before it goes out, catching an empty subject, a missing unsubscribe link, a body over Gmail's 102 KB clipping limit and links to localhost.

Features

Pick products in the block optionsimage, name, price and button fill in from Odoo. Prices from any pricelist with the old price struck through, 3 per row, 2 per row or list, description on or off, your own button text.
A score out of 100 on every mailingempty subject, spam words, missing preview text, missing unsubscribe link, Gmail clipping over 102 KB, images without alt text, links still pointing to "#".

Setting it up

  1. Install the module next to Odoo's Email Marketing app.
  2. Go to Email Marketing > Configuration > Settings and fill in the Mailing Brand Kit: brand colours, heading and body font, button shape and footer note. The Brand preview warns you if contrast is too low.
  3. Decide whether to tick Block sending on checklist errors, which refuses to send or schedule a mailing while its checklist reports errors.
  4. Create a new mailing and choose one of the twelve themes in the standard theme picker, for example Monthly Digest, Cart Reminder or Feedback Request.
  5. Drag extra blocks from the Commerce and Engagement groups. In a Product Grid, pick products and a pricelist in the block options; in a Countdown Timer, set the end date.
  6. Open the Email Checklist tab on the mailing, fix anything flagged, then press Light / Dark Preview to see desktop, forced dark and phone-width views side by side.

Mass Mailing Themes: questions

Which Odoo apps does it need?

It depends on Email Marketing (mass_mailing) and Products. Both are part of Community, so Enterprise isn't required.

How does the countdown work in mail apps that block JavaScript?

The timer is a GIF image drawn by Odoo each time the email is opened, so it shows the time left at that moment. The image link is signed with the database secret, so nobody can use your server to render other images.

Will product images load for people who aren't logged in?

Yes. When a product is placed in a block, the module makes a public copy of its image so recipients outside Odoo can see it.

What does the checklist score mean?

It starts at 100. Each error costs 30 points and each warning 10. Errors include an empty subject, empty body, no unsubscribe link and links to localhost; warnings include missing preview text, spam-like subjects, images without alt text and http links.

Can I still edit the themes freely?

Yes. Themes are built from normal blocks in the Odoo 19 mailing editor, so you can move, delete or restyle any part and save the result as your own template.

Does it send email itself or change deliverability?

No. Sending still goes through Odoo's own mail servers and Email Marketing queue. The checklist points out common problems, but it can't promise inbox placement.
