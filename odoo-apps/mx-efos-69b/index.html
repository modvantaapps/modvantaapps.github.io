EFOS 69-B SAT Mexico

The SAT 69-B list (lista negra SAT) checked against every RFC in your Odoo

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Download the official SAT list of taxpayers with simulated operations (EFOS, articulo 69-B del CFF), mark every contact whose RFC is Presunto or Definitivo, and stop or flag the vendor bills, invoices, sales and purchase orders that involve them.

Every day a scheduled action downloads the complete 69-B list published by the SAT as a CSV file and imports it into Odoo, with the notice number and the SAT and DOF publication dates of each stage: presunto, desvirtuado, definitivo and sentencia favorable. You can also upload the CSV yourself.

After each update every contact with an RFC is checked again. Contacts also get checked the moment you create them or change their RFC. The contact shows its 69-B status, a red banner when it is Presunto or Definitivo, and a smart button to its vendor bills.

Vendor bills, customer invoices, sales orders and purchase orders of a taxpayer at risk show the same banner. Per company you choose what happens when someone confirms them: no check, a warning written in the chatter, or a hard block.
