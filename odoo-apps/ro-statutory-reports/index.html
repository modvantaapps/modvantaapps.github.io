Romania Tax Declarations D300 D394 D390

Odoo ships the Romanian chart and the tax grids, but no file ANAF accepts. This module reads your posted invoices and bills, fills the three VAT declarations, checks them and gives you the XML to upload in SPV. It also prints the sales journal and the purchase journal for the same period.

What it does

Romanian companies and accounting firms on Odoo 19 that rebuild these declarations by hand every month. Odoo Community is enough.

Rows come from the tax grids of the core Romanian localisation. VAT on payment waits for the payment. Rows the form calculates stay locked; rows you type survive a recompute.

Partner lines by type (L, V, A, AI, C, N, LS, AS, ASI), partner type 1 to 4 and rate, with product codes for reverse charge, credit notes, cancelled invoices, self-billing, invoice series, simplified invoices, cash receipts (op2) and the refund questions.

Operators grouped by type L, T, A, P, S and R, Greece written EL, names cleaned to the characters ANAF allows, corrective statements, and section A and B entries.

Invalid CUI, missing county, missing product code, VAT charged by an unregistered supplier, a refund below 5,000 lei, D394 not matching D300: each error points at the document or partner to fix.

Features

One card per declarationthe next period, its status, the legal deadline and the last period filed. Generate opens the right period, already computed.
Jurnal de vanzari and jurnal de cumpararione row per document, base and VAT per rate, columns for exempt, EU, export and reverse charge, and VAT on payment. On screen, in PDF and in Excel.
