Milk Delivery Management

Complete milk & dairy subscription delivery — routes, stop sequence, wallet, auto-schedule, holidays, WhatsApp.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Milk deliveryDairy subscriptionDelivery routesPrepaid walletDriver round sheet

What it does

Run a doorstep milk round in Odoo. Each customer has subscriptions (product, quantity per delivery, price, days, start date) on a route with a driver and delivery weekdays, in stop order.

Every morning Generate Today's Sheets builds one delivery sheet per route from the active subscriptions, skipping holidays and customer vacations. Drivers open their round on their phone through a private link, and each delivered line is charged to the customer's prepaid wallet when there is enough balance. Unpaid deliveries can be invoiced in bulk.

Daily milk and dairy subscriptionsroutes, round sheets, drivers and prepaid wallets
