Vietnam VAT Return 01/GTGT

Vietnam VAT return for Odoo Community and Enterprise: the 01/GTGT VAT declaration (to khai thue GTGT) of Circular 89/2026/TT-BTC, monthly or quarterly, filled from posted invoices and bills by tax rate 0%, 5%, 8%, 10%, with carry forward [22]/[43], refund [42], sales and purchases invoice listing, PDF and Excel. What it does

The 01/GTGT VAT return of Circular 89/2026, filled from your posted invoices and bills

Circular 89/2026/TT-BTC replaced Circular 80/2021 from 1 July 2026 and issued a new form 01/GTGT with the indicators [32a], [32b] and [34a]. Choose the month or the quarter, click Compute, and every indicator from [21] to [43] is filled from the taxes on your posted customer invoices, credit notes and vendor bills.

Each Odoo tax says which box it feeds; the Odoo Vietnam taxes are mapped automatically. The VAT carried forward [22] comes from [43] of the previous filed return, and you type the adjustments [37], [38], [39a], [40b] and the refund request [42].

Print the return in the layout of the form or export it to Excel with the sales and purchases listings, then type the figures into HTKK or the eTax portal.
