What it does

Give every product and variant a valid barcode, a clean internal reference and a QR code - in bulk from any product list or automatically when the product is created. EAN-13, EAN-8 and UPC-A get a correct GS1 check digit from your company prefix, references follow your own pattern of category, sequence and attribute codes, and typed barcodes with a wrong check digit are caught before they reach a label. Existing codes are never overwritten unless you ask, and every run can be undone.

Barcodes, internal references and QR codes for every product, in one module.

Every numeric barcode gets the GS1 check digit, so scanners and marketplaces accept it. Code 128 and Code 39 are there too for in-house labels.

Enter the prefix you got from GS1 and the item numbers are counted after it. No prefix yet? Codes come from the GS1 in-store range (200..., 2..., 4...) meant for use inside your company.

Give a product category, and all its sub-categories, its own barcode prefix to keep departments in separate number blocks.
