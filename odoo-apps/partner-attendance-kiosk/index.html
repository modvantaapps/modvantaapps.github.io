Partner Attendance Kiosk

A self check in kiosk for customers, members and visitors: badge, QR code, barcode or PIN, a membership validity check, visit times, printable badges, reports and a portal history.

Partner Attendance Kiosk for Odoo 19

What it does

Put a tablet or a PC at the entrance and open the kiosk link: no Odoo login is needed.

Members scan their badge (barcode scanner or camera) or type their PIN to check in.

The same scan on the way out checks them out and shows the visit time.

Who it is for

  • Gyms and fitness studios. Members scan a badge or type a PIN on a tablet at the door. If their Valid Until date has passed, the kiosk can warn the front desk or refuse the check in, depending on how you set it.
  • Coworking spaces and clubs. You can see who is in the building right now, how long each visit lasted and how often each member comes, without asking anyone to sign a paper book.
  • Training centres and tuition classes. Students check themselves in when they arrive and out when they leave, and staff can print an attendance report for a date range, per student or per kiosk.
  • Venues that get walk-in visitors. With visitor registration switched on, a first-time guest types their name, email and phone on the kiosk and is checked in at once, so they're in your contacts from the first visit.

Setting it up

  1. Open Member Check In > Configuration > Kiosks and edit the Front Desk Kiosk or create a new one.
  2. Choose how people identify: Badge / QR / Barcode, Camera QR Scanner, Identification by PIN, Manual Selection or Register New Visitors. Tick PIN After Badge or Name if you want a second step.
  3. Pick the validity check (no check, warn, or refuse check in), set Auto Check Out After (Hours) and write the Welcome Message.
  4. On each contact open the Kiosk tab, tick Kiosk Member, set Valid Until if you sell passes, and click Generate Badge ID and Generate PIN. Use Print Badge for a card with the QR code and barcode.
  5. Click Open Kiosk, or copy the Kiosk URL to the tablet's browser. The tablet doesn't need an Odoo login.
  6. Follow visits under Member Check In > Inside Now and Visits, and use Reporting > Attendance Report or Attendance Analysis for totals.

Partner Attendance Kiosk: questions

Is this the same as Odoo Attendances?

No. Odoo's Attendances app is for employees. This kiosk checks in contacts (res.partner), such as members, students, customers and visitors, and keeps its own visit records.

Is the kiosk link safe to leave on a public tablet?

The link carries a secret token and only shows the kiosk screen, which can check members in and out and register new visitors if you allowed it. If the link leaks, click New Kiosk Link and the old one stops working.

What scanners can I use?

Any USB or Bluetooth scanner that types the code and then Enter. On browsers that can read QR codes and barcodes, a camera button lets people scan with the tablet's camera instead.

What if someone forgets to check out?

A scheduled action runs every hour and closes visits that have been open longer than the kiosk's Auto Check Out After (Hours) value, flagging them as Auto Checked Out. Set it to 0 to keep visits open.

Where does membership validity come from?

From the Valid Until date on the contact. If one of Modvanta's membership or gym management modules is installed, active memberships from it are taken into account too. Without either, there's nothing to check and you'd leave the check off.

Can members see their own visits?

Yes. Contacts with portal access get a My Visits page in their portal account listing each check in, the time spent and their validity. The module needs only Contacts, Discuss and Portal, so it runs on Community.
