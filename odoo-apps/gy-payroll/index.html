Guyana Payroll

Guyana Payroll for Odoo 19: GRA PAYE, NIS, Form 5, Form 2 and NIS Schedule

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Guyanese statutory payroll on Odoo Community: PAYE with the 2026 personal allowance, NIS for employee and employer, child, overtime, second job and insurance deductions, the GRA PAYE file for Form 5 and Form 2, the NIS electronic schedule and a 7B statement of earnings.

25% on chargeable income up to G$280,000 a month, 35% on the excess, on every payslip.

G$140,000 a month or one third of the balance of income after the second job and overtime allowances, whichever is greater.

Employee 5.6% and employer 8.4% up to the G$280,000 ceiling, so the deduction stops at G$15,680. Under 16 or over 59: employer 1.5%, employee nothing.

G$10,000 per child under 18 and medical and life premiums limited to the lowest of the premium, 10% of gross and G$50,000.

Who it is for

  • Employers with monthly-paid staff in Guyana. A shop, contractor or service company in Georgetown that pays salaries once a month can get PAYE and NIS worked out on each payslip, including the personal allowance and the 25% and 35% bands, instead of rebuilding the GRA arithmetic in a spreadsheet.
  • Firms that file Form 5 and the NIS schedule for clients. An accounting office preparing monthly PAYE returns can build the Form 5 CSV and the NIS electronic schedule text file from the payslips, then upload both through its own logins.
  • Businesses paying overtime or second-job salaries. Where staff work overtime or hold a second job, the module takes the first part of overtime and of the second job salary out of the PAYE base and can stop the personal allowance when the main employer already grants it.
  • Companies with older or very young workers. Workers under 16 or over 59 are picked up from their birthday, so the NIS employee share is dropped and the lower employer rate is applied without manual edits.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. Modvanta HR & Payroll comes with it, and any company whose country is Guyana gets Guyanese Statutory Payroll ticked automatically.
  2. Open the company form, go to the Guyana Payroll tab, check Guyanese Statutory Payroll and enter the NIS Employer Registration No.
  3. On each employee, open the Guyana Payroll tab and fill in GRA TIN, NIS Number, Children Under 18 Claimed and Medical and Life Premiums / Month, and tick This Is the Second Job or Personal Allowance Taken at Main Job where they apply. Set the birthday on the employee too.
  4. Check Employees > HR & Payroll > Guyana Payroll > Configuration > PAYE and NIS Rates, and map any extra earnings under Allowances and Pay Components to the right GRA File Column.
  5. Run the month's payslips under Employees > HR & Payroll > Payroll > Payslips. The Guyanese Statutory block shows chargeable income, PAYE, NIS and the total employment cost.
  6. Go to Guyana Payroll > PAYE Returns and NIS Schedule, create a Form 5 (month) or Form 2 (year) return, press Compute, then Generate Files, and Print 7B Statements if you need them.
  7. Upload the CSV and TXT files yourself, then press Mark Filed on the return.

Guyana Payroll: questions

Which payroll app does it run on?

It runs on Modvanta HR & Payroll, a Community payroll base that is installed as a dependency. It does not use Odoo Enterprise's hr_payroll, so you don't need an Enterprise subscription.

Does it send the return to GRA or NIS for me?

No. It produces the GRA PAYE CSV and the NIS schedule text file, and you upload them with your own GRA eServices and NIS logins. Before the first upload, run the CSV through the GRA validator, because it was only checked against the published column table.

Can I pay staff weekly or fortnightly?

Not with this module. The calculation and the NIS schedule are monthly. The Weeks Worked (NIS) field on each return line defaults to 4 and can be edited by hand.

What do I do when the budget changes the allowance or bands?

Open the current record under PAYE and NIS Rates and press Copy for Next Year, then type the new values and the date they start. Payslips use the record whose Effective From date applies. Confirm new figures with your accountant or the GRA notice, since thresholds change from year to year.

Is the 1.5% employer NIS rate for older workers certain?

It comes from an older NIS schedule manual and is loaded as an editable value. Check it with the National Insurance Scheme before you rely on it.

Does it cover self-employed people?

There is a small Self-Employed NIS Calculator that applies the self-employed rate to a declared income. It is a calculator only and doesn't create a return.
