POS Cash Limit

Cap the cash a customer may pay on one order, and tell the cashier to make a cash drop before the drawer holds too much.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
POS cash limitCash payment limitPOS cash alertCash drop reminder

What it does

Many countries cap cash payments, and every shop wants less cash lying in the till. This module limits the cash paid on one POS order, as an amount or a share of the total, and watches the expected cash in the drawer.

Features

Cash limit per ordera maximum amount of cash per order, net of the change given back.
Cash share limita maximum share of the order total paid in cash, for example 10%.
Block or warnBlock: the order cannot be paid in the POS and the server refuses it too. Warn: the cashier may go on and the order is flagged for review.
Limit on the payment screenthe cashier sees the limit before taking the cash; it turns red as soon as the cash is above it.
Cash methods to watcha Cash Limit Applies box on every payment method, ticked by default on cash methods.
Drawer cash alertwhen the expected cash in the drawer reaches your level, an alert and a top bar button ask for a cash drop; the button opens Cash In/Out.
Drawer cash stopoptional second level: new cash payments are refused until cash is taken out.
Orders over the limita report and a filter list the orders validated above the limit in Warn mode, with the reason on the order.
Mixed paymentscard and cash on the same order: only the cash counts toward the limit.
