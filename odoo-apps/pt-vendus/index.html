Vendus Connector

Portuguese invoices must come from AT certified software. With this module you keep invoicing in Odoo: posting a customer invoice or credit note issues the legal document in Vendus, and the legal number, ATCUD, QR code data and certified PDF come back to the invoice. Payments become Vendus receipts, deliveries can issue transport documents and the SAF-T (PT) file is one click away.
