PayFast Payment Gateway, Yoco Payment Gateway, Ozow Payment Gateway

PayFast, Yoco and Ozow in one Odoo module for South African payments

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Three payment providers for South Africa: the PayFast page with cards, Instant EFT and wallets, the Yoco card checkout and Ozow pay-by-bank - each confirmed by a verified notification or webhook, refundable from Odoo, and logged.

The module adds three payment providers on the standard Odoo payment framework, for the eCommerce checkout, portal invoices and payment links. PayFast opens its page with everything your account offers, or a single method such as Card, Instant EFT or Capitec Pay. Yoco sends the customer to its card checkout. Ozow lets the customer pay from their bank account.

A payment is never confirmed from the customer's browser. PayFast's notification (ITN) is checked for its signature, its sender and with PayFast's server before the order is confirmed; Yoco and Ozow confirm through signed webhooks that Odoo registers for you. Refunds go back through each gateway from the Odoo refund button, and every call and notification is written to a gateway log.
