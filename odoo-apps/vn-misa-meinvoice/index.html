MISA meInvoice

MISA meInvoice for Odoo 19: publish Vietnamese e-invoices (hoa don dien tu) from Odoo invoices, follow the tax authority status and keep the PDF and XML on the invoice.

What it does

In Vietnam every sales invoice must be an e-invoice registered with the tax authority. If you issue your e-invoices with MISA meInvoice, this module publishes them straight from Odoo: confirm the invoice, preview it, publish it with your HSM digital signature, and Odoo stores the series, number and lookup code, follows the tax authority status and downloads the official PDF and XML.

Features

Publish from Odoocustomer invoices are sent to the MISA publish API with your HSM signature provider; series, e-invoice number, date and lookup code come back to the invoice.
Preview firstopen the MISA preview of the e-invoice before it is published.
Invoice series from MISAdownload your registered series (for example 1C26TAA) per year and pick a default.
Adjustments and replacementscredit notes are published as adjustment e-invoices of the original; replacement e-invoices reference the invoice they replace.
Tax authority statuscheck the status (sent, accepted, rejected, code issued) on demand or every hour automatically.
PDF, XML and emaildownload the official PDF and XML to the invoice, ask MISA to email the e-invoice to the customer.
Cancelcancel a published e-invoice with its reason; published e-invoices cannot be reset to draft.
Tokens handledlogin with App ID, tax code, username and password; the 15 day token is renewed automatically.
