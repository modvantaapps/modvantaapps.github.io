B2B Wholesale Portal

Let wholesale customers choose the warehouse their order ships from

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Wholesale customers often collect from, or are delivered by, a specific depot. In Odoo the warehouse is chosen behind the scenes, so the buyer cannot say where the order should ship from or see whether that depot has the goods.

This add-on puts a Fulfillment Warehouse selector in the B2B block of the cart. Each cart line then says whether it is in stock at that warehouse. The choice is saved on the order as its warehouse, so delivery is planned from there.

On the customer you list the warehouses they may use and the default one for new carts. If the customer switches to a warehouse that does not have enough of some items, the cart warns first and offers Switch and Adjust Cart - which lowers those lines to what is available - or Keep Current Warehouse.

Features

Warehouses per customer
Fulfillment Warehouse on the cart
Warned before an incomplete switch
Switched and adjusted
Saved on the order
Stock notes per warehouse
