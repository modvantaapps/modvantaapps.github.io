Odoo QuickBooks Desktop integration

QuickBooks Desktop Connector

QuickBooks Desktop and Odoo, kept in step through the free Web Connector

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Customers, vendors, items, accounts, orders, invoices, bills, credit memos and payments move between Odoo 19 and QuickBooks Desktop. The QuickBooks PC calls Odoo over HTTPS on a schedule, so there is no ODBC driver to buy, no helper app to keep running and no port to open on the Windows machine.

Odoo answers the SOAP calls of the QuickBooks Web Connector, the free tool that comes with QuickBooks Desktop. When it runs, Odoo asks QuickBooks for what changed since the last run, page by page, and sends the records you queued, in the right order: accounts and names first, then items, then documents, then payments.

Each record is linked by its QuickBooks ListID or TxnID, so a rename on either side never creates a second record. A dry run, an export preview and a full log of every qbXML message show what happens before and after it happens.
