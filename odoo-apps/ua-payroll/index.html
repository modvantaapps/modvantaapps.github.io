Ukraine Payroll

Ukrainian payslips with PDFO, the tax social benefit, the 5% military levy and employer ESV worked out for you

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
UkrainePDFO 18%Military levy 5%ESV 22% / 8.41%Tax social benefit4DF CSV

What it does

ESV 22% or 8.41%: paid by the employer; 8.41% for employees with a disability

Features

PDFO 18%on wages, bonuses and other taxable pay, after the tax social benefit, as the Tax Code describes
Tax social benefitstandard, per child for two or more children, 150% categories (single parents, child with a disability and the others) and 200% categories, with the income limit of 4,660 UAH (or that limit times the number of children)
Military levy 5%on the same income, with no benefit taken off
Minimum and maximum ESVtopped up to the minimum wage at the main job; no top-up for second jobs, non-fixed working time or a month you mark as not full; capped at 20 minimum wages (172,940 UAH in 2026)
2026 values readyminimum wage 8,647 UAH and subsistence minimum 3,328 UAH, in an editable record with the sources
Pay component mappingmark any input as a wage, taxed without ESV, not taxed, or a deduction that lowers the taxable wage
Register per month or quarterincome, benefit, PDFO, military levy, ESV base and ESV per employee, hire and leave dates
CSV filesthe 4DF columns our Ukraine Tax Reports module imports, and an ESV list with base, rate and amount
Payslipthe Ukrainian block on the printed payslip and a warning when a full month at the main job pays less than the minimum wage
