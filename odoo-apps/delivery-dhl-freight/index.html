DHL Freight Shipping Integration

Book DHL Freight road freight from Odoo 19: validating the delivery order books the pallets with DHL Freight, prints the GS1 labels and the waybill, asks the planned delivery date and keeps the tracking up to date.

What it does

A delivery method type DHL Freight on the standard Odoo shipping framework, for DHL Freight business customers in Sweden and across Europe. It uses the public DHL Freight APIs: Shipment Booking, Print, Price Quote, Time Table, Product and Unified Shipment Tracking.

Features

Booking on validationevery package of the transfer becomes a piece with its DHL package type, weight, dimensions and volume; the booking carries consignor and consignee, your account number as freight payer, the delivery terms, references and instructions.
Pickup datethe earliest pickup date (working days from today) and the hours when the goods are ready are sent with the booking: the booking is also the pickup order.
Planned delivery datethe Time Table API gives the pickup and planned delivery date before booking; for Priority products (ERD, ERI, PPI) it is sent as the committed delivery date.
Labels and documentsGS1 labels as PDF (one, two or three per page) or ZPL 203/300/600 dpi, plus the waybill / CMR and the shipment list, attached to the transfer; part and full loads get no labels, as DHL documents.
Price quotethe indicative DHL Freight price of your contract (with your eID) on the quotation, with an optional own surcharge in percent; or a fixed price.
Additional servicespre-advice, driver pre-advice, tail lift loading and unloading, DHL insurance of the goods value and cash on delivery (DAP or DDP).
Product checkreads the DHL Freight product: package types, delivery terms, additional services and the weight limits, which are then checked on every rate and booking.
Trackinga scheduled job reads the DHL tracking events of every open shipment and moves it from booked to in transit and delivered; the tracking link opens the DHL Freight page.
CancelDHL Freight bookings cannot be cancelled through the API: the shipment is cancelled in Odoo with a clear note to inform DHL Freight Customer Service.
Sandbox and productionthe Environment button switches between the DHL sandbox and production APIs; every call is logged with the secrets masked, and a demo mode runs the whole flow without an account.
