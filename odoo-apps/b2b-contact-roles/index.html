B2B Contact Roles & Permissions

Assign B2B contact roles (buyer, approver, viewer) with fine-grained authority flags for quotes, invoices, and orders. B2B user roles, buyer permissions, purchasing authority.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

This module adds a B2B Contact Role and four authority flags to every contact person: Can Place Orders, Can View Invoices, Can Approve Quotes and Can Manage Team, plus an Is Primary Contact tick. Roles are kept in a small catalogue, and choosing a role fills in its default flags, which you can then adjust per person.

The flags are information for your team. They are recorded and tracked on the contact; they do not by themselves restrict what a portal user can do.

Features

In a B2B account several people talk to yousomeone orders, someone approves quotations, someone only cares about invoices. Odoo has a free-text Job Position, but nothing structured that says what each person is allowed to do for their company.
