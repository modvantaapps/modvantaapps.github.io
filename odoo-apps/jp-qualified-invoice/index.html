Japan Qualified Invoice

What it does

Japanese customers can only claim their input consumption tax when your invoice is a qualified invoice. This module prints Odoo customer invoices and credit notes as qualified invoices: issuer name and registration number, recipient with the honorific, transaction date, items with the reduced-rate mark, totals per tax rate with the rate, and the consumption tax of each rate, rounded once per rate.

Nothing is filed with the NTA or any other authority. The only call to the NTA is the registration-number lookup, and only when you enter your own application ID.

Features

It also checks registration numbersthe T + 13 digit format and the check digit published by the National Tax Agency (NTA), and, with the free application ID the NTA issues to you, the NTA invoice Web-API lookup (status, published name and address, registration and revocation dates). On vendor bills it shows whether the supplier was a registered issuer on the bill date and works out the transitional input credit.
