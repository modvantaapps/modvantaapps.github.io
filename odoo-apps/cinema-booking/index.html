Cinema Ticket Booking

Run one or many cinemas on Odoo: screens with a seat layout editor, films and a show schedule with prices per seat type, website seat booking with a seat hold and online payment, QR e-tickets checked at the door, a box office on the Point of Sale, cancellation and refund rules, food and drink combos and a dashboard with occupancy and revenue. One module, built on Odoo's own Website, Sales, Payments, Point of Sale and Invoicing.

What it does

One seat, one ticket - enforced by the database

Everything a cinema needs, from the seat layout to the door.

Several cinemas, each with its screens and time zone. Seat types (standard, premium, recliner, wheelchair space...) with colour, default price and couple seats.

Generate a layout from rows, seats per row, aisles, walkways, premium back rows and wheelchair places, then paint seats, remove seats for aisles and mark house seats on a grid. Row letters and seat numbers are set for you (left-to-right or right-to-left).

Title, poster, synopsis, running time, certificate with minimum age, genres, language, formats, release and last dates, cast and crew with photos, gallery, trailer link. Coming soon / now showing / ended move by themselves on the dates.

Who it is for

  • Independent and single-screen cinemas. A small cinema that sells mostly at the door can put its programme on the Odoo website, let people pick seats on a seat map and pay online, and still sell walk-ins from the box office till.
  • Multiplexes running several screens. Each screen has its own seat layout with seat types, wheelchair spaces and house or VIP seats, and shows are priced per seat type. Several cinemas can be run from one database.
  • Theatres and venues with snack bars. Food and drink combos can be added to a booking online or at the box office, so tickets and concessions end up on the same order.
  • Operators who need a door check. Every seat gets its own ticket with a QR code, and the Ticket Scanner screen checks it at the door so the same ticket can't be used twice.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module, then go to Cinema > Configuration > Cinemas and create your cinema. Add Seat Types, Show Formats and Certificates you need.
  2. Go to Cinema > Configuration > Screens & Seat Layouts, create a screen and click Generate Layout to build the rows, then edit seats, wheelchair spaces and House / VIP Seats on the map.
  3. Add films under Cinema > Programme > Films, with cast, genre and certificate, and food and drink items under Food & Drinks.
  4. Use Generate Shows to create a run of shows for chosen screens and start times, and keep Put on Sale ticked so they appear on the website.
  5. Go to Cinema > Configuration > Settings to set Seat Hold (minutes), when online booking closes, the maximum seats per online booking, online cancellation, e-ticket emails and refund behaviour. Add Refund Rules for how much is refunded by hours before the show.
  6. In Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings, set the Cinema Box Office on the register that sells tickets at the counter.
  7. Share /cinema with your customers, and check tickets at the door with Cinema > Box Office > Ticket Scanner.

Cinema Ticket Booking: questions

Which Odoo apps does it need?

Website, Sales, Point of Sale, Invoicing and Portal, all available in Community. Online payments go through the payment providers you set up in Odoo, so you'll need at least one provider configured to take card payments.

What stops two people booking the same seat?

Seats chosen online are held for the number of minutes set in Seat Hold, and released if payment doesn't arrive in time. If a payment comes in after the hold ran out and someone else took a seat, the booking is flagged as a conflict so staff can move the customer or refund.

How do cancellations and refunds work?

Refund Rules set the refund percentage and an optional fee per ticket based on how many hours before the show the customer cancels. If online cancellation is allowed, customers can cancel from their booking page. A credit note can be posted automatically, and online payments are refunded to the card when the payment provider supports refunds.

Can I sell tickets for age-restricted films?

Yes. Give the film a certificate with a minimum age, and the website asks the buyer to confirm every viewer meets it before the booking goes through.

Can customers see their bookings later?

Yes. Logged-in customers have a cinema section in their portal at /my/cinema, and each booking has a page with a download link for the tickets PDF.

Can staff take phone bookings that are paid later?

Yes. On a booking in the backend, Reserve (Pay at Cinema) keeps the seats for the customer without payment. Unpaid reservations are released automatically a set number of minutes before the show, which you choose under Release Unpaid Reservations in the settings.
