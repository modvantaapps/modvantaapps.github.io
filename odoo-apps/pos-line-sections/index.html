POS Order Line Sections

Split one point of sale order into named sections: courses, seats, rooms or customers, each with its own subtotal on screen and on the receipt.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
POS order line sectionOrderline divisionSection on POS receipt

What it does

POS order line section and POS line section: split a point of sale order into named sections (orderline division, POS section per course, seat, table, room, customer or delivery drop), active section for new lines, move line to section, section subtotal, section on POS receipt, section presets, sections on the backend POS order and order lines

Features

Some orders are really several orders in onestarters and mains, three seats at a table, two rooms, a family buying for different people. This module lets the cashier group the lines of one order under named sections.
Add a sectiontype any name or tap a ready-made one (Starters, Mains, Room 101...) from the Section button.
Active sectionevery new line goes into the active section; tap a heading in the cart to switch.
Lines never merge across sectionsthe same product added in two sections stays on two lines.
Move a lineMove to Section puts the selected line in another section or a new one.
Subtotalseach heading shows the total of its lines, in the cart and on the receipt.
Section on the POS receiptthe receipt prints a heading per section with its subtotal (empty sections are left out).
Rename and removerename a section or remove it; its lines stay on the order.
Optional ruleswarn when a product is already in another section, or refuse payment while a line has no section.
In the backendthe POS order keeps the section of every line, with a Sections tab and an order lines by section view.
