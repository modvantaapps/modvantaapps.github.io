School Management System

School / Coaching Institute Management and Education ERP: student information system, student management, admission management with online admission application, academic year and terms, classes and sections, subjects, timetable, student attendance, exam management, gradebook, report card and transcript, school fee management with instalments and fines, parent portal, student portal, teacher portal. What it does

Admissions with an online application, students and guardians, academic years and terms, classes, sections and subjects, the timetable and class sessions, attendance by day or by session, examinations with datesheets, marks, grading scales, report cards and transcripts, and school fee management with instalments, scholarships and fines on normal Odoo invoices.

Parents, students and teachers each get their own portal. Access is strict: a parent sees only their own children, a student only themself, a teacher only the sections they teach, and marks can only be entered by the subject teacher or an exam manager.

Who it is for

  • Schools with classes and sections. Each section has a class teacher, a subject teacher per subject and a weekly timetable. A slot that clashes on the section, the teacher or the room is refused when you save it. Class sessions are generated from the timetable, and holidays are skipped.
  • Colleges and universities. Subjects carry credits and can be electives, which only the students who chose them sit. Report cards and transcripts work out a credit-weighted GPA from your grading scale, and exam papers record a hall or room.
  • Coaching institutes and training centres. Courses can be listed on the public /courses page. Enrollments can bill a recurring course fee, and a daily job raises the next invoice when it falls due. Completed enrollments can print a certificate.
  • Parents, students and teachers away from the office. Parents and students log in to /my/school for the timetable, published results, attendance, notices and fee instalments, with a Pay Now link on unpaid invoices. Teachers use /my/teacher to take attendance and enter marks for their own papers.

Setting it up

  1. Give staff a School role from their user form: Teacher / Faculty, Exam Manager or Manager. Parents and students only need portal access.
  2. Under School > Configuration, create the academic year and its terms, then the holidays, subjects, grading scales, and classes / grades with their curriculum.
  3. Open School > Configuration > Settings to choose the attendance mode, whether guardians get an email on absence, and how many days before the due date instalments are invoiced.
  4. Add sections under School > Academics > Classes and Sections. Use Load Class Curriculum, enter the timetable, then press Generate Class Sessions.
  5. Build fee plans under School > Fees > Fee Plans. Each plan has fee heads, plus instalments whose shares add up to 100%. Also set the late fine and grace days.
  6. Review online applications under School > Admissions > Applications. Approve one, then press Enroll to create the student and the enrollment.
  7. For exams, create an examination under School > Exams > Examinations and press Generate Datesheet. Then print the hall tickets, and press Publish Results once marks are in.

School Management System: questions

Does this school ERP need Odoo Enterprise?

No. It depends on Invoicing, Discuss, Portal, Website, Payments and Surveys, all of which are in Odoo Community.

How do school fees and instalments work?

A student fee card follows a fee plan, less any scholarship. Confirming it splits the net fee into instalments. A daily job invoices each instalment a set number of days before it is due, and can post it straight away. Another job adds a fine when a posted invoice stays unpaid past the grace days.

Who can enter or change marks?

The subject teacher of a paper and exam managers. Only an exam manager can publish or unpublish results, and publishing emails each result to the student's contacts. A re-evaluation keeps the earlier marks on record.

Can online tests feed into marks?

Yes. Link a survey to an exam paper and press Import Online Test Scores. The highest finished score of each student is scaled to the paper's total marks.

Can a parent see another family's children?

No. Record rules limit a portal user to their own enrollments and to the students they are a guardian of. The portal pages check the same thing before showing anything.

What doesn't it do?

It has no library, transport or hostel features of its own. It only shows links to those apps when they are installed. Online payment needs a payment provider set up in Odoo. Admission documents accept PDF, JPG and PNG only, up to 5 files and the size limit you set.
