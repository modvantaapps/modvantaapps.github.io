BigCommerce Connector

Connect BigCommerce and Odoo - products, variants, orders, customers, stock and price lists in sync

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

BigCommerce orders arrive as Odoo sales orders with their invoice and payment, products and variants are linked by SKU, customers come in with their customer group, stock goes back to BigCommerce, and webhooks keep it all up to date.

The connector links one or more BigCommerce stores to Odoo through the BigCommerce REST API. Products are imported with their variants, options, categories and brands and linked to Odoo products by SKU; you can also export Odoo products to the store. Orders placed in BigCommerce become Odoo sales orders with the customer, lines, shipping and discounts, and can be confirmed, invoiced and paid automatically.

Customers are imported with their BigCommerce customer group, and BigCommerce price lists can be mapped to Odoo pricelists. Stock is exported per BigCommerce location from the Odoo warehouses you choose. Webhooks for orders, products and customers queue jobs as soon as something changes in the store, and every call is written to a sync log you can resolve and retry.
