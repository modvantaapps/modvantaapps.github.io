Contact Ownership & Salesperson History

Contact ownership history: log every salesperson assignment and reassignment on a contact, with previous and new salesperson, who changed it and when. Account ownership, salesperson change log, account manager history.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

In standard Odoo the Salesperson on a contact simply gets overwritten. When a customer asks who used to look after them, or a commission question comes up, the previous owner is hard to find.

This module keeps an Ownership & Assignment History tab on every contact. Each time a salesperson is set or changed, a line is added with the date, the user who made the change, the previous salesperson and the new one. Contacts created with a salesperson get an initial line too.
