SumUp POS Terminal

Take card payments on a SumUp reader from Odoo POS

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

The cashier presses SumUp on the Odoo payment screen, picks the reader, and the customer pays on the SumUp Solo. The receipt shows the card and the SumUp transaction code.

This module connects SumUp card readers (SumUp Solo and other readers paired through the SumUp Cloud API) to the Odoo 19 Point of Sale. Pair readers from Odoo with their pairing code, add them to a SumUp payment method, and each card payment is sent to the reader the cashier chooses or the register's default reader.

The POS shows Waiting for card while the customer pays, and explains declines, timeouts and offline readers. Approved payments print the card brand, last four digits, SumUp transaction code and entry method on the receipt.

Every reader payment is recorded with its SumUp IDs and reconciliation state; readers show their status, battery, network and firmware. Demo Mode on the SumUp provider simulates readers inside Odoo with set behaviours, so you can try every case without hardware.

Who it is for

  • Shops, cafes and market stalls already on SumUp. If you take cards on a SumUp Solo, this makes it a payment terminal inside the Odoo Point of Sale. The cashier presses SumUp on the payment screen and the amount goes straight to the reader.
  • Venues with several tills. Pair as many readers as you need and set a default reader per register. A bar, a terrace and a counter can each send payments to their own SumUp reader, or the cashier picks one.
  • Hospitality that takes tips. Tip on the Reader lets the customer choose a percentage on the SumUp screen, and the tip is added to the POS order with the tip product of that point of sale.
  • Managers who reconcile card takings. Each reader payment is stored with its SumUp transaction code, IDs, tip, collected amount and reconciliation state, under Point of Sale > Reporting > SumUp Reader Payments.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It also installs our SumUp payment provider module, which holds the SumUp account (Secret API Key and Merchant Code).
  2. Open the SumUp payment provider and enter your key and merchant code, or set it to Test Mode and tick Demo Mode to try simulated readers with no hardware.
  3. In Point of Sale > Configuration > Payment Methods, create a method and set Use a Payment Terminal to SumUp, then choose the SumUp Account.
  4. Press Pair a Reader. On the Solo open Connections > API > Connect, type the pairing code it shows, give the reader a name and press Pair.
  5. Set the Timeout (seconds), and if you want tips tick Tip on the Reader with your Tip Percentages. Add the Affiliate App ID and Affiliate Key if SumUp gave you one.
  6. Add the payment method to your point of sale. With several readers, choose the SumUp Reader for each register in the POS settings.

SumUp POS Terminal: questions

Does SumUp work with Odoo POS out of the box?

Odoo 19 Community has no SumUp terminal option in the Point of Sale. This add-on adds SumUp as a payment terminal choice on the POS payment method and talks to the reader through the SumUp Cloud API.

Which SumUp card readers work with Odoo?

Readers that pair with the SumUp Cloud API, such as the SumUp Solo. Tipping on the reader needs Solo firmware 3.3.38.0 or later. Older readers that only work over Bluetooth with the SumUp app are not supported.

Community or Enterprise?

Both, on Odoo 19. You need the Point of Sale app and a SumUp merchant account with an API key. Demo Mode works without any account.

Can I refund a card payment from the POS?

Yes. Refund the order in the POS and pick the SumUp method: the module finds the SumUp payment of the original order and refunds it through SumUp, in full or in part, up to what was paid. If the original order was not paid on a SumUp reader, refund it another way.

What if the reader is offline or the answer gets lost?

The POS shows SumUp's reason for a decline, a timeout or an offline reader so the cashier can retry or switch method. When your Odoo has no public HTTPS address, the POS asks SumUp for the status instead of waiting for the callback, and a scheduled action checks open requests every 5 minutes.

Is the full card number stored in Odoo?

No. Odoo keeps the card brand, the last four digits, the entry method and the SumUp IDs, and prints them on the receipt.
