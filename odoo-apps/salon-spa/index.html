Salon and Spa Management

Salon Management and Spa Management in Odoo: online booking, salon appointments on free time slots, chairs and staff, services and packages, and every visit billed as a salon order.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

For hair salons, beauty parlours, spas and wellness studios on Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise. Orders and invoices are normal Odoo sales orders.

Features

Online Bookinga Book Appointment page on your website. Customers pick services, a staff member (or any), a date and a free time, then book or book and pay online.
Free time slotscalculated from each staff member's working hours, the service duration and the bookings already made. Staff and chairs can never be double-booked.
Single and group appointmentsa group booking adds guests with their own services; each guest gets another free staff member at the same time.
Salon orderGenerate Order turns the appointment into a sales order with every service, the guest names and the products the services use. Paying online confirms the order and the appointment.
Services, packages and kitsservices with category, duration, price and the staff who perform them; packages that bundle services and products at one price; the products a service uses.
Chairs and staffchairs and stations linked to a staff member, a live chair board, staff types and working hours.
Dashboard and reportstoday's appointments, requests to confirm, orders and revenue, chair status, top services and top customers; pivot analysis and PDF reports.
Customer portal and feedbackcustomers request appointments, follow them and download a summary; after the visit they rate it with one click from an email.
