Odoo Webfleet integration

Webfleet Connector

Webfleet in Odoo Fleet: vehicles, live positions, trips, odometer and delivery orders for your drivers

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Webfleet integration - WEBFLEET.connect - GPS tracking - Trips - Driver orders - Odoo 19

If your vans already carry Webfleet units, the trip and mileage data sits in Webfleet while your fleet costs, contracts and deliveries sit in Odoo. This connector reads your Webfleet account through WEBFLEET.connect and fills Odoo Fleet with it: each vehicle with its last position, its trips and its odometer. It also goes the other way. A delivery from Odoo can be sent to a van as a Webfleet order, and the driver's progress (accepted, arrived, finished, signed) comes back to the delivery.

Features

VehiclesWebfleet objects become Odoo vehicles, or are linked to the ones you have by licence plate (same company only); VIN and fuel type come along
Positionslast position text and coordinates, speed, ignition, standstill, GPS status and the signed-on driver, with a map link
Odometera fleet odometer reading is added when Webfleet reports more kilometres than Odoo has; miles are converted
Tripsevery trip with start and end place, distance, duration, idle time, top speed and fuel, read from the last trip ID onwards
DriversWebfleet driver numbers linked to contacts of the same company, or new contacts created; optional driver on the vehicle
Orders to driversan outgoing delivery sent to a van as a Webfleet destination order with address, coordinates, contact and lines
Order status backaccepted, arrived, delivery finished, cancelled and the driver note, from the message queue or the order report
Message queueone WEBFLEET.connect queue per account; each batch is read and then acknowledged, as Webfleet asks
Log and scheduleevery call logged without secrets; a scheduled run every 10 minutes that keeps to the request limits
