Invoice Discount

Give a global discount on invoices, bills, quotations, sales and purchase orders - a percentage or a fixed amount, split per tax rate and booked on your own discount accounts - and stack line discounts like 10+5+2 on every line. Built on the Odoo tax engine, so totals, taxes and journal items stay exact.

Everything a sales team, a buyer and an accountant need from discounts.

A percentage or a fixed amount on customer invoices, credit notes, vendor bills, refunds, quotations, sales orders and purchase orders.

The discount is written as separate lines, one per tax rate, with the same tax engine Odoo uses for its own sales discount. Taxes, totals and journal items stay exact.

Book global discounts on a sales discount account and a purchase discount account instead of mixing them into revenue and expenses.
