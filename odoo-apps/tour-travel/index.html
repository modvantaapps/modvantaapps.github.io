Tour and Travel Management

For travel agencies and tour operators: tour packages with a day-by-day itinerary, fixed departures with seats, prices per adult, child and single room with season and group rules, website tour booking with an online deposit, travellers with passports and visas, hotel and transport suppliers with purchase orders, vouchers, a payment schedule, cancellation charges, profit per booking and travel agent commission. One module, built on Odoo's own Website, Sales, Purchase, CRM and Invoicing.

What it does

From the tour page to the voucher, in one place

Everything a travel agency or tour operator runs on, from the package to the profit.

Package type, destinations, domestic or international, group tour on fixed departures or private tour on any date. Cover photo, gallery, highlights, long description, FAQ, terms template and a printable PDF brochure.

One line per day with title, description, start time, overnight place and hotel, transport, activities and meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner). Days and nights are counted for you.

Price per adult, child and infant plus a single supplement. Rules on top: season by travel dates, group size, early booking - as a percent up or down. Departures can override the prices.

Features

Give a service tiers by travellersguide rate up to 6 guests and from 7, minibus up to 7 and coach from 8. Cost lines pick the tier from the travellers booked and follow every booking and cancellation.
