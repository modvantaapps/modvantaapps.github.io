Tour and Travel Management

For travel agencies and tour operators: tour packages with a day-by-day itinerary, fixed departures with seats, prices per adult, child and single room with season and group rules, website tour booking with an online deposit, travellers with passports and visas, hotel and transport suppliers with purchase orders, vouchers, a payment schedule, cancellation charges, profit per booking and travel agent commission, plus tour reviews on the website, travel insurance per traveller, guide and driver job cards with a day sheet, and flight and ferry segments. One module, built on Odoo's own Website, Sales, Purchase, CRM and Invoicing.

What it does

From the tour page to the voucher, in one place

Everything a travel agency or tour operator runs on, from the package to the profit. Built for tours and travels businesses: a tour inquiry sent from the website lands in the CRM as a lead, so the travel CRM and the bookings sit in one place.

Package type, destinations, domestic or international, group tour on fixed departures or private tour on any date. Cover photo, gallery, highlights, long description, FAQ, terms template and a printable PDF brochure.

One line per day with title, description, start time, overnight place and hotel, transport, activities and meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner). Days and nights are counted for you.

Price per adult, child and infant plus a single supplement. Rules on top: season by travel dates, group size, early booking - as a percent up or down. Departures can override the prices.

Who it is for

  • Group tour operators with fixed departures. Operators who sell the same itinerary on set dates have to watch seats and the minimum needed to run each trip. Departures carry their seats and a minimum to operate, and a row lock stops two bookings taking the last seat.
  • Travel agencies selling packages online. A small agency wants visitors to pick a date, see the price for their party and pay a deposit without a phone call. The /tours pages show live prices and seats left, and the booking opens Odoo's own payment page for the deposit.
  • Operators selling through agents and OTAs. When the same departure is sold direct, by agents and on online travel agencies, margins differ by channel. Each booking carries a sales channel, commission is booked as a cost with its own vendor bill, and the Profit by Channel report compares them.
  • Agencies handling international trips. Trips abroad mean passports, visas and insurance for every traveller. Passports expiring too soon after the return date are flagged, and confirmed bookings open a visa application for each traveller who needs one.

Features

Give a service tiers by travellersguide rate up to 6 guests and from 7, minibus up to 7 and coach from 8. Cost lines pick the tier from the travellers booked and follow every booking and cancellation.
Nothing changes for existing bookingsthe review e-mail is off until you switch it on, insurance is only required on the packages you choose.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It uses Website, Sales, Purchase, CRM and Invoicing, and adds a Tours app to the main menu.
  2. Go to Tours > Configuration > Settings and set the default deposit percentage, how many days before departure the balance is due, the website seat hold time and the default cancellation policy.
  3. Fill in Tours > Configuration > Destinations, Package Types and Cancellation Policies, and add Visa Checklists if you sell trips that need visas.
  4. Add your hotels, transport and activity suppliers under Tours > Tours > Hotels and Services, then build a package under Tours > Tours > Tour Packages with its day-by-day itinerary and prices per adult, child and infant.
  5. Create dates under Tours > Tours > Departures with seats and a minimum to operate, and publish the package so it appears on the /tours page.
  6. Track bookings under Tours > Bookings > Bookings and open instalments under Tours > Bookings > Payment Schedule. Website enquiries arrive under Tours > Bookings > Enquiries as CRM leads.

Tour and Travel Management: questions

Which payment providers can take the online deposit?

The website booking uses Odoo's own payment page, so any payment provider you've set up in Odoo can take it. The paid deposit confirms the booking. If the seat hold time runs out before payment, the seats are released again.

What happens when a customer cancels?

The cancellation wizard works out the charge from the policy's days before departure and shows the refund due. It cancels or credits the open invoices, invoices the cancellation charge and cancels draft supplier orders. If you cancel a departure yourself, every charge is waived.

Can I take bookings in a foreign currency?

Yes. A booking and its invoices can be in a foreign currency, while costs and profit are worked out in the company currency. Check your exchange rate setup in Odoo Invoicing first.

Can travel agents book for their own clients?

Yes. Agents get a portal where they book for their clients and see their commission. Commission can be a percentage, a fixed amount per traveller or a net rate, and it's raised as a vendor bill per booking.

Does it book flights or hotels with suppliers automatically?

No. It records flight, ferry, train and coach segments with PNR, ticket and seat details, and turns supplier costs into purchase orders grouped by supplier. The actual reservation with the airline or hotel is still made by your team.

Do I need Odoo Enterprise?

No. Website, Sales, Purchase, CRM and Invoicing are all part of Community, and the module needs nothing else from Odoo.
