deema Payment Gateway Kuwait

deema payment gateway for Odoo: deema buy now pay later Kuwait (deema BNPL, Sharia-compliant pay later in KWD) on eCommerce checkout, invoices, payment links and Point of Sale. Direct deema API: hosted checkout, order status read from deema, webhook with secret header, full and partial refunds, cancel order, deema widget on product page and cart, minimum amount, sandbox and production. Kuwait payment gateway, buy now pay later Kuwait, pay later, installments.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The module adds deema as an Odoo payment provider for Kuwaiti dinar. At checkout Odoo creates an online checkout with the deema API (amount, KWD, your order reference and the success and failure pages) and sends the customer to the deema page. When the customer comes back, when deema sends its webhook and in a scheduled check, Odoo reads the order from deema (Retrieve Order Details) and confirms, declines or cancels the payment. Refunds, full or partial, and cancellations of unfinished checkouts go to deema from Odoo.
