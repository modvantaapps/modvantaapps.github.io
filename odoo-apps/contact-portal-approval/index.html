Contact Portal Change Approval

Approve customer profile & address modification requests submitted via the customer portal before updating contact master data. Portal profile approval, customer self-service approval, change request.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

In standard Odoo a portal customer who edits My Account changes your contact record immediately: a new invoice address, a different email, even a new Tax ID, without anyone checking.

With this module, when a portal customer changes their name, email, phone, street, city or Tax ID on My Account, those changes are not written to the contact. They become a Portal Change Request that shows the current and requested values side by side. A user approves (the values are applied) or rejects it. Other address fields such as ZIP, state and country are saved as usual. The customer sees a confirmation that their request is waiting for approval.
