Bambora Payment Gateway

Take Bambora Checkout (Worldline Online Checkout, Europe) payments in Odoo 19: cards, Swish, MobilePay and Vipps.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Adds Bambora Checkout as a payment provider. Odoo creates a checkout session, the customer pays in the payment window, and the hashed callback plus a read of the transaction confirm the order. Capture and refund from Odoo.

For the European Bambora Checkout, now Worldline Online Checkout: not the North American Bambora gateway

One generic option, or Card, MobilePay, Vipps and Swish options that limit the window to that method

MD5 hash check on every callback with your MD5 key, then a read of the transaction
