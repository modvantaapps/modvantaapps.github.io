AI Chatbot

AI Chatbot for your Odoo Website and Live Chat

What it does

An AI assistant in the Odoo live chat window on your website. It answers visitors from your own products, FAQ, policies and pages, reads prices and stock live from Odoo, looks up orders for logged-in customers, hands the chat to a person when asked, and turns contact details into CRM leads - with Claude or any OpenAI compatible endpoint, a price guard, a full conversation log with token costs, a monthly budget, rate limits and spam protection. Built on Odoo's own Live Chat, Website, eCommerce and CRM.

The bot searches your knowledge entries (FAQ, shipping, returns, payment, policies), your published products and your website pages with PostgreSQL full-text search, ranks the matches and answers only from them. No vector database, no extra server, no embeddings bill.

When a product matches, its price is computed by Odoo at that moment - the customer's pricelist, with or without tax like your shop - together with in stock / out of stock (or the exact quantity) and the product link. Prices are never stored in the index.

Every amount in the AI's answer is checked against the prices and amounts Odoo gave it. If the model writes a price that is not in your data, the answer is replaced by the real prices from your catalogue. The visitor never sees an invented price.

Visitors can ask for a person at any time (your keywords, or the AI decides). The chat is forwarded to an available live chat operator with the whole conversation; the bot leaves. If nobody is online the chat is flagged Looking for help and the bot keeps helping.
