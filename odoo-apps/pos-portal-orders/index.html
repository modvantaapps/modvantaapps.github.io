POS Orders in Customer Portal

POS Orders in the Customer Portal for Odoo 19: your customers see their store purchases in My Account - receipts, products, taxes and payments - and download the receipt and the invoice as PDF.

What it does

Store Purchases card in My Account, with the number of point of sale orders.

Who it is for

  • Shops whose customers claim expenses. People who buy at your counter and later need a receipt for an expense claim or a warranty can log in to My Account and download it as a PDF. Nobody has to phone the shop or dig through a drawer of till rolls.
  • Trade counters with company accounts. A builder's merchant or a wholesale counter often sells to several employees of the same client. Every contact of that company sees the store purchases made under the company and its other contacts, so the office manager finds them all in one list.
  • Retailers selling online and in store. If you already give customers portal access for online orders and invoices, their till purchases now sit in the same My Account, on a Store Purchases card next to the invoices.
  • Stores that invoice some POS sales. When a point of sale order was invoiced, the purchase page shows the invoice number and date, a link to the invoice page and a direct PDF download of it.

Features

Order listreceipt number, date, shop, total and status (paid or invoiced); sort by newest, oldest, reference, status or total; date filter; pages of 80 orders.
Order detailevery product with its image, unit price, quantity, discount and subtotal; untaxed amount, taxes, total and the payments; shop and cashier.
Receipt PDFone click downloads the receipt of the purchase.
Invoicewhen the order was invoiced, the invoice number and date, a link to the invoice page and its PDF download.
Company accountsa contact of a company sees the store purchases of the company and of its other contacts.
Privatethe customer only sees his own orders; another order number typed in the address answers "not found". Draft and cancelled orders are not shown.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. There is no settings screen: it depends on Point of Sale and Portal only.
  2. At the till, pick the customer on the order with the Customer button before payment, as you normally would. Orders without a customer can't appear in anyone's portal.
  3. Give the customer portal access from Contacts: select the contact, open the Action menu and choose Grant portal access. Customers can also sign up on their own if your database allows it.
  4. The customer logs in and opens My Account. The Store Purchases card shows how many POS orders they have and opens /my/pos_orders.
  5. In the list they can sort by Newest, Oldest, Reference, Status or Total, filter by date and move through pages.
  6. Opening a purchase shows the lines with product images, the totals and payments, a Download Receipt (PDF) button and, if the order was invoiced and the invoice is posted, View Invoice and Download Invoice (PDF).

POS Orders in Customer Portal: questions

Which POS orders does a customer see?

Orders in the paid, posted or invoiced state whose customer is the logged-in contact or another contact under the same company. Draft and cancelled orders are left out.

Can someone open another customer's order by changing the number in the address?

No. The list, the detail page and the receipt download all check that the order belongs to the customer's company before showing anything. Any other order id returns a not found page.

What does the receipt PDF look like?

It is a separate PDF report called Store Receipt, printed with your company's standard document layout. It lists the products, quantities, unit prices, discounts and subtotals, then taxes, the total and each payment method. It isn't a pixel copy of the thermal ticket from the till.

Does it work on Community edition?

Yes. It only needs the Point of Sale and Portal apps, which are both part of Odoo Community. Enterprise works the same way.

Is the invoice always downloadable?

Only when the POS order was invoiced and that invoice is posted. A purchase without an invoice says so on its page, and a draft invoice is not shown.

Can staff jump from the portal page to the backend?

Yes. A user who belongs to the Point of Sale user group sees the standard edit-in-backend banner on the purchase page, which opens that POS order in Odoo.
