Odoo Attio CRM integration

Attio CRM Connector

Your sales team works in Attio. Quotes, invoices and delivery happen in Odoo. This keeps both sides in step.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Attio CRMAttio IntegrationPeopleCompaniesDealsDeal StagesListsNotes

What it does

Paste an Attio API key, or connect your own Attio OAuth app, and press Test Connection. Odoo reads the workspace members, the deal stages and your lists, then every company, person and deal. An Attio company becomes an Odoo company with its website and address, its people sit under it with e-mail, phone and job title, and each deal lands in the CRM pipeline with its value, stage and owner. Notes written in Attio show up in the chatter. Register the webhook and a change in Attio reaches Odoo within seconds instead of waiting for the hourly run.

Features

People and companiesAttio companies become Odoo companies (name, domain, primary location), people become contacts of their company. Both ways
No duplicatesrecords are matched on the Attio record ID, then on e-mail or company name. New contacts from Odoo go through the Attio upsert on e-mail address or domain
Dealsboth ways with CRM opportunities: name, value, deal stage, owner, associated company and people
Deal stageseach Attio stage maps to an Odoo stage. Won stages map to a won stage, lost stages to a lost reason, and you can change every line
Listslink an Attio list to an Odoo contact tag. People in the list get the tag; tag a linked contact in Odoo and it is added to the list, remove the tag and it comes out
NotesAttio notes on people, companies and deals are posted once in the chatter. Internal notes logged in Odoo are added to the Attio record
Tasksopen Attio tasks become Odoo activities for the right person, and Odoo activities become Attio tasks. Mark one done and the other is closed too
Membersworkspace members are matched to Odoo users by e-mail, with a default salesperson for the rest
Signed webhooksOdoo creates the webhook in Attio and checks the Attio-Signature on every delivery, then reads the changed record from Attio. Unsigned calls are refused
Sync logone line per record created, updated, sent or skipped, with the reason when something fails
