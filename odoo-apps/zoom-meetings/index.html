Zoom Meeting Integration

Zoom meetings from Odoo - with the link in the invitation and the attendance back in the lead

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Create Zoom meetings from calendar events, CRM leads, quotations and contacts. Moves, renames and cancellations follow the event. After the meeting Odoo records who joined, for how long, the cloud recordings and the meeting summary.

The module connects Odoo to your Zoom account with a Server-to-Server OAuth app, so nobody has to sign in to Zoom from Odoo. On a calendar event, Add Zoom Meeting creates the meeting for the organizer's own Zoom user and puts the join link where Odoo already prints it: the video call field of the invitation. The meeting ID and passcode are added to the description. When the event moves, is renamed, gets new attendees or is deleted, the Zoom meeting follows.

After the meeting, Zoom's signed notifications and its reports tell Odoo who joined and for how long, which invited people did not, the cloud recording files and the AI Companion summary. The outcome is posted on the lead or order, and the meeting activity is closed. Background calls are retried, every API call is logged with secrets masked, and Demo Mode lets you try all of it without a Zoom account.

Features

Sales teams schedule from where they worka Zoom Meeting button on leads, quotations and sale orders, and in the Action menu of contacts. The wizard proposes the customer and the salesperson, creates the calendar event and the Zoom meeting, and logs it on the record.
