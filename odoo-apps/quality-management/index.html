Quality Management System (QMS)

An ISO 9001 style quality management system for Odoo Community: quality control and quality inspection on receipts, manufacturing and deliveries with measurements, tolerances, photos and sampling, quality alerts, non-conformance reports with 5 why and fishbone root cause analysis, CAPA with effectiveness checks, internal and supplier audits, document control, supplier quality ratings, calibration of measuring equipment, customer complaints, risks, management review, electronic signatures and a sealed audit trail. One module.

What it does

Inspections are part of the receipt, the manufacturing order and the delivery, so nothing moves before it passes or a quality manager decides. Failed goods become scrap or a supplier return, failures become alerts and NCRs, NCRs close only when the CAPA worked, and instruments that are out of calibration cannot be used to measure. The dashboard numbers come from those records.

From the incoming inspection to the certification audit, in one module.

Quality control points on receipts, manufacturing orders, deliveries and internal transfers, by product, category, operation type or partner. Run every time, every Nth operation or once per period, one check per product or per lot / serial.

Pass / fail tests, measurements with target, lower and upper limits, photos and instructions to confirm. Results turn pass or fail by themselves, the deviation is shown and the first result is kept for first pass yield.

Inspect the full quantity, a fixed sample, a percentage, or the ISO 2859-1 general inspection level II sample size for the lot size.

Features

The quality manager decidesaccept under concession, rework, scrap the failed quantity (scrap order created) or return to the supplier (return created when the receipt is validated). A quality alert opens on its own.
