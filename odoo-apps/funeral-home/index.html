Funeral Home Management and Cemetery Management

Run the funeral home and the cemetery from one Odoo app: first call to after-care, plot map to interment register.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Funeral cases - Arrangements and contracts - Documents and custody - Obituaries - Cemetery plots - Pre-need plans

A case opens when the phone rings. You note who called, where the person died and when to collect. At the arrangement meeting you load a package or pick items from your price list, and one button turns it into an Odoo quotation the family signs online. Caskets and urns come out of stock. Chapels, hearses and staff are booked per case, and Odoo refuses a second booking of the same resource. The disposition cannot be authorised while a document is missing or the case is on hold. The cemetery side keeps the plot map, grave rights, renewals and the interment register.

Features

First call and case fileCaller, place of death, collection time, the deceased, the responsible party, the informant and next of kin with who has the right to decide.
Arrangements to contractFuneral packages and an itemised general price list (services, facilities, vehicles, merchandise, cash advances). The quotation is signed on the portal, with an optional online deposit.
Cash advances and marginThird-party fees (crematorium, florist, officiant) shown with who was paid and any markup. Charges, internal cost and margin per case.
Insurance assignmentRecord the insurer, policy and assigned amount; the family's contract is credited and the insurer gets its own invoice.
SchedulingChapels, rooms, vehicles and staff on a calendar. Overlapping bookings of the same resource are refused.
Documents and authorisationA checklist per disposition (burial, cremation...). Authorisation is refused while a needed document is missing, the case is on hold or the cremation implant check is not done.
Custody and storage baysA custody log of every movement that users cannot edit, and storage bays that hold one person at a time. Release is refused while the case is on hold.
Obituaries and condolencesObituary pages on your website with service details and a condolence book you moderate. Spam is filtered with a hidden field and a limit per connection.
Cemetery plotsSections drawn as a coloured grid map, plots with type and capacity, reservations with a deposit invoice, grave right sales and renewals invoiced from the plot.
Grave rights and intermentsRight holder, start and duration, expiry reminders by email, a grace period and a reclaim list. The interment register refuses a full plot and records removals.
Pre-need plansInstalments invoiced on their due dates. When the time comes the plan opens a case and the paid amount is credited on the contract.
After-careFollow-up activities planned for the director after the service: a call after a week, memorial options after a month, a card on the anniversary.
