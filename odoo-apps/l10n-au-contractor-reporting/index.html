Australia Contractor Payments: TPAR, RCTI and Fuel Tax Credit

What it does

Three Australian ATO obligations for businesses that pay contractors, in Odoo Community: the Taxable Payments Annual Report (TPAR), Recipient Created Tax Invoices (RCTI) with their agreements, and fuel tax credit claims for BAS label 7D.

Mark contractors as reportable and the TPAR run collects what you actually paid them in the financial year (1 July to 30 June): gross paid including GST, the GST, and amounts withheld for suppliers without an ABN. Bills invoiced in June but paid in July fall in the year they were paid. Anything left out - for example a bill that was never paid - is listed with the reason, and the run produces a printable summary and an export file.

An RCTI agreement records that you, the recipient, issue the tax invoice for a supplier: the supplier ABN, start and end dates and whether it is signed. Only while an agreement is in force can a vendor bill be turned into a numbered Recipient Created Tax Invoice, printed with the required wording and emailed to the supplier.

Fuel acquisitions are recorded with fuel type, use (heavy vehicle on public roads, off road, auxiliary equipment), vehicle and litres. A fuel tax credit claim collects the acquisitions of a BAS period, applies the dated rate for each fuel and use, and gives the amount for BAS label 7D with a worksheet.
