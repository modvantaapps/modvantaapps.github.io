Consignment Management

Consignment Management - Consignment Stock, Settlement Bills and Consignment Sales

What it does

Sell goods that still belong to someone else, and place your own goods at customers who pay only for what they sell. Consignment IN keeps the consignor as owner of the stock in your warehouse - never valued as your inventory - and turns what really sold into a settlement statement and a vendor bill. Consignment OUT moves your goods to a location per customer and invoices them from the customer's sales report. Built on the standard Odoo stock owner, sales, purchase and accounting.

Tick "Consignment" on a purchase order (or use Receive Goods on the agreement): the receipt puts the consignor as owner on the stock and the order is never billed from received quantities.

Consigned quants show a value of 0, no stock journal entry is posted for receipts, sales or scrap, and no cost of goods sold appears on the customer invoice. Proven by tests.

Sales orders and eCommerce orders deliver consigned goods like any other product; mixed deliveries settle only the consigned part.

Sold, returned by customers, consumed in production, scrapped or lost in a count: each consigned stock move line is settled once, with the sale order price (discounts and currency included).

Features

Per consignorpay the sale price minus your commission, or an agreed cost per unit. Commission or cost per product, minimum sale price, fee per settlement, start and end date, terms, printable agreement PDF.
