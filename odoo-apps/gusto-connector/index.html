Gusto Connector

Run payroll in Gusto, keep employees, time off and the payroll ledger in Odoo

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
GustoEmployeesDepartmentsTime OffPayroll journal entriesWebhooks

What it does

Connect your Gusto company once and Odoo reads the people who are on payroll there: names, work e-mails, job titles, departments, managers and pay rates. New hires you enter in Odoo can be created in Gusto. Time off accepted in Gusto shows up in Odoo Time Off, and every processed payroll is booked as one balanced journal entry on the accounts you choose, so your accountant stops retyping the payroll register.

Features

EmployeesGusto to Odoo, Odoo to Gusto or both ways. Name, personal and work e-mail, birthday, job title, department, manager, pay rate and unit, FLSA status, hire date and onboarding status
New hires and changesan employee created or edited in Odoo is sent to Gusto with the current object version; a stale version is read again and retried once
Departmentsread from Gusto, created and renamed from Odoo, and the people in them kept in step
Terminationsarchive an employee in Odoo and, if you switch it on, Gusto gets a termination dated today
Time offaccepted and pending Gusto requests become Odoo Time Off, mapped by policy type; cancelled or denied requests cancel the Odoo leave
Payroll importprocessed regular and off-cycle payrolls with gross pay, employee and employer taxes, deductions, company benefits, reimbursements and net pay, plus one line per employee
Journal entriesone balanced entry per payroll on your payroll journal, as draft or posted; any gap Gusto does not itemise is booked on net pay clearing and flagged on the payroll
Webhooksemployee and payroll events arrive signed with X-Gusto-Signature; unsigned calls are refused and repeated events are skipped
Scheduled sync and loghourly sync as a safety net and one log line per record and direction
