Singapore Payroll

Singapore Payroll for Odoo 19: CPF, SDL, SHG Funds, IR8A and AIS

What it does

Singapore PayrollCPF ContributionOW / AW CeilingsCPF EZPay FileSDLCDACECFMBMFSINDAIR8AAppendix 8AAppendix 8BAIS-API 2.0Employment Act Leave

What a Singapore payroll team needs every month and at year end.

Nil up to $50, employer only up to $500, phase-in up to $750 and full rates above, with the new age group from the month after the 55th, 60th, 65th and 70th birthday. Rounding exactly as the CPF Board states: total to the nearest dollar, employee share down.

1st and 2nd year SPR tables (G/G and F/G) and full rates after a joint application, with the SPR year worked out from the conversion date.

Monthly CPF, MBMF, SINDA, CDAC, ECF and SDL in the 150-byte CPF EZPay FTP format: header, summaries, detail records with employment status, trailer. File name from your CSN.
