HiBob Connector

Your people stay in Bob. Odoo gets the employees, time off and holidays it needs.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
HiBobBob HR APIEmployeesDepartmentsTime OffBalancesPublic holidaysSigned webhooks

What it does

Create a service user in Bob, paste its ID and token, and Odoo reads your company: departments with their tree, job titles, sites and every employee with manager and start date. Accepted Bob time off shows up in Odoo Time Off, and leave you validate in Odoo is submitted to Bob. Leavers get archived. Public holidays from the Bob calendars land in Odoo too, so planning and timesheets see the same days off as HR.

Features

EmployeesBob to Odoo, Odoo to Bob or both ways. Name, work e-mail, job title, department, manager, site, start date and status. An Odoo employee with the same work e-mail is linked, not duplicated
Org structurethe Bob department list (nested lists become the department tree), job titles as job positions and sites as work locations
New hires to Boban employee linked in Odoo is created in Bob with site, start date, department and title; later name and e-mail changes are sent too
Leaversterminated people are read when the service user may see them and are archived in Odoo
Time off inaccepted requests become validated Time Off, pending ones wait for approval, declined, cancelled or deleted ones cancel the Odoo leave
Time off outTime Off validated in Odoo is submitted to Bob (accepted there too if you allow it); cancelling it in Odoo cancels the Bob request
Balancesbalance, taken and annual allowance per employee and policy type, on the employee form
Public holidaysBob public holidays and company closures for the next twelve months
Webhooksemployee and time off events checked with the Bob-Signature header; access revoked in Bob can archive the Odoo user
