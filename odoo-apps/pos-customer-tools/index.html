POS Customer Tools

Know the customer at the till: their picture and tags in the customer list, your own extra customer fields without Studio, customer details on the receipt, and their address on a map.

What it does

In Odoo 19 the POS customer list shows a name, an address, a phone and an email. This module adds the customer picture and coloured tags (in the list, on the Customer button and on the payment screen), extra customer fields you create yourself, customer details printed on the receipt, and a map to see, search and save where the customer is.

Features

Customer imagethe contact picture next to every customer in the POS list, on the Customer button of the product screen and on the payment screen.
Customer tagsthe contact tags, in their colours, in the list and on the payment screen: VIP, wholesale, newsletter, anything you use.
Extra customer fields without Studiocreate Text, Long Text, Whole Number, Decimal, Yes/No, Date or Selection fields in Point of Sale - Configuration - Customer Fields, or reuse an existing contact field.
POS Info tabthe fields appear in a POS Info tab of the contact. The POS edits customers with that same form, so cashiers fill them in at the till.
Required in the POSa customer cannot be saved from the POS without the field; the server checks it, so it cannot be skipped. Backend users are not blocked.
Values in the customer listchoose which fields are shown under each customer in the POS list.
Customer info on the receiptphone, email and customer reference (each on or off per point of sale) and the fields marked Print on the Receipt, under the customer name.
Customer location on a mapLocation on Map in the customer menu: the saved location of the customer, or the one found from the address.
Search and drag the pinsearch an address or a place, click the map or drag the pin; the address at the pin is shown and can replace the customer address when the location is saved.
OpenStreetMap or GoogleOpenStreetMap with no key by default; the search uses the geolocation provider of the General Settings, Google when it is chosen there with an API key.
