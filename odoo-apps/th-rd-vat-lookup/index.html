Thailand RD VAT Lookup

Thailand RD VAT lookup for Odoo contacts: Thai RD VAT company search by name or by Thai tax ID (tax ID lookup), fetch the name and address from the Revenue Department VAT service, Thailand VAT registrant check, head office or branch number with the Thai head office / branch suffix, Thai address prefixes and bulk re-verification.

What it does

Type the Thai tax ID, save, and the company name and address come from the Revenue Department

The Thai Revenue Department publishes a VAT service that returns the registered name, branch, address and VAT registration date of every VAT registrant. This module calls it from Odoo contacts.

Type a 13-digit tax ID, choose head office or branch and save: the legal name with the head office or branch suffix, the address in the standard Odoo fields and the province are filled in. You can also search by Thai company name and pick the right branch from the list.

The tax ID check digit is validated before any call, the RD TIN service confirms that a tax ID exists, and a bulk action re-checks many contacts at once.
