Saudi VAT Return

Compute the ZATCA VAT return - fields 1 to 16 with the Adjustment column - from your own posted invoices and bills, open the journal items behind every figure, and run pre-filing checks before you key the return into the ZATCA portal.

What it does

The Saudi localisation of Odoo already loads the VAT return definition with the Saudi chart of accounts, and every posted invoice line already carries its tax grid. What Community lacks is the engine that adds them up. This module is that engine: it reads the same definition and totals your posted journal items for the tax period.

It adds the Adjustment column of the ZATCA form for fields 1 to 5 and 7 to 11, and computes 15% VAT on adjustments of the standard-rated fields. Field 14 corrections are checked against the SAR 5,000 limit, and the credit of the previous return can be brought forward into field 15 in one click.

The module does not connect to ZATCA and is not an e-invoicing module. It produces the figures and the evidence; you enter them on the ZATCA portal and record the reference back on the return.
