What it does

Run a tailor shop or boutique in Odoo: keep every customer's measurements per garment with their history, take stitching orders with styles, fabric and photos, follow them through cutting, stitching, trial and delivery, pay tailors by the piece, take advances, invoice, handle fittings and alterations, and let customers book, sign and follow their orders online.

A customer books a measurement on your website or walks in. You take their measurements on the garment's template, and the stitching order picks them up with the style options, fabric and reference pictures. Confirming the order sets the trial and delivery dates, creates the work orders for each tailor at their piece rate, and issues fabric and materials from stock when production starts. The order moves through cutting, stitching, finishing and trial on a board; fittings record what was agreed, the customer gets a ready message, pays the balance against the advance and rates the work.
