Odoo Pipedrive Connector

Pipedrive Odoo connector: two-way Pipedrive integration and Pipedrive sync for Pipedrive deals, leads, contacts, activities, notes, files and products. Odoo Pipedrive integration with webhooks, CRM connector, CRM integration

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The Pipedrive Odoo Connector keeps Pipedrive and Odoo CRM in step both ways. Organizations and persons become Odoo contacts, deals become opportunities in the matching pipeline and stage, and products, leads, activities, notes and files come along.

Before the first import you see a preview of what will happen to every record, and records that might be duplicates go to a review queue instead of being created twice. Conflict rules per field group decide which side wins when both changed.

A demo connection with a sample Pipedrive company built into the module lets you try every screen without an account.
