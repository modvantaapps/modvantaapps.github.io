Pipefy Connector

Your Pipefy pipes as Odoo projects, and the cards as tasks, both ways.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Pipefy and Odoo Project

What it does

Each pipe you switch on becomes an Odoo project with a stage per phase. Cards come in as tasks with their due date, assignees, labels, field values and comments. What your team changes in Odoo goes back to the card, and webhooks tell Odoo right away when a card moves in Pipefy.

You paste the client ID, client secret and token endpoint of a Pipefy service account. Test Connection shows who the account is, Load Pipes lists every pipe it can open with its organization and card count. You pick the Odoo project of each pipe yourself, or let the module create one.

Sync Now sends what changed in Odoo first, then reads the cards. A purchase request card in the Quoting phase, due Friday, assigned to Maria and labelled Office becomes a task in the Quoting stage, due Friday, assigned to Maria with the Office tag. Card comments land in the chatter under their author, each one once. Attachments are listed as links to Pipefy.

Rename the task, move it to another stage or post a note in its chatter, and the card is updated, moved or commented in Pipefy. New tasks in the project become cards. Register Webhook and Pipefy calls Odoo when a card is created, moved, edited, finished, commented or deleted; Odoo checks the secret and reads only that card again.

Features

Load Phases and Fields reads the pipephases become stages in Pipefy's order, final phases are folded, labels become project tags with the same name, and members are matched to Odoo users of the same company by email. On the Field Mapping tab you decide what each start form and phase field becomes: part of the task description, the deadline, a star on the task, a line on the Pipefy tab, or nothing.
