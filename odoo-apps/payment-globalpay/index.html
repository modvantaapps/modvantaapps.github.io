Global Payments Gateway

Take Global Payments on your Odoo 19 website, invoices and payment links

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Hosted Payment Page3-D SecureCapture and voidRefundsSaved cardsSigned webhooks

What it does

The customer pays on the Hosted Payment Page of Global Payments (GP API). Odoo creates the payment link, reads the transaction back from the API and updates the order. Authorize now and capture later from the Odoo transaction if you prefer.

Features

Hosted Payment PageOdoo creates a single-use link and redirects the customer to the page of Global Payments, which handles 3-D Secure.
Access tokensTokens are requested with the SHA-512 secret, kept until they expire and renewed once if refused. The app key is never sent.
Pre-authorization and captureWith Capture Amount Manually, the page authorizes and you capture or void from Odoo.
RefundsFull or partial refunds from Odoo.
Saved cardsWith Card Storage on your account, the customer can store the card on the page and pay later without it.
Signed notificationsX-GP-Signature is checked for POST and GET notifications with the two signed examples of the documentation. A wrong signature is refused and the data is always read back from the API.
RecoveryA scheduled job looks up payments by order reference when the customer closed the browser on the page.
API logEvery call is logged with secrets masked.
