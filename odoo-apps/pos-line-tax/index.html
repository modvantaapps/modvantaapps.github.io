POS Dynamic Taxes

Change the tax of an order line in the POS: one or several taxes, on one line or on every line, from the taxes you allow.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
POS dynamic taxesChange tax per linePOS tax selection

What it does

The cashier selects a line, opens Line Taxes, ticks the taxes to apply and presses Apply. Totals, the tax summary, the receipt, the POS order, the invoice and the session entry use the chosen taxes.

Who it is for

  • Shops that sometimes sell to tax-exempt buyers. A charity, embassy or export customer walks in and the tax has to come off one sale. The cashier opens Line Taxes, unticks the tax and the receipt, invoice and session entry follow.
  • Stores with products taxed at more than one rate. Some goods take a reduced rate only in certain situations, such as a takeaway versus eat-in sale. The cashier picks the right rate on that line instead of you creating a second product.
  • Retailers adding a levy on top of VAT. If an eco fee or deposit tax applies to a single line, the cashier can tick it alongside the normal VAT, so the line carries both taxes.
  • Owners who want tight control over tax changes. You decide which sales taxes appear at each till, whether a line can be left with no tax, and whether only managers may change taxes at all.

Setting it up

  1. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings and select the point of sale you want to set up.
  2. Turn on Change Taxes per Line.
  3. In Allowed taxes, pick the sales taxes cashiers may use. Leave it empty to offer every sales tax of the company.
  4. Decide on Allow no tax and Managers only, then choose whether Show taxes on each line should print tax names on the screen and receipt. Save.
  5. On the till, select an order line, open the Actions menu and press Line Taxes.
  6. Tick the taxes for that line (or tick Apply to every line of the order) and press Apply. Product Taxes puts the original taxes back.

POS Dynamic Taxes: questions

Does the unit price change when I change the tax?

No. The unit price stays as it is and the taxes are worked out on top of it, so totals move with the tax you pick.

What happens with fiscal positions?

Normal lines are still mapped by the fiscal position. A line whose taxes the cashier changed keeps exactly those taxes, and the fiscal position doesn't map them a second time.

Can a cashier get around the allowed list?

No. When the order reaches the server it's checked again. A tax that isn't allowed on that point of sale, a line left without tax when Allow no tax is off, or a change by a non-manager when Managers only is on, is refused.

How do I find orders where the tax was changed?

The POS order form has a Tax Changed column on its lines, and the POS orders list has a Line Tax Changed filter so you can review them later.

Does it need Enterprise or another app?

No. It only depends on Point of Sale, so it runs on Community as well as Enterprise.

Will it decide which tax is legally correct?

No. It only applies the tax the cashier picks from your allowed list. Which rate applies to which sale is a question for your accountant or tax office.
