Odoo DocuSeal integration

DocuSeal Connector

Send any Odoo document for signature with DocuSeal, cloud or your own server, and get the signed PDF back.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
DocuSealE-signatureAny Odoo modelPDF reportTemplatesSigner rolesPre-filled fieldsAudit log

What it does

Connect DocuSeal cloud, DocuSeal EU or a DocuSeal you host yourself. Pick the documents you want signed: quotations, invoices, contracts, any model with a PDF report. A Send with DocuSeal entry appears in their Action menu. Odoo sends the PDF (or a DocuSeal template with its roles), follows each signer, and when everybody has signed attaches the signed PDF and the audit log to the record.

Features

Cloud or self-hostedapi.docuseal.com, api.docuseal.eu or the https address of your own DocuSeal
Any modeldocument types per model with the PDF report, the partner field that signs, a counter-signer and the email text; one click adds Send with DocuSeal to the Action menu
Three sourcesthe PDF report, a PDF already attached to the record, or a DocuSeal template
Signature boxesPDFs without tags get a signature and a date box per signer on the last page; PDFs with {{...}} text tags use the tags
Templates and rolestemplates loaded with their signer roles; field values pre-filled from Odoo field paths such as partner_id.name
Signer statusinvited, opened, signed or declined with the reason, with each signing link
Signed PDF and audit logattached to the record and posted in its chatter; quotations can be confirmed when signed
Follow-upsend the invitation again, archive the submission, activities when declined or expired
WebhooksX-Docuseal-Signature HMAC with a five minute window and/or the secret header; the submission is read again from DocuSeal
