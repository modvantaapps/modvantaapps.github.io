POS FBR Integration

Pakistan FBR POS integration for Odoo 19: every POS bill is posted to FBR, and the FBR invoice number is printed on the receipt with its QR code.

What it does

Tier-1 retailers in Pakistan must report every sale invoice to the Federal Board of Revenue in real time. This module connects the Odoo Point of Sale to the FBR POS integration (FBR IMS): when a bill is paid, Odoo posts it to FBR with your FBR POS ID, receives the FBR invoice number and prints it on the receipt with a QR code. Bills that cannot reach FBR (no internet, FBR down) are kept in a queue and sent again automatically.

FBR registration is done by the business, not by the module: register each POS on e-FBR (Registration > POS Client Registration) to get the POS ID and the token (or the fiscal component). The module is not made or endorsed by FBR or PRAL.

Features

Real-time postingeach paid POS order is posted to FBR the moment it reaches the server; the cashier never waits for a second screen.
FBR invoice number on the receiptprinted with its QR code, the FBR POS ID, the company NTN and STRN, "Integrated with FBR" and the FBR logo image you upload.
Two ways to connectthe FBR web API (cloud, bearer token) in sandbox or production, or the FBR software fiscal component (Windows service) on your network.
Refunds as credit notesa POS refund is sent with invoice type 3 and the USIN of the original bill as RefUSIN; refunds can also be kept out.
Offline queue and retryfailed bills are retried every 10 minutes by a scheduled action, from the order with Send to FBR, or in bulk from the list.
Never posted twicea bill that has its FBR number is never sent again, and a retry keeps the same USIN.
Item data FBR asks forPCT code (Pakistan Customs Tariff) per product with a default per point of sale, tax rate, sale value, tax, discount, Third Schedule items (type 11 / 12).
Buyer dataname, NTN, CNIC and phone of the customer when one is set on the order.
Payment modecash, card, gift voucher, loyalty card or cheque per payment method; several methods on one bill are sent as Mixed.
FBR logevery invoice sent and every answer of FBR, with filters for posted and failed orders.
