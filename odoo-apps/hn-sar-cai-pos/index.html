Honduras POS SAR CAI

Honduras POS SAR CAI - CAI numbering and fiscal receipt on the Odoo Point of Sale

What it does

Every paid order of the Odoo 19 point of sale takes the next 16-digit SAR correlative of the CAI range of its emission point, and the receipt prints the CAI, the authorized range, the emission deadline and the article 11 breakdown required by Acuerdo 481-2017.

Each shop has its establishment and emission point (caja). The paid order takes the next correlative of the active CAI range for that prefix, e.g. 000-002-01-00000001.

The number is taken on the server with a lock on the range. Values sent by the POS client for the fiscal fields are discarded.

Document name and number, C.A.I., authorized range, fecha limite de emision, Original: Cliente / Copia: Obligado Tributario Emisor.

Importe exento, exonerado, gravado 15% and 18%, ISV 15% and 18%, discounts, the total in words and the currency (Lempiras).

Who it is for

  • Shops and supermarkets in Honduras selling at the till. The invoicing side may already number facturas under a CAI, but a POS ticket isn't an invoice, so it carries no CAI or SAR number. This app gives each paid POS order the next correlative from the CAI range of its caja.
  • Businesses with several cajas or branches. Every point of sale has its own establishment and emission point code, so each till draws from its own range, for example 000-002-01 for the second caja.
  • Pharmacies and restaurants with exonerated or identified buyers. Above the consumidor final limit the till asks for a customer with an RTN or ID. For exonerated buyers, the exempt purchase order number can be typed at the till and the registry numbers come from the customer record.
  • Stores that can't afford to stop selling when a range runs out. If no CAI range is active, the range is used up or its deadline has passed, the sale still goes through. It's marked Pending CAI number and numbered once a valid range is active.

Setting it up

  1. Install Honduras SAR CAI Invoicing first and register the CAI ranges for each caja under Accounting > Configuration > Honduras SAR > CAI authorizations (one for Factura, one for Nota de Credito).
  2. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings, pick the shop and find the Honduras SAR CAI block.
  3. Turn on SAR CAI numbering and enter the three-digit Establishment and Emission point (caja). Leave them empty to use the company defaults.
  4. Check the Sales document type and Refund document type, and the Consumer final limit (L), which starts at 10,000.00. Save.
  5. Look at CAI range status in the same block. It shows the active range with the correlatives left and the emission deadline, or warns that no range is active.
  6. Sell as usual. Orders still waiting for a number are listed under Point of Sale > Orders > Pending SAR numbers, where Assign SAR number retries one by hand.

Honduras POS SAR CAI: questions

Do I need another module?

Yes. Honduras SAR CAI Invoicing holds the CAI ranges, the SAR document type catalogue and the ISV taxes, and it depends on Odoo's Honduras localization. This app only adds the point of sale part.

Does it send anything to the SAR?

No. It numbers documents from the CAI ranges you register and prints the required details on the receipt. Nothing is transmitted. Please confirm with your accountant or the SAR that your setup and document codes match the current rules.

Can the till make up its own number?

No. The number is taken on the server with a lock on the range, and any fiscal values the POS client sends are dropped before the order is saved.

What happens with refunds and invoiced orders?

A refund uses the refund document type range of the caja and records the original number, CAI and date. If an order is invoiced, the invoice carries the CAI number from the shop's emission point, and an order that already has a factura number can't be invoiced a second time.

What does the receipt show?

The document name and number, the CAI, the authorized range, the emission deadline, the buyer or CONSUMIDOR FINAL with RTN, the exempt, exonerated and 15% and 18% taxed amounts with their ISV, discounts and the total in words.

Which languages are included?

The interface is in English with a Spanish translation included.
