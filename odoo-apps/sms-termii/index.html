Termii SMS Gateway

Send Odoo SMS in Nigeria and across Africa through your own Termii account

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Termii Messaging APISender IDGeneric and DND routeSigned delivery reports

What it does

Choose Termii in Settings > SMS and the SMS Odoo already sends (the SMS button on a contact, SMS templates, automation rules and SMS Marketing) leave through your Termii account, on your registered sender ID. Termii posts a delivery report back for each SMS. Odoo checks its X-Termii-Signature, updates the SMS, and remembers which numbers have Do-Not-Disturb switched on.

Features

All Odoo SMSchatter SMS, templates, automation rules and SMS Marketing use Termii once it is the provider.
Generic or DND routepromotional SMS on the generic route, order and payment messages on the DND route. Text with non-GSM characters goes out as unicode.
DND detectionwhen Termii reports "DND Active on Phone Number", the SMS is marked and, if you want, that number gets the DND route from then on.
Sender IDs from Odooread your sender IDs with their status (active, pending, blocked), request a new one, and pick the one Odoo uses.
Signed delivery reportsDelivered, Expired, Rejected, Message Failed and DND reports update the SMS and the Odoo notification, after the HMAC SHA512 signature is checked with your secret key.
Status checkSMS without a report after 6 hours (you choose) are looked up in the Termii message history.
Walletbalance from every send and every 30 minutes, with an email alert below the amount you set.
Retry and logSMS refused during an outage are sent again up to three times. The log keeps route, status, parts and cost.
Allowed usersoptionally only members of "Termii: Send SMS" can send.
