HSE Management

Health and safety in Odoo: incidents, near misses, risks, inspections, permits to work, PPE and training

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Workers report from their phone. The HSE team investigates, fixes the cause and can show what was done.

Most safety programmes live in spreadsheets, paper permits and group chats. This app puts the whole loop in one place, from the first report to the check that the fix worked.

Features

Incident reportsInjuries, near misses, property damage, spills, occupational illness and unsafe conditions. Each has a type, place, severity (actual and potential), photos and a reference like INC/2026/0012.
InvestigationLead investigator, team, witness statements, five whys, a root cause category and the root cause itself. The incident cannot move on without it.
Corrective and preventive actionsOwner, deadline, level of control (elimination down to PPE), reminders when late, and an effectiveness check that the owner is not allowed to do.
Hazard register and risk matrixLikelihood x severity before and after controls. You choose the levels and the score bands. A high residual risk waits for an HSE Manager who did not write it.
Safety inspectionsChecklist templates, planned weekly, monthly or yearly, answered pass / fail / N/A with photos. Every failed item becomes an action with an owner.
Permits to workHot work, confined space, work at height, LOTO, excavation and lifting, each with its own checklist, gas tests, isolation points and a training check for every worker.
PPE and trainingPPE issued per employee with a replacement date, training records with expiry, and a gap list against what each job position needs.
Dashboard and reportsDays without a lost time injury, TRIR and LTIFR from your hours worked, a live risk matrix, incident report PDF, permit PDF with QR code and an injury log CSV.
