Hospital Management System

Hospital Management System for Odoo 19 - Clinic and Healthcare Suite

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A clinic and hospital management suite for Odoo 19: patients, online appointment booking, a patient portal, outpatient clinical notes, prescriptions, laboratory and imaging orders, inpatient beds and admissions, nursing, pharmacy, billing and insurance, doctor commission and referral commission - in one install.

Patients get a medical record number, demographics, allergies and conditions, and a record with notes, appointments, observations, a timeline, prescriptions, documents and an access log. Practitioners belong to departments and branches; appointments are booked at the desk, by phone or online and move from booked to checked in, in consultation and completed.

Starting a consultation opens a clinical note in SOAP form with observations, diagnoses, follow-up and referrals. From the note the practitioner prescribes, orders laboratory tests or requests imaging. Signing locks the note; later corrections go in an addendum. Prescriptions print their directions from the dose, frequency and route.

Features

Inpatient care runs on wards and bedsthe bed board shows each bed as available, occupied or awaiting cleaning, and admissions allocate a bed and open a charge account. Nursing, pharmacy dispensing and controlled drugs, billing, insurance claims, consents and compliance tools are included.
