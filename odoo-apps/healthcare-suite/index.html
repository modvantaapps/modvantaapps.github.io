Hospital Management System

Hospital Management System for Odoo 19 - Clinic and Healthcare Suite

What it does

A clinic and hospital management suite for Odoo 19: patients, online appointment booking, a patient portal, outpatient clinical notes, prescriptions, laboratory and imaging orders, inpatient beds and admissions, nursing, pharmacy, billing and insurance, doctor commission and referral commission - in one install.

Patients get a medical record number, demographics, allergies and conditions, and a record with notes, appointments, observations, a timeline, prescriptions, documents and an access log. Practitioners belong to departments and branches; appointments are booked at the desk, by phone or online and move from booked to checked in, in consultation and completed.

Starting a consultation opens a clinical note in SOAP form with observations, diagnoses, follow-up and referrals. From the note the practitioner prescribes, orders laboratory tests or requests imaging. Signing locks the note; later corrections go in an addendum. Prescriptions print their directions from the dose, frequency and route.

Who it is for

  • Outpatient clinics and polyclinics. Reception books patients in Healthcare > Diary and checks them in from Today's Queue. The doctor writes the consultation as a SOAP clinical note and can prescribe, order lab tests or request imaging from it. A signed note is locked, and any correction goes in as an addendum.
  • Hospitals with wards and inpatient beds. Admitting a patient takes a bed and opens a charge account. The Bed Board under Inpatients shows each bed's state. Each night a scheduled audit adds one bed charge per admitted patient, and it never charges the same night twice.
  • Clinics that want patients to book on the website. The /healthcare/book page lets a visitor choose a department, a doctor and a free slot, then confirm with a name and phone number, without logging in. Only doctors with Accepts Online Booking ticked are offered. Patients with portal access can cancel from the portal until the branch's Online Cancellation Notice (hours) runs out.
  • Practices that pay doctors and referrers a share. Commission is worked out from the patient invoices you already post, by percentage, fixed amount or tiered slabs. Settle Commission groups what each doctor earned up to a date into one vendor bill.

Features

Inpatient care runs on wards and bedsthe bed board shows each bed as available, occupied or awaiting cleaning, and admissions allocate a bed and open a charge account. Nursing, pharmacy dispensing and controlled drugs, billing, insurance claims, consents and compliance tools are included.

Setting it up

  1. After installing, open Settings > Users & Companies > Users and give each person a Healthcare role: Reception / Front Desk, Clinician or Healthcare Manager.
  2. Under Healthcare > Configuration > Sites, add your branches, departments, rooms, and wards with their beds.
  3. Add doctors under Healthcare > Staff > Practitioners. Link each one to an employee, then set their Working Hours, Slot Length (min) and whether they accept online bookings.
  4. Fill the catalogues under Healthcare > Configuration: services and tariffs, lab tests with their analytes and ranges, imaging studies, and the medicines in the Formulary.
  5. Set reminder timing per branch with Reminder Lead Time (hours). Then pick the channel for each event under Healthcare > Configuration > Notifications > Notification Rules.
  6. If you pay commission, open Settings > Invoicing and use the Doctor Commission block to choose when it is earned, plus the product and journal for bills. Rules go under Healthcare > Configuration > Catalogue > Doctor Commission Rules.

Hospital Management System: questions

Do I need Odoo Enterprise for this hospital module?

No. It depends on Employees, Invoicing, Inventory with expiry dates, Portal, SMS and Website, and all of these ship with Odoo Community.

What can patients do on the portal?

They see their next appointment, their appointment list, issued prescriptions and their non-sensitive documents. They can cancel an appointment online within the notice the branch allows. A refill request only creates a to-do for staff. It never issues a new prescription.

Does it check drug interactions?

No. It ships no interaction data. Healthcare > Configuration > Interaction Checking is a place to register an external service that a developer connects. Until one is marked as validated, the prescribing screen says no checking is in place.

How do lab results get approved?

An order goes from ordered to sample collected and received in lab, then results are entered. Next they are verified, then authorised, and the system refuses authorisation by the same person who verified. A printable lab report is included.

Is there an audit of who looked at a record?

Yes, partly. The Record Access Log under Governance records timeline views, emergency access grants, disclosures, and patients opening their own record or documents on the portal. Emergency access needs a typed reason and runs out after a set number of hours.

What doesn't it do?

It doesn't store images in DICOM or talk to a PACS. An imaging order holds a link to the images or a few attachments. Insurance claims are tracked in Odoo, not sent to payers electronically. Lab results are typed in, as there is no analyser connection. SMS goes through Odoo's own SMS app. WhatsApp messages are logged but not sent unless a developer connects a gateway.
