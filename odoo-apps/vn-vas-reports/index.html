Vietnam Financial Statements

Vietnam financial statements for Odoo Community and Enterprise under Circular 99/2025/TT-BTC (TT99, replaces Circular 200 / TT200): B01-DN statement of financial position, B02-DN income statement, B03-DN cash flow statement (direct method) and the S06-DN trial balance. VAS reports with legal line codes (Ma so), PDF and Excel, Vietnam accounting reports (bao cao tai chinh) in Vietnamese.

What it does

Vietnam financial statements under Circular 99, every Ma so, from your Odoo ledger

Circular 99/2025/TT-BTC replaces Circular 200 for financial years starting on or after 1 January 2026. This module fills its statements from the journal items you posted in Odoo: B01-DN statement of financial position, B02-DN income statement, B03-DN cash flow statement (direct method) and the S06-DN trial balance.

Each line carries its legal code (Ma so) and its Vietnamese wording from Appendix IV, and is mapped to the Vietnam chart of accounts that ships with Odoo, including the short-term and long-term sub-accounts.

Compute, check that code 280 equals code 440, open any figure down to its journal items, print the PDF with the Circular header and the three signatures, or export to Excel.
