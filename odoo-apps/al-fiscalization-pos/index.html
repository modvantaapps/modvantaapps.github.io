Albania Fiscalization for POS

Albania Fiscalization for POS - NIVF, NSLF and QR on every Odoo receipt

What it does

Every paid order of the Odoo 19 point of sale is signed with your fiscalization certificate and registered with the CIS of the Albanian tax administration (fiskalizimi). The receipt prints the invoice number, the NSLF, the NIVF and the verification QR code.

Each paid order is sent as a signed RegisterInvoice message (version 3). The NIVF returned by the CIS is stored on the order and printed on the receipt.

The NSLF (IIC) is the RSA-SHA256 signature of the invoice data made with your .p12 certificate, then MD5, as in chapter 5.3.3. Checked against the sample of the specification.

The receipt carries the QR code of the invoice check web page with the NSLF, NUIS, date, ordinal number, business unit, TCR, software code and total.

Register and deregister each device with the CIS from Odoo, or type a TCR code from the self-care portal. One ordinal numbering per device, restarted every year.

Who it is for

  • Shops and supermarkets in Albania using the Odoo till. Albanian retailers have to register receipts with the tax administration's fiscalization service (CIS). Standard Odoo has no link to it, so this module signs each paid POS order with your certificate and sends it to the CIS, then prints the NSLF, NIVF and verification QR code on the receipt.
  • Restaurants and bars with patchy internet. A sale is never held up because the CIS can't be reached. The receipt prints with its NSLF and QR code, and a scheduled job retries the delivery every 5 minutes until the CIS answers.
  • Businesses running several cash registers. Each till is linked to its own registered cash register (TCR) inside a business unit, with its own yearly ordinal numbering. Opening the register sends the day's initial cash, and every cash in or cash out is sent as a deposit or withdrawal.
  • Stores that take refunds at the counter. A refund made from the original receipt is sent as a corrective invoice that references the NSLF of that original receipt.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. Your company currency must be Albanian lek (ALL).
  2. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings, find the Albania: Fiscalization (CIS) block and, under CIS connection, choose the test or production environment.
  3. Upload the Fiscalization Certificate (.p12) with its Certificate Password, press Read Certificate, and enter the Software Code and Maintainer Code from the self-care portal.
  4. Go to Point of Sale > Albania Fiscalization > Business Units and create your business unit with its Business Unit Code.
  5. Under Cash Registers (TCR), add a device and press Register with the CIS, or type the code of a device already registered on the portal.
  6. Give each cashier an operator code on the Albania CIS tab of their user, and check the CIS payment type on your POS payment methods.
  7. Back in the POS settings, pick the Cash register (TCR) for the till, then open a session and make a test sale.

Albania Fiscalization for POS: questions

Do I need a certificate?

Yes. A fiscalization certificate (.p12) is issued by NAIS, and the business unit, software, maintainer and operator codes come from the tax administration's self-care portal. Please confirm the exact registration steps with your accountant or the tax administration.

Has it been tested against the live CIS?

Not against the real service yet, because no certificate was available. Messages are checked against the published XSD, signed and verified, and the NSLF matches the worked example in the technical specification. The CIS answers are simulated in the automated tests.

What does the till check before a sale?

VAT rates must be 0, 6, 10 or 20 percent, cash payments between taxpayers must stay under the limit, an order can have at most 1000 items, and a sale and refund can't be mixed. The checks run in the till and again on the server.

What if the CIS refuses a receipt?

Refused receipts stay in Fiscal Invoices with the CIS error code and the request and answer. After fixing the cause, press Send Now to try again.

Does it cover back-office invoices or e-invoices?

No. It covers the point of sale only: POS receipts, cash deposits and cash registers. Back-office invoices, B2B e-invoices and warehouse transfer notes aren't included.

Which Odoo edition does it need?

Community or Enterprise with Point of Sale. The server also needs the Python libraries requests, cryptography and qrcode.
