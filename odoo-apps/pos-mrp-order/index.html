POS Manufacturing Order

Create manufacturing order from POS: POS manufacturing order and POS MRP order when a product is sold in the Point of Sale, per product and per variant, draft, confirmed or done MRP order (produce at once, components consumed), make to order only for the quantity missing in stock, bill of materials check at the till, manufacturing orders smart button on the POS order.

What it does

In Odoo 19 a product sold at the till only leaves the stock: the POS explodes kits, but it never creates a manufacturing order. This module creates one for the products you choose, when the order is paid, from their bill of materials.

Who it is for

  • Bakeries and kitchens taking orders at the counter. A cake or platter sold at the till needs to go to production with its recipe. Odoo's POS only moves stock, so this creates the manufacturing order from the product's bill of materials when the order is paid.
  • Gift, hamper and flower shops. Hampers and bouquets are put together from items already on the shelf. Set the product to Done and the components are consumed and the finished item added to stock as it's sold.
  • Furniture makers and workshops with a showroom. With the policy set to only the quantity missing in stock, selling three benches when one is in stock creates a manufacturing order for two.
  • Shops that make only some variants to order. You can limit manufacturing to chosen variants, for example the custom size of a table, while standard variants sell straight from stock.

Features

Per productManufacture from POS: never, always (the quantity sold) or only the quantity missing in stock at the till's location.
Per variantlimit it to some variants of the product; variant bills of materials are used.
State of the orderdraft (the workshop plans it), confirmed (components reserved) or done: produced at once, components consumed and the finished product put in stock before the POS delivery takes it.
Made to order on the tilllines that will create a manufacturing order carry a Made to order tag.
Bill of materials checkwhen such a product has no bill of materials the cashier is warned, or the payment is refused (setting); the order notes which products got no manufacturing order.
Linked both waysthe manufacturing order has the POS order as source and a POS Order field; the POS order has a Manufacturing smart button; Manufacturing has a From Point of Sale filter.
Safe produceproducts tracked by lots or serial numbers, or orders that need a person, are left confirmed with a note instead of failing the sale.
Works with refundsreturned lines never create manufacturing orders.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It brings in the Manufacturing app if it isn't installed yet.
  2. Give the product a bill of materials of type manufacture in Manufacturing > Products > Bills of Materials.
  3. Open the product, go to the Point of Sale tab and set Manufacture from POS to Always (the quantity sold) or Only the quantity missing in stock.
  4. Pick the POS Manufacturing Order State: Draft, Confirmed or Done (produced at once). Fill Only for Variants if just some variants should be manufactured.
  5. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings, check Manufacturing Orders from POS is on for the shop, and turn on Block Products without Bill of Materials if you want the payment refused when a BoM is missing.
  6. Sell the product at the till. The line shows Made to order, and after payment the Manufacturing smart button on the POS order opens the manufacturing orders.

POS Manufacturing Order: questions

Do I need the Manufacturing app?

Yes. The module depends on Point of Sale and Manufacturing (mrp), both available in Community. Nothing extra is needed on the till.

What does the Done state actually do?

The manufacturing order is confirmed and marked done straight away, so components are consumed and the finished product goes into stock before the POS delivery takes it out.

What if a product is tracked by lots or serial numbers?

Those can't be produced without someone picking the lot, so the manufacturing order is left confirmed and a note is posted on the POS order. The same happens if a manual step is needed.

What if the product has no bill of materials?

The cashier gets a warning and can still sell it, and the POS order gets a note saying no manufacturing order was created. If Block Products without Bill of Materials is on, the payment is refused instead.

Does it work with kits?

Kit bills of materials are left to Odoo's normal handling. This module only looks for normal manufacturing bills of materials.

Do refunds create manufacturing orders?

No. Only lines with a positive quantity are considered, and returned lines don't get the Made to order tag.
