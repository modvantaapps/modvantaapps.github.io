Thailand Payroll

Thailand Payroll for Odoo 19: Social Security, PND1, PND1 Kor and 50 Tawi

What it does

Thai statutory payroll on Odoo Community. Social security on the 2026 wage base, PND1 withholding tax with the Revenue Department's section 50(1) method for salary, bonus and overtime, the December true-up, provident fund and severance, the PND1 and PND1 Kor e-filing text files and the 50 Tawi certificate.

5% from the employee and 5% from the employer on wages between 1,650 and 17,500 baht (875 baht maximum), with the 20,000 (2029) and 23,000 (2032) ceilings loaded as future dated lines. Rounded to the baht, 0.50 up.

Regular pay annualised over the employee's pay periods in the year (remaining periods for a joiner), the employment expense and declared allowances deducted, the annual tax divided back per period.

December, or the month an employee leaves, recomputes the year on actual income and withholds the difference; an over-withholding becomes a refund line on the payslip.

Spouse, children (with the extra for the 2nd+ child born 2018 or later), parents, disabled dependants, life and health insurance, parents' health insurance, home loan interest, other allowances and the 65+ exemption.

Features

Irregular pay uses the cumulative methodtax on regular plus all irregular pay so far, minus the tax on regular pay and the irregular tax already withheld.
