Czech Kontrolni Hlaseni and DPH Return for Odoo 19

What it does

The Czech VAT control statement (kontrolni hlaseni, DPHKH1) and the Czech VAT return (priznani k DPH, DPHDP3) built from your posted invoices and bills, checked, and exported as EPO XML that passes the official Financni sprava schema.

Odoo's Czech localisation already tags every posted invoice line with its DPH row. What Odoo Community does not have is anything that turns those grids into the two monthly filings a Czech VAT payer sends to the Financni sprava. This module adds both.

The control statement is built document by document: each posted invoice, credit note and bill is routed into its section from its tax grids, the partner's Czech DIC and the 10 000 CZK limit including VAT. Reverse charge supplies get their subject code, EU acquisitions their supplier's VAT ID, and the small documents are added up into A.5 and B.3. The DPH return is computed from the official row definition of l10n_cz, in whole crowns, with the coefficient and adjustment rows typed in with a reason.

Both files are written in the EPO structure and validated against the XSD published by the Ministry of Finance before you can download them. The module does not submit anything: you upload the XML on the EPO portal or send it from your data box, then record the submission number.
