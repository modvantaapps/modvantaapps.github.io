Odoo Searoutes integration

Searoutes Connector

Searoutes for Odoo 19: sea distance, days at sea and CO2e between two ports, on your quotations and transfers

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Port to port distance - Days at sea and arrival - SECA and high risk areas - Suez and Panama options - CO2e per sea leg - Vessel ETA by IMO

Searoutes calculates sea routes the way ships sail them, with traffic schemes, canals and port entries, and the emissions of a shipment on those routes. This module puts it in Odoo: pick a port of loading and a port of discharge on a quotation or a transfer, press Plan Sea Route, and Odoo shows the distance in nautical miles, the days at sea, the arrival date and the CO2e of the sea leg. If you know the vessel, the IMO number gives a vessel-specific route and its last known position and ETA. Nothing is sent to Searoutes unless someone presses a button.

Features

Port searchby name or UN/LOCODE; ports kept in Odoo with country, size, coordinates and SECA flag
Sea routedistance in km and nautical miles, days at sea and arrival from your departure time and speed
Zones on the waydistance inside SECA zones and high risk areas, ice areas, and the named areas crossed
Routing choicesavoid SECA zones or high risk areas, avoid Suez or Panama, allow ice areas
Vessel-specific routeIMO number and draft for a route the vessel can sail
CO2e of the sea legFCL by container count and size/type code, LCL, bulk, breakbulk or parcel by weight
Emission detailstotal, well-to-tank and tank-to-wake in kg, and intensity per tonne-km
Vessel ETAlast AIS position, speed, destination, remaining distance and two ETAs
On sales orders and transfersports on the document, a Plan Sea Route button and the latest figures
Weight filled infrom the product weights of a quotation or the shipping weight of a transfer
Calls you controlone account per company, API key for administrators only, every call logged
