POS Order Transfer

POS order transfer and POS order sync between different points of sale and shops: send an open order from one register to another, alert it live, and let it accept, load part or reject it - with the customer, prices, notes and table kept, and a full transfer log.

What it does

POS Order TransferPOS Order SyncPOS Send OrderPOS Receive OrderTransfer Between ShopsRestaurant Order TransferMulti Session SyncAccept or RejectLoad Part of an OrderBroadcast to RegistersLive NotificationTake BackTransfer LogTransfer SlipRetail and Restaurant

Hand an open order to the right register, with nothing typed twice.

The receiving register hears about it at once over the Odoo bus: a notification, a sound and a counter in the top bar. No reload, no polling screen.

Preview every line first. Accept and open it, load only some lines or quantities (the rest keeps waiting), or reject it with a reason that goes back to the sender.

Prices, discounts, customer, pricelist, notes, combos, table and guest count travel with the order. The sender's copy is closed as transferred, never voided.

Features

Transfer Order in the till's actionspick the receiving point of sale, a reason and a message. Sales desk to cashier, waiter tablet to bar, one shop to another of the same company.

Wanted timewhen the customer wants the order; must not be in the past.
Customer phone and delivery addresstaken from the customer and editable; a delivery needs an address.

Forwarda branch passes an order it has not loaded yet to another branch.
Cancel with a codethe sender or a branch cancels with the authorization code of its register (or as POS manager) and a reason; everyone concerned is told.
