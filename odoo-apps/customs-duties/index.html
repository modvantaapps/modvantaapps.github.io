Customs Duties & Tariffs

Customs duties and import duty in Odoo - by HS code, origin and trade agreement, straight into landed costs

What it does

Enter your tariff rates once, see the duty, import fees and import VAT on every purchase order and quotation, then let one click split the real customs duty over the received products by HS code. Built on Odoo landed costs: the module never posts journal entries itself.

Odoo can carry an HS code on a product, and it can add landed costs to a receipt, but nothing works out the customs duty. Importers do the sums in a spreadsheet and type one lump sum into the landed cost, which Odoo then spreads over every product, even the ones with a different tariff.

Purchase orders and quotations show the estimated duty, fees, import VAT and landed unit cost per line. On the landed cost, Compute Customs Duties builds the CIF (or CFR / FOB) customs value from the goods and the freight and insurance lines, finds the right rate for each HS code and origin, and Apply to Cost Lines writes duty and fees as landed cost lines that go only to the products of each HS code.

Features

This module keeps a tariff table per HS codea rate for all origins, for a country group (region) or for one country of origin, plus preferential rates under free trade agreements. Each rate can be ad valorem, specific (per unit, kg, litre or m3), mixed or exempt, with a minimum duty, import VAT and any extra fees (surcharge, excise, levies) computed on the customs value, on the duty or on the running total.
