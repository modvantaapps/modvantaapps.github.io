Apollo Connector

Apollo connector and Apollo integration for Odoo CRM (Apollo Odoo, Apollo.io to Odoo): Apollo prospecting with people search and prospect search, company search, lead enrichment, contact enrichment, company enrichment (organization enrichment), email enrichment and phone enrichment as an email finder for B2B leads, data enrichment with a bulk enrichment queue, Apollo sync of deals and stages (Apollo CRM to Odoo CRM), Apollo sequences, Apollo API credits and usage, no duplicates.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Apollo Connector brings your Apollo.io workspace into Odoo CRM. Search Apollo's people and company database from Odoo, review the results, and turn the prospects you keep into CRM leads, opportunities or contacts with their company. Nothing is imported twice: every Apollo record is linked to the Odoo record it created.

Lead enrichment and contact enrichment work on the records you already have: email, job title, LinkedIn, seniority, location, industry, headcount, revenue and technologies, filled in from Apollo. Look at Apollo's values next to yours before you apply them, or let the bulk enrichment queue do hundreds in the background, 10 per Apollo request.

It also imports Apollo deals and stages, saved contacts and accounts, sequences and sending mailboxes; pushes contacts, companies and leads back to Apollo; adds contacts to Apollo sequences; and shows your credit balance and request limits inside Odoo. Try every screen first in Demo Mode, a built-in simulator of the Apollo API that needs no Apollo account.
