Tap Payment Gateway

Accept cards, mada, KNET, Benefit and the other methods of your Tap account in Odoo eCommerce, with the Tap result, card details and refunds recorded on the Odoo transaction.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Tap Payments for your Odoo checkout

What it does

This module connects Odoo 19 to Tap Payments. At checkout the customer picks a Tap payment method (Card, mada, KNET, Benefit and more, filtered by the customer's country and currency), pays on the secure Tap page, and comes back to the Odoo order.

On the return the module reads the charge back from the Tap API before it confirms anything, then stores the Tap status, response code, card brand, masked card number, 3-D Secure result and all Tap references on the transaction. A declined payment shows Tap's reason and code to the customer.

Refunds are sent to Tap from the standard Odoo refund wizard, and every request to Tap is kept in an API log.

Features

Keys, merchant ID and webhook
Tap payment methods and currencies
Checkout, statement, receipt and refund options
Card and mada at checkout
KNET for Kuwait
Back to a paid order
