POS Purchase Order

Create purchase orders from the Point of Sale: put what you need in the cart, pick the vendor, and the till creates the RFQ or the confirmed purchase order - received and billed if you want.

What it does

Create purchase order from POS: turn the POS cart into a request for quotation (RFQ) or a confirmed purchase order for the selected vendor, POS purchase order with vendor prices, expected arrival, vendor reference, auto validate receipt, auto create vendor bill, print purchase order from POS, purchase orders of the POS session, for cashiers without Purchase rights.

Who it is for

  • Stores where the supplier rep visits the counter. The manager can build the order on the till while the rep is standing there, choose the vendor and get a purchase order number to hand over, without walking to the back office.
  • Cafes and bakeries ordering for tomorrow. Staff add milk, eggs or flour to the cart, pick the supplier and create a request for quotation or a confirmed order. A Print button on the confirmation opens the PDF for the driver or the email.
  • Farm shops and cash and carry buyers. When goods are brought to the counter and bought on the spot, a confirmed order can validate its receipt straight away so the stock goes up, and a draft vendor bill can be created with it.
  • Teams where cashiers have no Purchase access. Cashiers only need Point of Sale rights. The server checks the session and the settings, then creates the order and records which till, session and cashier it came from.

Features

From the cartthe products and quantities of the cart, for the vendor chosen with the Customer button; lines of the same product are added together.
RFQ or purchase orderthe cashier chooses, or the setting decides; expected arrival, vendor reference and a note can be given.
Purchase prices, not sale pricesthe vendor pricelist of the product (then its cost) gives the price; the cart prices are never used; vendor taxes apply.
Receive at onceoptional: a confirmed order's receipt is validated straight away, so the goods are in stock.
Vendor billoptional: a draft vendor bill is created with the order.
Print from the tillthe confirmation shows the order number, the total and a Print button for the PDF; the cart is cleared (optional).
For cashierscashiers need no Purchase rights; the order records the point of sale, the session and the cashier.
TraceablePurchases smart button on the session, From Point of Sale filter in Purchase.
Checked by the serveropen session, feature enabled, purchasable products, positive quantities, known vendor.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It needs Point of Sale and Purchase with Inventory (it depends on purchase_stock).
  2. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings, select your point of sale and find the Purchase block. Tick Purchase Orders from POS.
  3. Choose Create as (Request for Quotation or Confirmed Purchase Order) and the Purchase Price source: the vendor pricelist then the cost, or the product cost only.
  4. Decide whether the cashier may switch between RFQ and order (Cashier Chooses RFQ or PO), and whether confirmed orders should Validate the Receipt, Create the Vendor Bill and Clear the Cart After Creation.
  5. On the till, add the products to order, then use the Customer button to select the vendor.
  6. Open the more (...) control buttons and press Create Purchase Order. Set an expected arrival, a vendor reference or a note if needed, then press Create.
  7. Find the orders in Purchase with the From Point of Sale filter, or from the Purchases smart button on the POS session.

POS Purchase Order: questions

Which price goes on the purchase order lines?

With the vendor price setting, the module looks up the vendor's pricelist entry for that product, quantity and date, converting currency and unit when needed. If there is none it falls back to the product cost. The sale price shown in the cart is never used, and the product's vendor taxes are applied.

Does it create a sale or a payment?

No. The cart is turned into a purchase order and, by default, the till starts a fresh empty order. No POS order or payment is recorded for it.

Which warehouse receives the goods?

The incoming operation type of the warehouse that the point of sale's operation type belongs to. If validating the receipt is enabled, the full ordered quantity is received at once with no backorder.

Who can print the purchase order PDF?

The cashier who created it from the till, and any user with Purchase rights. Anyone else gets a not found page. Draft orders print as a request for quotation, confirmed ones as a purchase order.

What can stop the order from being created?

The session must be open, the feature enabled on that point of sale, a vendor selected, and at least one product with a positive quantity. Products that can't be purchased are refused by name, and an expected arrival date in the past is rejected in the dialog.

Does it work on Community?

Yes. Point of Sale, Purchase and Inventory are all Community apps, and the module adds nothing that needs Enterprise.
