Subcontracting Work Order

Subcontract one step of a manufacturing order - not the whole product

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Mark an operation as done by a vendor: the work order orders the service, sends the components or semi-finished goods to the subcontractor, waits for them to come back and adds the vendor cost to the manufacturing order. The rest of the routing stays in house.

On a bill of materials operation (or a whole work center) you set the subcontractor, the service product, the price per unit and the lead time, and what travels: the components of the step, the semi-finished goods, materials consumed at the vendor, or nothing. When the manufacturing order is confirmed, or when the work order becomes ready, the service is ordered and the transfers are prepared.

The module builds on Odoo's own subcontracting locations and resupply operation type, so the goods at a vendor are visible in your inventory like any other stock.

Features

Odoo subcontracting works per producta subcontracting bill of materials, and Odoo refuses operations on it. When only one step goes out - powder coating, plating, heat treatment, CNC machining - you need the step itself to be subcontracted inside your normal routing. That is what this module adds.
