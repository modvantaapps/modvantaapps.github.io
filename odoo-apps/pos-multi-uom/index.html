POS Multi UOM

POS Multi UOM for Odoo 19: sell one product by the bottle, the Pack of 6 or the dozen - each unit with its own price and barcode - and keep stock right in base units.

What it does

POS multi UOM for Odoo Point of Sale: sell one product in several units of measure (Pack of 6, Box, Dozen, kg, lb) with its own price and its own barcode, change unit button and unit popup on the till, default POS unit, POS unit of measure on the receipt, the invoice and the stock moves, uom barcode scanning, package pricelist and packaging price rules (package based pricelist, sell by box).

Features

POS selling units on the productlist the extra units a product can be sold in (Pack of 6, Dozens, Box, lb, kg...) on the Point of Sale tab, with their own price. A price of 0 uses the product price x the number of base units in the unit.
Change Unit buttonshown on the till when the selected line has selling units: a popup lists the base unit and every selling unit with the price it would have on this order.
Default unit and ask on adda unit can be the default (paper cups are added by the dozen), or the till asks the unit every time such a product is tapped.
Barcode per unitthe barcode of a unit is saved as an Odoo packaging barcode; scanning it adds one package at its own price, and a second scan adds a second package to the same line.
Package based pricelista pricelist rule can apply to one unit only: its minimum quantity counts packages and a fixed price is the price of one package (5 packs or more: 4.80 a pack). Package rules never touch the base unit price, and they work on sale orders too.
Pricelists still applya percentage pricelist moves the price of every unit in the same proportion as the base price.
Unit everywherethe unit is printed on the order line, the receipt, the backend POS order and the invoice line.
Stock and cost in base unitsthe delivery counts base units (5 packs of 6 + 1 bottle = 31 bottles), ship-later orders too, and the cost and margin of the line are multiplied by the number of base units.
Checked by the serveran order line in a unit the product does not have is refused; refunds keep the unit of the line they return.
