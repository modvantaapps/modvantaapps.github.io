POS Multi UOM

POS Multi UOM for Odoo 19: sell one product by the bottle, the Pack of 6 or the dozen - each unit with its own price and barcode - and keep stock right in base units.

What it does

POS multi UOM for Odoo Point of Sale: sell one product in several units of measure (Pack of 6, Box, Dozen, kg, lb) with its own price and its own barcode, change unit button and unit popup on the till, default POS unit, POS unit of measure on the receipt, the invoice and the stock moves, uom barcode scanning, package pricelist and packaging price rules (package based pricelist, sell by box).

Who it is for

  • Supermarkets and drinks shops. The same bottle is sold on its own, as a six-pack and by the crate, each at a different price. The cashier switches the line's unit or scans the pack barcode, and stock still goes out as single bottles.
  • Wholesale counters. Trade customers on a wholesale pricelist can get a package price, for example 4.80 a pack from five packs, while walk-in customers buying single items keep the normal price.
  • Shops selling loose and weighed goods. Rice, nuts or fabric stocked in kilograms can also be sold by the pound from the same product, because any unit that converts to the base unit can be added.
  • Cafes and stores with a usual pack size. Paper cups bought by the dozen can be set so that tapping the product adds one dozen straight away, saving the cashier a step on every order.

Features

POS selling units on the productlist the extra units a product can be sold in (Pack of 6, Dozens, Box, lb, kg...) on the Point of Sale tab, with their own price. A price of 0 uses the product price x the number of base units in the unit.
Change Unit buttonshown on the till when the selected line has selling units: a popup lists the base unit and every selling unit with the price it would have on this order.
Default unit and ask on adda unit can be the default (paper cups are added by the dozen), or the till asks the unit every time such a product is tapped.
Barcode per unitthe barcode of a unit is saved as an Odoo packaging barcode; scanning it adds one package at its own price, and a second scan adds a second package to the same line.
Package based pricelista pricelist rule can apply to one unit only: its minimum quantity counts packages and a fixed price is the price of one package (5 packs or more: 4.80 a pack). Package rules never touch the base unit price, and they work on sale orders too.
Pricelists still applya percentage pricelist moves the price of every unit in the same proportion as the base price.
Unit everywherethe unit is printed on the order line, the receipt, the backend POS order and the invoice line.
Stock and cost in base unitsthe delivery counts base units (5 packs of 6 + 1 bottle = 31 bottles), ship-later orders too, and the cost and margin of the line are multiplied by the number of base units.
Checked by the serveran order line in a unit the product does not have is refused; refunds keep the unit of the line they return.

Setting it up

  1. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings, pick the point of sale and check that Sell in Several Units is on. It's on by default.
  2. Decide whether to turn on Ask the Unit When Adding, and whether Packaging Barcode Adds One Package should replace Odoo's normal packaging barcode behaviour.
  3. Open a product, go to its Point of Sale tab and add rows under POS Selling Units: the unit, its price (or 0 to use the product price times the Base Units), a barcode, and Default in POS if this unit should be added first.
  4. For package prices, open a pricelist rule and set Sold in Unit. The minimum quantity then counts packages and a fixed price is the price of one package.
  5. Open the till, add the product and press Change Unit to switch the selected line, or scan the unit's barcode.

POS Multi UOM: questions

Does it change the stock unit of my products?

No. Stock and costs stay in the product's base unit. When the delivery is created, each line is converted, so 5 packs of 6 and 1 bottle leave stock as 31 bottles.

Can I sell lot or serial tracked products by the pack?

No. A lot quantity is counted in the base unit, so tracked products have to be sold in their base unit and the server refuses another unit on them.

What happens on a refund?

The refund line keeps the unit of the line being returned, so a returned six-pack goes back as a six-pack at the price it was sold for.

Do the package pricelist rules work on sales orders too?

Yes. A rule with Sold in Unit also applies on a quotation when the line is in that unit. Rules without a unit work as normal, and a percentage pricelist moves every unit's price in proportion.

Does it work when the till is offline?

Yes. The selling units and package rules are loaded with the session, so prices are worked out on the till without calling the server.

Are there limits on barcodes?

Each unit's barcode is stored as a standard Odoo packaging barcode. For a product with several variants you set the barcode per variant in its packagings, not on the selling unit row.
