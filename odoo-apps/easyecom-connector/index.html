EasyEcom Connector

EasyEcom for Odoo 19: marketplace orders become sales orders, SKUs map to your products, and EasyEcom stock sits next to Odoo stock

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

EasyEcom integration - Amazon, Flipkart, Shopify channels - SKU mapping - GST - COD - AWB - Odoo 19

Many Indian sellers run their marketplaces through EasyEcom and keep the books in Odoo. This connector brings the EasyEcom orders into Odoo as sales orders, with the customer, the marketplace order number, the GST rate per line, the shipping charge and the amount to collect on COD orders. EasyEcom locations map to Odoo warehouses and each marketplace can go to its own sales team. Status, AWB number, courier, the invoice and the label come back from EasyEcom on the order. A stock comparison shows the EasyEcom quantity next to the Odoo quantity for every SKU and location, without touching Odoo stock.

Features

Order importnew and changed orders every hour or on demand, with nextUrl paging; the same order is never imported twice
WebhooksEasyEcom posts to a secret URL; Odoo reads each order again from the API before writing
Customersmatched inside your company by e-mail, phone or name and postcode, else created with the address and GSTIN
ProductsSKU mapping by internal reference or barcode, missing products created on request, barcode filled from EAN/UPC
Prices and GSTline prices from EasyEcom, entered without tax when a sale tax with the same GST rate exists
Channelsevery marketplace listed once, with a sales team you choose
WarehousesEasyEcom child locations mapped to Odoo warehouses
Shipping datastatus, AWB number, courier, shipping status and expected date on the order
Documentsinvoice and label PDFs from EasyEcom attached to the sales order
Cancelorders cancelled in EasyEcom are cancelled in Odoo; open orders can be cancelled in EasyEcom from Odoo
Stock comparisonEasyEcom available stock per SKU and location next to Odoo on hand, read only
