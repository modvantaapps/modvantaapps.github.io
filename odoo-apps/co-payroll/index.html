Colombia Payroll

Colombia Payroll for Odoo 19: PILA Planilla, Nomina, Retencion en la Fuente and Prestaciones

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Colombia PayrollNomina ColombiaPILAPlanilla PILAResolucion 2388IBCARLFSP

What it does

Colombian statutory payroll on Odoo Community: salud, pension, Fondo de Solidaridad Pensional, ARL and parafiscales with the art. 114-1 exoneration, retencion en la fuente procedure 1, auxilio de transporte, prima, cesantias and the PILA planilla flat file.

Salud 4% + 8.5%, pension 4% + 12%, on an IBC floored at 1 SMMLV by days and capped at 25 SMMLV.

1% from 4 SMMLV and the 0.2% to 1% extra from 16 SMMLV, split into the two accounts.

ARL classes I to V, Caja 4%, SENA 2% and ICBF 3%, with the art. 114-1 exoneration under 10 SMMLV.

70% IBC for an integral salary, and non-salary pay above 40% of the total counted in the IBC.
