ZKTeco Biometric Attendance

ZKTeco fingerprint and face devices feeding Odoo Attendances

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Download punches from ZKTeco terminals over TCP or UDP, or let the device push them to Odoo through ADMS. Punches are matched to employees and turned into Odoo attendances with your pairing rules, then checked against working schedules for late arrivals, early departures and absences.

Each ZKTeco device is a record in Odoo with its protocol, location, timezone and direction. Test Connection reads the serial number, firmware, user and log counts; Download Logs fetches the punches on demand or on a schedule. ADMS devices send every punch to Odoo themselves.

Device users are matched to employees by device user ID, PIN or badge ID. Unknown users are kept as unmapped, never lost. Punches become hr.attendance records: first in and last out per shift, alternate in and out, or the key pressed on the device, with a minimum gap between punches and a rule for missing check-outs.

Reports compare attendances with each employee's working schedule, minus public holidays and time off: late arrivals and early departures with grace minutes, absent employees, and a punch analysis per employee and day. A built-in simulator lets you try everything without hardware.
