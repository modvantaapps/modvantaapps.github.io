Shared Login with Employee PIN

One Odoo user on the warehouse PC, the shop-floor tablet or the reception desk - and still know exactly who did what. Employees unlock the shared login with their own PIN or badge, the session locks itself when they walk away, and the chatter, tracking and audit log show the employee, not the shared user.

What it does

Mark an ordinary internal user as a shared login and pick the employees permitted on it. After the normal password login the terminal shows a lock screen: "Who is working?"

Each employee taps their name and types their PIN on a large on-screen keypad, or scans their badge (the Badge ID already on the employee). Badge + PIN by default; badge only if you allow it.

PBKDF2-SHA512 with a random salt, the same kind of hash Odoo uses for passwords. The PIN is never stored in clear, never logged and cannot be read back, not even by an administrator.

After 5 wrong PINs the employee is locked for 15 minutes; after 10 wrong attempts the whole terminal is locked for 5 minutes. Both numbers are settings. Managers can unlock an employee early.
