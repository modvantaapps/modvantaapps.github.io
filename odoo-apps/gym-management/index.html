Gym Management

Run your gym or fitness center in Odoo: members, gym membership plans and renewals, trainers, workout and diet plans, check-in attendance and classes - with a member portal.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Gym Management for Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise. Membership invoices come from Odoo Invoicing, so payments and taxes work the normal way.

Who it is for

  • Independent gyms and fitness centres. The front desk sells a membership, activates it and the invoice is created in Odoo Invoicing straight away. A daily job expires finished memberships and emails members a set number of days before their end date.
  • Personal training studios. Each member has a trainer, and a trainer only sees their own members along with those members' workout plans, diet plans, activity log and body measurements.
  • Clubs that run group classes. Classes have a lead trainer, extra trainers, a capacity and a list of attendees. A full class refuses extra bookings, and Send Invitations emails everyone with an address.
  • Gyms that want members to see their own plans. With portal access, a member gets a My Gym page showing their membership, workout and diet plans, measurements and recent visits, and can download the plans as PDF.

Features

Gym Membership plansday pass, monthly, quarterly or yearly plans with a price and a service product. With Invoice Memberships on Activation on (the default), activating a membership creates and posts its invoice. With it off, no invoice is made; click Create Invoice on the membership to get a draft.
Renewals and remindersone click renews a membership from the day after it ends. A daily job expires finished memberships, starts waiting renewals and emails members before their end date.
Members and trainersprofiles with photo, goal, emergency contact and medical notes; each member has a trainer, each trainer sees the members assigned to them.
Workout plans and diet plansexercises by day with sets, reps, weight and rest; meals with calories, protein, carbs and fat, totalled per day. Assign a plan to many members at once.
Attendance check-incheck members and trainers in and out from their profile; hours are counted and a member without a running membership is refused (optional).
Progress trackingactivity log of exercises done and body measurements with BMI, kept per member.
Classes and special sessionscalendar of classes with a lead trainer, capacity and attendees, and email invitations to everyone in one click.
Member rating and portalsend a rating email with three faces; the member portal shows the membership, plans, measurements and visits, with PDF downloads.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module and, under Settings > Users & Companies > Users, give each staff member the Gym Management role of Trainer or Manager.
  2. Go to Gym > Configuration > Settings and decide on Invoice on activation, the Expiry reminder days (0 turns it off) and Check-in needs active membership.
  3. Create your plans under Gym > Configuration > Membership Plans with a name, Duration in days, weeks, months or years, and a price. A service product is created for the plan if you leave Membership Product empty.
  4. Add trainers under Gym > Members > Trainers and link each one to an Odoo user, then add members under Gym > Members > Members and pick their trainer.
  5. From a member, create a membership, choose the plan and press Activate. Use Renew on an active or expired membership to start the next period.
  6. Build plans under Gym > Training > Workout Plans and Diet Plans, then assign them to several members at once from the Actions menu on the member list.

Gym Management: questions

Does it need Odoo Enterprise?

No. It depends on Invoicing, Discuss, Portal and Customer Rating, which are all part of Community.

How are memberships invoiced?

Each plan has a service product. With Invoice on activation on, activating a membership creates and posts a customer invoice for the plan and its dates. With it off, nothing is invoiced automatically and you can create a draft invoice from the membership when you want.

What happens when a membership runs out?

A daily scheduled action marks it expired. A renewal waiting in draft becomes active once the previous membership has ended and its start date has arrived, and it's invoiced like any other activation.

Can a member hold two memberships at once?

Not overlapping active ones. Activation is refused if the member already has an active membership covering the same dates.

Can members check in without a valid membership?

Only if you allow it. With Check-in needs active membership on, a member check-in is refused when no membership is running. Trainers can check in and out too, and hours are counted.

What doesn't it do?

It doesn't handle door hardware or turnstiles, and there's no online sign-up or class booking from the website. Members see their records on the portal, while staff do the booking and check-ins in the backend.
