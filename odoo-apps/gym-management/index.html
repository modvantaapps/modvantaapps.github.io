Gym Management

Run your gym or fitness center in Odoo: members, gym membership plans and renewals, trainers, workout and diet plans, check-in attendance and classes - with a member portal.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Gym Management for Odoo 19 Community and Enterprise. Membership invoices come from Odoo Invoicing, so payments and taxes work the normal way.

Features

Gym Membership plansday pass, monthly, quarterly or yearly plans with a price and a service product. Activating a membership creates and posts its invoice.
Renewals and remindersone click renews a membership from the day after it ends. A daily job expires finished memberships, starts waiting renewals and emails members before their end date.
Members and trainersprofiles with photo, goal, emergency contact and medical notes; each member has a trainer, each trainer sees the members assigned to them.
Workout plans and diet plansexercises by day with sets, reps, weight and rest; meals with calories, protein, carbs and fat, totalled per day. Assign a plan to many members at once.
Attendance check-incheck members and trainers in and out from their profile; hours are counted and a member without a running membership is refused (optional).
Progress trackingactivity log of exercises done and body measurements with BMI, kept per member.
Classes and special sessionscalendar of classes with a lead trainer, capacity and attendees, and email invitations to everyone in one click.
Member rating and portalsend a rating email with three faces; the member portal shows the membership, plans, measurements and visits, with PDF downloads.
