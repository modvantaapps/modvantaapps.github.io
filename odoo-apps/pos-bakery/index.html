POS Bakery and Cake Shop

Take custom cake orders at the till, collect a deposit, run them through the kitchen, and plan the day's baking.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
POS bakeryCake shopCustom cake orderCake configuratorBakery production plan

What it does

POS bakery and POS cake shop: custom cake order and cake configurator at the till (size, flavour, filling, frosting, theme, add-ons, layers, eggless, message, photo cake) with server pricing (price per kg or fixed, layer, eggless, photo, message, urgent and delivery fees), cake deposit and balance at pickup, pickup or home delivery, kitchen board with production stages, kitchen ticket, cake ready email, calendar, and a daily bakery production plan from par levels and cake orders with manufacturing orders

Features

A cake shop sells two waysfrom the counter and to order. This module adds the second to the point of sale: a cake configurator priced by the server, deposits and balance at pickup, a kitchen board, and a daily production plan.
Cake configurator at the tillsize, flavour, filling, frosting, theme, add-ons, layers, eggless, message, photo or design, dietary note, wanted date, pickup or home delivery.
Server pricingfixed price or price per kg of the chosen size, plus option extras and your fees for layers, eggless, photo, message, urgent orders and delivery.
Rulesminimum lead time, urgent window, maximum layers, options not available eggless.
Deposit or full paymenta deposit line (at least your minimum %) or the full price; the balance is collected later.
Cake Orders at the tillsearch open cake orders, collect the balance, hand over a ready cake.
Kitchen boardConfirmed, In Production, Decorating, Ready and Handed Over, with urgent cakes highlighted.
Kitchen ticketa printable ticket with every detail and the photo.
Cake ready emailsent to the customer when the cake reaches Ready.
Calendar and pivotcakes by wanted date, amounts due.
Server checksdeposits below the minimum, changed prices and over-collected balances are noted on the cake order.
Daily production planpar levels per weekday plus the cakes wanted that day; manufacturing orders for products with a bill of materials.
