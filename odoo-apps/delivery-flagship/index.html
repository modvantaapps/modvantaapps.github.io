FlagShip Shipping Integration

Ship with FlagShip from Odoo - live courier rates, labels, pickups and LTL freight

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A FlagShip connector for Odoo 19: the cheapest, the fastest or one chosen courier service on the quotation, the label on the delivery order when you validate it, courier pickups, tracking and LTL freight orders - all through your FlagShip account.

The module adds FlagShip as a delivery method provider. Each delivery method uses one FlagShip account (SmartShip API token, test or production host) and decides the service: always one courier service, the cheapest rate, or the fastest rate, optionally limited to one courier.

Validating the delivery order confirms the shipment at FlagShip with one item per Odoo package, attaches the label PDF (regular page or 4x6 thermal) and, for cross-border shipments, the commercial invoice. Options per method: signature, Saturday delivery, insurance, address correction, cash on delivery and tracking e-mails to the customer.
