Customer RFM Segmentation

Score customers on Recency, Frequency and Monetary value and segment them into Champions, Loyal, At Risk, Hibernating and Lost. RFM analysis, customer segmentation, VIP customers, churn risk, win-back list, buying behaviour.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Not all customers are equal. Some bought last week and buy every month; others spent a lot two years ago and have gone quiet. Odoo shows sales per customer, but does not group customers by buying behaviour.

This module looks at each customer's confirmed sales orders in an analysis window (two years by default), rolled up onto the company. It scores Recency (days since the last order), Frequency (number of orders) and Monetary value (total spend) from 1 to 5 against thresholds you set, and assigns a segment: Champion, Loyal Customer, Potential Loyalist, New Customer, Needs Attention, At Risk, Hibernating or Lost. Scores are refreshed nightly and can be recomputed on demand.
