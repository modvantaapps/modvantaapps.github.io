InPost Shipping Integration

Ship with InPost from Odoo - Paczkomat lockers at the checkout, ShipX labels, courier pickups and tracking

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

An InPost connector for Odoo 19 on the ShipX REST API: customers choose a Paczkomat or PaczkoPunkt on a map at the checkout, parcel sizes are picked for you, labels arrive on validation, the courier is ordered from Odoo, and the InPost tracking history sits on every shipment.

The module adds InPost as a delivery method provider in Odoo and uses the InPost ShipX API: locker and point search, shipments and labels, dispatch orders for courier pickup, return labels and tracking.

Each delivery method holds your organization id and ShipX access token, the sender address, the InPost service - Paczkomat 24/7, InPost Kurier, Paczka w Weekend and others - the default parcel size with automatic A / B / C sizing, the price (fixed, a rate table by parcel size and weight, Odoo rules or InPost's own price calculation), label format and size, cash on delivery and insurance.

Features

All InPost shipments in one list
Organization, parcel size, price and service
Choose a Paczkomat on the delivery order
InPost tab on the delivery order
Shipment with locker and tracking events
Order the InPost courier
Return labels for courier shipments
Every InPost service at the checkout
Paczkomat map for the customer
