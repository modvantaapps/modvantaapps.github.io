Optical Store Management

Run an optical store or optician shop on Odoo: eye prescriptions and optometrist eye tests, frames, spectacle lenses and contact lenses as products, a prescription check on sales orders and at the optical POS, the optical job card with lens orders to the lab, lensmeter verification and remakes, contact lens stock by power and expiry, recall reminders and a customer portal. One module, built on Odoo's own Sales, Point of Sale, Purchase and Inventory.

What it does

Prescription lenses cannot be sold without a valid prescription of that customer, a lens is refused when the prescription is outside its power range, a progressive lens cannot go to the lab without fitting heights, and finished lenses are checked on the lensmeter before the patient collects them.

Everything an optical store needs, from the eye test to the hand-over.

Steps of 0.25 D, an axis for every cylinder and no axis without one, plausible additions, prisms and pupillary distances - every error listed at once. Spherical equivalent, anisometropia and high-power flags, one-click cylinder transposition.

History and symptoms, unaided and corrected visual acuity, eye pressure with an alert above 21 mmHg, refraction, findings, referral and recommended lens design. Completing the test writes and validates the prescription; the fee is invoiced in one click. Calendar of eye tests.

Frames and sunglasses with model, material, rim, shape, size and colour. Spectacle lenses with design (single vision, bifocal, progressive, office), index, material, coatings, stock or lab supply and the power range you can supply. Find Matching Lenses lists the lenses that fit a prescription.

Features

Right and left eyesphere, cylinder, axis, addition, prism and base, visual acuity, mono PD and fitting height, plus distance and near PD. Contact lens prescriptions with base curve and diameter. Full history per patient with the change since the last prescription.
Confirming the sale builds the jobframe (or the customer's own frame) and the lens pair, coatings, mono PD and fitting heights from the prescription. Progressive and bifocal lenses cannot go out without fitting heights. Printable job card with sign-off steps.
