InvoiceXpress Connector

Portuguese invoices must come from AT certified software. With this module you keep invoicing in Odoo: posting a customer invoice or credit note issues the legal document in InvoiceXpress, and the legal number, ATCUD, QR code and certified PDF come back to the invoice. Payments become InvoiceXpress receipts, deliveries can issue transport documents and the SAF-T (PT) file is one click away.

Enable "Issue with InvoiceXpress" on a sales journal and choose Fatura, Fatura-Recibo or Fatura Simplificada and the series.

Account name and API key, e-mail through InvoiceXpress, certified PDF, receipts for payments, transport documents; series and taxes are read from InvoiceXpress.

Every issued document with its legal number and state, next to the Odoo invoice number.

Validating a delivery can issue a Guia de Remessa or Guia de Transporte with load and delivery addresses, licence plate and the AT code.
