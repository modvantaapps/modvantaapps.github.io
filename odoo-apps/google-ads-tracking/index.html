Google Ads Conversion Tracking, Google Ads Enhanced Conversions, Google Consent Mode v2

Google Ads conversion tracking for Odoo eCommerce - purchases, carts, leads, remarketing and offline conversions

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Send your Google Ads conversion actions from the Odoo shop and website forms with the Google tag or Google Tag Manager, add enhanced conversions and cart data, run dynamic remarketing, capture click IDs and export offline conversions - all behind Google Consent Mode v2 on the Odoo cookies bar.

One configuration per website holds your Google Ads tag ID (AW-...) and a line per conversion action with its send_to value: purchase on order confirmation, add to cart, begin checkout, shipping and payment steps, sign up, login, lead and contact forms, phone and email clicks and more. The tag can be the Google tag (gtag.js) or a Google Tag Manager dataLayer.

Enhanced conversions add hashed customer data, dynamic remarketing sends product views, lists and carts with item ids that match your Merchant Center feed, and conversions with cart data add the Merchant Center ID, feed country and language. Purchases can be counted for new customers only within a returning-customer window.

With consent, the GCLID, GBRAID and WBRAID of the ad click are kept and stored with website form records, and the offline export builds the file Google Ads accepts under Goals > Conversions > Uploads from confirmed orders or form records. A debug mode shows every event in a panel on the page, and an event log in Odoo lists what was sent.
