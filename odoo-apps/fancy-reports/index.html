Report Templates

Report templates for invoices, quotations, orders, delivery slips and receipts

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question

What it does

Eight document layout designs for the invoice report, credit notes, vendor bills, quotations, sales orders, RFQs, purchase orders, delivery slips, picking operations and payment receipts. Pick a template per company, per document type, per customer or on one document. Colours, fonts, logo position, watermarks, bank details, QR codes, product images, signature and stamp - and append your terms or brochure PDF to every pdf report.

Odoo prints every document with one company-wide layout. This module gives you report templates: a template is a design plus every option - colours, font, logo position, header and footer, what to print after the lines, watermark and the PDF files to append.

The standard document content stays the standard one: lines, taxes, totals, payment terms and the e-invoice QR codes of your localisation are not replaced, only restyled and completed.

Who it is for

  • Small companies that want a branded invoice. Odoo's document layout applies one look to everything. Here you pick one of eight designs, set your own colours, one of ten fonts and the logo position, and the invoice, credit note and payment receipt follow it.
  • Sales teams sending quotations. A quotation can show product images, line numbers, the amount in words, bank details and a QR code that opens it in the customer portal. Terms and a brochure PDF can be appended after the last page.
  • Groups with several companies or key accounts. Each company chooses its own default and a template per document type. A single customer can have its own template too, and one document can override everything, for example a quotation for a trade fair.
  • Accounts teams that print a lot of invoices. Draft, paid, cancelled and overdue invoices can carry a status watermark automatically. Invoices already sent can print with a duplicate label, and the payment history can be added below the lines.

Features

You decide where each template is usedfor all documents of a company, per document type (invoices, quotations, purchase orders, delivery slips, payment receipts), per customer or vendor, or on a single document. The most specific one wins. Documents without a template print exactly as before.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It needs Invoicing, Sales, Purchase and Inventory, and nothing changes on your documents until a template is assigned.
  2. Open Accounting > Configuration > Report Templates (or Sales > Configuration > Report Templates) and open one of the eight starter templates, or duplicate one.
  3. Work through the tabs: Style for design, colours, font and logo, Header and Footer, Lines for images, line numbers and hidden columns, After the Lines for bank details, QR codes, terms, signature and stamp.
  4. Use the Watermark tab for status stamps or your own text, and the Append PDF tab to add PDF files such as terms and conditions. Pick a document in Preview on to watch the Live Preview update.
  5. Assign it in Settings > General Settings under Report Templates, either for All documents or per type (invoices, bills, quotations, purchase orders, delivery slips, payment receipts). The Use for All Company Documents button on the template form sets it as the company default in one click.
  6. For a single customer or vendor, set Report Template on the contact's Sales & Purchase tab. For one document, set it on the Other Info tab of the invoice, order, transfer or payment.
  7. On the Print Menu tab, tick document types to add the template as its own Print menu entry, with an optional Duplicate copy entry.

Report Templates: questions

Does it replace Odoo's standard invoice or order report?

No. Lines, taxes, totals, payment terms and any localization QR codes still come from the standard report. The template restyles the page and adds blocks around that content.

Which template wins if several are set?

A Print menu entry of a specific template comes first, then the template on the document, then the one on the customer or vendor, then the company's choice for that document type, then the company default. With nothing set, the normal Odoo layout prints.

Is it a drag-and-drop report designer?

No. You choose one of eight designs and switch options on and off. You can't move elements freely or edit QWeb, which keeps the templates safe across module updates.

Why does my PDF have no header or logo?

Odoo's repeated headers and footers need a wkhtmltopdf build with patched Qt. If your server runs the generic build, set the header to First page only, which prints it inside the document body instead.

Can I append any file?

Only PDF files can be added on the Append PDF tab, and the module refuses anything else. You can also choose to append the PDF attachments already on the record's chatter.

Does it handle languages and several companies?

Footer text, terms, tagline, signature caption, watermark text and the duplicate label are translatable, and documents render in the partner's language. A template can belong to one company or be left shared, and each company keeps its own assignments.
