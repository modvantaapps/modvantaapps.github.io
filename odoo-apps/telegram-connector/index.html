Telegram Connector

Connect your Telegram bot, link customers with an invitation link, and read and answer their Telegram messages in the Odoo chatter of contacts, leads and any document.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Talk to customers on Telegram from Odoo

What it does

This module connects an Odoo 19 database to a Telegram bot through the Telegram Bot API. Customers open an invitation link to the bot; from then on their messages, documents and photos arrive in Odoo, on a Telegram chat record and in the chatter of their contact.

Your team answers from the chat or from any document with a Send Telegram button, with templates, formatting and attachments. Group and supergroup chats are supported, and a dashboard shows chats to answer, contacts not linked, messages sent and received and anything queued or failed.

Every call to Telegram is kept in an API log. Demo Mode runs a built-in simulator of the Bot API, so you can try every screen - including a set of sample conversations - without creating a bot.
