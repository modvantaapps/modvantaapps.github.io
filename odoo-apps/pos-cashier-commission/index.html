POS Cashier Commission

See what each cashier sells, and pay commission on it

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A cashier performance report for Odoo Point of Sale - net sales, discounts given and refunds per cashier - plus commission rules and commission runs that work out what each cashier or salesperson earned and freeze it once confirmed. Works with or without employee login.

Odoo Point of Sale records which employee rang up every order, but it has no report that puts the cashiers side by side, and no way to turn their sales into a commission figure.

This module adds Cashier Performance under Point of Sale -> Reporting: net sales before tax, discount given and quantity per cashier, in pivot, graph and list views, with filters for refunds and discounted lines and grouping by till, session, product category or day.

Commission rules say how much is paid and to whom: a percentage of net sales, an amount per unit sold, a percentage of margin (cost at the time of sale), a fixed amount per order or order amount slabs, optionally only above a threshold, for some employees or users, tills, products, product categories or customers, between two dates, with a different rate per discount band, and only on lines under a maximum discount. A rule pays the cashier logged in with employees, the user who ran the till, or the salesperson of each order line. Each rule says when commission is earned: on the paid order, on the posted invoice or on payment. A commission run works out one period for every cashier, and once confirmed it can no longer be recomputed by accident. A confirmed run is paid out as draft vendor bills or employee expenses, with a commission statement PDF per employee.

Who it is for

  • Retail managers comparing their till staff. Odoo records who rang up each order but never puts cashiers side by side. The Cashier Performance report shows net sales before tax, discount given, quantity and refunds per cashier, by till, session, category or day.
  • Shops that pay a monthly sales commission. Instead of exporting orders to a spreadsheet, you write the rules once and run a period. Each person gets a line per rule with the base, the rate and what they earned, and the run is frozen when you confirm it.
  • Stores worried about staff discounting too much. The discount given column shows who gives away the most and on what. A commission rule can also cap the discount, so lines sold below a set price earn nothing, or use discount tiers that lower the rate as the discount grows.
  • Businesses that sell on account or by invoice. Each rule says when commission is earned: on the paid till order, on the posted invoice, or when the payment comes in. Sales put on a customer account wait until they're paid.

Setting it up

  1. Go to Point of Sale > Reporting > Cashier Performance to see sales, discounts and refunds per cashier straight away. No setup is needed for the report.
  2. Go to Point of Sale > Reporting > Commission > Rules and create a rule. Pick Commission To: Cashier (employee login), Cashier (user), or Salesperson of the line or order.
  3. Choose the basis (percentage of net sales, amount per unit sold, percentage of margin, fixed amount per order, or order amount slabs), enter the Rate, and set Commission Earned On.
  4. Narrow the rule if you need to with Employees, Users, Points of Sale, Products, Product Categories, Customers, Valid From and Valid Until, Maximum Discount (%) or Discount Tiers. Add a Threshold and choose whether it pays on everything once reached or only on the part above it.
  5. Go to Point of Sale > Reporting > Commission > Runs, create a run for a period and press Work it out. Check the earnings lines, then press Confirm.
  6. Set the Bill Journal and Commission Expense Account on the run, then press Create Vendor Bills (or Create Expenses if the Expenses app is installed) and Print Statements for each employee.

POS Cashier Commission: questions

Is commission worked out on the price with tax?

No. Net sales are before tax. The idea is that tax was never the shop's money, so the rate you set means the same thing wherever you trade.

Can a confirmed run change if an old order is edited?

No. A run stores its lines and confirming freezes them. To change it you reset it to draft, which asks you to confirm first. A run that's already been paid out can't be reset until its bills or expenses are cancelled.

How does the margin rule get the cost?

It uses the cost Point of Sale stored on the order line when it was sold, not today's product cost. That cost is usually filled when the session closes, so lines from an open session earn nothing on a margin rule yet, and the run tells you how many lines were left out.

Do refunds reduce commission?

Yes. A refund line has a negative quantity and subtotal, so it lowers the base of the cashier who took the refund with no extra setting.

Do I need employee login at the till?

No. A rule set to Cashier (user) pays the user who ran the till. The module depends on Point of Sale and the Employees app, which are both in Community. Salesperson rules read the POS Salesperson module if it's installed, but it isn't required.

How is commission paid out?

A confirmed run creates draft vendor bills, one per employee, addressed to the employee's work contact. If the Expenses app is installed you can create employee expenses instead. Each earning line remembers the bill or expense that paid it, so nothing is paid twice.
