Armenia Payroll

Armenia Payroll for Odoo 19: Income Tax, Social Payment, Stamp Duty and Health Insurance

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Armenia PayrollArmenian PayrollIncome TaxSocial PaymentFunded PensionStamp DutyHealth InsurancePayroll 2026

What it does

Armenian payroll withholdings on Odoo Community, with the 2026 rules: 20% income tax, the funded pension social payment, the new 1,000 AMD stamp duty and the universal health insurance premium. A salary of 550,000 AMD gives 110,000 income tax, 30,000 social payment, 1,000 stamp duty and 10,800 premium, the same as the State Revenue Committee's own example.

5% up to 500,000 AMD, 10% less 25,000 above, capped at 15 x minimum wage.

Decided by date of birth (1974 or later), or set as member, voluntary or none.

1,000 AMD a month, 15,000 AMD above 1,000,000 AMD of salary.

From the month after a salary of 200,001 AMD, kept for 12 months.

Features

Not includedincome tax refunds for mortgage interest and tuition, civil contract payments, and the monthly tax return upload. Nothing is sent to the State Revenue Committee.
