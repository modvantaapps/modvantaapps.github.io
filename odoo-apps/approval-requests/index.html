Approval Requests

Approvals app for Odoo Community: approval requests of any kind, approval types (approval category) with required, optional or hidden fields, multi level approval with minimum approvers, approver order and required approvers, purchase request approval to RFQ, expense approval, time off approval, payment approval and borrow items. Approval delegation for out of office, approval escalation, portal approval requests, request calendar, approval dashboard, approval management and approval workflow reports.

What it does

Purchases, business trips, borrowed equipment, expenses, payments, time off or anything else that needs a yes: define an approval type for it, choose its fields and its approvers, and let people raise requests from the backend or the portal.

Requests go to the right approvers, in order or all at once, until the minimum number of approvals is reached and every required approver has agreed. One refusal ends the request. Approvers who are away delegate, and requests nobody answers are escalated.

When a request is approved it can create the follow-up document for you: a purchase order for each vendor, an expense, a time off request, an allocation or a draft vendor payment.
