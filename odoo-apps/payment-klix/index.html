Klix Payment Gateway

Take Klix by Citadele payments in Odoo 19: cards, bank payments from the Baltics and Klix Pay Later.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Adds Klix as a payment provider. Odoo creates a purchase, the customer pays on the Klix checkout, and the RSA-signed callback or a status read confirms the order. Saved cards, hold and capture, and refunds work from Odoo.

One Klix option with every method of your agreement, or separate Card, Bank payment and Pay Later options

RSA-SHA256 check on every callback; Odoo reads the public key from Klix by itself

Hold and capture for cards, a lower capture releases the rest, and release of a hold

Features

Klix checkoutcard and bank data never touch Odoo
A return to your site is never trustedOdoo reads the purchase from Klix
Saved cardsthe first payment saves the card, later payments are charged from Odoo
