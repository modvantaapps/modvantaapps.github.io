Odoo Amazon integration

Amazon Connector

Run your Amazon Seller Central business from Odoo - orders, listings, FBA inbound and stock, removals, reimbursements, VAT invoices and settlements

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Amazon orders arrive as Odoo sales orders, listings are linked to products with stock and price sync, FBA stock and Multi-Channel Fulfilment are handled, FBA inbound shipments are planned from Odoo, removal orders and reimbursements come back into stock and accounting, and settlement fees are mapped to your accounts - through the Selling Partner API with a demo mode to try it first.

The connector links one or more Amazon seller accounts to Odoo through the Selling Partner API (Login with Amazon). Merchant-fulfilled (FBM) and FBA orders are imported as sales orders with the customer, shipping service and an Amazon tab on the order; they can be confirmed and invoiced automatically. Listings are linked to Odoo products by internal reference, with stock and price sent back to Amazon and a Buy Box check.

For FBA, the connector reads the stock held at Amazon into an FBA warehouse, requests the FBA inventory, returns and orders reports, and can send any Odoo order to Amazon as a Multi-Channel Fulfilment order. Returns from FBA and merchant-fulfilled reports are listed with reason and disposition, ready for a credit note.

Removal orders are created in Odoo or imported from Seller Central; returned units get a receipt in your warehouse and disposed units are scrapped from the FBA warehouse. Reimbursements are booked to your reimbursement account, lost, damaged and found units of the inventory ledger become stock moves (with a reason mapping), and estimated FBA fees per SKU and monthly storage fees are imported. In Europe, PAN-EU, EFN, MCI and CEP decide which FBA warehouse holds the stock of each country, FBA and FBM orders can follow their own workflow per marketplace, and the VAT Calculation Service (VCS) reports are matched with your Odoo invoices, whose PDF is uploaded to Amazon.

Who it is for

  • Merchant-fulfilled (FBM) sellers shipping from their own warehouse. Orders are imported every 15 minutes per marketplace. Validating the delivery queues a shipment confirmation to Amazon with carrier and tracking, and stock is sent every hour with a buffer and a cap.
  • FBA sellers who want Amazon stock visible in Odoo. FBA inventory is read every 2 hours into an FBA warehouse, and FBA orders come in already shipped with the delivery validated. FBA Inbound Plans, Removal Orders, Inventory Adjustments and Stock per Country are all under the FBA menu.
  • Sellers on several Amazon marketplaces. One seller account covers a region (North America, Europe or Far East) and its marketplaces. Each marketplace on the Marketplaces tab has its own currency, pricelist, warehouse and fiscal position, and you choose whether it gets stock and prices.
  • Accountants booking Amazon fees and VAT. Financial transactions are imported daily and grouped per settlement, with Charge Mapping deciding the account for each fee. Reimbursements, FBA storage fees and VAT Invoices (VCS) each have their own screen under Finances.

Features

Sending stock to Amazon is planned in Odooan FBA inbound plan (from scratch or from a transfer) gets its packing options, your boxes, the placement options with their fees and the carrier - the Amazon partnered carrier, or your own carrier with a delivery window. Box labels and FNSKU item labels are downloaded, tracking numbers are sent, and the units Amazon receives are booked into the FBA warehouse; a missing unit becomes a receipt discrepancy with an activity. Inbound plans made in Seller Central are imported too.

Setting it up

  1. In Seller Central register as a private developer, add an SP-API app client and authorise it. You need the LWA client identifier, the client secret, the refresh token and your Merchant Token.
  2. In Odoo go to Amazon > Configuration > Seller Accounts and create an account. Pick the Region and Environment, then fill in Seller ID (Merchant Token), LWA Client Identifier, LWA Client Secret and Refresh Token.
  3. Press Connect / Test Connection. Start with the Sandbox environment or Read only if you want to look before Odoo sends any change.
  4. On the Marketplaces tab enable the marketplaces you sell on and give each one a pricelist and a warehouse.
  5. On the Orders tab choose Import FBM Orders and Import FBA Orders, how SKUs match products (Internal Reference or Barcode), the tax mode and the order line products.
  6. On the FBA & Multi-Channel tab press Create FBA Warehouse, then press Import Listings and Read FBA Stock to load your catalogue and Amazon stock.

Amazon Connector: questions

Does this Odoo Amazon connector handle FBA?

Yes. FBA orders are imported as shipped, FBA stock is read into its own warehouse, and you can create FBA inbound plans with box labels and FNSKU labels. Removal orders, reimbursements, inventory adjustments and storage fees come in from Amazon reports.

Can Amazon ship my other sales channels' orders?

Yes, through Multi-Channel Fulfilment. On a sales order press Fulfil with Amazon (MCF) to send it to Amazon, and an hourly job refreshes its status and tracking.

Is it for Odoo Community or Enterprise?

Both. It depends only on Sales, Inventory, Delivery and Invoicing, which ship with Odoo Community 19, and needs no extra Python library.

How does this Odoo Amazon integration get buyer names and addresses?

Only if your developer app has the restricted Direct-to-Consumer Shipping role. Without it, orders go to a generic Amazon customer you choose. Buyer data is removed after the retention period you set.

Can I test it without touching my live account?

Yes. Demo mode never calls Amazon and answers with sample data shaped like the official SP-API models, Sandbox uses Amazon's static test responses, and Read only reads from Amazon but never sends a change.

What doesn't it do?

It doesn't cover Vendor Central, Amazon Warehousing and Distribution or pallet inbound shipments, and it doesn't buy Amazon shipping labels. Orders can't be cancelled from Odoo: cancel in Seller Central and Odoo follows. Real-time notifications need a relay that signs each request, otherwise the 15-minute import does the work.
