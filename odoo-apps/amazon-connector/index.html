Amazon Connector

Run your Amazon Seller Central business from Odoo - orders, listings, FBA inbound and stock, removals, reimbursements, VAT invoices and settlements

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Amazon orders arrive as Odoo sales orders, listings are linked to products with stock and price sync, FBA stock and Multi-Channel Fulfilment are handled, FBA inbound shipments are planned from Odoo, removal orders and reimbursements come back into stock and accounting, and settlement fees are mapped to your accounts - through the Selling Partner API with a demo mode to try it first.

The connector links one or more Amazon seller accounts to Odoo through the Selling Partner API (Login with Amazon). Merchant-fulfilled (FBM) and FBA orders are imported as sales orders with the customer, shipping service and an Amazon tab on the order; they can be confirmed and invoiced automatically. Listings are linked to Odoo products by internal reference, with stock and price sent back to Amazon and a Buy Box check.

For FBA, the connector reads the stock held at Amazon into an FBA warehouse, requests the FBA inventory, returns and orders reports, and can send any Odoo order to Amazon as a Multi-Channel Fulfilment order. Returns from FBA and merchant-fulfilled reports are listed with reason and disposition, ready for a credit note.

Removal orders are created in Odoo or imported from Seller Central; returned units get a receipt in your warehouse and disposed units are scrapped from the FBA warehouse. Reimbursements are booked to your reimbursement account, lost, damaged and found units of the inventory ledger become stock moves (with a reason mapping), and estimated FBA fees per SKU and monthly storage fees are imported. In Europe, PAN-EU, EFN, MCI and CEP decide which FBA warehouse holds the stock of each country, FBA and FBM orders can follow their own workflow per marketplace, and the VAT Calculation Service (VCS) reports are matched with your Odoo invoices, whose PDF is uploaded to Amazon.

Features

Sending stock to Amazon is planned in Odooan FBA inbound plan (from scratch or from a transfer) gets its packing options, your boxes, the placement options with their fees and the carrier - the Amazon partnered carrier, or your own carrier with a delivery window. Box labels and FNSKU item labels are downloaded, tracking numbers are sent, and the units Amazon receives are booked into the FBA warehouse; a missing unit becomes a receipt discrepancy with an activity. Inbound plans made in Seller Central are imported too.
