Microfinance Management for MFI and SACCO

Run a microfinance institution, SACCO or lending group on Odoo: borrower groups and centres, loan officers, group and individual loans with flat or declining interest, application, approval and disbursement with accounting, centre collection sheets, repayments, penalties, early settlement, rescheduling and write-off, savings accounts, guarantors, collateral and KYC, portfolio at risk (PAR 30, 60, 90) and a borrower portal. One module on Odoo's own Invoicing.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Microfinance Management for Odoo 19MicrofinanceMFISACCOMicrocreditGroup LendingGroup LoanJoint Liability Group

What it does

Microfinance happens at the weekly centre meeting, not at a bank counter. Schedules fall on the meeting day, the loan officer prints the collection sheet before leaving the office, marks who came and what each member paid and saved, and posts the whole meeting when back. Repayments, savings deposits and journal entries are created in one click, and the portfolio at risk is up to date the same day.

Everything a microfinance institution needs, from the first group meeting to the PAR report.

Branches with their own cash journal, centres with a meeting day, time, place and loan officer, and borrower groups (joint liability groups) with a leader, secretary, minimum and maximum size.

Client code, ID number, date of birth, household, business and income, KYC documents with expiry and one-click verification, loan cycle and an automatic credit score and grade.

Group or individual lending, flat rate or declining balance (equal installments or equal principal), daily, weekly, fortnightly or monthly repayment, rate per year or per month, grace periods and rounded installments.

Features

One sheet per centre meetingevery member with arrears, amount due and savings due. Print it, mark attendance, enter what was paid and saved, then post all repayments and deposits in one click.
