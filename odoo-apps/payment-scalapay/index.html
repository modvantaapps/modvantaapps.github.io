Scalapay Payment Provider

Let customers pay in 3, in 4 or later with Scalapay, and capture, void or refund from Odoo.

What it does

Adds Scalapay as a payment provider for shops selling in euro to customers in Italy, France, Spain, Germany, Portugal, Belgium, the Netherlands, Austria, Finland and the French overseas departments. When the customer picks a Scalapay plan on the website, a portal invoice or a payment link, Odoo creates the Scalapay order with the customer, the billing and shipping address and, when they add up, the order lines, then sends the customer to the Scalapay page. When the customer comes back, or when Scalapay sends its notification, Odoo reads the order from Scalapay and captures it. If you capture by hand, Odoo asks Scalapay to keep the authorization for up to 5 days instead, so you can capture what you ship and void the rest.

Features

Four plansPay in 3, Pay in 4, Pay Later and Pay in Full as separate payment methods; turn on the ones your contract has
Contract limitsset your minimum and maximum order total: Scalapay is hidden at checkout outside that range
Checkout widgetthe Scalapay widget under the payment method shows the plans and costs of your contract for the amount, in English, Italian, French, Spanish, German or Portuguese
Order detailscustomer, phone, billing and shipping address, order lines with SKU and category, discounts and tax, sent with each order
Immediate capturethe order is captured as soon as the customer authorizes it
Delayed capturewith "Capture Amount Manually", the authorization is kept up to 5 days; capture all or part of it from Odoo and void the rest
Refundsfull or partial, from the payment, with your refund reference
Signed webhookseach notification is checked against x-scalapay-hmac-v1, then the order is read again from Scalapay before anything changes
Missed returnsa check every few minutes reads orders whose customer never came back
Sandbox and productionTest mode uses the Scalapay sandbox, Enabled uses production
API logevery call and notification with its status; customer names, phones and addresses masked
