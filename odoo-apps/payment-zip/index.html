Zip Payment Provider

Offer Zip Pay in 4 at checkout. Capture now or when you ship, and refund from Odoo.

What it does

Adds Zip (formerly Quadpay) as a payment provider for US merchants, on the Zip North America Gateway API. When a customer picks Zip on your website, a portal invoice or a payment link, Odoo creates the Zip order with the amount, the customer and, when they match, the order lines, then sends the customer to the Zip checkout. Zip reports the result to Odoo with a signed callback, so a customer who closes the tab or approves on another device is still recorded. Pay-on-ship merchants authorize at checkout and capture from Odoo when they deliver, in one go or in parts, and void what is left. Refunds, full or partial, are sent from the payment.

Features

Zip at checkoutshown with your other payment methods on the website, the customer portal and payment links, for amounts in US dollars
Order detailscustomer name, e-mail, phone, billing and shipping address, and the order lines, tax and shipping when they add up to the amount
Immediate capturethe payment is done as soon as Zip confirms it
Authorize and capturewith "Capture Amount Manually", capture the full amount or part of it from Odoo, and void the rest
Refundsfull or partial, from the payment in Odoo
Signed callbacksevery Zip callback is checked against its X-QP-Signature (HMAC-SHA256 with your secret key) and the merchant ID before anything changes
Amount checkthe amount and currency Zip reports must match the transaction
Customer returncancelled checkouts are marked cancelled, declined ones as errors; the order status is asked from Zip, never read from the browser
Sandbox and productionTest mode uses the Zip sandbox, Enabled uses production
API logeach call to Zip and each callback with its HTTP status, personal data masked, cleaned after 90 days
