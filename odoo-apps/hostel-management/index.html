Hostel Management

Run a student hostel, boys or girls hostel, PG or boarding house on Odoo: rooms and beds with bed-level occupancy, online admission with live vacancy, bed allocation without double booking, security deposits held on a liability account and refunded at checkout, monthly rent and mess invoices with late fees, residents with guardians, gate passes scanned at the gate, roll call with guardian alerts, visitors, complaints and a resident portal. One module, built on Odoo's own Website, Portal and Invoicing.

What it does

Allocations from an application, the bed board, a move or a swap are all checked against every reserved and current stay on that bed. The leaving day can be the next joining day; anything that overlaps is refused, and so is a resident in a room their gender policy does not allow.

Everything a hostel or PG needs, from the application to the deposit refund.

Several hostels with their own gender policy (boys, girls or co-ed), warden, curfew and visiting hours. Blocks and floors, rooms with a room type and beds named 101-A, 101-B... Generate a whole building of floors, rooms and beds in one click.

Every bed is vacant, reserved, occupied or under maintenance. The bed board shows each room with its beds and residents; the dashboard shows beds, occupied, vacant and occupancy for every hostel.

A public Hostels page with live vacancy per room type and the monthly rent, and an application form with the guardian's details. Applications land in Odoo with a reference, an email receipt and a to-do for the warden; when no bed matches, the office sees it at once and can put the application on the waiting list.
