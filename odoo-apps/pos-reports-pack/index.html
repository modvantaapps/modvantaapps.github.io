POS Reports

POS Z report and X report: Z-report session closing report with a running Z number, X report of the open session, printed from the till or as PDF, emailed at closing. All POS reports from the POS screen and the backend: pos sales report, pos session report, pos payment report, pos product report, top selling product, hourly sales, pos profit, pos audit, pos excel report and 80 mm receipt; pos order list with pos reorder and pos reprint; cancel pos order with stock and accounting reversal and approval; sales and pos profitability report and product profitability report by product, category, customer, salesperson, point of sale and period, with delivered and invoiced quantities and the stock valuation cost

What it does

Every POS report, order correction and profit figure in one module

Print the reports a shop manager asks for every day - sales summary, products, categories, payment methods, taxes, sessions, session audit, stock at the shop, top products, top customers, top categories, hourly and daily sales, profit and margin, cashier detail, point of sale comparison and the cancellation history - straight from the till on the receipt printer, or from the backend as A4 PDF, 80 mm PDF or Excel.

The order list of the till gets two buttons on past orders: Re-order starts a new order with the same products, quantities and customer, and Void / Reverse cancels a paid order with a reason. An order of the open session is voided (stock returned, left out of the session accounting); any other paid, posted or invoiced order is reversed with a refund order (stock returned, payments reversed, credit note when it was invoiced). Cancellations can wait for a manager's approval.

A Sales and POS profitability analysis puts sales order lines and POS order lines side by side with cost, profit, margin, sold and returned quantity and return rate, as pivot, graph or list, and as a printable PDF or Excel report grouped by product, category, day, week, month, year or source.

Who it is for

  • Shops that close the till every evening. The manager prints an X report at lunch to see how the open session is doing, then gets a numbered Z report at closing with expected cash, counted cash and the difference. Each point of sale keeps its own Z counter.
  • Owners who aren't at the shop at closing time. Turn on the Z report email and the PDF is sent to the people you pick as soon as the session closes. If the mail fails, the session still closes.
  • Stores where cashiers make mistakes on paid orders. A wrong sale can be voided from the till if it belongs to the open session, or reversed with a refund order, stock return and credit note if it's older or invoiced. You can require a reason and a manager's approval first.
  • Businesses selling both through Sales and the POS. The profitability analysis puts sales order lines and POS order lines in one pivot, graph or list with cost, profit, margin and returns, so month-end figures don't have to be stitched together in a spreadsheet.

Features

Closing the till ends with a POS Z reportone numbered session closing report with the sales, refunds, discounts, taxes, payment methods and the cash count, printed on the receipt printer, kept as a PDF on the session and emailed to the owner if you want. During the day the X report shows the same figures for the session that is still open.

Setting it up

  1. Go to Point of Sale > Configuration > Settings, select the point of sale and scroll to the Reports, Order List & Cancellations section.
  2. Under POS Reports in the Till, decide whether the reports menu in the till is open to all cashiers or Only for Point of Sale administrators.
  3. Under Order List, choose Orders shown (All sessions, Current session, Today, Last N sessions or Last N days), set N if needed, and tick the Re-order button.
  4. Under Cancel Orders from the Till, tick Reason required and Manager approval for non-administrators, then pick the Approvers.
  5. Under X Report and Z Report, choose which extra sections to print (Top products, Product categories, Cashiers, List of refunds, Pricelists, Customers), and tick Email the Z report when the session closes with the Send to contacts.
  6. In the backend, use Point of Sale > Reporting > POS Reports (PDF / Excel) for the report wizard and Sales and POS Profitability for the analysis. Cancellation requests are under Point of Sale > Orders > Cancellations.

POS Reports: questions

Which Odoo apps does it need?

Point of Sale, plus Sales with stock (sale_stock) and Sales margin (sale_margin), which it uses for the combined profitability report. All of these are in Community, so Enterprise isn't required.

What's the difference between voiding and reversing?

An order from the open session that hasn't been invoiced is voided: its stock is returned and it's left out of the session closing entry. Any other paid, posted or invoiced order is reversed with a refund order in the open session, using the same payment methods, with a stock return and a credit note if it had an invoice.

Can a cashier cancel an order without approval?

Only if approval is switched off. With Manager approval for non-administrators on, a cashier's cancellation becomes a request in the To Approve state and the approvers get an activity. The check runs on the server, not just in the till.

Which formats can the reports be printed in?

From the till they print on the receipt printer. From the backend you can download A4 PDF, 80 mm PDF or Excel. The X and Z reports also have buttons on the session form.

Where does the cost in the profitability report come from?

For sales orders it uses the line cost from sale_margin, and there's also a valuation cost column taken from stock valuation for goods actually delivered, net of returns. For POS lines it uses the cost Point of Sale recorded. Check with your accountant which cost basis suits your reporting.

Can I reprint an old Z report?

Yes. Pick any closed session in the till and print its Z report again, or print it as PDF from the session in the backend. The Z number stays the one it got when the session closed.
