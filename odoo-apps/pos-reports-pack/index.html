POS Reports

All POS reports from the POS screen and the backend: pos sales report, pos session report, pos payment report, pos product report, top selling product, hourly sales, pos profit, pos audit, pos excel report and 80 mm receipt; pos order list with pos reorder and pos reprint; cancel pos order with stock and accounting reversal and approval; sales and pos profitability report and product profitability report by product, category, customer, salesperson, point of sale and period, with delivered and invoiced quantities and the stock valuation cost

What it does

Every POS report, order correction and profit figure in one module

Print the reports a shop manager asks for every day - sales summary, products, categories, payment methods, taxes, sessions, session audit, stock at the shop, top products, top customers, top categories, hourly and daily sales, profit and margin, cashier detail, point of sale comparison and the cancellation history - straight from the till on the receipt printer, or from the backend as A4 PDF, 80 mm PDF or Excel.

The order list of the till gets two buttons on past orders: Re-order starts a new order with the same products, quantities and customer, and Void / Reverse cancels a paid order with a reason. An order of the open session is voided (stock returned, left out of the session accounting); any other paid, posted or invoiced order is reversed with a refund order (stock returned, payments reversed, credit note when it was invoiced). Cancellations can wait for a manager's approval.

A Sales and POS profitability analysis puts sales order lines and POS order lines side by side with cost, profit, margin, sold and returned quantity and return rate, as pivot, graph or list, and as a printable PDF or Excel report grouped by product, category, day, week, month, year or source.
