What it does

Razorpay at your Odoo checkout, with a setup note and a cleaner payment label

This add-on works on top of Odoo's own Razorpay provider. It adds a setup note on the provider form explaining which keys and webhook to configure, labels the option simply "Razorpay" on the checkout, and after the Razorpay popup returns, verifies the payment signature on the server and sends the shopper to Odoo's payment status page.

You keep Odoo's Razorpay integration and its settings; this module adds the guidance and the checkout handling around it.
