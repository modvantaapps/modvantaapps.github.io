All In One Cancel

Cancel an order and everything that came from it, in one step

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Cancel sale orders with their deliveries, invoices and payments, purchase orders with their receipts and bills, invoices and transfers - one at a time from the form or in bulk from the list, with the choice to only cancel, reset to draft, or cancel and delete.

Cancelling a confirmed sale in standard Odoo takes several screens: cancel the payment, reset and cancel the invoice, cancel the delivery, then cancel the order. With many documents it is slow and easy to leave something half cancelled.

This module adds a Cancel button to sale orders, purchase orders, invoices and transfers, and a Cancel action in their list views. A wizard asks how far to go: Cancel Only, Cancel and Reset to Draft, or Cancel and Delete. For sale orders you can also cancel the deliveries and the invoices with their payments; for purchase orders the receipts and the bills with their payments.

Features

In bulk, every document is handled on its ownone that cannot be cancelled does not undo the others, and the result lists what worked and what did not, with the reason.
