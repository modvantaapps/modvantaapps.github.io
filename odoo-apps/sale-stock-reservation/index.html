Sale Stock Reservation

Sale Stock Reservation - Reserve Stock from Quotation with Expiry

What it does

Your customer says yes next week - will the stock still be there? Reserve it from the quotation, before the order is confirmed. The goods are held for that customer only, released automatically when the reservation expires, and handed over to the delivery order when the customer confirms. Built on standard Odoo stock reservations, lots and serial numbers.

A Reserve Stock button on every quotation opens a wizard with the lines not reserved yet: quantity, lot or serial number, the location to reserve from, the expiry date and who to notify. Partial or full quantities, any line.

Reserved stock is held on the quants exactly like a delivery reservation: the free quantity drops at once and no other delivery, transfer or quotation can take it. Proven by tests.

The salesperson gets an activity a set number of hours before the stock is released - time to call the customer or extend the reservation in one click.

Reserve one lot or specific serial numbers (the wizard shows the free quantity per lot), or let Odoo pick by its removal strategy and see which lots were held.

Features

Each reservation has an expirya default number of days or the quotation expiration date. A scheduled job releases expired reservations, logs it on the quotation and tells the salesperson.
