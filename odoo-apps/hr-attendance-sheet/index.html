HR Attendance Sheet and Policies

One attendance sheet per employee and period: planned hours from the working schedule against real check-ins, with late in, early out, overtime, missing hours and absences, priced by your attendance policy and sent to payroll.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Odoo records attendances and extra hours, but it does not tell you, for a month, how late each employee came, how often they left early, which working days had no attendance at all, or how much that should cost in payroll. This app builds that sheet. It reads the working schedule (including split shifts), public holidays, validated time off and the attendances, day by day, and applies your attendance policy: grace minutes, late in and early out steps by minutes and by number of occurrences, absence steps by number of absences, and a rate for missing hours. Overtime comes from the extra hours already approved in the Attendances app, with their ruleset rates. Sheets are generated for a whole department in one click, corrected day by day with a logged reason, approved, and exported to payslip inputs or to Excel.
