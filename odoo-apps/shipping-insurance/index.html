Shipping Insurance at Checkout

Offer shipping insurance with one checkbox at checkout

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Shoppers can insure their shipment on the payment step for a percentage of the goods value, with a minimum and maximum fee. The fee shows in the order summary, and the sales order and delivery order are marked as insured with the insured value.

Customers worry about parcels lost or damaged in transit, and many shops already pay for cover. Odoo eCommerce has no way to let the shopper choose and pay for that protection.

This module adds an Insure my shipment checkbox above the payment methods, with the fee already worked out. Ticking it adds a Shipping insurance line to the order and a row to the order summary; unticking removes it. The fee is a percentage of the products in the cart - not delivery or other fees - with a minimum and a maximum.

If the cart changes, the fee is recalculated before payment. On the confirmed sales order and on its delivery order you see Shipping Insured and the Insured Value, so the warehouse knows which parcels to ship with cover. A filter lists the insured orders.
