Colissimo Shipping Integration

Ship with Colissimo from Odoo - labels, Point Retrait, deposit slips, returns and tracking

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A Colissimo connector for Odoo 19: Colissimo prices at the checkout with a pick-up point map, labels on validation of the delivery order, the daily deposit slip, return labels and letter box returns, and the Colissimo tracking history on every parcel.

The module adds Colissimo (La Poste) as a delivery method provider in Odoo and uses the Colissimo web services for labels, pick-up points (Point Retrait), deposit slips and tracking.

Each delivery method is one Colissimo product - Domicile with or without signature, Point Retrait and the others. Prices come from a Colissimo rate table by zone and weight, or a fixed price. Validating the delivery order books the parcel and attaches the label to the transfer; one deposit slip (bordereau) groups the day's parcels for the hand-over.

Features

Every Colissimo parcel in one list
Account, label, price and options on the delivery method
Choose a Point Retrait on the delivery order
Colissimo tab on the delivery order
Parcel record with Colissimo tracking
Deposit slip for the hand-over
Return from the customer's letter box
Colissimo prices at the website checkout
Pick-up point map for the customer
