SMS Gateway Hub

Send Odoo SMS in India through SMSGatewayHub, with the right DLT template on every message.

What it does

In India every business SMS needs a DLT template ID, the Entity ID of your company and a registered sender ID, or the operator drops it. This module makes SMSGatewayHub the SMS provider of Odoo and puts the DLT part where it belongs. You enter your DLT templates once with the text exactly as registered (variables written {#var#}). Each Odoo SMS template can point to its DLT template; when an SMS has no template, the module compares the text with your registered texts and picks the one that matches. If nothing matches, the SMS is stopped in Odoo with a clear error instead of being rejected later. The channel (transactional, promotional, OTP) and sender ID come from the DLT template, numbers outside India go on the INT channel, and Hindi or other Unicode text is detected on its own.

Features

Odoo SMS through SMSGatewayHubchatter SMS, SMS templates, mass SMS, automation rules and SMS marketing, chosen per company
DLT templatestemplate ID, registered text, channel and an optional sender ID per template
DLT on Odoo SMS templatespick the DLT template on each Odoo SMS template; its ID goes with every SMS sent from it
Automatic matchingSMS without a template are matched against the registered texts ({#var#} up to 30 characters), with a fallback template if you want one
Check a textpaste any SMS text on a DLT template to see if it matches before you send
Channelstransactional, promotional and OTP; numbers outside India go on the INT channel without DLT
UnicodeHindi and other regional scripts sent with DCS 8 automatically; optional flash SMS
Bulk sendingSMS with the same channel and sender go out in one API call, each with its own message ID
Delivery reportsfetched every 10 minutes: delivered, failed, NDNC (number on the DND list)
Creditscredit count checked every 6 hours, with a low credit e-mail sent once
SMS logevery SMS with DLT template, channel, job ID and status, by day or by DLT template
Who may sendoptionally only users in the "SMSGatewayHub: Send SMS" group can use the SMS composer
