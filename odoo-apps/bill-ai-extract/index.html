AI Bill Extraction

Read a supplier invoice into a draft bill - and check it before anything is written

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Attach the supplier's PDF to a draft vendor bill and click Read the attachment with AI. A review screen shows the supplier, invoice number, dates, totals and lines it read, the text on the document each value came from, and whether the lines add up. Nothing is written until you apply, and nothing is ever posted.

Typing vendor bills from PDFs is slow and error-prone, and the bill digitisation in Odoo Enterprise is not available on Community. This module sends the attached PDF to Claude, Anthropic's AI model, with your own Anthropic API key.

Apply to the bill writes the header - supplier, reference and dates - to the draft bill. The lines are shown for keying in, because the account and tax of each line are your decision. The bill stays in draft.

Features

The result opens as a review screenwhat was read from the document next to what the bill holds now, the totals as printed, the lines, and for each field the exact text on the document it came from. Lines are checked against the subtotal and a gap is shown in red with the AI's own note.
