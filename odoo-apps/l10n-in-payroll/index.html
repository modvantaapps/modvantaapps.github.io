India Payroll

Indian payroll statutory compliance on every payslip - PF, ESI, PT, LWF and TDS, with the returns and Form 130 built from the same numbers

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Each payslip of an Indian company gets an India Statutory section: earned basic and DA, EPF, EPS and EDLI wages against the 15,000 PF wage ceiling, the employee PF and the employer split into EPF (A/c 1), EPS (A/c 10), EDLI (A/c 21) and PF admin (A/c 2), ESI coverage and contributions, the professional tax of the employee's state, labour welfare fund in the months your state collects it, TDS for the month and the cost to company.

Income tax is projected for the financial year under the new regime (115BAC) or the old regime, using tax slabs, surcharge, the Section 87A rebate, standard deduction and the 4% health and education cess stored per financial year. Old-regime employees can file a tax declaration with rent for HRA, the landlord PAN, metro city, previous employer income and TDS, and investments under 80C, 80D and 80CCD(1B), shown with their new Income-tax Act 2025 section numbers.

At month end the Statutory Returns wizard produces the EPFO ECR text file, the ESIC monthly contribution sheet, the PF register, the professional tax register and the TDS / Form 24Q salary register for a payroll period. At year end, Generate Form 16 / 130 builds Part B for every employee from the approved payslips, and prints it.

Features

India statutory figures on the payslip
Statutory details on the employee
