Easyship Shipping

Ship with every courier of your Easyship account straight from Odoo. Customers see the best Easyship rate at the checkout, with import taxes and duties handled the way you choose, and validating the delivery creates the Easyship shipment, buys the label and attaches it with the commercial invoice. Tracking arrives by webhook, and pickups, returns and cancellations are handled from Odoo.

What it does

Easyship API 2024-09Many couriersCheapest / fastest / best valueLabelsTaxes and dutiesDDP or DDUInsurancePickupsWebhooksReturnsAddress validation

The module adds an "Easyship" provider to Odoo delivery methods. A method picks the cheapest, the fastest or the best value courier across your Easyship account, only among the couriers you allow, or always one courier service. International shipments are sent DDU (the buyer pays duties) or DDP (you pay, and the duties can be part of the delivery price). When a delivery is validated the shipment is created and the label bought in one call; the label, commercial invoice and packing slip are attached to the transfer.
