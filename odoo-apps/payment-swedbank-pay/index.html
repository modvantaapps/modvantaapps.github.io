Swedbank Pay Payment Gateway

Take cards, Swish, Vipps, MobilePay and Trustly in Odoo through your Swedbank Pay agreement.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Swedbank PayOnline PaymentsPayment Order v3.1SwishVippsMobilePayTrustlyCards

What it does

Adds Swedbank Pay as a payment provider for Nordic and Baltic shops. When the customer pays an order, a portal invoice or a payment link, Odoo creates a Swedbank Pay payment order and sends the customer to the Swedbank Pay payment menu, where they pay by card, Swish, Vipps, MobilePay or Trustly, depending on your agreement. When they come back, and whenever Swedbank Pay sends a callback, Odoo reads the payment order from the Swedbank Pay API and records the result. Captures, cancellations and reversals (refunds) are made from the payment in Odoo.

Features

Payment menuredirect to the Swedbank Pay page with the payment order Odoo creates (v3.1), in the customer's language or the one you pick
Card capturecards and Vipps are authorized first: Odoo captures them at once, or you capture all or part later and cancel the rest
Swish and Trustlypaid in one step, the order is confirmed when Swedbank Pay reports it paid
Reversalsrefund in full or in part, several times, while captured money is left on the payment order
Callbacksaccepted only on a secret address per provider; Odoo then reads the payment order back from the API, as Swedbank Pay asks
Instrument modeopen the page with only the method picked in Odoo, for agreements that include it
VAT amountsent from the taxes of the linked invoice or order
Status checksa button on the payment and a check every 15 minutes for open payment orders
Payments reportgraph and pivot of Swedbank Pay payments by day and state
API logevery call and callback with its result; token and card details masked
