POS Quantity Rules

POS minimum quantity, multiple quantity and maximum quantity per product, a minimum order amount, and a price per lot - enforced at the till and checked again by the server.

What it does

POS minimum quantity per product (the product is added at its minimum), POS multiple quantity (sold in multiples of 6, 12...), POS maximum quantity per order, POS quantity limit per product, minimum order amount before payment, POS lot pricing (price per lot, batch price) for Odoo Point of Sale, warn or block, checked by the server, works offline.

Features

Minimum quantitythe product is added at its minimum (6 cans), and the order cannot go below it.
Sold in multipleseach tap adds one more multiple (6, 12, 18...) and the total must be a multiple.
Maximum quantitya limit per order, for example 2 bottles of hand sanitizer per customer.
Correct in one clickbefore payment the till lists what breaks a rule and offers to correct the quantities (13 cans -> 18).
Warn or blockWarn lets the cashier pay anyway; Block refuses the payment, and the server refuses such orders too.
Minimum order amountthe payment screen only opens from the total you set per point of sale (taxes included).
Price per lota lot with a POS Lot Price sets the price of the line as soon as it is chosen or scanned - the batch price printed on the pack.
Refunds stay freereturns are never blocked by the rules.
Offlinerules and lot prices are loaded with the session; the till checks them without a server call.
