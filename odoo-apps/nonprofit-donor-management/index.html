Nonprofit Donor Management

Run the donor side of a charity, NGO, church or foundation in Odoo: gifts booked to the right fund, fundraising campaigns with live progress, pledges and monthly giving, an online donation page, tax receipts that stay as issued (UK, France, United States, generic), UK Gift Aid claims written into the HMRC schedule, a donor portal, volunteers and grants.

Nonprofit Donor Management for Odoo 19

What it does

One app for donors, money and receipts, on Odoo Community or Enterprise.

Cash, cheque, card, bank, direct debit or online. Confirming a gift posts cash or bank against the fund's income account with its analytic account. Online, invoice and payment gifts are not booked twice.

Unrestricted, restricted (purpose or time) and endowment funds. Campaigns with a goal, amount raised, donors, open pledges and a goal-reached ribbon.

Pledges split into instalments with reminders; recurring gifts recorded on each due date, confirmed or left in draft to check against the bank. Batch entry checked against the deposit slip.

Who it is for

  • UK charities that claim Gift Aid. Each confirmed gift is tested for a declaration in force, an individual donor, money only, pounds sterling, the relevant value limit and the four-year limit. A Gift Aid claim then collects the gifts that pass and writes them into the empty HMRC schedule (.ods) you uploaded from gov.uk, plus a CSV copy.
  • French associations issuing recus fiscaux. Receipts can follow the Cerfa 11580 layout, with the amount in words, the object of the organisation and its status (general interest, public utility, fondation, association cultuelle). Value the donor received is deducted from the eligible amount.
  • US nonprofits sending written acknowledgments. The United States format prints your EIN and the goods-or-services statement. An issued receipt is never edited. You void it with a reason and Odoo issues a replacement that names the old serial number.
  • Small teams running gifts, volunteers and grants in one place. Gifts are booked to unrestricted, restricted or endowment funds with their analytic account. The same app holds pledges with instalments, recurring gifts, volunteer shifts with a double-booking check, grants with reporting deadlines and auction lots.

Setting it up

  1. Open Donations > Configuration > Settings and pick Receipting (one receipt per gift, one per year, or none) and the Receipt Format, or leave it on From the company country.
  2. Fill in the Charity Registration Number, Receipt Signatory and Signatory Title, and set the Default Donation Journal. For goods, set the Gifts in Kind Account, otherwise those gifts are recorded but not posted.
  3. UK only: tick UK Gift Aid, enter the HMRC Charities Reference and upload the empty Gift Aid schedule spreadsheet.
  4. Create funds under Donations > Configuration > Funds, then add a campaign under Donations > Campaigns, press Start and tick Show on the Website to list it on /donate.
  5. Record a gift under Donations > Giving > Gifts and press Confirm. Use Issue Receipt and Send Thank You on the gift, or Gift Batches when you key in a whole collection against its deposit slip.
  6. At year end run Donations > Receipts > Issue Annual Receipts. For Gift Aid open Gift Aid Claims, press Collect Eligible Gifts, then Prepare Schedule, and Mark Submitted to HMRC once you have uploaded it.

Nonprofit Donor Management: questions

Does it work on Odoo Community?

Yes. It is built for Odoo 19 and needs Invoicing (account), website payments, Portal, Discuss and analytic accounting, all of which ship with Community. It also installs on Enterprise.

Does it file the Gift Aid claim with HMRC for me?

No. It fills HMRC's schedule spreadsheet. You upload that file in Charities Online yourself and then press Mark Submitted to HMRC on the claim.

How are oral Gift Aid declarations handled?

You record the oral declaration and press Send Written Record. It only counts as effective once 30 days have passed since that record was sent without the donor cancelling.

Will online gifts be counted twice?

No. A payment from the /donate page is matched to the gift it prepared by reference, amount, currency and donor. Gifts made through Odoo's own donation form land on the default fund. An inbound payment also has a Record as Gift button, and it hides once the payment is linked.

How are donors put into segments?

A scheduled job sorts donors into new, regular, major and lapsed. Lapsed means no gift for 18 months. Major is lifetime giving of 1,000 or more by default, and new means a first gift in the last 12 months.

Are Canadian receipts included?

No. The formats are United Kingdom, France, United States and a generic receipt for every other country.
