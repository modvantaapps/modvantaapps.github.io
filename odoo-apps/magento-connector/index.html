Magento Connector

Magento Connector and Magento 2 Connector (Magento Odoo Connector, Magento Bridge, Adobe Commerce and Mage-OS) for Odoo Magento Integration and Magento 2 Integration: Magento Sync with Magento Order Sync, Magento Stock Sync per MSI source and Magento Product Sync of simple, configurable, bundle and grouped products, pricelist, special and tier prices, translations and SEO per store view, customers, partial invoices and shipments with tracking, credit memos, multi website and store view, Magento custom options on order lines and invoices, Magento category export, setup wizard, Magento profit report, Magento 2.4 and Adobe Commerce 2.4

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The Magento Odoo Connector links a Magento 2 or Adobe Commerce store to Odoo. Products with their configurable variants and bundles, customers, orders and credit memos are imported into Odoo; stock, prices, translations and SEO fields are sent back to Magento.

Each website and store view maps to an Odoo language, currency and warehouse, and Magento inventory sources (MSI) map to Odoo locations. When the Odoo delivery is done, the shipment and tracking number go to Magento.

A setup wizard walks through connection, store views, sources, taxes, payments and order states, and a demo store lets you try it all before you connect your own.
