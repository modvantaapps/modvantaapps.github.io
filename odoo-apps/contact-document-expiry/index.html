Contact Document Expiry & Compliance

Track expiry dates for contact compliance documents (licenses, certificates, IDs) with expiring-soon and expired alerts and a document register. Document expiry tracker, compliance documents, license renewal alert.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Trade licences, tax certificates, contracts and NDAs all expire. When the paper sits in a shared drive, nobody notices until an auditor or a customer asks for a valid copy.

This module adds a document register to contacts. Each document has a type, number, issuing authority, issue and expiry date and an attached file. Its status is worked out from the expiry date: Valid, Expiring Soon (within 30 days) or Expired. Contacts show a yellow alert when a document is expiring soon and a red warning when one has expired, and a daily job puts a To Do on the contact's salesperson for documents about to expire.
