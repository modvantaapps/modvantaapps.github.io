Petty Cash Management, Recurring Invoice and Interest on Overdue Invoices

Three small-money jobs every finance team does by hand - the cash box, the invoices that repeat every month and the late-payment interest - done inside Odoo with real journal entries.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Petty Cash ManagementRecurring Invoice and Recurring BillInterest on Overdue Invoices

What it does

Petty Cash Management with petty cash funds per custodian, petty cash request, petty cash voucher approval, petty cash expense receipts and petty cash replenishment; Recurring Invoice and recurring bill templates generated by schedule; Interest on Overdue Invoices with late payment interest, late fee and overdue interest debit note

Features

Petty casha fund per cash box and custodian. Staff request cash on a voucher, an approver signs it off, the cashier pays it, and the voucher is settled with the receipts. Every step that moves money posts a journal entry, and a replenishment request tops the box up again.
Recurring invoices and billsa template with lines and a schedule (every N days, weeks, months or years). A scheduled action creates each invoice or vendor bill on time, can post it, email it to the customer and tell your team.
Interest on overdue invoicesrules with a rate or a flat fee, grace days, simple or compound interest, a one-time penalty and minimum / maximum caps. Preview the charge on the invoice, then create a draft interest invoice or debit note linked to it.
