Ukraine NBU QR Payment Code on Invoices

Ukraine NBU QR payment code on Odoo 19 invoices and sales orders: customers scan it with their banking app and the payment details fill in.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

The National Bank of Ukraine defines one payment QR code for credit transfers. This module builds that code, format version 002, from the bank account of the document and prints it on the invoice and the sales order. Everything is generated inside your Odoo.

Registers in the payment QR framework of Odoo, so an invoice in UAH on a Ukrainian IBAN picks it by itself

On the invoice printout and the customer portal, on the invoice form, on the sales order form and on the sales order printout

Win1251, as the NBU recommends, and UTF-8 only when a character does not exist in Win1251

Features

NBU QR format 002the https://bank.gov.ua/qr/ link with the Base64URL data, in the row order of the NBU Rules
No external serviceprinting an invoice sends nothing anywhere
