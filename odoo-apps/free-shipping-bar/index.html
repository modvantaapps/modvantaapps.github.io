Free Shipping Progress Bar

What it does

"Add $X more for FREE shipping" with a progress bar in the cart

Shoppers spend more when they can see how close they are to free shipping. This module adds a message and a progress bar to the cart page: "Add 80.00 $ more for FREE shipping!", updated with every cart change, and "You've unlocked FREE shipping!" once the cart is over the limit.

Features

The threshold is not a separate settingit is read from your delivery method's own "Free if order amount is above" rule, so the bar and the real delivery price always agree.
