Field Service Management

Run your field service on Odoo Community: jobs at the customer address on Project tasks, a drag and drop dispatch board, worksheets and checklists per job type, photos, the customer signature on site, materials from the technician van, travel and work time as timesheets, the service report emailed to the customer, invoicing of time and materials, recurring service contracts, a customer portal with visit booking and a phone-friendly technician page. One module.

What it does

back office, technician phone, customer portal

Everything a service company needs, from the booking to the invoice.

Field service projects on standard Odoo Project: every job is a task at the customer address with planned start, duration, technicians, priority and its own stages.

Technicians by day for a day, a week or two weeks. Drag a waiting job onto a technician and a day, move it to someone else, or drop it back to unplan it. Daily hours per technician.

Build checklists with checkboxes, pass / fail, numbers with limits, choices, dates, text and photo questions. Several worksheets per job, plus manufacturer, model and serial number.
