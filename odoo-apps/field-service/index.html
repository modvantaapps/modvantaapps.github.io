Field Service Management

Run your field service on Odoo Community: jobs at the customer address on Project tasks, a drag and drop dispatch board, worksheets and checklists per job type, photos, the customer signature on site, materials from the technician van, travel and work time as timesheets, the service report emailed to the customer, invoicing of time and materials, recurring service contracts, a customer portal with visit booking and a phone-friendly technician page. One module.

What it does

back office, technician phone, customer portal

Everything a service company needs, from the booking to the invoice.

Field service projects on standard Odoo Project: every job is a task at the customer address with planned start, duration, technicians, priority and its own stages.

Technicians by day for a day, a week or two weeks. Drag a waiting job onto a technician and a day, move it to someone else, or drop it back to unplan it. Daily hours per technician.

Build checklists with checkboxes, pass / fail, numbers with limits, choices, dates, text and photo questions. Several worksheets per job, plus manufacturer, model and serial number.

Who it is for

  • Repair and maintenance companies on Odoo Community. Jobs are standard Project tasks in a project marked Field Service, so timesheets and project reports keep working. Each job has a service address, Site Access Notes, a planned start and duration, and one or more technicians.
  • Dispatchers planning a team's week. Field Service > Planning > Dispatch Board shows technicians by day, a week or two weeks. Drag a waiting job onto a technician and a day, or drop it back on To plan. Overlapping jobs for one technician show a double booking warning.
  • Technicians working from a phone. The Technician App menu opens /fsm in the phone's browser. It lists today's jobs with a route link, and has buttons for travel, start, pause and done. Technicians fill in worksheets, add photos and materials, and take the customer's signature from the same page.
  • Businesses with service contracts. A Service Contract creates a visit every so many days, weeks, months or years at a set time, and invoices a monthly, quarterly or yearly fee. A daily job creates the visits ahead and expires ended contracts. It also adds a renewal to-do 30 days before the end date.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. Then give each user the Field Service access of Technician or Manager under Settings > Users & Companies > Users. On the user's Field Service tab, set their Van Stock Location.
  2. Open Field Service > Configuration > Settings to pick the Default project and the job report email. Here you also turn on the on my way message and the van stock check, and set how many days ahead contract visits are created.
  3. Build checklists under Field Service > Configuration > Worksheet Templates. The question types are checkbox, pass / fail, number with limits, choice, date, text and photo.
  4. Create job types under Field Service > Configuration > Job Types with a default duration, worksheets and billing mode. Then tick the rules on site, such as Customer Signature Required, and Bookable on the Portal.
  5. Plan jobs on the Dispatch Board or the Schedule calendar, then press Send Visit Confirmation to email the customer.
  6. When a job is done, press Create Invoice to bill time, travel and materials, or the fixed price. You can also work through Field Service > Planning > Jobs to Invoice.

Field Service Management: questions

Does it need Odoo Enterprise Field Service?

No. Odoo's own Field Service app is Enterprise only, and this module doesn't use it. It depends on sale_timesheet, project_stock and portal, which pull in Project, Timesheets, Sales, Inventory and Invoicing from Odoo Community.

Is there a field service mobile app?

There is no app to install. The technician page at /fsm is a web page built for phone screens, and technicians log in with their normal Odoo user. Only internal users with the Technician role can open it.

Does it work offline?

No. The technician page needs a connection. Each button, worksheet save, photo upload and signature is sent to the server when it is pressed.

How do field service worksheets work?

A job type can add several worksheet templates to each job, and you can add more with Add Worksheet. Required questions must be answered before Complete. Numbers outside their limits and failed checks are flagged as issues. Each sheet can also record the manufacturer, model and serial number.

How do materials leave stock?

Materials default to the technician's van location. When the job is marked done, they move to the customer in a validated transfer linked to the job. With the van stock check on, a job can't be finished while a material is missing from its location.

What doesn't it do?

It doesn't work offline, and there is no native phone app or push notification. Route links open in an outside map site, so there is no route optimisation. Customers book visits from their portal account, not from a public page. Jobs that come from a sales order are billed through that order, not with Create Invoice.
