Zoho Projects Connector

Bring Zoho Projects work into Odoo: projects, phases, tasks and time logs, and send Odoo projects and tasks back.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Zoho ProjectsZoho Projects integrationProject syncTasksMilestonesTimesheets

What it does

Connect your own Zoho Self Client and pick the Zoho Projects portal. The sync reads your Zoho projects and creates or updates Odoo projects with their dates, turns Zoho phases into Odoo milestones and Zoho tasks into Odoo tasks with name, description, due date, priority, owners (matched by e-mail) and stage. Each Zoho task status is mapped to an Odoo stage in a small table you can edit. Time logs on Zoho tasks become timesheet lines on the employee with the same e-mail. In the other direction, a Push to Zoho Projects button on the project and task creates or updates the record in Zoho, and you can let every Odoo change go out on the next sync.

Features

ProjectsZoho projects imported as Odoo projects with start and end dates; Odoo projects pushed to Zoho
PhasesZoho phases become Odoo milestones with their deadline, marked reached when closed
Tasksname, description, due date, priority, owners and milestone, both ways
Status to stageevery Zoho status seen gets a row; map it to an Odoo stage or let the module create one
TimesheetsZoho time logs become Odoo timesheet lines; logs of unknown people are listed in the sync log
Conflictsa task changed in Zoho since the last sync updates Odoo; with Send Odoo Changes on, Odoo edits go out first
Request limitZoho allows 100 calls per 2 minutes; a large sync stops at 90 and goes on next run
Data centresUS, Europe, India, Australia and China
Accessrecords written as a chosen internal user; users push, managers connect and sync; multi-company
