Picking Sections

Picking Sections - Section and Note Lines on Delivery Orders, Receipts and Transfers for Odoo 19

What it does

Your sales orders and purchase orders are organised in sections - the warehouse should see the same. Add sections and notes on any transfer, carry them over from sales and purchase orders, print them on the delivery slip and the picking list, and keep them on backorders and returns. Section lines never touch stock.

Sections and notes on deliveries, receipts and internal transfers.

Add a section, subsection or note line on delivery orders, receipts and internal transfers with the same Add a section / Add a note buttons you know from quotations.

Confirm a quotation with sections and its delivery order opens with the same sections and notes, in the same order, around the right products.

Sections and notes of a purchase order flow into its receipt, so the goods-in team reads the layout the buyer wrote.

Who it is for

  • Furniture and fit-out suppliers. A quotation split into rooms or floors, like Ground floor and First floor, loses that layout on the delivery order in standard Odoo. With this module the delivery order and the slip show the same sections, so the driver unloads room by room.
  • Distributors who write packing instructions. Notes such as handle with care or pack the spare parts separately are typed on the sales order. They now reach the picker on the transfer and the printed picking list instead of staying in the sales screen.
  • Goods-in teams receiving large purchase orders. When the buyer groups a purchase order by project or site, the receipt shows the same grouping, which makes checking a mixed delivery against the order quicker.
  • Warehouses with partial shipments. If an order ships in several parts, the backorder keeps the sections of the products it still holds, and a return gets the sections of the goods coming back.

Setting it up

  1. Install the module. It needs Sales and Purchase with Inventory (sale_stock and purchase_stock).
  2. Go to Inventory > Configuration > Settings and find the block Sections and Notes on Transfers.
  3. Choose per company whether to copy sections from sales orders and from purchase orders, whether to copy notes too, and whether to drop empty sections.
  4. In the same block decide if sections are kept on backorders and returns, and whether they're printed on the delivery slip and picking operations report.
  5. Confirm a quotation that has sections and notes, then open its delivery order. The lines appear around the right products in the same order.
  6. On any transfer, use Add a section or Add a note in the operations list to add your own lines, and drag them into place.
  7. Print the delivery slip or picking operations report to check the section titles and notes between the products.

Picking Sections: questions

Do section lines affect stock or valuation?

No. Section and note lines have no product and a quantity of zero, which the database enforces. They aren't reserved, valued or moved, and they don't change the status of the transfer.

What happens when I edit the sales order after confirmation?

Adding a section or product to a confirmed order updates the open transfer without duplicating lines. Renaming a section or note on the order changes the text on transfers that aren't done or cancelled yet.

Why is a section missing from my delivery?

With Drop Empty Sections switched on, a section isn't copied to a transfer that holds none of its products, for example a section of services or of items shipped in another delivery. Switch the setting off if you want every section copied.

Does it need Enterprise?

No. It runs on Community with Sales, Purchase and Inventory installed.

What happens if I uninstall it?

The section and note lines are deleted from transfers on uninstall, because standard Odoo requires a product on every stock move. The product lines stay as they were.

Can different companies use different settings?

Yes. Every option in the Sections and Notes on Transfers block is stored per company.
