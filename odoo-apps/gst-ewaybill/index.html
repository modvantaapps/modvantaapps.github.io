GST e-Invoice IRN + e-Way Bill Tracker

GST e-Invoice IRN and e-Way Bill tracking for Odoo 19 India: GSTIN, IRN, acknowledgement number, signed QR code, e-Way Bill number, transporter and vehicle details, and a JSON payload preview on every customer invoice. GST compliance India, e-invoicing Odoo, GSTN document tracker for accountants and exporters.

What it does

Keep the IRN, acknowledgement and e-Way Bill details on every customer invoice

Indian businesses have to record an e-Invoice IRN and, for goods on the move, an e-Way Bill against their invoices. This module gives every customer invoice a GST document where you keep all of it: seller and buyer GSTIN, place of supply, IRN, Ack No. and date, signed QR code, e-Way Bill number and validity, distance, transporter and vehicle.

The module builds a JSON payload preview from the invoice (document, seller, buyer, item lines and transport) that you can hand to your GSP or portal workflow, and tracks each document from Draft to Ready to Generated.

Features

Importantit does not submit anything to the government portals by itself. It is the tracker and the data base for your filing workflow, or for a connection to an approved GSP.
