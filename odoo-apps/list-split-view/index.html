List Split View

List Split View - Master Detail, List and Form Side by Side

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Click a row and the record opens beside the list instead of replacing it. A split view for every list and kanban view in Odoo: side by side or stacked, a resizable divider, Arrow Up / Down to walk through the records, and a guard for unsaved changes.

In standard Odoo, checking ten records means opening one, reading it, going back to the list, finding your place again and opening the next. With this module the list stays on screen: the record you click opens in a pane next to it (or below it), you edit it there, and the list follows every change. Up and Down move to the previous and next record, the divider sets the width, and every choice is remembered per user and per menu.
