Backend Card Payment

Take card payments from the Odoo backend - on invoices, sales orders and customers, for phone and mail orders

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A Charge Card button on invoices and sales orders and a Virtual Terminal for any customer, using the Authorize.Net and Stripe providers Odoo already ships. The card goes straight from the browser to the provider; Odoo never receives or stores the card number.

When a customer pays by phone or by mail, your team should not have to leave Odoo. This module adds Charge Card to customer invoices and sales orders, and a Virtual Terminal under Invoicing -> Customers -> Card Payments where you pick any customer and take a payment.

The charge dialog shows the customer, the open documents and the amount, lets you choose the provider (Authorize.Net or Stripe) and whether to charge now or only authorize, and opens the provider's own card fields. You can keep the card on file, add a note such as the phone order reference, email the receipt, or send a payment link instead.

Every backend card payment is listed with the card brand, the amount, who took it and the address and CVV check results, and every attempt - approved or declined - is written to an audit log. Authorized payments can be captured (also partially) or voided, and paid ones refunded. The payments on this page were run against a local mock of the gateways, never against a real card.

Features

Charge Card on the invoice
The charge dialog
Authorize on a sales order, capture later
Virtual Terminal for any customer
All backend card payments in one list
Capture all or part of an authorization
An audit log of every attempt
Stripe settings with MOTO
Authorize.Net settings
