Backend Card Payment

Take card payments from the Odoo backend - on invoices, sales orders and customers, for phone and mail orders

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

A Charge Card button on invoices and sales orders and a Virtual Terminal for any customer, using the Authorize.Net and Stripe providers Odoo already ships. The card goes straight from the browser to the provider; Odoo never receives or stores the card number.

When a customer pays by phone or by mail, your team should not have to leave Odoo. This module adds Charge Card to customer invoices and sales orders, and a Virtual Terminal under Invoicing -> Customers -> Card Payments where you pick any customer and take a payment.

The charge dialog shows the customer, the open documents and the amount, lets you choose the provider (Authorize.Net or Stripe) and whether to charge now or only authorize, and opens the provider's own card fields. You can keep the card on file, add a note such as the phone order reference, email the receipt, or send a payment link instead.

Every backend card payment is listed with the card brand, the amount, who took it and the address and CVV check results, and every attempt - approved or declined - is written to an audit log. Authorized payments can be captured (also partially) or voided, and paid ones refunded. The payments on this page were run against a local mock of the gateways, never against a real card.

Who it is for

  • Phone and mail order teams. The customer reads out their card while you have the invoice open. Charge Card opens the provider's own card fields inside Odoo, so the payment is taken and recorded without leaving the backend.
  • B2B sellers taking deposits. On a sales order you can charge the full amount, a 50% or 25% deposit, or any amount, or only authorize it and capture the final figure when the goods ship.
  • Businesses that bill repeat customers. With the customer's consent recorded, the card can be kept on file and charged again later from an invoice, several invoices of the same customer, or the Virtual Terminal.
  • Finance managers who need control over who charges what. Each user can be given a card charge limit, and every authorize, charge, capture, void and refund attempt is written to an audit log, declined ones included.

Setting it up

  1. Go to Invoicing > Configuration > Payment Providers and set up Authorize.Net or Stripe as usual, in Test or Enabled state. Authorize.Net also needs its API Client Key, which the Generate Client Key button fills.
  2. On the provider, open the Backend Card Payments tab and tick Backend Card Payments. Pick the Default Backend Action and, for Authorize.Net, the Card Entry Form and Duplicate Window (seconds).
  3. For Stripe, tick Flag Backend Charges as MOTO only after Stripe has enabled MOTO payments on your account. Set a Backend Surcharge if you add one.
  4. Under Settings > Users & Companies > Users, give each person the Backend Card Payments role of User or Manager, and set a Card Charge Limit on the user if needed.
  5. In Invoicing > Configuration > Settings, set the Duplicate charge guard in minutes and decide whether Email the card receipt is ticked by default.
  6. Charge from the Charge Card button on an invoice or sales order, or open Invoicing > Customers > Card Payments > Virtual Terminal for any customer.

Backend Card Payment: questions

Does Odoo see or store the card number?

No. The card is typed into the provider's form (the Authorize.Net hosted form or Accept.js fields, or Stripe Elements) and Odoo only receives a one-time token. The server also refuses any request in which a field looks like a full card number.

Which providers and editions does it work with?

The Authorize.Net and Stripe providers that ship with Odoo 19, on Community or Enterprise. It also needs Invoicing and Sales. Authorize.Net only opens its card form when the page is served over HTTPS.

Can I refund an Authorize.Net payment straight away?

Authorize.Net only refunds a transaction after it has settled. Before that, the module offers to void the whole payment instead. Partial refunds are possible once it has settled.

What happens if the bank asks for 3-D Secure?

Phone payments don't wait on a 3-D Secure challenge. The charge is refused, and you can send the customer a payment link by email or SMS from the same dialog.

Who can capture, void and refund?

Users can charge and authorize. Managers can also capture, void and refund, see the whole audit log and set user limits. Regular users only see their own audit lines.

What isn't included?

No eCheck or ACH, no Level 2 or 3 card data, no 3-D Secure in the backend, and no card-present terminals. Your PCI scope is still something to agree with your acquirer.
