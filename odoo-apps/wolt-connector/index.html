Odoo Wolt integration

Wolt Connector

Wolt couriers for your own orders, and Wolt orders in your Odoo.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask a question
Wolt integration for Odoo 19

What it does

Two Wolt APIs in one module: book Wolt Drive couriers from your deliveries, and run the orders your venue gets on the Wolt app from an order board that creates real sales orders.

Wolt Drive is for shops that sell on their own website or by phone and want a Wolt courier to take the order to the customer. Paste the merchant key and venue ID Wolt gave you on an Odoo delivery method. On the quotation or in the checkout Odoo asks Wolt for a shipment promise and shows the price with the arrival time. When the warehouse validates the transfer, Odoo books the courier with the customer, the phone, the parcels and their weight, and keeps the tracking link, the ETAs and the courier vehicle on the transfer.

Press Register Webhook and Wolt sends every courier step back to Odoo. Each event is a JWT signed with a secret Odoo made for you, so a forged call is refused. Steps never go backwards when events arrive late, and a failed delivery creates an activity with Wolt's reason.

The marketplace side is for restaurants and shops that sell on the Wolt app. Each Wolt order notification is checked against your webhook secret, then Odoo reads the order from the Wolt Order API and creates a sales order with the items, the chosen options and the delivery fee, matched to your products by SKU, POS ID or barcode. Unknown items or totals that do not match are flagged.

Staff accept, reject with a reason, mark ready and hand over from the board. Pre-orders are confirmed instead of accepted. Auto accept can be off, always on, or on only when every item is in stock. Stock and prices go back to Wolt by SKU, opening hours come from a working schedule, and the venue can go offline from Odoo when the kitchen is overloaded.
