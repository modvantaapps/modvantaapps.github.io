Fixed Sales Price per Variant

Type the price of each variant - and that is the price everywhere

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

Switch a product to Fixed Price per Variant and give every variant its own sales price instead of template price plus attribute extras. Quotations, pricelists, the website shop and the Point of Sale all use it.

In standard Odoo a variant's price is the template price plus the extra price of each attribute value. That works for simple surcharges, but when every size or finish has its own price you end up calculating extras backwards.

This module adds a Fixed Price per Variant switch to the product. Turn it on and the Attributes & Variants tab shows a grid with one price per variant. That price is used by quotations and pricelist rules, on the product page, shop grid and cart, and in the Point of Sale, with no + extra badges.

A Bulk Update wizard sets, raises, lowers or resets the prices of many variants at once, with rounding.

Who it is for

  • Clothing shops with size-based prices. When S costs 18.00, M 19.50 and XL 29.90, working out attribute extras backwards gets messy fast. Here you type each size's price directly in a grid on the product.
  • Furniture and fittings sellers. A desk sold with different leg finishes and top sizes often has a price list per combination rather than a surcharge per option. Each combination gets its own figure, and quotes match the price list.
  • Packaging and print suppliers. Box sizes or paper weights rarely follow a neat extra-per-option rule. Fixed variant prices, plus the Bulk Update wizard for yearly increases with rounding, keep the catalogue tidy.
  • Stores selling online and at the till. With eCommerce or Point of Sale installed, the product page, cart and POS variant picker all show the variant's own price, without the + extra badges on attribute values.

Setting it up

  1. Open a product that has variants from Sales > Products > Products (or Inventory > Products) and go to the Attributes & Variants tab.
  2. Tick Fixed Price per Variant. Save once so the grid of variants appears below the attributes.
  3. Type each variant's Fixed Sales Price in the grid. The product's Sales Price then shows the price of the default variant.
  4. To change many prices at once, click Bulk Update, or select variants in the product variants list and open Actions > Update Variant Prices. Choose Set a price, a percentage or an amount, and an optional rounding step, then click Apply.
  5. If you want to start again from the old pricing, click Reset to standard prices to rebuild every variant price from the template price plus the attribute extras.
  6. Check the result on a test quotation with a pricelist before you change prices across the whole catalogue.

Fixed Sales Price per Variant: questions

Do pricelist rules still apply?

Yes. Fixed prices, discounts, formulas and pricelists based on other pricelists all start from the variant's fixed price, just as they would start from the normal sales price.

Do I need eCommerce or Point of Sale installed?

No. The module depends only on Product. If Sales, eCommerce or Point of Sale are installed, they pick up the fixed prices with their own unchanged code, so no extra bridge module is needed.

What happens to my attribute extras when I switch it on?

The template price and the attribute extra prices are set aside, not deleted. Switching the option off on a product, or uninstalling the module, puts them back so pricing works exactly as before.

Can I import variant prices from a spreadsheet?

The Fixed Sales Price is a normal field on the product variant, so you can export the variant list, fill in that column and import it back with Odoo's standard import.

Can each company have a different variant price?

No. The fixed price is a single value per variant, shared by every company that sells the product. Use pricelists if you need different prices per company or customer group.

Which edition does it need?

It runs on Community and Enterprise. It doesn't change cost prices, only the sales price.
