Activity Dashboard & Bulk Tools

One screen for every scheduled activity - yours and your team's - with bulk actions

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation

What it does

See overdue, today and planned activities across all apps, open any number to the list behind it, and reschedule, reassign, mark done, tag or cancel many activities at once.

Odoo shows activities document by document and in the clock menu. This module adds an Activities app with a dashboard: for me and for my team, how many activities are overdue, due today and planned, how many were done or cancelled in the last 30 days and the share done on time, plus what is due in the next 7 days and what is high priority. Breakdowns by activity type, by user and by document type show where the work is, and every figure opens the matching list.

My Activities, Team Activities and All Activities are lists over every application, with kanban by status, calendar and pivot views and a side panel to filter by status, activity type and priority. Select activities and use Mark as Done (with one feedback), Reschedule (to a date or by N days), Reassign, Change Type, Priority, Tags or Cancel with a reason.

Activities get a priority and coloured tags. Done and cancelled activities stay in Activity History, and a cancelled one can be reopened. Activity Teams group a leader and members; an optional daily digest email sends each user their overdue and due activities, with a team summary for leaders.
