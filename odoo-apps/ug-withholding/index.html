Uganda Withholding Tax

Withhold income tax and 6% VAT from supplier bills with URA nature-of-payment codes, record what your customers withhold from you, print withholding certificates and prepare the monthly DT-2006 withholding tax return with its Schedule 1 and Schedule 2 CSV files.

What it does

Uganda withholding agents must withhold income tax from payments to suppliers at the rate of the nature of payment, remit it to URA and file the monthly withholding tax return (DT-2006) within 15 days after the month. Designated VAT withholding agents also withhold 6% of the taxable value and report it in the monthly VAT return. Odoo has no withholding tax for Uganda.

This module loads the URA nature-of-payment codes with their rates and validity dates from the DT-2006 template (including the changes from 1 July 2026), withholds income tax and VAT from a posted vendor bill in one step, books the amounts to URA payable accounts matched with the bill, prints the certificate and builds the monthly return.

Nothing is sent to URA. You upload the CSV files or key the figures on the URA portal yourself.

Who it is for

  • Ugandan companies appointed as withholding agents. An agent has to hold back income tax from many supplier payments at the rate for each nature of payment, pay it to URA and file DT-2006 every month. This app does the withholding from the posted vendor bill and gathers the month's figures into the return.
  • Designated VAT withholding agents. If your company was designated to withhold VAT, the same step can also take 6% of the taxable value from the bill and book it to a VAT withheld payable account, with Section G figures ready for the monthly VAT return.
  • Suppliers who get paid net of WHT. When a customer withholds tax from your invoice, you can record it against that invoice so the credit sits on a claimable account instead of looking like an unpaid balance.
  • Bookkeepers preparing URA returns for clients. Each certificate keeps the supplier's TIN, the nature of payment and the voucher number, and contacts in Uganda without a valid 10-digit TIN are flagged before the return goes in.

Setting it up

  1. Make sure the company uses the Uganda chart of accounts (l10n_ug), then open Invoicing > Configuration > Settings and find the Uganda Withholding (URA) block.
  2. Enter the URA TIN, tick Withholding Tax Agent and Designated VAT Withholding Agent if they apply, and set the Withholding Accounts and Withholding Journal. Create Missing Accounts adds any accounts you don't have yet.
  3. On supplier contacts set the Default Nature of Payment, and tick On URA WHT Exemption List or Exempt from VAT Withholding where URA has granted it.
  4. Open a posted vendor bill and click URA Withholding. Choose the Nature of Payment, check the amounts and payment date, and click Apply to post the entry and print the certificate.
  5. For tax a customer withheld from you, open the posted customer invoice, click URA Withholding and choose that the customer withheld from you.
  6. At month end go to Invoicing > Reporting > Uganda Withholding > Withholding Returns, create a return, click Collect, review Section C and the schedules, then Export CSV.
  7. Upload the files or key the figures on the URA portal yourself, then Close the return.

Uganda Withholding Tax: questions

Does it file the return with URA?

No. It prepares the figures, the CSV files and the PDF. You upload or enter them on the URA portal with your own login.

Where do the rates come from?

The nature-of-payment codes, rates and validity dates were taken from URA's DT-2006 template, including the changes from 1 July 2026, and the 6% VAT withholding from the monthly VAT return template. Rates do change with each budget, so check them against current URA guidance and your tax adviser, and edit them under URA Nature of Payment if needed.

What if the template doesn't fix a rate?

For natures where the template leaves the rate open, such as rent, dividends or royalties, you type the rate on the withholding itself.

Can I undo a withholding entered by mistake?

Yes, as long as it isn't on a closed return. Cancelling it unreconciles and cancels the journal entry, and you can reset it to draft and redo it.

Is the CSV in URA's official upload format?

It follows the column order of Schedule 1, Schedule 2 and Sections G-I and G-II in URA's templates, with dd/mm/yyyy dates. URA doesn't publish a separate CSV specification, so check the first upload carefully.

Does it need Odoo Enterprise?

No. It depends on Invoicing, the Uganda localisation l10n_ug and Discuss, all available in Community. Returns and certificates are kept per company.
