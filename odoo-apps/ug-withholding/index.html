Uganda Withholding Tax

Withhold income tax and 6% VAT from supplier bills with URA nature-of-payment codes, record what your customers withhold from you, print withholding certificates and prepare the monthly DT-2006 withholding tax return with its Schedule 1 and Schedule 2 CSV files.

What it does

Uganda withholding agents must withhold income tax from payments to suppliers at the rate of the nature of payment, remit it to URA and file the monthly withholding tax return (DT-2006) within 15 days after the month. Designated VAT withholding agents also withhold 6% of the taxable value and report it in the monthly VAT return. Odoo has no withholding tax for Uganda.

This module loads the URA nature-of-payment codes with their rates and validity dates from the DT-2006 template (including the changes from 1 July 2026), withholds income tax and VAT from a posted vendor bill in one step, books the amounts to URA payable accounts matched with the bill, prints the certificate and builds the monthly return.

Nothing is sent to URA. You upload the CSV files or key the figures on the URA portal yourself.
