Save Cart / Share Cart

Save a cart, email it, and resume later from a share link.

View on Odoo Apps Store Ask about customisation
Save cartShare cart linkEmail carteCommerce

What it does

The cart page gets a Save or share this cart box. The shopper can type an email address and click Save & get link: they get a page with a link to the cart, and if they gave an email, the link is emailed too.

Opening the link - on another device, or by a colleague - puts a copy of the saved cart into the visitor's own cart. The original stays untouched, and the copy is set to whoever opened it, so the first customer's details are not passed on.
