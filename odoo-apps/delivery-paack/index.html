Paack Shipping Integration

Ship with Paack from Odoo 19: the Paack order and label are created when the delivery is validated, with time slot, pickup window, cash on delivery, return labels and tracking that updates by itself.

What it does

A delivery method type Paack on the standard Odoo shipping framework. Validating a delivery order sends the order to Paack with the recipient, the delivery time slot, the pickup window and one barcode per parcel, and attaches the label to the transfer. Tracking events come from the Paack Tracking API.

Features

Order and labelvalidating the delivery order creates the Paack order and attaches the label (PDF, or ZPL for thermal printers) with one page per parcel.
Multi-parcelevery package of the transfer becomes a parcel with its own barcode, weight and dimensions.
Delivery time slotexpected delivery window (days ahead, from and to) sent in UTC from your local time; Sundays are skipped.
Pickup windowwhen Paack collects at the warehouse, with pickup instructions.
Cash on deliverythe order total or the amount still unpaid, in EUR or GBP.
Return labelsthe standard Odoo return creates a Paack reverse order (pickup at the customer, delivery to the warehouse) with its return label.
Coverage checkthe quotation asks the Paack Coverage API whether the postcode is served; the price is your contract price: base price plus a price per extra kg.
TrackingUpdate Paack Tracking and an hourly job (in batches of 50 orders) store the Paack events, the delivery attempts and the Paack tracking link, and move the order state.
Cancelcancel the Paack order from the transfer.
Instructionsdelivery instructions from the transfer note or a default text, and instructions when the parcel cannot be delivered.
Staging and productionthe Environment button switches between the Paack staging and production APIs; the Auth0 audience can be set.
API log and demo modeevery call logged with the secrets masked; a demo mode answers locally so you can try the whole flow without credentials.
