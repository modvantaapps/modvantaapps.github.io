Modvanta Back in Stock Alerts: User Guide

Modvanta Back in Stock Alerts shows a Notify me form on out-of-stock products and variants. Shoppers leave their email. When stock comes back, the app emails each of them once, with a one-click unsubscribe link. The dashboard shows waiting requests, your most-requested products and the orders that came after an alert.

Requirements

  • A Stencil theme. The form is placed for Cornerstone by default; other themes can set a mount selector.
  • The store owner or a staff user allowed to install apps.

Install

  1. In the control panel, open Apps > Marketplace, find Modvanta Back in Stock Alerts and click Install.
  2. Confirm the permissions. The app opens in your control panel and adds its script to Storefront > Script Manager.
  3. Open a product that is out of stock to see the form.

Storefront form

  • Show the Notify me form on out-of-stock product pages.
  • Heading, intro text, button text, email placeholder, thank-you message, button colour.
  • Ask for a phone number: optional for shoppers. The number is stored only. The app never sends text messages.
  • Show a consent checkbox (on by default) and the consent text. Shoppers must tick it, and the text is saved with each request as proof of consent.
  • Double opt-in: the app first emails a confirmation link, and only confirmed shoppers get the alert. Unconfirmed requests are deleted after 7 days.
  • Mount on (CSS selector) and placement (before, after or inside that element). Leave it empty for Cornerstone, or add <div data-mdv-back-in-stock></div> to your product template.
  • Extra storefront domains: your main and mybigcommerce.com domains are allowed automatically. Add any other https storefront domains.

When stock comes back

The app watches your stock changes. When an item goes from zero or less to more than zero, it emails the waiting shoppers, oldest request first. Turn on Only email as many shoppers as there are units and set shoppers per unit to send fewer emails; the rest are emailed when more stock arrives. Every hour the app also re-checks products that have waiting shoppers. Use Check stock now on a product to check it at once.

Alert email

Write the subject and a plain-text body. Placeholders: {product_name}, {product_url}, {variant}, {store_name}, {image} and {unsubscribe_url}. The app always adds a View product button and an unsubscribe footer. Use Send a test email to send it with sample data to your own staff email.

Email sending

By default alerts are sent from our mail server. The From name is your store name, and shopper replies go to your store's email, or to the Reply-To you set. You can send from your own SMTP server instead: enter the host, port, username, password and from address, and tick Send alerts from my SMTP server. The password is stored encrypted and is never shown again. The email log lists every send, with the address partly hidden.

Subscriptions and conversions

Filter requests by product, status or email, export them as CSV, or delete one. When a shopper who got an alert orders that product within 30 days, the order and its line total are shown as a conversion.

Your shoppers' data

Requests contain your shoppers' email addresses. You are responsible for telling shoppers about this in your own privacy notice. To delete everything for one shopper, use Erase a shopper (GDPR) and Delete all their data. Sent, failed and unsubscribed requests are deleted after 365 days. See the privacy policy.

Limits

  • The form only shows on the main product page, not in quick-view windows.
  • Bounces and spam complaints are not processed. An address that is refused is marked as failed.

Uninstalling

Uninstall the app from Apps > My Apps. The script and webhooks are removed, and every request, email address, log and setting for your store is deleted from our servers. Export your requests first if you need them.

Troubleshooting

  • The form does not show: check the product or the chosen variant is really out of stock, and set a mount selector if your theme is not Cornerstone.
  • Alerts not received: check the email log, ask the shopper to check spam, and if you use your own SMTP server, send a test email.
