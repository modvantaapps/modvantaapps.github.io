BigCommerce App Support

Support for Modvanta apps on the BigCommerce App Marketplace:

  • Modvanta Odoo Connector
  • Modvanta Payment & Shipping Rules
  • Modvanta Order Automation
  • Modvanta Store Hours & Holiday Mode
  • Modvanta Delivery Date & ZIP Checker
  • Modvanta Back in Stock Alerts

Contact

Email support@modvantaapps.tech. We answer within one business day (Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 India Standard Time, UTC+5:30), usually sooner.

What to send

  • The app name and your store URL (for example store-abc123.mybigcommerce.com).
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or the exact error message.
  • For the Odoo Connector: your Odoo version and the address of your Odoo server.
  • For an order question: the order number, and the line the app wrote in the order's staff notes.
  • For a storefront widget: the page address and the name of your theme.

Never send passwords, API tokens or card details by email. We will never ask for them.

Uninstalling

Open Apps > My Apps in your BigCommerce control panel and uninstall the app. Everything the app added to your store (scripts, webhooks) is removed, and the store's data is deleted from our servers. Staff notes, order comments, statuses and order metafields that an app already wrote to your orders stay on those orders.
