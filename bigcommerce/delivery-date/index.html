Modvanta Delivery Date & ZIP Checker: User Guide

Modvanta Delivery Date & ZIP Checker adds two things to your BigCommerce store: a postcode checker on product pages, which tells shoppers whether and when you deliver to them, and a delivery date picker at checkout. The chosen date is written to the order, and every new order is checked on our server.

Requirements

  • A Stencil theme. The product page checker is placed for Cornerstone by default; other themes can set a mount selector.
  • Optimized One-Page Checkout for the date picker.
  • The store owner or a staff user allowed to install apps. Products (read-only) is only needed for zones limited to categories.

Install

  1. In the control panel, open Apps > Marketplace, find Modvanta Delivery Date & ZIP Checker and click Install.
  2. Confirm the permissions. The app opens in your control panel and adds its scripts to Storefront > Script Manager.

Delivery zones

Each zone has a name, a list of postcodes, a message, minimum and maximum days, and can be limited to some product IDs or category IDs. Zones are checked top to bottom and the first match wins. Postcodes can be written as:

  • 10001: that postcode. For UK and Canadian postcodes, SW1A or M5V also match the full postcodes in that area.
  • 100*: every postcode starting with 100. * on its own matches everything.
  • 10001-10099: a range. Both ends must be the same length.
  • !10005: never matches this postcode.

Case, spaces and dashes are ignored. The message can use {min}, {max}, {zone} and {zip}. Use the postcode tester to check a postcode before you save. Zones can be exported and imported as CSV with the columns zone, postcodes, message, min_days, max_days, product_ids, category_ids. Importing replaces all zones.

Product page ZIP checker

  • Show the postcode checker on product pages.
  • Title, input placeholder, button text, the not serviceable message ({zip} is the postcode typed) and the invalid postcode message.
  • Mount selector: the page element the box is added to. The default suits Cornerstone.

Delivery date at checkout

  • Show the delivery date picker (off when you install) and Shoppers must choose a date.
  • Add the zone's min days to the lead time.
  • Also write the date into the order comments.
  • Title, mount selector and time zone.
  • Lead time (days), counted on every day or on delivery days only.
  • Same-day cutoff: orders after this time count from tomorrow.
  • Max days ahead and deliveries per day (0 = no limit).
  • Delivery days of the week, and holidays, one per line: 2026-12-25, 2026-12-24..2026-12-26, or 12-25 for every year.
  • Time slots (optional, up to 24), each with a label, a start time for same-day orders and its own capacity.

How the date is saved on the order

When the order is created, the app checks the chosen date again and writes it to the order: a line in the staff notes, a line in the order comments (if turned on), and order metafields modvanta_delivery / delivery_date and delivery_slot that other apps and integrations can read. The order is added to Upcoming deliveries, which you can list by date and export as CSV. Cancelled and declined orders give their slot back.

The order check

When a date is missing (and required), no longer valid, or its slot is full, the app can:

  • Flag (default): add a staff note and set the order to Manual Verification Required.
  • Note: add a staff note only.
  • Off: only list it in the app.

Also flag orders whose shipping postcode is in no zone adds the postcode to the check. The app never cancels or refunds an order. Check whether your store emails shoppers when an order changes status before you use Flag.

Limits

  • Express wallet buttons such as PayPal or Apple Pay on the cart page skip the checkout picker. With a required date, those orders are flagged as missing a date.
  • Orders created by staff or on other channels do not use the picker.
  • Quick-view windows on product cards do not show the postcode checker.
  • If you sell on more storefront domains, add them under Extra storefront domains, or the checker and picker cannot reach our server from there.

Uninstalling

Uninstall the app from Apps > My Apps. The scripts and webhooks are removed, and your zones, settings and delivery list are deleted from our servers. Notes, comments and metafields already on orders stay. Export your upcoming deliveries first if you need them.

Troubleshooting

  • The checker does not show: check it is turned on, and set a mount selector if your theme is not Cornerstone.
  • No dates in the picker: check the lead time, cutoff, delivery days, holidays and capacity.
  • A postcode is not found: try it in the postcode tester and check the zone order.
