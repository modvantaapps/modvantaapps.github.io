Modvanta Odoo Connector: Installation Guide

The Modvanta Odoo Connector links a BigCommerce store to Odoo, the open-source ERP. Orders, products, stock, customers, price lists, shipments and refunds are synchronised by the Modvanta BigCommerce Connector module in Odoo. The BigCommerce app handles the connection: it gives your Odoo server secure API access to your store, with no API account to create and no token to copy.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19, Community or Enterprise.
  • The Modvanta BigCommerce Connector module (mdv_bigcommerce_connector, version 19.0.1.1.0 or later), sold separately on the Odoo Apps Store.
  • Your Odoo server reachable from the internet over HTTPS with a valid certificate, on port 443 or 8443.
  • An Odoo user with the BigCommerce Administrator access right, and the BigCommerce store owner or a staff user allowed to install apps.

1. Install the Odoo module

  1. Add mdv_bigcommerce_connector to your Odoo addons path and restart Odoo.
  2. In Odoo, open Apps, update the apps list, search for BigCommerce and install it.
  3. Open BigCommerce > Configuration > Stores and create a store record. Choose the company, warehouse, pricelist and sales team that BigCommerce orders should use.

2. Install the app in BigCommerce

  1. In the BigCommerce control panel, open Apps > Marketplace, find Modvanta Odoo Connector and click Install.
  2. Review the permissions and confirm. The app opens inside your control panel and shows the pairing form.

3. Pair the store with Odoo

  1. In Odoo, open your store record and click Connect with the BigCommerce App. Odoo shows a 16-character pairing code. It works once and expires after 15 minutes.
  2. In the BigCommerce app, enter your Odoo address (for example https://erp.example.com) and the code, then click Connect to Odoo.
  3. The app sends the store's API access to Odoo, server to server, and shows Connected to Odoo. The app then deletes its own copy of the access token. Odoo stores it encrypted on your server.

4. Choose what to synchronise

On the Odoo store record, set:

  • Orders: which BigCommerce statuses to import, and when Odoo confirms, invoices and registers payment.
  • Products: import from BigCommerce or export from Odoo. Products are matched by SKU, then barcode, so existing products are not duplicated.
  • Stock: which Odoo warehouses feed which BigCommerce inventory locations. Stock is pushed as it moves.
  • Customers and price lists: map BigCommerce customer groups to Odoo pricelists.

Use BigCommerce > Operations > Synchronise... for a first full import, then let webhooks and scheduled jobs keep everything current. Shipments with tracking numbers, cancellations and refunds made in Odoo are sent back to BigCommerce.

Moving to another Odoo database

Because the app does not keep your access token after pairing, uninstall and reinstall the app, then pair with a new code from the other database.

Uninstalling

Uninstall the app from Apps > My Apps. BigCommerce revokes the access token, the app tells your Odoo server, and Odoo marks the store as disconnected. Data already in Odoo stays in Odoo. The app deletes its record of your store.

Troubleshooting

  • "Code not valid": the code expired, was already used, or was mistyped. Generate a new one in Odoo.
  • "Odoo server not reachable": check the address opens in a browser from outside your network, uses HTTPS with a valid certificate, and runs on port 443 or 8443.
  • Orders not arriving: open BigCommerce > Operations > Sync Log and Webhook Log in Odoo.
