Modvanta Payment & Shipping Rules: User Guide

Modvanta Payment & Shipping Rules hides payment and shipping methods at your BigCommerce checkout, based on who is buying and what is in the cart. Each new storefront order is also checked on our server, so an order that used a hidden method is flagged for you.

Requirements

  • A BigCommerce store using Optimized One-Page Checkout.
  • The store owner or a staff user allowed to install apps.

Install

  1. In the control panel, open Apps > Marketplace, find Modvanta Payment & Shipping Rules and click Install.
  2. Confirm the permissions. The app opens in your control panel and adds its checkout script to Storefront > Script Manager.

Create a rule

  1. Click Add payment rule or Add shipping rule and give it a name.
  2. Choose the action: Hide these methods, or Show only these methods.
  3. Enter the methods by name, matched by Name contains or Name is exactly. The app lists the methods enabled in your store.
  4. Add conditions. A rule applies only when all of its conditions are true. A rule with no conditions always applies.
  5. Click Save. The checkout uses the new rules straight away.

Conditions

  • Customer group: in or not in the chosen groups. Guests have no group.
  • Shipping country and billing country: in or not in a list.
  • Subtotal above or below an amount, in the order currency.
  • Quantity of items above or below a number.
  • Currency of the order.
  • Products, SKUs, brands or categories in the cart.
  • Chosen shipping method (payment rules only), for example hide Cash on Delivery unless the shopper picked local delivery.

Examples

  • Show only Bank Deposit to the Wholesale customer group.
  • Hide Cash on Delivery for orders over 500.
  • Hide express shipping when the cart has a product from the Furniture category.
  • Hide all methods except Local Pickup when the shipping country is not your own.

The order check

The checkout script hides methods in the shopper's browser. Some paths bypass the checkout page, for example express wallet buttons such as PayPal or Apple Pay on the cart page, or orders created through the API. So the app also checks every new storefront order against your rules:

  • Flag (default): an order that used a hidden method is set to Manual Verification Required with a staff note explaining which rule it broke. Nothing is cancelled or refunded.
  • Log only: the result is recorded in the app, the order is not changed.
  • Off: no order check.

Check whether your store emails shoppers when an order changes status (Settings > Order Notifications) before you use Flag.

Uninstalling

Uninstall the app from Apps > My Apps. The checkout script and the webhook are removed, your checkout returns to normal, and your rules are deleted from our servers.
