Modvanta Order Automation: User Guide

Modvanta Order Automation works on your BigCommerce orders from our server. It does three things: it can cancel unpaid orders after a time limit, it holds new orders that match your rules for review, and it moves new orders to another status by rule. It adds nothing to your storefront or checkout.

Requirements

  • Any BigCommerce store. No theme or checkout changes are needed.
  • The store owner or a staff user allowed to install apps.

Install

  1. In the control panel, open Apps > Marketplace, find Modvanta Order Automation and click Install.
  2. Confirm the permissions: Orders (modify), Customers (read-only), and Information & settings (read-only). The app opens in your control panel and starts listening for new orders.

Nothing happens to your orders until you add a rule or turn on auto-cancel. If a permission is missing, the app shows a banner at the top.

The master switch

Automation is ON for this store pauses everything when you turn it off. While it is off, no order is cancelled, held or moved.

Auto-cancel unpaid orders

Auto-cancel is off when you install. It is the only part of the app that cancels orders, and only after you turn it on.

  • Cancel unpaid orders automatically: turns the auto-cancel run on.
  • Orders in these statuses: Awaiting Payment and Pending are chosen by default. Incomplete can also be chosen. No other status can be auto-cancelled.
  • Cancel after (hours): the default time limit for every payment method (24 hours when you install).
  • Ignore orders older than (days): 30 by default. Older orders are never touched, so turning auto-cancel on does not cancel years of old orders. 0 means no limit.
  • Add a staff note to cancelled orders: the note gives the age of the order and the limit used.
  • Different time limit per payment method: up to 20 limits, matched when the order's payment method name contains your text, for example Bank Deposit 72 hours while everything else waits 24 hours.

Click Preview orders to cancel to list the orders these settings would cancel now. The preview changes nothing. The app runs auto-cancel about every 15 minutes. Each order is read again just before it is cancelled, so an order paid in the meantime is left alone. A run cancels at most 200 orders per store; the rest wait for the next run.

New order rules

Every new order is checked once. Hold rules run first, then status rules. The first rule that matches sets the order status and adds a staff note naming the rule, plus your extra note if you wrote one. You can have up to 50 rules. A rule needs at least one condition, and all of its conditions must match.

  • Set order status to: Awaiting Payment, Awaiting Pickup, Awaiting Shipment, Completed, Awaiting Fulfillment or Manual Verification Required. A rule can never cancel, refund, decline or ship an order.
  • Order total above / below, and item quantity above.
  • Shipping country or billing country is one of a list of two-letter codes, for example US, CA.
  • Payment method contains a name, for example Cash on Delivery.
  • Customer group is one of the groups you pick.
  • Current status is one of the statuses you pick.
  • First order of this customer. Guests are matched by billing email. Incomplete orders are not counted.
  • Shipping country differs from billing country.
  • Only digital items.

Examples

  • Hold rule: order total above 1000 and first order, set to Manual Verification Required.
  • Hold rule: shipping country differs from billing country, set to Manual Verification Required.
  • Status rule: only digital items and current status Awaiting Fulfillment, set to Completed.

Activity

The activity table lists the last 500 things the app did: the order, the action, the rule and the details. The auto-cancel card also shows the result of the last run.

Good to know

  • An order that is still Incomplete when it arrives (payment not finished) is looked at again after 5, 15 and 60 minutes.
  • Whether stock comes back when the app cancels an order depends on your store's inventory settings. Test it with one order before you rely on it.
  • Your store may email the shopper when an order changes status. Check Settings > Order Notifications before you use auto-cancel or rules.
  • Payment methods are matched by the name on the order. Check the exact name on a real order before you write a rule or a time limit.

Uninstalling

Uninstall the app from Apps > My Apps. The order webhook is removed and your settings, rules and activity log are deleted from our servers. Statuses and staff notes already set on orders stay as they are.

Troubleshooting

  • A rule did not run: check the master switch and that the rule is enabled. Each order is checked only once, when it is created, so rules do not apply to older orders.
  • An order was not auto-cancelled: check its age against the limit for its payment method, and the Ignore orders older than setting. Use Preview.
  • Nothing happens at all: open the app once. Opening it turns the order webhook back on if BigCommerce paused it.
