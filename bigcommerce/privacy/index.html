Privacy Policy for BigCommerce Apps

Last updated 14 November 2026.

This policy covers the Modvanta apps on the BigCommerce App Marketplace (together, "the apps"):

  • Modvanta Odoo Connector
  • Modvanta Payment & Shipping Rules
  • Modvanta Order Automation
  • Modvanta Store Hours & Holiday Mode
  • Modvanta Delivery Date & ZIP Checker
  • Modvanta Back in Stock Alerts

The apps are provided by Modvanta Apps, Chandigarh, India ("Modvanta", "we"). Contact: support@modvantaapps.tech.

Our role

When you (the merchant) install an app, we process your store's data on your behalf, only to run the app. For personal data of your shoppers, you are the controller and Modvanta is your processor: we act only on your instructions, which are the app's settings. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the hosting provider that runs our servers and, for Back in Stock Alerts, the email delivery service that sends alerts from our mail server.

Every app below keeps the store hash, the store owner ID, the permissions granted and the BigCommerce staff user IDs that opened the app, to identify the install and its users. Unless stated otherwise, the store's API access token is kept encrypted (AES-256-GCM) until uninstall, because the app needs it to work.

Modvanta Odoo Connector

The app keeps:

  • Store hash, store name, store owner ID and the permissions granted, to identify the install. Kept until uninstall.
  • The store's API access token, encrypted (AES-256-GCM), only until it has been handed to your Odoo server. It is deleted as soon as pairing succeeds, or at uninstall.
  • Your Odoo server address, the Odoo store name and the pairing date, to show the connection status. Kept until uninstall.
  • BigCommerce staff user IDs that opened the app and whether each is the owner. Kept until the user is removed or the app is uninstalled.

The app stores no email addresses, customer records or orders. Orders, products, customers and stock move directly between BigCommerce and your own Odoo server. That data is held on your Odoo server under your control, not by Modvanta.

Modvanta Payment & Shipping Rules

The app keeps:

  • Store hash, store owner ID, the permissions granted and the store's API access token, encrypted. Kept until uninstall, because the app needs the token to manage its checkout script and check new orders.
  • Your rules and settings, and the checkout script built from them.
  • BigCommerce staff user IDs that opened the app.
  • For each order checked: the order ID, the result (passed or flagged) and which rule it broke. No shopper name, address, email or payment details are stored.

To check an order, the app reads it from the BigCommerce API when BigCommerce notifies us that it was created. It looks at the payment method, shipping method, customer group, countries, items and totals, compares them with your rules, and keeps only the result above. The checkout script runs in the shopper's browser, reads the cart from your store, and sends nothing to Modvanta. It sets no cookies.

Modvanta Order Automation

The app keeps:

  • The store details and encrypted access token described above, and a random secret that verifies order notifications from BigCommerce. Kept until uninstall.
  • Your settings and rules, and a summary of the last auto-cancel run (orders checked, cancelled, errors).
  • For each new order handled: the order ID, the result and the time, so an order is never handled twice. Deleted after 30 days.
  • An activity log: order ID, what was done, the rule name, the old and new status and a short message. Only the newest 500 entries per store are kept.

To apply your rules, the app reads new orders from the BigCommerce API: total, item count, status, payment method, billing and shipping countries, products (to tell digital items apart), the customer's group, and the number of orders by the same customer, looked up by customer ID or, for guests, by billing email. These details are used only to decide which rule applies and are not stored. The app adds nothing to your storefront and reads nothing from shoppers' browsers.

Modvanta Store Hours & Holiday Mode

The app keeps:

  • The store details and encrypted access token described above, a webhook secret, and your store's time zone. Kept until uninstall.
  • Your schedule, holidays, banner text and settings, and the storefront script built from them. The five most recent script versions are kept so cached pages keep working.
  • For each new order checked: the order ID, the result, and for orders placed while closed, the reason (closed hours, holiday or close now), the holiday name and the time the order was placed. Kept until uninstall.
  • An activity log of the newest 200 entries, such as "order #1234 placed while closed".

The app reads each new order's status, source and creation time from the BigCommerce API, and stores none of the shopper's details. The storefront script contains only your public schedule and banner text and makes no requests to Modvanta. It sets no cookies. When a shopper closes the banner, the browser's session storage remembers that until the tab is closed.

Modvanta Delivery Date & ZIP Checker

The app keeps:

  • The store details and encrypted access token described above, a webhook secret, your store's time zone and storefront domains. Kept until uninstall.
  • Your delivery zones (postcode lists), calendar settings, time slots and the storefront scripts built from them.
  • Delivery choices made at checkout: the cart ID, the chosen date and time slot, and when it was chosen. A choice is deleted when its order is recorded, or after 30 days if the cart never became an order.
  • For each order recorded: the order ID, the delivery date and slot, the shipping postcode, the order status, the check result and any problems found. These rows make up the upcoming deliveries list and the slot capacity counts, and are kept until uninstall. You can export them as CSV.

The app stores no shopper names, street addresses, emails or payment details. On product pages, the postcode a shopper types is checked in the browser when your zone list is small. For large zone lists, or zones limited to categories, it is sent to our server to look up the zone, answered, and not stored or logged. The product page widget remembers the shopper's last postcode in the browser's local storage, on the shopper's own device. The app writes the chosen delivery date to the order in your store (staff notes, order comments if turned on, and order metafields), where it stays under your control.

Modvanta Back in Stock Alerts

This app stores personal data of your shoppers. The app keeps:

  • The store details and encrypted access token described above, a webhook secret, your store name, domain and store email (used as the Reply-To), and the email addresses of staff users who opened the app (used only for test emails). Kept until uninstall.
  • Your settings, email template and, if you use your own mail server, its SMTP settings, with the password encrypted.
  • For each alert request: the shopper's email address, an optional phone number (only if you turn on the phone field; it is stored only and never used to send messages), the product and variant, the product name, the status, the consent text the shopper agreed to and when, the dates the request was made, confirmed and sent, send attempts and the last error, and, if the shopper later ordered the product, the order ID, date and line total.
  • An email log: the partly hidden address (for example jo***@example.com), the status and any error. The newest 1,000 entries per store are kept.
  • Whether each requested item is in stock, and its stock level, to detect restocks.

Lawful basis. Shoppers give their email address themselves to receive an alert. With the consent checkbox turned on (the default), they must tick it, and the consent text and time are saved with the request. With double opt-in, the alert is sent only after they confirm by email. Each shopper gets one alert per request. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. You, as the controller, decide whether to use the checkbox and are responsible for your own privacy notice to shoppers.

Sending. Alerts are sent from our mail server with your store name as the sender name and your store email as Reply-To, or from your own SMTP server if you set one up. Shopper addresses are passed to the email delivery service only to deliver the email.

Conversions. When an order is created, the app reads its billing email (or the customer's email) and products from the BigCommerce API, and compares them with alerts sent in the last 30 days. Only a match is stored, on the alert request.

Shopper IP addresses are used only in memory, as a keyed hash, to limit repeated sign-ups. They are not stored.

Retention.

  • Unconfirmed requests (double opt-in): deleted after 7 days.
  • Waiting requests: kept until the alert is sent, the shopper unsubscribes, you delete them, or you uninstall.
  • Sent, failed and unsubscribed requests: deleted 365 days after the request was made. When a shopper unsubscribes, the phone number is removed at once and the email is kept only so that alert is never sent again.
  • Email log entries: deleted after 365 days.

Deletion on request. You can delete one request, or every request and log entry for an email address, with Erase a shopper (GDPR) in the app. A shopper can also ask you, or write to support@modvantaapps.tech; we will act on it for the store concerned, with your agreement, within 30 days.

On uninstall, every request, email address, phone number, log entry and setting for the store is deleted.

What we never collect

Card numbers or other payment credentials, shopper passwords, or shopper data for marketing. Back in Stock Alerts emails a shopper only about the product they asked about. The apps set no tracking cookies and use no analytics.

Where data is kept and how it is protected

Our servers are hosted by Hostinger. Data is sent over HTTPS only. Access tokens and SMTP passwords are encrypted at rest. Requests from BigCommerce are verified with signed tokens and per-store secrets. Storefront scripts are pinned with integrity hashes, and our public endpoints answer only your store's own domains. Server logs record the request method, path and status, and app events such as a failed email send (with the address partly hidden). They never contain full shopper email addresses or postcodes, and are kept for 30 days.

Deletion

Uninstalling an app deletes everything that app keeps about your store, straight away. Backups that contain it are overwritten within 30 days. Data an app wrote to your BigCommerce orders (statuses, staff notes, order comments, metafields) stays in your store. To ask for deletion or a copy of your data without uninstalling, email support@modvantaapps.tech. We answer within 30 days.

Your rights and your shoppers

Depending on where you are, you and your shoppers may have rights under laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, including access, correction and deletion. Send requests to support@modvantaapps.tech. Back in Stock Alerts is the only app that stores shopper email addresses; for it, see the section above. Delivery Date & ZIP Checker stores order IDs with postcodes and delivery dates. Other shopper requests are handled in BigCommerce or in your Odoo system.

Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the date above. If a change affects how store data is used, we will tell store owners by email before it takes effect.
