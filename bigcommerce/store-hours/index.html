Modvanta Store Hours & Holiday Mode: User Guide

Modvanta Store Hours & Holiday Mode opens and closes your BigCommerce store on a schedule. While the store is closed, shoppers see a banner with the next opening time. You can let them keep ordering with a warning, or block ordering. Every new order is also checked on our server, so an order placed while closed is flagged for you.

Requirements

  • Any BigCommerce store for the banner. Blocking Place order works on Optimized One-Page Checkout.
  • The store owner or a staff user allowed to install apps.

Install

  1. In the control panel, open Apps > Marketplace, find Modvanta Store Hours & Holiday Mode and click Install.
  2. Confirm the permissions. The app opens in your control panel.

Store hours are off when you install, so installing never closes a live store. Set your hours, then tick Turn on store hours and click Save. The app then adds its script to Storefront > Script Manager.

Right now

The top card shows whether the store is open, the store time and the next opening time. Close now closes the store straight away, until you click Reopen now or until a date and time you choose.

General settings

  • While closed: Block ordering, or Accept orders, show a warning.
  • Disable Add to Cart buttons while closed and Block Place order at checkout while closed (block mode only).
  • Order check: flag orders placed while closed (block mode), only log them, or do not check orders.
  • Time zone: empty uses your store's time zone. You can type another, for example Europe/London.
  • Skip orders created by staff in the control panel: on by default.

Weekly opening hours

Use 24-hour times, with up to 6 ranges per day. A closing time earlier than the opening time runs past midnight (22:00 to 02:00). Use 00:00 to 24:00 for a whole day. A day with no ranges is closed all day. If you turn weekly hours off, the store is open every day except holidays.

Holidays and closed dates

Add up to 100 holidays. Each is one or more whole days in store time, first and last day included, and can repeat every year. A holiday closes the store even inside opening hours. Close now comes first, then holidays, then weekly hours.

Closed banner

  • Message, with the placeholders {next_open} (the next opening time) and {holiday} (the holiday name).
  • Background and text colours, and position: top of page or bottom of screen.
  • Show a countdown to opening.

A live preview shows the banner as you type. Shoppers can dismiss it for the rest of their visit.

The order check

The banner and the blocking run in the shopper's browser and use the shopper's clock, so they can be bypassed. The app therefore checks every new order against your schedule at the time the order was placed:

  • Flag (block mode): an order placed while closed is set to Manual Verification Required with a staff note saying when and why. Nothing is cancelled or refunded.
  • Only log: the order is listed in the app and not changed. In warn mode orders are always only logged.
  • Off: no order check.

The app lists orders placed while closed and an activity log. Check whether your store emails shoppers when an order changes status (Settings > Order Notifications) before you use Flag.

Limits

  • Add to Cart is disabled on Cornerstone and on themes that use the common BigCommerce button and form markup. On other themes only the banner shows. Tell us your theme and we can check it.
  • Express wallet buttons such as PayPal or Apple Pay on the cart page are not blocked. The order check flags those orders.
  • The script is added to your default storefront. Other storefront channels are not covered yet.

Uninstalling

Uninstall the app from Apps > My Apps. The script and the webhook are removed, your storefront returns to normal, and your schedule and logs are deleted from our servers. Turning store hours off in the app also removes the script.

Troubleshooting

  • The banner does not show: check that store hours are on and saved, and that the store is closed now in the Right now card. Script changes can take about a minute to reach your storefront.
  • Wrong opening time: check the time zone in General.
  • Orders not flagged: Flag only works in block mode, and staff orders are skipped by default.
